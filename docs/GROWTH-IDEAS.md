# Syllab.in — Growth Ideas & Content Expansion Brief (research-backed)

Synthesized from research on India's top exam/college sites (Careers360, Collegedunia,
Shiksha, Testbook, Adda247, PhysicsWallah, Embibe), a state-by-state board/exam map,
and global edtech leaders (Khan Academy, Brilliant, Duolingo, Quizlet/Anki, Photomath).

**How to read this:** every idea is scored on `traffic potential × content-depth
feasibility × can-we-source-real-data`. ⭐ ratings are net value for a FREE, low-authority
site. **You decide** — this is a menu, not a build order.

## Three hard rules that shape everything below
1. **Evergreen beats date-churn.** Syllabus / pattern / eligibility / study-plan / PYQ-style
   / predictor pages rank for 12+ months. "JEE 2026 exam date / admit card / result" pages
   die in 2 weeks and add nothing long-term. Build the evergreen kind.
2. **Official data only for facts.** Cutoffs, dates, seat matrices, exam patterns must come
   from official board/exam sites (upmsp.edu.in, mahahsscboard.in, reaprajasthan.co.in, etc.)
   or be marked "verify at [official URL], updated annually." Never fabricate. (Syllab already
   generates ORIGINAL exam-pattern questions via importantQuestions.ts — that's the safe model;
   never reproduce copyrighted real papers.)
3. **Substantive, not scaled-thin.** More pages only help if each is genuinely deep
   (1,000+ words, real value). Bulk thin pages trigger the March-2026 domain-quality penalty
   (what hurt LandingPrep). Depth over count, always.

## NEW CONTENT CLUSTER: an "Exams after 12th" hub (under More)
A distinct hub is a good idea — but be selective and evergreen. Ranked by opportunity:

| Cluster | Why | Rating | Notes / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CUET** (Common University Entrance Test) | ~1M+ takers, UNDERSERVED vs JEE/NEET, covers 50+ central unis | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Syllabus, subject-wise pattern, prep strategy, original practice Qs. Evergreen. |
| **Govt exams after 12th** — SSC CHSL, NDA, RRB NTPC, banking clerk | MASSIVE sustained demand, multiple cycles/yr, underserved by CBSE-focused sites | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Eligibility, pattern, syllabus, study plans, GK, original practice. Adda247 owns Hindi here — gap remains. |
| **State engineering/medical entrances** — MHT-CET, KCET, WBJEE, EAMCET (AP/TS), COMEDK, GUJCET, KEAM, REAP, Vyapam | Niche but real; low content competition; you already have EAMCET/some | ⭐⭐⭐ | Deepen existing + add. Cutoffs = official-source only. State-specific long-tail = winnable at low DA. |
| **CLAT / law** (+ AILET) | Growing, high-intent, fewer free resources | ⭐⭐⭐ | Pattern, legal-reasoning practice, prep strategy. |
| **Design/Architecture** — NATA, NID, NIFT, UCEED | Small audience, very low competition | ⭐⭐ | Niche; do later if bandwidth. |
| **Commerce** — CA Foundation, IPMAT, CUET-commerce | Evergreen professional demand | ⭐⭐ | Foundation-level only. |

**Winning content types (evergreen, per top sites):** exam syllabus + subject-wise pattern,
eligibility, **prep/study plans** ("6-week X plan"), **original practice questions & mocks**,
**exam-comparison guides** ("MHT-CET vs JEE — which is harder", "CUET vs IPMAT for BBA" — low
competition, high intent), and **rank/college predictor tools** (you already have predictors).
**Avoid:** exam-date / admit-card / result-notification pages (thin, seasonal, Careers360 owns them).

**Low-DA winning angles:** long-tail + state-specific ("KCET counselling timeline Karnataka",
"colleges accepting JEE 50 percentile"), exam-combination guides, and study-strategy pages.

### Skip / don't build (low ROI or high risk)
- **Standalone resume/CV builder** — solved problem (Naukri/Zety), one-and-done, no retention.
  Only worth it tied to coding-project output. ⭐
- **Robotics** — hardware-dependent (₹5-20k kits); you're software. Skip unless a sim-only or
  a school/hardware partnership. ⭐
- **Livestream/synchronous classes** — payroll + scheduling burden for a free site. Study Room +
  async doubt-solver is better ROI. ⭐
- **Thin exam date/result/admit-card pages, certificates without accreditation, UGC course marketplace.**
