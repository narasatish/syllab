# Syllab.in — Backlink & Outreach Kit

Backlinks are the #1 ceiling on organic traffic. Syllab ranks at position 9–14 for
hundreds of queries mainly because the domain has little authority. On-page work is
mostly done (v208–v216); links are now the lever. **You** post these — never buy links
(post-2026 spam updates = domain penalty). Aim for 5–10 genuine links from real
education sites/communities. Track what you send in a simple sheet.

## Your best assets to share (lead with these, not the homepage)
People link to *tools and resources*, not to a homepage.
- **Printable formula posters** (8, A4 PDF): https://syllab.in/formula-sheets — e.g.
  https://syllab.in/posters/class-12-physics-formulas.html (93 formulas on one page)
- **Story-based lessons**: https://syllab.in/story-lessons (zero real competitors)
- **Free NCERT solutions**: https://syllab.in/ncert-solutions
- **Free student calculators** (CGPA, attendance, percentage): https://syllab.in/calculators
- **Hindi concept notes** (new): https://syllab.in/hi/concepts
- The hook that beats paid competitors: **100% free, no signup, no paywall.**

## Where to post (highest signal first)
1. **r/CBSE, r/JEENEETards, r/Indian_Academia** (Reddit) — share a *specific* free resource
   that answers the thread, not a promo. Comment value first, link second.
2. **Teacher WhatsApp / Telegram groups** — the formula posters are made for this
   ("free printable, share with your class"). Highest conversion to real usage + links.
3. **Facebook groups**: "CBSE Teachers", "Class 10 / 12 CBSE Students", board-specific groups.
4. **ProductHunt** — launch Syllab as "Free AI learning for Indian students, no paywall".
   One good launch = several .edu-adjacent links + traffic spike.
5. **Free education directories**: StartupIndia listing, YourStory/Inc42 startup lists,
   AlternativeTo (list Syllab as a free alternative to BYJU'S/Vedantu/Toppr),
   Saasworthy, education-app roundups.
6. **Quora** — answer real questions ("best free app for Class 10 CBSE?", "free NCERT
   solutions site?") with a genuine, specific answer + one link.
7. **Blogger/YouTuber outreach** — email 15–20 Indian edu creators offering the formula
   PDFs / story lessons as a free resource to share with their audience.

## Rules
- Never buy links or use link farms (domain-level penalty risk).
- Don't post the identical message everywhere — vary it, lead with value.
- Prefer links from real education sites/communities over generic directories.
- One strong ProductHunt launch + 10 teacher-group shares beats 100 low-quality directory links.
