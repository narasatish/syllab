What is a Sector?

  • Region bounded by two radii and an arc
  • Like a 'slice' of pizza or cake
  • Defined by a central angle (θ)

🎙️ A sector is essentially a 'slice' of the circle. It's formed by two radii extending from the center and the arc connecting their endpoints. The angle at the center is key to defining its size.

Area of a Sector Formula

  • Proportional to the central angle
  • Fraction of the total circle area
  • Area = (θ/360°) × πr²

🎙️ The area of a sector is a fraction of the total area of the circle. This fraction is determined by the central angle (θ) out of the total 360 degrees in a circle. It's a simple proportion!

Arc Length Formula

Area of a Segment Formula

  • Area of Sector - Area of Triangle
  • Triangle formed by radii and chord
  • Requires trigonometry for triangle area

🎙️ To find the area of a segment, we take the area of the entire sector and subtract the area of the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord. This often involves using trigonometric formulas for the triangle's area.
