What is Coordination?

  • Working together in an organized way
  • Different parts performing specific roles
  • Ensuring smooth functioning of the whole

  • Automatic, involuntary response to a stimulus
  • Fastest way to react to danger or sudden changes
  • Involves sensory neuron, relay neuron, motor neuron

🎙️ A reflex arc is a super-fast pathway. If you touch something hot, your hand pulls back even before your brain fully registers the pain. This protects us from harm.

Types of Nerves

  • Sensory Nerves: carry information to brain/spinal cord
  • Motor Nerves: carry information from brain/spinal cord to muscles/glands
  • Mixed Nerves: contain both sensory and motor fibres

🎙️ Think of sensory nerves as messengers bringing news TO the command center, and motor nerves as messengers carrying orders FROM the command center to the workers.

Types of Plant Hormones and Their Functions

  • Auxins: cell elongation, phototropism (bending towards light)
  • Gibberellins: stem elongation, seed germination
  • Cytokinins: cell division, delay aging in leaves
  • Abscisic Acid: inhibits growth, promotes dormancy

🎙️ Each plant hormone has a specific job. Auxins help the plant bend towards light, while abscisic acid tells it to stop growing and prepare for winter.

A plant cell is a eukaryotic cell with a rigid cell wall, large vacuole, and chloroplasts.
21 / 25

Plant Movements: Tropic Movements

  • Growth responses towards or away from a stimulus
  • Phototropism: response to light (e.g., stem bends towards light)
  • Geotropism: response to gravity (e.g., roots grow downwards)
  • Hydrotropism: response to water; Thigmotropism: response to touch

🎙️ Have you ever seen a plant bend towards a window? That's phototropism! Roots growing downwards is geotropism, responding to gravity.

Interplay of Nervous and Endocrine Systems

  • These systems are not isolated; they work together
  • The Hypothalamus in the brain links both systems
  • Example: Stress response involves both fast nerve signals and slow hormone release

🎙️ It's important to remember that these systems don't work alone. They constantly communicate and influence each other, creating a truly integrated control system for our bodies.
