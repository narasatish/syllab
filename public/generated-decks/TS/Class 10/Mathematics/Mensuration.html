Cuboid: Definition & Properties

  • Six rectangular faces.
  • 12 edges, 8 vertices.
  • Length (l), Breadth (b), Height (h).

🎙️ A cuboid is a 3D shape with six rectangular faces. Think of a brick or a shoebox. It has distinct length, breadth, and height.

Cuboid: Surface Area (TSA & LSA)

  • TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl) square units.
  • LSA = 2h(l + b) square units.
  • TSA: Total surface, LSA: Side surfaces.

🎙️ The Total Surface Area (TSA) is the sum of the areas of all six faces. The Lateral Surface Area (LSA) is the sum of the areas of the four side faces, excluding top and bottom.

Cuboid: Volume

  • Space occupied by the cuboid.
  • Volume = l × b × h cubic units.
  • Represents capacity.

🎙️ Volume tells us how much space a cuboid occupies or how much it can hold. It's calculated by multiplying its length, breadth, and height.

Cube: Definition & Formulas

  • Special cuboid, all faces are squares.
  • All sides (edges) are equal, let side = 'a'.
  • TSA = 6a²; LSA = 4a²; Volume = a³.

🎙️ A cube is a special type of cuboid where all its faces are squares, meaning all its sides (edges) are equal. Its formulas are simpler due to this equality.

Cylinder: Definition & Properties

  • Two circular bases.
  • One curved surface.
  • Radius (r) and Height (h).

🎙️ A cylinder is a 3D shape with two circular bases and a curved surface. Think of a soft drink can or a pipe. It has a radius and a height.

Cylinder: Surface Area (TSA & CSA)

  • CSA = 2πrh square units.
  • TSA = 2πr(r + h) square units.
  • CSA: Curved surface, TSA: Total surface.

🎙️ The Curved Surface Area (CSA) is the area of the curved part. The Total Surface Area (TSA) includes the CSA plus the areas of the two circular bases.

Cylinder: Volume

  • Volume = Area of base × height.
  • Volume = πr²h cubic units.
  • Capacity of the cylinder.

🎙️ The volume of a cylinder represents how much liquid it can hold or the space it occupies. It's calculated by multiplying the area of its base by its height.

Cone: Definition & Properties

  • Circular base, single vertex.
  • Radius (r), Height (h), Slant Height (l).
  • l² = r² + h².

🎙️ A cone is a 3D shape with a circular base and a single vertex at the top. Think of an ice cream cone or a party hat. It has radius, height, and slant height.

🍦
Ice Cream Cone
17 / 25

Cone: Surface Area (TSA & CSA)

  • CSA = πrl square units.
  • TSA = πr(l + r) square units.
  • CSA: Curved surface, TSA: Total surface.

🎙️ The Curved Surface Area (CSA) of a cone involves its slant height. The Total Surface Area (TSA) also includes the area of its circular base.

🔺
Triangular Shape

Cone: Volume

  • Volume = (1/3) × Area of base × height.
  • Volume = (1/3)πr²h cubic units.
  • One-third of a cylinder's volume.

🎙️ The volume of a cone is related to the volume of a cylinder with the same base and height. It's exactly one-third of the cylinder's volume.

Sphere: Definition & Formulas

  • Perfectly round 3D object.
  • Every point equidistant from center.
  • Surface Area = 4πr²; Volume = (4/3)πr³.

🎙️ A sphere is a perfectly round 3D object, where every point on its surface is equidistant from its center. Think of a ball. It only has a radius.

Hemisphere: Definition & Formulas

  • Exactly half of a sphere.
  • Curved surface + flat circular base.
  • CSA = 2πr²; TSA = 3πr²; Volume = (2/3)πr³.

🎙️ A hemisphere is exactly half of a sphere. It has a flat circular base and a curved surface. Its formulas are derived from those of a sphere.
