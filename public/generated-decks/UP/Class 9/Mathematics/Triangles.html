What is a Triangle?

  • A polygon with three sides.
  • Three vertices (corners).
  • Three angles.

🎙️ A triangle is the simplest polygon. It's a closed figure made up of three line segments. Remember, 'tri' means three, so it has three sides, three vertices, and three angles.

🔺
A basic triangle shape
3 / 25

Elements of a Triangle

  • Vertices: Points where sides meet (A, B, C).
  • Sides: Line segments forming the triangle (AB, BC, CA).
  • Angles: Formed at each vertex (∠A, ∠B, ∠C).

🎙️ Consider a triangle ABC. The points A, B, and C are its vertices. The line segments AB, BC, and CA are its sides. And the angles formed at these vertices are ∠A, ∠B, and ∠C.

🎙️ We can classify triangles based on how long their sides are. This is a very common way to categorize them and helps us understand their properties better.

Equilateral Triangle

  • All three sides are equal in length.
  • All three angles are also equal (60° each).
  • A perfectly balanced triangle.

🎙️ In an equilateral triangle, if one side is 5 cm, then all other sides are also 5 cm. Because all sides are equal, all its angles are also equal, each measuring 60 degrees.

✨
All sides equal
7 / 25

Isosceles Triangle

  • Two sides are equal in length.
  • The angles opposite to the equal sides are also equal.
  • Like two legs of the same length.

🎙️ An isosceles triangle has at least two sides of the same length. The angles that are opposite to these equal sides are also equal. This is a key property to remember.

↔️
Two sides equal
8 / 25

Scalene Triangle

  • All three sides are of different lengths.
  • All three angles are also different.
  • No equal sides or angles.

🎙️ A scalene triangle is the opposite of an equilateral triangle. Here, every side has a different length, and consequently, every angle has a different measure.

🚶
All sides different

Acute-angled Triangle

  • All three angles are acute (less than 90°).
  • All angles are 'sharp' or 'small'.
  • Example: An equilateral triangle is always acute-angled.

🎙️ In an acute-angled triangle, every single angle inside the triangle is smaller than 90 degrees. Think of it as having three 'sharp' corners.

🤏
All angles < 90°
12 / 25

Right-angled Triangle

  • One angle is exactly 90° (a right angle).
  • The other two angles are acute.
  • The side opposite the right angle is called the hypotenuse.

🎙️ This is a very important type of triangle. The presence of one 90-degree angle defines it. The side opposite to this right angle is the longest side, known as the hypotenuse.

🔲
One angle = 90°
13 / 25

Obtuse-angled Triangle

  • One angle is obtuse (greater than 90°).
  • The other two angles are acute.
  • One 'wide' angle.

🎙️ An obtuse-angled triangle has one angle that is wider than 90 degrees. Because the sum of angles must be 180 degrees, it can only have one such obtuse angle.

👐
One angle > 90°

Angle Sum Property of a Triangle

  • The sum of all three interior angles of any triangle is always 180°.
  • ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°.
  • A fundamental property.

🎙️ This is one of the most important properties of triangles. No matter what type of triangle it is, if you add up its three interior angles, the total will always be 180 degrees.

➕
Angles add up to 180°

Worked Example: Finding a Missing Angle

  • Given two angles of a triangle are 70° and 50°.
  • Find the third angle.

🎙️ Let the three angles be x, y, and z. We know x + y + z = 180°. If x = 70° and y = 50°, then 70° + 50° + z = 180°. This means 120° + z = 180°, so z = 60°. The third angle is 60°.

Exterior Angle Property

  • An exterior angle is formed by extending one side of a triangle.
  • It is equal to the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
  • Exterior angle = Opposite interior angle 1 + Opposite interior angle 2.

🎙️ Imagine extending one side of a triangle outwards. The angle formed outside the triangle is called an exterior angle. This exterior angle has a special relationship with the two interior angles that are not adjacent to it.

↗️
Angle outside the triangle

Worked Example: Exterior Angle

  • Interior opposite angles are 60° and 80°.
  • Find the exterior angle.

🎙️ If the two interior opposite angles are 60° and 80°, then their sum is 60° + 80° = 140°. Therefore, the exterior angle is 140°. This property simplifies finding exterior angles greatly.

Introduction to Congruence of Triangles

  • Two triangles are congruent if they are exactly the same shape and size.
  • They can be placed on top of each other and perfectly match.
  • Think of identical twins!

🎙️ Congruence is a very important concept. It means that two figures are identical in every way – their corresponding sides are equal, and their corresponding angles are equal. We'll dive deeper into this in future lessons.
