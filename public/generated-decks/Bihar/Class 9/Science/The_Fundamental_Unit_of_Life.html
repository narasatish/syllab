The Cell Theory: Schleiden and Schwann

  • Matthias Schleiden (1838): All plants are made of cells
  • Theodor Schwann (1839): All animals are made of cells
  • Initial Cell Theory formulated

🎙️ In the 19th century, two scientists, Schleiden and Schwann, independently proposed that both plants and animals are fundamentally composed of cells, laying the groundwork for the Cell Theory.

Rudolf Virchow: Cells from Pre-existing Cells

  • In 1855, added a crucial point to Cell Theory
  • Stated 'Omnis cellula e cellula'
  • Means: All cells arise from pre-existing cells

🎙️ Rudolf Virchow completed the modern Cell Theory by stating that cells don't just appear spontaneously; they always come from other cells through division. This was a major breakthrough!

  • Provides structural support and protection
  • Prevents bursting in hypotonic solutions

🎙️ Plant cells have an additional rigid outer layer called the cell wall. This strong wall provides structural support, prevents over-expansion, and protects the plant cell from external stress.

Chapter Recap: The Fundamental Unit of Life

  • Cells are the basic structural and functional units of life.
  • Discovered by Hooke (dead cells) and Leeuwenhoek (living cells).
  • Cell theory states all living things are made of cells and arise from pre-existing cells.
  • Organisms are unicellular (one cell) or multicellular (many cells).
  • Cells have a plasma membrane, nucleus, and cytoplasm.
  • Diffusion and osmosis are key transport mechanisms.

🎙️ Today, we explored the incredible world of cells, from their discovery to their basic structure and functions. Remember, every living thing, from the smallest bacteria to the largest whale, is made of these fundamental units!
