What is a Triangle?

  • A polygon with three sides.
  • Has three vertices and three angles.
  • Sum of angles is always 180 degrees.

🎙️ A triangle is one of the most basic and important geometric shapes. It's a closed figure made of three line segments, forming three corners called vertices and three angles inside. Remember, a key property is that all its interior angles add up to 180 degrees.

Types of Triangles by Sides

  • Scalene: No sides equal.

🎙️ We can classify triangles based on the lengths of their sides. An equilateral triangle is perfectly balanced with all sides equal. An isosceles triangle has two equal sides, while a scalene triangle has all sides of different lengths.

What are Congruent Figures?

  • Same shape AND same size.
  • One can be placed exactly over the other.
  • They are identical copies.

🎙️ Congruent figures are like identical twins in geometry. If you have two figures that are congruent, it means they have exactly the same shape and the same size. You could pick one up and place it perfectly on top of the other.

Identical Twins

Congruent Triangles

  • Corresponding sides are equal.
  • Corresponding angles are equal.
  • Written as △ABC ≅ △PQR.

🎙️ For triangles, congruence means all three corresponding sides are equal in length, and all three corresponding angles are equal in measure. If two triangles are congruent, they are essentially the same triangle, just possibly in a different orientation.

🤝
Perfect Match

SSS Congruence Rule

  • Side-Side-Side.
  • If all three sides of one triangle are equal to the three corresponding sides of another triangle, then the triangles are congruent.

🎙️ The SSS rule is a powerful way to prove congruence. If you can show that all three sides of one triangle match exactly with the three corresponding sides of another triangle, you don't need to check the angles – the triangles must be congruent.

1Side 1 = Side 1
2Side 2 = Side 2
3Side 3 = Side 3
9 / 25

SAS Congruence Rule

  • Side-Angle-Side.
  • If two sides and the included angle of one triangle are equal to the two corresponding sides and the included angle of another triangle, then the triangles are congruent.

🎙️ The SAS rule tells us that if two sides and the angle *between* them in one triangle are equal to the corresponding parts in another triangle, then the triangles are congruent. The 'included angle' part is very important here!

1Side = Side
2Included Angle = Included Angle
3Side = Side
10 / 25

ASA and AAS Congruence Rules

  • ASA: Angle-Side-Angle.
  • AAS: Angle-Angle-Side.
  • If two angles and one side of one triangle are equal to the corresponding parts of another triangle, then the triangles are congruent.

🎙️ These two rules are closely related. ASA means two angles and the side *between* them. AAS means two angles and a non-included side. In both cases, if these parts match, the triangles are congruent. Remember to correctly identify the corresponding parts.

1Angle = Angle
2Side = Side
3Angle = Angle

What are Similar Figures?

  • Same shape, but not necessarily same size.
  • One is an enlargement or reduction of the other.
  • Corresponding angles are equal, corresponding sides are proportional.

🎙️ Similar figures are like photographs of the same object, but one might be zoomed in or out. They maintain the same shape but can be different in size. This means their angles are identical, but their sides are in proportion.

Similar Triangles

  • Corresponding angles are equal.
  • Ratio of corresponding sides is equal (proportional).
  • Written as △ABC ~ △PQR.

🎙️ For triangles to be similar, two conditions must be met: first, all their corresponding angles must be equal. Second, the lengths of their corresponding sides must be in the same ratio. This ratio is called the scale factor.

AAA / AA Similarity Rule

  • Angle-Angle-Angle.
  • If all three corresponding angles of two triangles are equal, then the triangles are similar.
  • Since sum of angles is 180°, if two angles are equal, the third must also be equal (AA Similarity).

🎙️ This is the most straightforward similarity rule. If you can show that all three angles of one triangle are equal to the three angles of another, then they are similar. In fact, if just two pairs of corresponding angles are equal, the triangles are already similar (AA rule).

1Angle 1 = Angle 1
2Angle 2 = Angle 2
3Angle 3 = Angle 3
15 / 25

SSS Similarity Rule

  • Side-Side-Side Proportionality.
  • If the ratio of the corresponding sides of two triangles is equal, then the triangles are similar.

🎙️ The SSS similarity rule is about proportionality, not equality. If the ratios of all three pairs of corresponding sides are the same, meaning one triangle is a scaled version of the other, then the triangles are similar.

1Side A / Side P = k
2Side B / Side Q = k
3Side C / Side R = k
16 / 25

SAS Similarity Rule

  • Side-Angle-Side Proportionality.
  • If one angle of a triangle is equal to one angle of another triangle, AND the sides including these angles are proportional, then the triangles are similar.

🎙️ Similar to the congruence rule, but with proportionality. If an angle in one triangle matches an angle in another, and the sides *forming* that angle are in proportion, then the triangles are similar. Again, the 'included angle' is key.

1Side A / Side P = k
2Included Angle = Included Angle
3Side B / Side Q = k

Measuring Heights with Similarity

  • Shadows of objects at the same time are proportional.
  • We can find the height of a tall building or tree.
  • Uses the concept of similar triangles formed by objects and their shadows.

🎙️ Imagine you want to find the height of a tall tree without climbing it. You can use similar triangles! By comparing the length of the tree's shadow to your own shadow, and knowing your height, you can calculate the tree's height because the triangles formed are similar.

Worked Example: Finding Unknown Sides

  • Given △ABC ~ △PQR.
  • If AB=4, BC=6, CA=8 and PQ=2, find QR and RP.
  • Solution: Since triangles are similar, corresponding sides are proportional.

🎙️ Let's apply what we've learned. If we know two triangles are similar, we can set up ratios of their corresponding sides. This allows us to find unknown side lengths easily, as shown in this example where we found QR and RP.

1AB/PQ = BC/QR = CA/RP
24/2 = 6/QR = 8/RP
32 = 6/QR => QR = 3
42 = 8/RP => RP = 4

Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)

  • Also known as Thales Theorem.
  • If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

🎙️ The BPT is a foundational theorem for similar triangles. It states that if you draw a line inside a triangle parallel to one of its sides, it will cut the other two sides proportionally. This creates a smaller triangle similar to the original one.

⚖️
Balanced Ratios

Converse of Basic Proportionality Theorem

  • If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.

🎙️ The converse of BPT is equally important. It's the reverse statement: if you observe that a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, you can conclude that this line must be parallel to the third side. This is useful for proving lines are parallel.

Area of Similar Triangles

  • The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.

🎙️ This theorem connects the areas of similar triangles to their side lengths. If one triangle has sides twice as long as a similar triangle, its area will be four times larger (2 squared). This relationship is very useful in many problems.

📐
Area vs. Side Ratio

Pythagoras Theorem (Recap)

  • For a right-angled triangle.
  • The square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.
  • a² + b² = c²

🎙️ You've learned about Pythagoras Theorem in earlier classes. It's crucial for right-angled triangles. It provides a fundamental relationship between the lengths of the three sides, allowing us to find an unknown side if two are known.

📏
Right Angle Rule
23 / 25

Converse of Pythagoras Theorem

  • If in a triangle, the square of one side is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides, then the angle opposite the first side is a right angle.

🎙️ Just like with BPT, there's a converse to Pythagoras Theorem. If you find that the sides of a triangle satisfy the a² + b² = c² relationship, you can confidently say that the triangle is a right-angled triangle, with the right angle opposite side 'c'.
