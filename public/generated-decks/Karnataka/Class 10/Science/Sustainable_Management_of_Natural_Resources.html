Two Types: Renewable & Non-Renewable

  • Renewable: Replenish naturally over time (e.g., sunlight, wind, water).
  • Non-renewable: Finite, cannot be replaced easily (e.g., coal, petroleum, minerals).

🎙️ Imagine having a magic pen that refills itself – that's like a renewable resource! But a normal pen, once its ink is gone, is like a non-renewable resource. We must be extra careful with the non-renewable ones as they take millions of years to form.

  • Recycle: Make new from old.

Reduce: Use Less, Save More!

  • Minimising consumption of resources.
  • Switching off lights/fans when not needed.
  • Using public transport or cycling.

🎙️ Reducing means being mindful of how much we use. Just like you turn off the tap while brushing your teeth, we can reduce our water usage. Turning off unnecessary lights saves electricity, which in turn saves coal used to generate it.

Reuse: Give Things a Second Life!

  • Using items again instead of throwing them away.
  • Using old jars for storage.
  • Donating old clothes or books.

🎙️ Reusing is about creativity! Instead of buying new, can you find another purpose for something? An old t-shirt can become a cleaning cloth, or plastic bottles can be used as planters. This saves resources and reduces waste.

Recycle: Transform & Create!

  • Processing waste materials into new products.
  • Separating plastic, paper, glass waste at home.
  • Reduces demand for raw materials.

🎙️ Recycling is like magic! Old newspapers become new paper, plastic bottles become new plastic items. This requires collecting, sorting, and processing waste. Remember to separate your waste at home – it makes recycling much easier!

Power from the Past: Coal & Petroleum

  • Primary source of energy for electricity and transport.

A Million-Year Process, A Finite Supply

  • Formed from dead organic matter under heat & pressure.
  • Takes millions of years to form.
  • Burning them releases greenhouse gases.

🎙️ Imagine waiting millions of years for something to form! That's how long it takes for coal and petroleum. This long formation time means their supply is truly finite, and we are consuming them much faster than they can be made.

Using Wisely: Judicious Use

  • Conserve fossil fuels to extend their availability.
  • Shift to renewable energy sources (solar, wind).
  • Reduce energy consumption at home and work.

🎙️ Since coal and petroleum are limited, we must use them wisely. This means not just reducing our consumption, but also actively exploring and adopting cleaner, renewable energy sources like solar and wind power for a sustainable future.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Confusing renewable and non-renewable resources.
  • Thinking 'Recycle' is the only solution (forgetting Reduce/Reuse).
  • Believing conservation is only the government's responsibility.

🎙️ Many students mix up the resource types or think recycling is enough. Remember, 'Reduce' is always the first and best step. Also, conservation is a collective effort, not just for authorities. Every individual plays a part.
