Aerobic Respiration: Energy Harvest

  • Glucose -> Pyruvate (cytoplasm).
  • Pyruvate -> CO2 + Water + ATP (mitochondria).
  • Requires oxygen.

🎙️ In aerobic respiration, glucose is first broken down into pyruvate in the cytoplasm. Then, in the presence of oxygen, pyruvate is further broken down in the mitochondria to produce carbon dioxide, water, and a large amount of ATP (energy).
