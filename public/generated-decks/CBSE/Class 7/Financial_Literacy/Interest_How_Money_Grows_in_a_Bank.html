  • Represented by 'T' in formulas.

Key Term: Rate of Interest

  • The percentage at which interest is calculated.
  • Always expressed 'per annum' (per year).
  • Represented by 'R' in formulas.

🎙️ The Rate of Interest tells you how much extra money you'll get as a percentage of your principal, typically for one year. For example, a 5% rate means you get 5% of your principal as interest each year.

Key Term: Simple Interest

  • Calculated only on the original principal amount.
  • Interest remains the same each year.
  • It is the easiest type of interest to calculate.

🎙️ Simple Interest means the bank calculates interest only on the money you initially deposited. It doesn't add the previous year's interest to the principal for the next calculation, making it straightforward.

➡️
Straightforward

Formula for Simple Interest

  • Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100
  • I = (P × R × T) / 100

🎙️ This is the magic formula! Just plug in the values for Principal, Rate, and Time. Remember, the rate 'R' is already a percentage, so we divide by 100 in the formula.

1P: Principal
2R: Rate
3T: Time
4Divide by 100
14 / 25

Worked Example 1: Calculating Interest

  • P = ₹1000, R = 5%, T = 2 years
  • I = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100
  • I = ₹100

🎙️ Let's try an example. If you deposit ₹1000 for 2 years at 5% interest per year, you'll earn ₹100 as interest. That's extra money just for saving!

Worked Example 2: More Practice

Real-life Example: Saving for a Toy

What is Amount?

  • The total money you get back.
  • Principal + Interest = Amount
  • Represented by 'A' in calculations.

🎙️ Amount is the final sum you receive when you take your money out of the bank. It's your original money plus all the interest you've earned, giving you a bigger total.

➡️
Total Money

Calculating Amount

  • P = ₹1000, I = ₹100 (from Ex. 1)
  • Amount = Principal + Interest
  • Amount = ₹1000 + ₹100 = ₹1100

🎙️ Using our first example, if your principal was ₹1000 and you earned ₹100 interest, your total amount after the period would be ₹1100. This is the real gain from saving.
