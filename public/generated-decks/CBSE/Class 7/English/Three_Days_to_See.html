  • An inspiring writer and activist
  • Taught to communicate by Anne Sullivan

Visualizing vs. Seeing

  • Looking is passive, observing is active
  • One brings information, the other brings understanding
  • Truly seeing involves your mind and heart

🎙️ There's a big difference between just 'looking' at something and truly 'observing' it. When you look, you see the surface. When you observe, you understand the depth, the details, and the story.

  • Forgetting the empathy aspect
  • Not seeing the call to action for us
