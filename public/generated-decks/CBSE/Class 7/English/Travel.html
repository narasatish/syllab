What Exactly is "Travel"?

  • Moving from one place to another.
  • Often for a specific purpose.
  • Can be short or long distances.

🎙️ Think of it like moving your game piece on a Ludo board. You start at one point and move to another. Travel is similar, but in the real world!

Different Kinds of Journeys

  • Local: Within your city/town.
  • National: Within your country.
  • International: To another country.

🎙️ Imagine going from your home to your friend's house – that's local. Going from Delhi to Mumbai is national. And going from India to Japan is international!

Common Travel Talk Mistakes

  • Confusing 'travel' (verb) with 'trip' (noun).
  • Incorrect prepositions: 'travel *to* a place', not 'travel *in* a place' (when going there).
  • Using 'journey' for very short, daily commutes.

🎙️ It's easy to mix up similar words. Remember, you 'travel' to Delhi, and you 'take a trip' to Delhi. A 'journey' usually implies a longer, more significant travel experience.
