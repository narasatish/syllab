What is Travel?

  • Moving from one place to another.
  • Can be for short or long distances.
  • Often involves exploring new environments.

🎙️ Simply put, travel means going from one location to another. It could be a short trip to a nearby town or a long journey across countries. The main idea is movement and often, discovery.

Mode 1: Road Travel

  • Using vehicles like cars, buses, bikes.
  • Flexible and can reach many places.
  • Good for short to medium distances.

Important Travel Vocabulary

  • **Itinerary**: A planned route or journey.
  • **Luggage**: Bags and suitcases.
  • **Passport**: Document for international travel.
  • **Visa**: Official permission to enter a country.

🎙️ Learning some common travel words makes your journeys easier. An 'itinerary' is your trip plan, and 'luggage' refers to your bags. For international trips, 'passport' and 'visa' are crucial documents.

Safety Tips While Travelling

  • Keep your belongings safe.
  • Inform family about your plans.
  • Be aware of your surroundings.
  • Carry emergency contact numbers.

🎙️ Safety is very important when you travel. Always keep an eye on your bags, let someone know your travel plans, and be aware of what's happening around you. Having emergency numbers handy is also a good idea.
