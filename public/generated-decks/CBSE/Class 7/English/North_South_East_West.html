Understanding North

  • Often points towards the top of a map.
  • Where the North Star (Dhruv Tara) is.
  • Opposite to South.

🎙️ North is generally considered the 'up' direction on most maps. If you face the North Star at night, you are facing North. It's a very important reference point.

Understanding South

  • Directly opposite to North.
  • Points towards the bottom of a map.
  • Think of the South Pole!

🎙️ South is always directly opposite to North. If you know where North is, South is easily found by looking in the exact opposite direction. It's like a mirror image.

Understanding East

  • The direction where the sun rises.
  • To your right if you face North.
  • Opposite to West.

🎙️ East is a very easy direction to find! Just look for where the sun rises in the morning. This is a reliable way to determine East anywhere you are.

☀️

Understanding West

  • The direction where the sun sets.
  • To your left if you face North.
  • Opposite to East.

🎙️ Just as the sun rises in the East, it always sets in the West. So, if you know where the sun sets, you've found West! It's directly opposite to East.

Sub-Directions (Intermediate Directions)

  • Between the main directions.
  • Northeast, Northwest, Southeast, Southwest.
  • They help us be more precise!

🎙️ Sometimes, just North, South, East, or West isn't enough. We need to be more specific! That's where sub-directions come in, like Northeast or Southwest.

Understanding Northeast

  • Between North and East.
  • Example: The Himalayas are to the Northeast of India.
  • A mix of two main directions.

🎙️ Northeast is the direction exactly halfway between North and East. Think of it as a diagonal path. For example, many beautiful regions of India are in the Northeast.

Understanding Northwest

  • Between North and West.
  • Example: Many historical forts are in the Northwest of India.
  • Points towards the upper-left on a map.

🎙️ Northwest is the direction between North and West. It's like going diagonally up and to the left on a map. Rajasthan, for instance, is in the Northwest of India.

Understanding Southeast

  • Between South and East.
  • Example: The Bay of Bengal is to the Southeast of India.
  • Points towards the lower-right on a map.

🎙️ Southeast is the direction between South and East. Imagine a diagonal line going down and to the right. The beautiful coastal areas of India often lie in the Southeast.

Understanding Southwest

🎙️ Southwest is the direction between South and West. It's like moving diagonally down and to the left. States like Kerala are located in the Southwest of India.

Using Directions in Sentences (Prepositions)

  • We use words like 'to the', 'in the', 'of' with directions.
  • Example: 'The temple is *to the east of* the market.'
  • Helps describe relative positions.

🎙️ In English, we use specific prepositions to talk about directions. Understanding these phrases helps us give clear instructions and describe locations accurately to others.

Example Sentences

  • My school is *in the North* of the city.
  • The sun rises *in the East*.
  • Go *towards the West* to find the park.
  • Kolkata is *to the East of* Delhi.

🎙️ Here are some practical examples of how we use directions in everyday sentences. Notice how the prepositions change depending on the context, like 'in the' or 'to the'.

Map Reading with Directions

  • Maps usually have a North arrow.
  • Use this arrow to orient your map.
  • Then find other places using N, S, E, W.

🎙️ Maps are designed to use directions. Always look for the 'North arrow' on a map. Once you know North, you can easily find everything else!
