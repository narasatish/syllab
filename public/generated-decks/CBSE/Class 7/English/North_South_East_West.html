The Four Cardinal Directions

  • North (N)
  • South (S)
  • East (E)
  • West (W)
  • These are the main points on a compass

🎙️ These four directions are known as the cardinal directions. They form the basic framework for all our spatial understanding, just like the primary colours form the basis for all other colours.

Understanding North

  • Usually at the top of a map
  • Points towards the North Pole
  • Often associated with cold regions

🎙️ North is typically considered the 'up' direction on most maps. If you imagine standing on the Earth, the North Pole is in this direction, which is why we often associate it with colder climates.

  • Many rivers like the Ganga originate from the North.

🎙️ Think of the towering Himalayas, home to some of the world's highest peaks. These magnificent mountains form the northern boundary of our country, making it a natural landmark for the North.

Understanding South

  • Opposite to North
  • Points towards the South Pole
  • Often associated with warmer regions

🎙️ South is directly opposite to North. If you travel south from India, you would eventually reach the warmer waters of the Indian Ocean and eventually the South Pole, far down on the globe.

  • The Indian Ocean lies to the South of India.

🎙️ Kanyakumari, in Tamil Nadu, is a beautiful coastal town famous for its sunrise and sunset views over three seas. It marks the extreme south of our mainland, a perfect real-world example of the South direction.

Understanding East

  • Where the Sun rises
  • To your right if you face North
  • Associated with new beginnings

🎙️ The East is a very familiar direction because it's where we see the sun rise every morning. If you stand facing North, East will always be to your right side.

🎙️ In India, the state of Arunachal Pradesh is known as the 'Land of the Dawn-lit Mountains' because it is where the sun's first rays touch our country each day. This clearly shows us the East.

Understanding West

  • Where the Sun sets
  • Opposite to East
  • To your left if you face North

🎙️ Just as the sun rises in the East, it sets in the West, marking the end of the day. If you are facing North, West will be directly to your left side.

Beyond Cardinal: Ordinal Directions

  • North-East (NE)
  • South-East (SE)
  • South-West (SW)
  • North-West (NW)
  • These provide more precise locations

🎙️ While the four cardinal directions are fundamental, sometimes we need to be more specific. That's where ordinal directions come in, helping us pinpoint locations between the main points.

Why Use Ordinal Directions?

  • For greater accuracy
  • When a location isn't exactly N, S, E, or W
  • Important for navigation and mapping

🎙️ Imagine trying to describe a house that isn't exactly north or east of you, but somewhere in between. Ordinal directions like North-East allow us to describe its position with much greater accuracy and clarity.

Using Directions in Sentences

  • My house is east of the market.
  • The library is to the north-west of the school.
  • Go south for two blocks, then turn east.

🎙️ We use directions as adjectives or adverbs to describe where things are or which way to go. Practice forming sentences using these words to make your descriptions clearer and more precise.

Real-Life: Reading Maps

  • Look for the compass rose on the map.
  • Identify North to orient the map correctly.
  • Use directions to locate places and routes.

🎙️ Maps always have a compass rose or a North arrow to help you orient yourself. Once you know which way is North, you can easily find East, South, and West, making map reading much simpler.
