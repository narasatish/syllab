Animals Around Us: A Quick Look at Different Groups

  • Mammals: Animals that give birth to live young and feed them milk (e.g., dogs, elephants).
  • Birds: Feathered creatures with wings, lay eggs (e.g., parrots, sparrows).
  • Reptiles: Cold-blooded animals with scales (e.g., snakes, lizards).
  • Insects: Small creatures with six legs and usually wings (e.g., bees, butterflies).

🎙️ Dr. Dolittle interacted with many types of animals. It's good to remember the broad categories they fall into, as each group has unique characteristics and needs.

The Creator: Hugh Lofting, The Author

  • The charming stories of Dr. Dolittle were written by Hugh Lofting.
  • He was an English author who served in World War I.
  • He wrote these imaginative letters to his children from the trenches to cheer them up.

🎙️ It's fascinating to know that these wonderful stories were born out of a desire to bring joy during a difficult time. Hugh Lofting used his imagination to create a world of hope and kindness for his children.
