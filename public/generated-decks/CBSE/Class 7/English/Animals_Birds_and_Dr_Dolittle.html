What is a Pet?

  • An animal kept for companionship or pleasure.
  • Usually lives with humans and is cared for by them.
  • Examples: dogs, cats, rabbits, fish.

🎙️ A pet is an animal that lives in our homes and becomes part of our family. We provide them with food, shelter, and love, and in return, they give us companionship and joy.

Wild Animals

  • Animals that live independently in nature.
  • They are not tamed or domesticated by humans.
  • Examples: lions, tigers, elephants, bears.

🎙️ Wild animals live in their natural habitats like forests, jungles, and oceans. They find their own food and shelter and are not dependent on humans for survival.

🐅
Untamed nature
6 / 25

Wild vs. Domestic

  • Wild animals live freely, domestic animals live with humans.
  • Wild animals hunt/forage, domestic animals are fed by humans.
  • Wild animals retain natural instincts, domestic animals are often trained.

🎙️ The main difference lies in their interaction with humans. Domestic animals have been bred and tamed over generations, while wild animals remain untouched by human intervention in their daily lives.

Bird Habitats

Concept: Empathy

  • The ability to understand and share the feelings of another.
  • Putting yourself in someone else's shoes.
  • It helps us connect better with others, including animals.

🎙️ Empathy is a very important quality. It allows us to see the world from another's perspective and respond with kindness and understanding, whether it's a friend or an animal in distress.

Caring for Animals

  • Provide food, water, and shelter.
  • Ensure they get proper medical care.
  • Show them love, respect, and kindness.

🎙️ Whether they are pets or animals we encounter in nature, it's our duty to treat all living creatures with care. They depend on us for their well-being.

🤲
Our responsibility

Animal Welfare

  • Ensuring animals are treated humanely and have a good quality of life.
  • Protecting them from cruelty and neglect.
  • Promoting their health and natural behaviour.

🎙️ Animal welfare means making sure animals are healthy, comfortable, well-fed, safe, and able to express their natural behaviours. It's about their overall happiness and well-being.
