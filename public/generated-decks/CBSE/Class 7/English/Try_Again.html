Stanza 1 & 2: The First Steps

  • Tis a lesson you should heed,
  • Try again;
  • If at first you don’t succeed,
  • Try again.

🎙️ The poem starts with a direct instruction: 'Tis a lesson you should heed,' meaning 'This is a lesson you should pay attention to.' And what is that lesson? To 'Try again' if you don't succeed initially.

🎙️ Let's quickly understand some words. 'Heed' means to listen and follow advice. 'Succeed' means to accomplish something. So, if your first attempt isn't successful, the advice is clear.

Identifying Poetic Devices: Rhyme Scheme

  • The pattern of rhymes at the end of lines.
  • In 'Try Again', each stanza follows a specific pattern.
  • Example: heed (A), again (B), succeed (A), again (B).

🎙️ A rhyme scheme helps give a poem its musicality. In 'Try Again', you'll notice a consistent AABB rhyme scheme in each four-line stanza, making it easy to read and remember.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Feeling ashamed of mistakes.
  • Comparing your progress unfairly with others.
