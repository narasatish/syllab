Exploring Poetic Devices

  • Rhyme Scheme: AABB (e.g., succeed/indeed, rule/school).
  • Repetition: 'Try, try, try again' emphasizes the core message.
  • Alliteration: 'If at first you don't succeed' (s sound).

🎙️ The poem uses simple but effective poetic devices. The clear AABB rhyme scheme makes it easy to read and remember, and the repetition of 'Try, try, try again' drills the main idea into our minds.
