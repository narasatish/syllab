Literary Device: Imagery

  • Language that appeals to the senses.
  • Creates vivid pictures in the reader's mind.
  • Uses descriptive words and phrases.

🎙️ Imagery is like painting a picture with words. Authors use descriptive language to help us see, hear, smell, taste, and feel what's happening in the story or poem, making it more engaging.

🖼️
Painting Pictures with Words

Example of Imagery in Poetry

  • Line: 'The icy wind whispered secrets through the barren trees.'
  • Visual: 'icy wind', 'barren trees'
  • Auditory: 'whispered secrets'

🎙️ In this example, 'icy wind' makes you feel the cold, and 'whispered secrets' helps you hear the sound. This combination creates a strong image and feeling for the reader, drawing them into the scene.

Literary Device: Metaphor

  • A figure of speech directly comparing two unlike things.
  • States one thing IS another, without 'like' or 'as'.
  • Adds depth and meaning to descriptions.

🎙️ A metaphor is a powerful way to compare two very different things by saying one *is* the other. It helps us understand a concept more deeply by linking it to something familiar, creating a stronger impact.

🎭
Hidden Comparisons
17 / 25

Example of Metaphor in a Sentence

  • Sentence: 'Our soldiers are lions on the battlefield.'
  • Comparison: Soldiers are directly compared to lions.
  • Implies: Bravery, strength, fierceness.

🎙️ Here, we don't say soldiers are *like* lions, we say they *are* lions. This immediately conveys their immense courage and power in battle, without needing many words, making the description very vivid.

🦁
