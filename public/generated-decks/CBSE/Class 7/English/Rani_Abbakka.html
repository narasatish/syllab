Who Was Rani Abbakka?

  • The Queen of Ullal, a small coastal town.
  • Ruled during the 16th century.
  • Known for her fierce resistance against the Portuguese.

🎙️ Rani Abbakka was the courageous queen of Ullal, a principality located near present-day Mangaluru in Karnataka. She reigned in the 16th century, a time when European powers were trying to establish control over India's coastal regions. She stood out as one of the earliest Indian rulers to challenge the might of the Portuguese.

The Threat: Portuguese Invasion
