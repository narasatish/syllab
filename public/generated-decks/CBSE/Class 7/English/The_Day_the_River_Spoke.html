When Objects "Talk": Personification

  • Giving human qualities to non-human things.
  • Makes stories more vivid and engaging.
  • The river 'spoke' is an example.

🎙️ This story uses a powerful literary device called personification. It's when we give human actions or feelings to animals or inanimate objects. Think of a 'grumpy cloud' or 'the wind whispered'. It brings stories to life and helps us connect with them.
