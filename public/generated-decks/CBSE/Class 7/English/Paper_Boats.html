Imagery: Painting Pictures with Words

  • Using descriptive language to appeal to senses
  • Helps readers visualize and feel
  • Example: 'tiny black letters', 'shiuli flowers'

🎙️ Imagery is a key poetic device that creates vivid pictures in our minds. Tagore uses beautiful imagery like 'tiny black letters' and 'shiuli flowers' to help us see and feel the child's world, making the poem come alive.

🖼️
Visual Language

Personification: Giving Life to Inanimate

  • Attributing human qualities to non-human things
  • Makes objects relatable and dynamic
  • Example: 'clouds swell their sails'

🎙️ Personification is when we give human characteristics to animals or inanimate objects. In the poem, the clouds are described as 'swelling their sails,' as if they are actively helping the boats, making nature feel like a living, breathing entity.

🗣️
Human-like Traits

Metaphor: Implied Comparison

  • Comparing two unlike things without 'like' or 'as'
  • Suggests one thing IS another
  • Example: Boats as 'messages in a bottle'

🎙️ A metaphor is a figure of speech where a word or phrase is applied to an object or action to which it is not literally applicable. The paper boats themselves can be seen as a metaphor for the child's hopes and dreams, silently sailing towards the future.

Hidden Link
