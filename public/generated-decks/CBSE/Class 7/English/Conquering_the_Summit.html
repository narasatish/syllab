Simple Past Tense

  • Used for actions completed in the past.
  • Subject + Verb (past form) + Object.
  • Example: 'They **climbed** the mountain last year.'

🎙️ The simple past tense is straightforward. For regular verbs, we usually add '-ed'. For irregular verbs, the form changes. It tells us that an action began and ended at a specific time in the past.

✍️

Worked Example: Simple Past

  • Original: 'He *try* hard.'
  • Corrected: 'He **tried** hard to reach the peak.'
  • Original: 'They *go* to the base camp.'
  • Corrected: 'They **went** to the base camp early.'

🎙️ Let's apply this to sentences similar to those in our chapter. Notice how 'try' becomes 'tried' (regular with a spelling change) and 'go' becomes 'went' (irregular). This makes the action clearly in the past.

Adverbs of Manner

  • Describe *how* an action is done.
  • Often end in '-ly' (e.g., quickly, bravely).
  • Add detail and vividness to descriptions.

🎙️ Adverbs of manner help us paint a clearer picture of an action. They tell us *how* something happened, making our sentences more descriptive and engaging, which is important in a narrative like 'Conquering the Summit'.

🚀
How it's Done
19 / 25

Worked Example: Adverbs of Manner

  • Original: 'She climbed.'
  • Improved: 'She climbed **steadily** towards the summit.'
  • Original: 'He spoke.'
  • Improved: 'He spoke **courageously** about his fears.'

🎙️ See how adding an adverb changes the perception of the action? 'Steadily' tells us about the careful pace of climbing, and 'courageously' highlights the bravery in speaking. These words enhance the story.
