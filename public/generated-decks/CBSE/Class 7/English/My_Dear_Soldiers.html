Similes: Comparing with 'Like' or 'As'

  • A figure of speech comparing two different things.
  • Uses 'like' or 'as' to make the comparison.
  • Example: 'Brave as a lion.'

🎙️ Similes are a simple way to make descriptions more powerful. When we say a soldier is 'brave as a lion,' we immediately understand their immense courage by comparing it to a known symbol of bravery.

↔️
Direct Comparisons
13 / 25

Metaphors: Direct Comparisons

  • A figure of speech directly stating one thing is another.
  • Does NOT use 'like' or 'as'.
  • Example: 'The soldier is a shield for our nation.'

🎙️ Metaphors are like similes but more direct. Instead of saying a soldier is *like* a shield, we say they *are* a shield, emphasizing their protective role even more strongly. It's a powerful way to convey meaning.
