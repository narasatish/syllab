What Exactly is a Tunnel?

  • An underground passage
  • Connects two places
  • Built through mountains or under water

🎙️ A tunnel is essentially a pathway dug through the earth, often connecting two points that are otherwise separated by obstacles like mountains or water bodies. They are incredible feats of engineering!

Purpose of Tunnels

  • Faster travel
  • Overcome natural barriers
  • Safety from surface hazards

🎙️ Tunnels serve many vital purposes. They help us travel faster by providing direct routes, avoid natural barriers like mountains, and sometimes offer a safer passage away from surface dangers.

Vocabulary: Echo

  • A sound reflected back
  • Heard after the original sound
  • Common in tunnels or empty spaces
