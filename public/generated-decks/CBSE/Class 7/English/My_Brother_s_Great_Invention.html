Understanding Imagery

  • Using descriptive language to create mental pictures.
  • Appeals to our five senses (sight, sound, smell, taste, touch).
  • Helps readers visualize the story's events and settings.

🎙️ Imagery is like painting with words. Authors use it to make stories vivid and real for us. As we read, pay attention to how the author describes the brother's workshop or his inventions, making us feel like we are right there.

Imagery in Action

  • 'The workshop smelled of sawdust and old oil.' (Smell)
  • 'Gears whirred and clanked with a metallic symphony.' (Sound)
  • 'A tangle of wires, gleaming copper, and dull steel.' (Sight)

🎙️ Here are some examples of imagery you might find in a story like ours. The author doesn't just say 'the workshop was messy,' but uses sensory details to bring it to life. This helps us connect more deeply with the narrative.
