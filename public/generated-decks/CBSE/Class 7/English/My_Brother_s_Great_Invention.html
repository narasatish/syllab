Narrating Past Events: Simple Past Tense

  • Used to describe actions completed in the past.
  • Often uses '-ed' for regular verbs (walked, played).
  • Irregular verbs change forms (went, saw, made).

🎙️ Since 'My Brother's Great Invention' is a story, it's narrated in the past. The simple past tense is essential for recounting events that have already happened.
