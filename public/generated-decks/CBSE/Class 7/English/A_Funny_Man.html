Understanding Different Kinds of Humour

  • Slapstick: Physical comedy, like falling or tripping.
  • Wordplay: Jokes using words with double meanings.
  • Absurdity: Humour from illogical or silly situations.

🎙️ Humour comes in many forms. Slapstick involves physical comedy, while wordplay relies on clever use of language. Absurdity, which is often seen in our funny man, comes from situations that are illogical or nonsensical, but in a delightful way.
