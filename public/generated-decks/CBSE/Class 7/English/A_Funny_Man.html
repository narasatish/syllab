Humour Through Exaggeration

  • Making something seem much bigger or more extreme than it truly is.
  • It creates a comic and often unbelievable effect.
  • Authors use it to highlight funny traits or situations.

🎙️ Exaggeration is like blowing up a small balloon into a huge one! In stories, it means describing something in an over-the-top way to make it funnier. For example, saying 'I waited an eternity' instead of 'I waited a long time'.

The Magic of Wordplay

  • Using words in a clever or humorous way.
  • Often involves puns, double meanings, or witty remarks.
  • Adds charm and intelligence to the language.

🎙️ Wordplay is like a game with words! It's when an author uses words that sound similar but have different meanings, or uses a word in a surprising context. This can create a quick chuckle and show the author's cleverness.
