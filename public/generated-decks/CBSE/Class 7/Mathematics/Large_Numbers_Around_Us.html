Reading Large Numbers: Indian System

  • Read each period together.
  • Say the period name after the number.
  • Example: 5,43,21,000 is 'Five crore forty-three lakh twenty-one thousand'.

🎙️ To read a large number in the Indian system, first, group the digits using commas. Then, read the number in each period from left to right, followed by its period name. For instance, 5,43,21,000 is 'Five crore forty-three lakh twenty-one thousand'.

Writing Large Numbers: Indian System

  • Write digits for each period.
  • Use commas to separate periods.
  • Example: 'Seven crore fifty lakh' is 7,50,00,000.

🎙️ When writing a large number from words, identify the periods first. Then, write the digits for each period and place commas appropriately. Remember, the first comma is after three digits from the right, and then after every two digits.

Using Commas in the Indian System

  • First comma after 3 digits from the right (Hundreds).
  • Subsequent commas after every 2 digits (Lakhs, Crores).

🎙️ Commas are crucial for readability. In the Indian system, the first comma comes after the hundreds place (three digits from the right). After that, commas are placed after every two digits, separating thousands, lakhs, and crores.

Steps for Rounding Large Numbers

  • Identify the place to which the number is to be rounded.
  • Look at the digit to its right.
  • If >= 5, round up. If < 5, round down.

🎙️ To round a number, first, decide the place you want to round to. Then, examine the digit immediately to its right. If it's 5 or greater, increase the digit in the rounding place by one and change all digits to its right to zero. If it's less than 5, keep the digit in the rounding place as is, and change all digits to its right to zero.

2Check Right Digit
3Round Up/Down
