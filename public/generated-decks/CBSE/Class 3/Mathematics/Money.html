🎙️ Think of Rupees as the big brother and Paisa as the little sister. They are always together! One big Rupee is actually made up of 100 small Paisa. This is a very important rule to remember!

Changing Rupees to Paisa!

  • Sometimes we need to change Rupees into Paisa.
  • To do this, we multiply the Rupees by 100.
  • Remember, 1 Rupee = 100 Paisa!

🎙️ Imagine you have a big ₹1 coin, and you want to know how many small Paisa coins it's worth. Since 1 Rupee is 100 Paisa, you just multiply the number of Rupees by 100. It's like having 100 tiny candies for every big candy!

2Multiply by 100
3It becomes Paisa!

Let's Try: Rupees to Paisa

  • Question: Convert ₹5 to Paisa.
  • Solution: We know 1 Rupee = 100 Paisa.
  • So, ₹5 = 5 x 100 Paisa = 500 Paisa.

🎙️ Here's an example! If you have five 1-Rupee coins, and you want to know how many Paisa that is, you simply do 5 times 100. That gives you 500 Paisa. Easy, right?

➡️
₹5 is 500 P

Changing Paisa to Rupees!

  • What if we have many Paisa and want to know how many Rupees?
  • To do this, we divide the Paisa by 100.
  • It's like grouping 100 Paisa to make 1 Rupee.

🎙️ Now let's do the opposite! If you have a big pile of Paisa, and you want to count them in Rupees, you need to make groups of 100. So, you divide the total Paisa by 100 to find out how many Rupees you have.

1Take Paisa
2Divide by 100
3It becomes Rupees!
14 / 24

Let's Try: Paisa to Rupees

  • Question: Convert 250 Paisa to Rupees.
  • Solution: We know 100 Paisa = 1 Rupee.
  • So, 250 Paisa = 250 ÷ 100 Rupees = ₹2.50.

🎙️ If you have 250 Paisa, you can make two full groups of 100 Paisa, which are two Rupees. And you'll have 50 Paisa left over. So, 250 Paisa is ₹2 and 50 Paisa, written as ₹2.50.

⬅️
250 P is ₹2.50

Let's Add Money!

  • Ria has ₹10.50 and her brother gives her ₹5.25.
  • How much money does Ria have in total?
  • Always line up the decimal point when adding.

🎙️ When adding money, imagine a straight line for the decimal point. Paisa go on the right, and Rupees go on the left. Add Paisa first, then Rupees. So, ₹10.50 + ₹5.25 = ₹15.75.
