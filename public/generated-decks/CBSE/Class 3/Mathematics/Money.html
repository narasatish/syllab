What is Indian Money Called?

  • In India, our money is called 'Rupees' (₹).
  • Smaller parts of a Rupee are called 'Paise'.

🎙️ Every country has its own special name for money. In our country, India, we call our money Rupees, and its symbol looks like this: ₹. Sometimes, we also use smaller units called Paise, just like how we have smaller parts of a whole.

Rupees and Paise Together

  • 1 Rupee is equal to 100 Paise.
  • Paise are smaller parts of a Rupee.

🎙️ Just like 1 metre is 100 centimetres, 1 Rupee is equal to 100 Paise. Sometimes, prices are given in Rupees and Paise, like ₹10.50. This means 10 Rupees and 50 Paise. We write them together using a special dot.

Writing Money Correctly

  • We use '₹' symbol for Rupees.
  • We use a dot '.' to separate Rupees and Paise.
  • Example: ₹15.50 means 15 Rupees and 50 Paise.

🎙️ When we write money, we always put the '₹' symbol first. Then, we write the Rupees. If there are Paise, we put a dot and then write the Paise. The dot helps us clearly see what is Rupee and what is Paise.

Adding Money (With Paise)

  • Add Paise with Paise, and Rupees with Rupees.
  • Example: ₹10.50 + ₹5.25 = ₹15.75

🎙️ When adding money with Paise, always add the Paise first, and then the Rupees. Make sure the dots are in a straight line. Here, 50 Paise + 25 Paise = 75 Paise. And 10 Rupees + 5 Rupees = 15 Rupees. So, the total is ₹15.75.

1₹10.50
2+ ₹5.25
3= ₹15.75

Subtracting Money (No Paise)

  • Subtracting money is like subtracting numbers.

🎙️ What if you spend some money? That's when we subtract! If you have ₹20 and you buy a chocolate for ₹10, you subtract 10 from 20. You are left with ₹10. This helps you know how much money remains.

1₹20
2- ₹10

Subtracting Money (With Paise)

  • Subtract Paise from Paise, and Rupees from Rupees.
  • Example: ₹50.75 - ₹20.50 = ₹30.25

🎙️ Similar to addition, when subtracting money with Paise, start with the Paise first. 75 Paise minus 50 Paise is 25 Paise. Then, 50 Rupees minus 20 Rupees is 30 Rupees. So, ₹30.25 is what's left.

1₹50.75
2- ₹20.50
3= ₹30.25
