What is Money?

  • Money is what we use to buy things.
  • It can be coins or paper notes.

🎙️ Imagine you want a yummy chocolate bar. How do you get it? You give money to the shopkeeper! Money is like a special token that everyone agrees to use for buying and selling.

Why do we use Money?

  • We use money to buy food.
  • We use money to buy toys and books.
  • It helps us get things we need.

🎙️ Long, long ago, people used to exchange things directly, like giving apples for oranges. But money makes it much easier! Now, with money, we can buy clothes, pay for rides, and even go to the movies.

Buying a Toy Car!

  • Toy Car costs 30 Rupees.
  • We have a 20 Rupee note and a 10 Rupee coin.
  • 20 + 10 = 30 Rupees. We can buy it!

🎙️ Imagine you want to buy a toy car that costs 30 rupees. You have a twenty-rupee note and a ten-rupee coin. If you add them, 20 + 10 makes 30 rupees! So, yes, you have enough money to buy the car.
