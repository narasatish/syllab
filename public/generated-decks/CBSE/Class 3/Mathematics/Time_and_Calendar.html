The Hour Hand

  • It is the shorter, fatter hand.
  • It points to the hour.

🎙️ This little hand is called the hour hand. It moves slowly and tells us the main hour. When it points to a number, that's the hour we are in.

The Minute Hand

  • It is the longer, thinner hand.
  • It points to the minutes.

🎙️ And this long, thin hand is the minute hand. It moves faster than the hour hand. When we learn to read minutes, this hand will be our guide!

Reading O'Clock Time

  • Minute hand points to 12.
  • Hour hand points to the hour.
  • Example: 3 o'clock!

🎙️ When the minute hand is exactly on the number 12, it means 'o'clock'. Then, we just look at where the hour hand is pointing to know the time. So simple!

Half Past the Hour

  • Minute hand points to 6.
  • Hour hand is between two numbers.
  • Example: Half past 3!

🎙️ When the minute hand is on the number 6, it means 'half past' the hour. The hour hand will be exactly in the middle of two numbers, showing that half an hour has passed.

Lunch at Half Past 12!

  • Hour hand between 12 and 1.
  • Minute hand on 6.
  • Time for yummy food!

🎙️ Often, we have our lunch around half past 12. This means the minute hand is on 6 and the hour hand is halfway between 12 and 1. Yum, yum!
