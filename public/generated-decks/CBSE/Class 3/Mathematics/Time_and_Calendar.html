The Short Hand: Hour Hand

  • The short hand is called the 'hour hand'.
  • It tells us the hour, like 3 o'clock or 7 o'clock.
  • The hour hand moves slowly, taking a full day to go around twice.

🎙️ The hour hand is the bossy one, telling us the main time, the 'hour'. It moves very slowly, so slowly that you might not even see it moving! It takes 12 hours to complete one full round.

The Long Hand: Minute Hand

  • The long hand is called the 'minute hand'.
  • It tells us the minutes past the hour.
  • The minute hand moves faster than the hour hand.

🎙️ The minute hand is the faster one. It tells us the smaller units of time, the 'minutes'. It moves around the clock much quicker, completing one full round in just one hour!

Minute Hand (fast)

Reading O'Clock: Sharp Time!

  • When the long (minute) hand points exactly to 12, it's 'o'clock'.
  • The short (hour) hand tells us which hour it is.
  • For example, if the short hand is on 3 and long hand on 12, it's 3 o'clock.

🎙️ This is the easiest time to read! When the minute hand is straight up at 12, we just look at where the hour hand is pointing. That's the 'o'clock' time. Like when school starts at 9 o'clock!

Half Past: Halfway Through!

  • When the long (minute) hand points exactly to 6, it's 'half past' an hour.
  • The short (hour) hand will be exactly between two numbers.
  • This means 30 minutes have passed since the hour.

🎙️ Sometimes, the minute hand is not on 12, but on 6. This means half an hour has passed. So, we say 'half past'. The hour hand will be in the middle of two numbers, showing it's halfway to the next hour.
