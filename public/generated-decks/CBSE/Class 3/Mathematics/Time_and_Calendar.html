Parts of a Clock: The Hands

  • A clock usually has two main hands.
  • The small hand tells us the hour.
  • The big hand tells us the minutes.

🎙️ Imagine the clock hands as two friends running a race. The small hand is a bit slower, showing us the hours. The big hand is faster, moving around to show the minutes. They work together to show the exact time.

Reading Hours: The Small Hand

  • The small hand points to the hour.
  • If it points to 3, it's 3 o'clock.
  • If it points to 7, it's 7 o'clock.

🎙️ The small hand is very important for telling us the main time, the hour. It moves slowly from one number to the next. When it is exactly on a number, and the big hand is on 12, we say 'o'clock'.

Reading Minutes: The Big Hand

  • The big hand points to the minutes.
  • Each number on the clock means 5 minutes.
  • 12 is 0 minutes, 1 is 5 minutes, 2 is 10 minutes, and so on.

🎙️ The big hand moves much faster than the small hand. To read the minutes, we count by fives for each number. So, if the big hand is on 4, it means 4 times 5, which is 20 minutes past the hour.

Time Example: 3 O'Clock

  • Small hand points exactly to 3.
  • Big hand points exactly to 12.
  • We say '3 o'clock'!

🎙️ This is one of the easiest times to read on a clock. When the big hand is on the 12, it means 0 minutes, so we just say 'o'clock'. So, if the small hand is on 3, it's 3 o'clock.

Time Example: Half Past 3 (3:30)

  • Small hand is between 3 and 4.
  • Big hand points exactly to 6.
  • We say 'half past 3' or 'three thirty'.

🎙️ When the big hand is on 6, it means half of the hour has passed, which is 30 minutes (6 times 5). The small hand will be halfway between the current hour and the next hour. So, it's half past 3.

Time Example: Quarter Past 3 (3:15)

  • Small hand is a little past 3.
  • Big hand points exactly to 3.
  • We say 'quarter past 3' or 'three fifteen'.

🎙️ When the big hand is on 3, it means a quarter of the hour has passed, which is 15 minutes (3 times 5). The small hand will have moved just a little bit past the hour. So, it's quarter past 3.
