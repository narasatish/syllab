Reading Our New Numbers

  • Example: 245 is 'Two hundred forty-five'.

🎙️ Reading 3-digit numbers is fun! First, say how many hundreds you see. Then, read the last two digits just like you read 2-digit numbers, for example, 'forty-five'.

Writing Numbers: From Words to Digits

Comparing Numbers: Who is Bigger?

  • Start by comparing the Hundreds digit.
  • If hundreds are same, compare Tens. Then Ones.

🎙️ To know which number is bigger or smaller, we start with the biggest place, the Hundreds. If the hundreds are the same, we move to the tens, and then the ones, like checking steps in order.

Let's Compare: 345 and 321

  • Hundreds are same (3).
  • Tens: 4 is greater than 2.
  • So, 345 is greater than 321 (345 > 321).

🎙️ See how we first look at the hundreds? Both have 3 hundreds. So, we move to the tens. 4 tens is more than 2 tens, so 345 is definitely bigger than 321.

Ordering Numbers: Smallest to Biggest & Biggest to Smallest

  • Ascending order: arrange numbers from smallest to largest (like climbing up stairs).
  • Descending order: arrange numbers from largest to smallest (like coming down stairs).

🎙️ We can arrange numbers in two ways: from smallest to biggest, called ascending order, or from biggest to smallest, called descending order. It's like putting things in a line by their size.
