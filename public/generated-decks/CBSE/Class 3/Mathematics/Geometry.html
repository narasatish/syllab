What is a Point?

  • A point shows an exact position.
  • It has no length, no width, no thickness.
  • It's just a location!

🎙️ A point is like a secret treasure spot on a map. It tells you exactly where something is, but it doesn't take up any space itself. We usually name points with a capital letter.

•
A tiny dot

What is a Line?

  • A line is a straight path.
  • It goes on forever in both directions.
  • It has no endpoints.

🎙️ A line is like an endless journey. It never stops! We draw arrows on both ends to show that it continues without end. It’s always straight.

↔️
Goes on forever

A Part of a Line: Line Segment

  • Cut a piece from a long thread.
  • The edge of your ruler is a line segment.

🎙️ What if you take that super long thread and cut a piece from it? That piece has a start and an end. That's exactly what a line segment is.

✂️
Cutting a thread
9 / 24

What is a Line Segment?

  • A part of a line.
  • It has two endpoints.
  • It has a definite length.

🎙️ Unlike a line, a line segment has a clear beginning and a clear end. You can measure its length with a ruler. Think of the side of your book, it's a line segment.

📏
A measurable piece

Meet the Ray!

  • Think of a sunbeam coming from the sun.
  • A torch light starting from the torch.

🎙️ Imagine the sun sending out light. The light starts at the sun and goes outward, but it doesn't come back to the sun. This one-way path is like a ray.

☀️
Sun's rays
12 / 24

What is a Ray?

  • A line that starts at one point.
  • It goes on forever in one direction.
  • It has one endpoint.

🎙️ A ray is like a road with a starting point, but no ending point in one direction. It has only one arrow on one side to show it continues endlessly. Think of an arrow.

➡️
One-way path

What is a Plane?

  • A flat surface.
  • It extends endlessly in all directions.
  • Like an infinitely large, flat sheet.

🎙️ A plane is a two-dimensional flat surface. We can draw shapes like squares and circles on a plane. It has length and width, but no thickness.

Meet the Square!

  • It has four straight sides.
  • All four sides are exactly equal in length.
  • It has four corners (vertices).

🎙️ The square is a very neat and tidy shape. All its sides are the same, making it look perfectly balanced. Think of a carrom board or a Rubik's cube face.

🟦
Four equal sides

Meet the Rectangle!

  • It also has four straight sides.
  • Opposite sides are equal in length.
  • It has four corners (vertices).

🎙️ The rectangle is like a stretched-out square! Its opposite sides are equal. Your classroom blackboard or your mobile phone are good examples of rectangles.

Opposite sides equal

Watch Out for These!

  • Confusing a line and a line segment.
  • Miscounting sides or corners of shapes.
  • Forgetting that a ray has only ONE endpoint.

🎙️ It's easy to get confused sometimes, but don't worry! Always remember that a line goes on forever, but a line segment has two clear ends. Also, count the sides and corners carefully.
