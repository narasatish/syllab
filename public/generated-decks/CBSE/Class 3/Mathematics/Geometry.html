What is a Line Segment?

  • It's a part of a line.
  • It has a starting point and an ending point.

🎙️ A line segment is like taking a piece out of that very long line. It has a clear start and a clear end. You can measure its length easily, unlike a line that goes on forever.

📏
Measurable Part

  • Your arm!

🎙️ Your pencil has a start and an end, right? That's a line segment! The side of your book or even your arm can be thought of as a line segment. They are everywhere in things we can hold and measure.

Meet the Bright Ray

  • A ray starts at one point.
  • It goes on and on in only one direction.

🎙️ Imagine a line that starts at a point, like a sun, and then shoots out forever in one direction. That's a ray! It has a starting point but no end, only one way to go.
