What is Addition? Putting Things Together!

  • Addition means joining two or more groups.
  • We count how many we have in total.

🎙️ Imagine you have one apple, and your friend gives you another apple. How many do you have now? Addition helps us find the total when we put things together.

Adding Numbers: Step by Step!

  • We start adding from the right side (ones place).
  • Then we add the tens place.

🎙️ When we add bigger numbers, we line them up neatly. Always start with the numbers on the right, which are the ones. Then move to the left, adding the tens. It's like building blocks!

Addition with Carrying: When Numbers are Too Big!

  • If the sum of ones is 10 or more, we 'carry over' to the tens place.
  • It's like giving extra blocks to the next column!

🎙️ Sometimes, when we add the ones, we get a number like 12. We can't write 12 in the ones place! So, we write the '2' in the ones place and 'carry' the '1' to the tens place. This '1' gets added to the tens.

What is Subtraction? Taking Things Away!

  • Subtraction means taking a part from a whole group.
  • We find out how many are left or the difference.

🎙️ Imagine you had 5 balloons, and 2 flew away! How many are left? Subtraction helps us find out how many remain when something is taken away from a group. It's the opposite of addition.

🎈🎈🎈
5 balloons - 2 balloons = 3 balloons

  • Also 'less', 'minus', 'take away'.

🎙️ Just like with addition, subtraction also has its own secret words! When you see these words in a problem, it tells you to subtract. The minus sign is our special symbol for subtraction.

Subtracting Chocolates: Yummy, but fewer now!

  • Mom bought 8 chocolates.
  • You ate 3 chocolates.
  • How many chocolates are left?

🎙️ Oh, chocolates! You had 8, but you enjoyed 3 of them. To find out how many are still in the box, we take away the ones you ate from the total. So, 8 minus 3 leaves 5 chocolates.

🍫🍫🍫🍫🍫
8 chocolates - 3 chocolates = 5 chocolates

🎙️ Imagine a tree full of chirping birds! Then, some decided to fly off. To know how many are still sitting there, we subtract the birds that left from the original number. Seven minus two equals five birds.

Subtracting Numbers: Step by Step!

  • We start subtracting from the right side (ones place).
  • Then we subtract the tens place.

🎙️ Like addition, we line up numbers for subtraction. Always begin with the ones place on the right. Then move to the tens place on the left. It's a neat and tidy way to find the difference.

Subtraction with Borrowing: When You Need More!

  • If the top digit is smaller than the bottom digit, we 'borrow' from the tens place.
  • It's like asking a neighbor for help!

🎙️ Sometimes, you have 2 in the ones place and need to subtract 7. You can't do that! So, you 'borrow' 1 ten from the tens place, making your 2 into 12. Remember to reduce the tens place by 1 after borrowing.
