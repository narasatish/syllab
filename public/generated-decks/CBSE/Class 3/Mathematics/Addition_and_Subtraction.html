Adding Bigger Numbers!

  • When numbers are big, we stack them up.
  • We add 'ones' first, then 'tens'.

🎙️ Sometimes, numbers are too big to count on our fingers. For bigger numbers, we write them one below the other, like building a tower. This helps us add them easily.

🔢
Numbers Stacked

Steps to Add Numbers

  • 1. Write numbers one below the other.
  • 2. Align ones, tens, hundreds.
  • 3. Add the ones column first.
  • 4. Add the tens column.

🎙️ Here are the simple steps to add big numbers. Always remember to line up the numbers correctly, and start adding from the right side, which is the ones column.

1Align Numbers
2Add Ones
3Add Tens

Let's Add 23 + 14

  • 23 (2 tens, 3 ones) + 14 (1 ten, 4 ones).
  • Add ones: 3 + 4 = 7.
  • Add tens: 2 + 1 = 3.
  • Total is 37.

🎙️ Watch how we add 23 and 14. First, we add the ones: 3 plus 4 is 7. Then, we add the tens: 2 plus 1 is 3. So, 23 plus 14 equals 37. Easy, right?

➡️
Result: 37

What is Subtraction?

  • Subtraction means taking away.
  • We find out 'how many are left'.
  • We use the '-' sign for subtraction.

🎙️ Subtraction is the opposite of addition. It's about taking things away and seeing what remains. The minus sign is our special symbol for subtraction.

Subtracting Bigger Numbers!

Measuring Difference

Steps to Subtract Numbers

  • 1. Write the bigger number on top.
  • 2. Align ones, tens, hundreds.
  • 3. Subtract the ones column first.
  • 4. Subtract the tens column.

🎙️ These are the steps for subtracting numbers. Always remember to put the larger number on top, and subtract column by column, starting from the ones column on the right.

1Align Numbers
2Subtract Ones
3Subtract Tens
14 / 20

Let's Subtract 38 - 15

  • 38 (3 tens, 8 ones) - 15 (1 ten, 5 ones).
  • Subtract ones: 8 - 5 = 3.
  • Subtract tens: 3 - 1 = 2.
  • Total is 23.

🎙️ Let's subtract 15 from 38. First, we subtract the ones: 8 minus 5 is 3. Then, we subtract the tens: 3 minus 1 is 2. So, 38 minus 15 equals 23.

➡️
Result: 23
