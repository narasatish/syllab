Adding Big Numbers! (No Carrying)

  • Add the 'ones' column first.
  • Then add the 'tens' column.
  • It's like adding two small problems!

🎙️ When we add numbers like 23 and 14, we start from the right side, with the 'ones' place. After adding the ones, we move to the 'tens' place. It's simple, no carrying over yet!

1Add Ones
2Add Tens

Example: Add 23 + 14

  • Step 1: Add the ones. 3 + 4 = 7
  • Step 2: Add the tens. 2 + 1 = 3
  • The sum is 37.

🎙️ Let's try this together. Look at the ones place: 3 plus 4 is 7. Now, look at the tens place: 2 plus 1 is 3. So, when we put 3 and 7 together, our answer is 37!

Adding with Regrouping (Carrying Over)

  • Sometimes, the 'ones' sum is 10 or more.
  • We 'carry over' the tens part to the 'tens' column.
  • It's like giving extra sweets to the next group!

🎙️ Imagine you have 7 chocolates and your friend gives you 5 more. That's 12 chocolates! You can't keep 12 in the 'ones' box. So, you keep 2 in the ones box and give 1 (which is 1 ten) to the 'tens' box. This is called regrouping or carrying over.

⬆️
Carry Over
11 / 24

Example: Add 38 + 25

  • Step 1: Add ones. 8 + 5 = 13 (Write 3, carry 1 to tens)
  • Step 2: Add tens. 3 + 2 + 1 (carried) = 6
  • The sum is 63.

🎙️ Let's see how it works. 8 plus 5 is 13. We write down 3 in the ones place and carry over 1 to the tens place. Now, in the tens place, we add 3, 2, and the carried-over 1, which gives us 6. So the answer is 63.

18 + 5 = 13 (Carry 1)
23 + 2 + 1 = 6 (Tens)

Subtracting Big Numbers! (No Borrowing)

  • Subtract the 'ones' column first.
  • Then subtract the 'tens' column.
  • Always subtract the smaller number from the larger one.

🎙️ Just like addition, we start subtracting from the 'ones' place on the right. Then we move to the 'tens' place. In these simple problems, the top number will always be bigger in each column.

1Subtract Ones
2Subtract Tens
16 / 24

Example: Subtract 48 - 23

  • Step 1: Subtract the ones. 8 - 3 = 5
  • Step 2: Subtract the tens. 4 - 2 = 2
  • The difference is 25.

🎙️ Let's solve this! In the ones place, 8 minus 3 is 5. In the tens place, 4 minus 2 is 2. So, when we put 2 and 5 together, our answer is 25. Easy, isn't it?

18 - 3 = 5 (Ones)
24 - 2 = 2 (Tens)

Subtracting with Regrouping (Borrowing)

  • Sometimes, the top 'ones' digit is smaller.
  • We 'borrow' 1 ten from the 'tens' column.
  • This 1 ten becomes 10 ones for the 'ones' column.

🎙️ Imagine you have 3 sweets but need to give away 7. You don't have enough! So you 'borrow' from your friend, the tens place. Your friend (tens) gives you one of their tens, which is like 10 sweets for you (ones). This is called borrowing.

19 / 24

Example: Subtract 52 - 27

  • Step 1: Ones. Cannot do 2 - 7. Borrow 1 ten from 5 (tens).
  • 5 becomes 4. 2 becomes 12. So, 12 - 7 = 5.
  • Step 2: Tens. Now, 4 - 2 = 2.
  • The difference is 25.

🎙️ Let's try this tricky one. We can't take 7 from 2, so we borrow 1 ten from the 5 in the tens place. The 5 becomes 4, and the 2 becomes 12. Now, 12 minus 7 is 5. In the tens place, 4 minus 2 is 2. The answer is 25.

1Borrow 1 ten (12 - 7 = 5)
2Subtract tens (4 - 2 = 2)

Watch Out for These!

  • Not aligning numbers by place value (ones under ones, tens under tens).
  • Forgetting to carry over in addition.
  • Forgetting to borrow correctly in subtraction.

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, but it's important to learn from them! Always remember to write numbers neatly in their correct columns. Double-check your carrying over and borrowing steps, as these are common places where errors happen.
