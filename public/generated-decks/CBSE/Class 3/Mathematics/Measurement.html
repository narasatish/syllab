Measuring with Our Hands and Feet!

  • Long ago, people used their body parts to measure.
  • Like handspan (distance from thumb to pinky) and footspan (length of your foot).

🎙️ Before we had rulers, people used their own hands and feet to measure things! A 'handspan' is like stretching your fingers wide and seeing how many 'hands' long something is. A 'footspan' is simply using your foot to measure.

Standard Units: Metre and Centimetre

  • To avoid confusion, we use 'standard units'.
  • Metre (m) and Centimetre (cm) are standard units of length.

🎙️ To make sure everyone gets the same answer when measuring, we use special 'standard units'. The most common ones for length are the Metre and the Centimetre. These units are always the same, no matter who is measuring.

Standard Units: Kilogram and Gram

  • Kilogram (kg) is used for heavier things.
  • Gram (g) is used for lighter things.

🎙️ Just like length, we have standard units for weight too. For heavy things, like a sack of rice or a person, we use 'kilograms' (kg). For lighter things, like a small candy or a pinch of salt, we use 'grams' (g).

  • The bucket has more capacity.

Standard Units: Litre and Millilitre

  • Litre (L) is used for larger amounts of liquid.
  • Millilitre (mL) is used for smaller amounts of liquid.

🎙️ For capacity, our standard units are 'Litre' (L) and 'Millilitre' (mL). We use litres for big quantities, like a bottle of cold drink or a petrol tank. We use millilitres for small amounts, like medicine in a spoon or a small juice box.
