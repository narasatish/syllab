What is Measurement?

  • Measurement means finding out the size, length, or weight of things.
  • It tells us 'how much' of something there is.
  • Like knowing how tall you are!

🎙️ Measurement is simply about knowing 'how much'. For example, if you want to know how tall you are, you measure your height. If you want to know how long your pencil is, you measure its length.

Measuring with Handspans

  • We can use our handspan to measure small lengths.
  • A handspan is the distance from your thumb to your little finger.

🎙️ Long ago, people used their body parts to measure. One way was using a 'handspan'. Try measuring your table with your handspans. You place your hand, then move it and place it again.

Our Friend: The Centimeter (cm)

  • Centimeter (cm) is a standard unit for measuring length.
  • It is used for small things, like your pencil or eraser.
  • A ruler has centimeters marked on it.

🎙️ To get the same answer every time, we use 'standard units'. One such unit is the 'centimeter', written as 'cm'. We use centimeters to measure smaller things, and you can see them on your ruler.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

For Longer Things: The Meter (m)

  • Meter (m) is used for measuring longer lengths.
  • Like the length of your classroom or a big cloth.
  • 1 meter is equal to 100 centimeters.

🎙️ When things are much longer, like the length of a wall or a big table, we use 'meters', written as 'm'. Remember, 1 meter is the same as 100 centimeters. That's a lot of centimeters!

Let's Use a Ruler!

  • Place the object at the '0' mark on the ruler.
  • Read the number where the object ends.
  • That's its length in centimeters!

🎙️ Using a ruler is easy! Just place one end of the object exactly at the '0' mark. Then, look at where the other end of the object finishes on the ruler. That number tells you its length in centimeters.

Measuring Weight: How Heavy?

  • Weight tells us how heavy or light something is.
  • Some things are heavy, some are light.
  • Like a feather is light, a stone is heavy.

🎙️ Now, let's move to 'weight'. Weight helps us know if something is heavy or light. Have you ever tried to lift a big bag of rice? It's very heavy, right? But a small feather is very light.

Our Friend: The Kilogram (kg)

  • Kilogram (kg) is a standard unit for measuring weight.
  • It is used for heavier things, like bags of rice or vegetables.
  • Your school bag might weigh a few kilograms!

🎙️ Just like with length, we have standard units for weight. For heavier things, we use 'kilograms', written as 'kg'. When your parents buy potatoes or rice from the market, they buy them in kilograms.

For Lighter Things: The Gram (g)

  • Gram (g) is used for measuring lighter weights.
  • Like a small candy or a pinch of salt.
  • 1 kilogram is equal to 1000 grams.

🎙️ For very light things, like a small cookie or a tiny amount of spice, we use 'grams', written as 'g'. Grams are much smaller than kilograms. Remember, 1 kilogram is a thousand grams!
