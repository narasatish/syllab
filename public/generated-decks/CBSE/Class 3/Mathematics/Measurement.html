Standard Units of Length

  • Centimeter (cm) for small things.
  • Meter (m) for bigger things.
  • 1 meter = 100 centimeters.

🎙️ To make sure everyone gets the same measurement, we use 'standard units'. For small things like pencils, we use centimeters. For bigger things like a room, we use meters. Remember, 1 meter is like 100 small centimeters!

🎙️ This is our amazing tool, the ruler! It has numbers and tiny lines to help us measure accurately. When you measure, always remember to start from the '0' mark on the ruler. It's like the starting line of a race!

Standard Units of Weight

  • Gram (g) for light things.
  • Kilogram (kg) for heavy things.
  • 1 kilogram = 1000 grams.

🎙️ Just like length, weight also has standard units. For light things like a packet of chips, we use grams (g). For heavier things like a bag of sugar, we use kilograms (kg). Remember, 1 kilogram is like 1000 tiny grams!

What is Capacity?

  • It tells us how much a container can hold.
  • Mostly for liquids like water or milk.
  • Like a bottle of juice.

🎙️ Capacity is all about how much space there is inside a container for liquids. Think about a water bottle – its capacity is how much water it can hold. A small cup has less capacity than a big jug.

Standard Units of Capacity

  • Millilitre (ml) for small amounts.
  • Litre (l) for larger amounts.
  • 1 litre = 1000 millilitres.

🎙️ For liquids, we use standard units called millilitres (ml) for small amounts, like medicine, and litres (l) for bigger amounts, like a bottle of cold drink. Remember, 1 litre is equal to 1000 millilitres!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing cm with ml or g.
  • Not starting from zero on a ruler.
  • Guessing instead of measuring.

🎙️ Sometimes, we can get a little mixed up! A common mistake is using the wrong unit, like saying '5 kg of water' instead of '5 litres of water'. Always remember to start from '0' on your ruler, and never just guess – always measure!
