Who's Who in 5 x 3 = 15?

  • 5 is the Multiplier (how many groups).
  • 3 is the Multiplicand (how many in each group).
  • 15 is the Product (the total answer).

🎙️ In our example, 5 x 3 = 15: The number 5 tells us how many groups we have, so it's the Multiplier. The number 3 tells us how many items are in each group, so it's the Multiplicand. And the answer, 15, is called the Product. It's the total result of multiplying!

Special Rules: Multiply by 1 and 0

  • Anything multiplied by 1 is itself (like a mirror).
  • Anything multiplied by 0 is always 0 (like a black hole).

🎙️ These are two very important rules! If you multiply any number by 1, the number stays the same, just like looking in a mirror. But if you multiply any number by 0, the answer is always 0. It's like 0 eats up all the other numbers!

Multiply 2-Digit by 1-Digit (No Regrouping)

  • Start multiplying from the Ones place.
  • Then multiply the Tens place.
  • It's like two friends helping out!

🎙️ Now, let's try multiplying bigger numbers! When we multiply a 2-digit number by a 1-digit number, we start with the Ones column, then move to the Tens column. It's a simple step-by-step process.

17 / 24

Example: 12 x 3

  • Step 1: Multiply Ones. 2 x 3 = 6.
  • Step 2: Multiply Tens. 1 x 3 = 3.
  • Answer: 36.

🎙️ Let's do 12 multiplied by 3. First, we multiply the 2 in the Ones place by 3, which gives us 6. Then, we multiply the 1 in the Tens place by 3, which gives us 3. So, our answer is 36. Easy, right?

Multiply 2-Digit by 1-Digit (With Regrouping)

  • Sometimes, the product of Ones is too big.
  • We 'carry over' to the Tens place.
  • It's like carrying a gift to the next house!

🎙️ What if multiplying the Ones gives us a 2-digit number? That's when we 'regroup'! We write down the Ones digit and 'carry over' the Tens digit to the Tens column, just like we do in addition. It's like passing a gift to the next person.

19 / 24

Example: 24 x 3

  • Step 1: Multiply Ones. 4 x 3 = 12 (write 2, carry 1).
  • Step 2: Multiply Tens. 2 x 3 = 6.
  • Step 3: Add carried over 1. 6 + 1 = 7.
  • Answer: 72.

🎙️ Let's try 24 multiplied by 3. First, 4 x 3 is 12. We write down 2 in the Ones place and carry over 1 to the Tens place. Next, 2 x 3 is 6. Now, remember to add the 1 we carried over, so 6 + 1 makes 7. The final answer is 72.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Forgetting to add the 'carried over' number.
  • Not knowing your multiplication tables well.
  • Mixing up addition and multiplication.

🎙️ Even experts make mistakes sometimes! The most common ones are forgetting to add the number you carried over, or not knowing your tables by heart. Always double-check your work and practice your tables regularly!

Exam Tips for Multiplication!

  • Practice tables daily (up to 10 or 12).
  • Read word problems carefully to find 'groups of'.

🎙️ To do well in your exams, make sure you know your tables by heart. When you see word problems, look for clues like 'how many in each group' or 'how many groups'. And always show how you solved the problem, step by step!
