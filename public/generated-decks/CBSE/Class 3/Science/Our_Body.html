  • It helps us think, learn, and remember things.

The Heart: A Pumping Machine

  • Our heart is inside our chest.
  • It works non-stop, pumping blood to all parts.
  • Can you feel your heartbeat? Put your hand on your chest!

🎙️ The heart is like a powerful pump. It makes sure that every part of our body gets the fresh blood it needs to work well and stay healthy.

The Lungs: For Breathing Air

  • We have two lungs inside our chest.
  • They help us breathe in fresh air (oxygen).
  • And breathe out the used air (carbon dioxide).

🎙️ Our lungs are like big sponges. They take in oxygen from the air we breathe and give it to our blood, which is essential for life.

The Stomach: Food's Mixer

  • Our stomach is inside our tummy.
  • It helps to mix and digest the food we eat.
  • It turns food into energy for us to play and learn.

🎙️ When we eat, the food goes into our stomach. It's like a special blender that breaks down food so our body can use all its nutrients.
