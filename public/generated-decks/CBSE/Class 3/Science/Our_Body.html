Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Confusing sense organs (e.g., nose for taste).
  • Forgetting internal organs are hidden.
  • Not knowing all 5 sense organs.

🎙️ Sometimes, we might get a little confused. Remember, each sense organ has a special job – the nose smells, the tongue tastes. Also, don't forget that important organs like the heart and brain are hidden inside, not outside! Make sure you know all five sense organs well.
