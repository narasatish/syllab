What are Materials?

  • Everything around us is made up of something.
  • This 'something' is called a material.
  • Think of it like ingredients for a yummy cake!

🎙️ Just like a cake needs flour, sugar, and eggs, the table you sit at needs wood, and your clothes need fabric. These 'ingredients' are materials.

Material Type 4: Glass

  • Glass is transparent, we can see through it.
  • It is smooth and can break easily.
  • Windows, mirrors, and drinking glasses are made of glass.

🎙️ Look out the window! You can see through it because it's made of glass. Glass is beautiful and clear, but we need to be careful as it can break.

Why Choose Different Materials?

  • We choose materials based on what we need them for.
  • A chair needs to be strong, so we use wood or metal.
  • A shirt needs to be soft, so we use fabric.

🎙️ Imagine trying to make a chair out of cloth, or a shirt out of metal! It wouldn't work, right? Each material has special qualities that make it suitable for certain things.

One Thing, Many Materials

  • Sometimes, one thing is made of many different materials.
  • Look at a pencil!

🎙️ A simple pencil is a great example! It uses wood for its body, a special material called graphite for writing, a tiny metal piece, and a rubber eraser. All these materials work together!

Common Mistakes

  • Thinking all toys are plastic (some are wood, metal, fabric).
  • Confusing hard materials (like wood) with soft materials (like fabric).
  • Forgetting that glass is transparent but can break easily.

🎙️ It's easy to get confused sometimes, but remember to look closely at the properties of each material. Not all toys are plastic, and not all hard things are metal!
