Why Are Materials Different?

  • Materials have different qualities.
  • Some are hard, some are soft.
  • Some are smooth, some are rough.

🎙️ Why do we use different materials for different things? Because each material has special qualities! Wood is hard, but fabric is soft. This helps us choose the right material for the right job.

Choosing the Right Material

  • We use wood for tables because it's strong.
  • We use plastic for water bottles because it's light and doesn't break easily.
  • We use fabric for clothes because it's soft and comfortable.

🎙️ Imagine if your clothes were made of metal – that would be very uncomfortable! And if your window was made of wood, no light would come in. So, we always pick the best material for what we want to make.
