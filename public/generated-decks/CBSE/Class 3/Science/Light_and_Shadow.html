What is Light?

  • Light helps us see everything.
  • Without light, everything is dark.

🎙️ Imagine if it was always dark! We wouldn't be able to see our toys, our friends, or even our books. Light is like a special helper that shows us the world around us. The biggest light we see every day is the Sun!

  • That's how we see colours and shapes!

🎙️ Light is very clever! It travels from its source, hits an object like your toy car, and then bounces into your eyes. It's like light is showing you the way to see everything clearly. Isn't that amazing?

What is a Shadow?

  • A shadow is a dark shape.
  • It appears when something blocks light.

🎙️ Have you ever seen a dark shape following you when you walk in the sun? That's your shadow! A shadow is formed when something stands in the way of light, stopping it from reaching a surface.

Shapes are all around us.
7 / 20

How a Shadow is Made

  • Light shines from a source.
  • An object stands in the way.
  • A dark shape appears behind it.

🎙️ It's like playing hide-and-seek with light! First, there's light. Then, something like you, or a tree, or a box, blocks that light. Because the light cannot pass through, a dark area forms behind the object, which is the shadow.

Shadows Need Light to Form

  • No light, no shadow.
  • Shadows are friends of light!

🎙️ This is a very important rule: shadows can only be made if there is light! If you are in a completely dark room, you won't see any shadows. Shadows are always there when light is around and gets blocked.

Tree Shadows

  • Trees also make shadows.
  • They give us cool shade.
  • Perfect for resting under!

🎙️ Just like you, big trees also block sunlight and make shadows. That's why it feels cooler under a tree on a hot day. The tree's shadow acts like a big umbrella, keeping the sun's rays away from you.

Objects and Light: Opaque

  • Some things block all light.
  • We call them opaque objects.
  • Like a wall or a thick book.

🎙️ Imagine a wall. Can you see through it? No, right? Objects that do not let any light pass through them are called opaque. These are the objects that make clear, dark shadows.

🧱

14 / 20

Objects and Light: Transparent

  • Some things let all light pass.
  • We call them transparent objects.
  • Like clear glass or clean water.

🎙️ Now, think about a window pane. You can see everything clearly through it! Objects that let almost all light pass through them are called transparent. They don't make shadows or make very, very faint ones.

🪟
Window lets light in

Objects and Light: Translucent

  • Some things let only some light pass.
  • We call them translucent objects.
  • Like frosted glass or butter paper.

🎙️ There's a third type too! Some objects let a little bit of light through, but not enough to see clearly. They make fuzzy or blurry shadows. Think of looking through frosted glass; you can see light but not clear shapes.
