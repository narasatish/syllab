  • A shadow is a dark patch.
  • It is formed when light is blocked.
  • Your own body can make a shadow!

🎙️ When you stand in the sun, do you see a dark shape behind or in front of you? That's your shadow! It's like your silent friend following you.

How is a Shadow Formed?

  • Light shines on an object.
  • The object blocks the light.
  • A dark shadow appears behind the object.

🎙️ It's like playing hide-and-seek with light! When an object stands in light's way, the light can't pass through. So, a dark area forms where the light is stopped.

Things that make Shadows

  • Objects that block all light make clear shadows.
  • These are called opaque objects.
  • Like a book, a wall, or a tree.

🎙️ Some things are like a strong wall for light; they don't let any light pass through them. These objects create very clear and strong shadows. Can you think of more?

Opaque Objects

Not all Objects Make Clear Shadows

  • Some objects let light pass through easily (transparent).
  • Like a clear glass window.
  • Some objects let some light pass (translucent).

🎙️ Think about a clear glass. You can see right through it, right? So, light passes easily, and it makes no shadow. But a frosted glass lets some light through, making a faint shadow.

Shadow Changes Size

  • Your shadow changes throughout the day.
  • It is long in the morning and evening.
  • It is short at noon.

🎙️ Have you noticed your shadow looking like a giant in the morning and evening, but a dwarf at lunchtime? This is because the sun's position in the sky changes.

Why Does Shadow Size Change?

  • The sun's position changes in the sky.
  • When the sun is low, shadows are long.
  • When the sun is high, shadows are short.

🎙️ Think of it like this: if you shine a torch from the side, the shadow is long. If you shine it directly from above, the shadow is small. The sun does the same thing!
