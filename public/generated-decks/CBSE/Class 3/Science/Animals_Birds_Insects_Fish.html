Insects Have Six Legs

Fish Have Fins and Gills

  • Fish have fins to help them swim.
  • They breathe using special parts called gills.

🎙️ Fins are like the hands and feet of a fish, helping them steer and move. Gills are like our lungs, but they help fish breathe under water.

Fish live and breathe in water.

Fish Swim in Water

  • Fish move their tails and fins to swim.
  • They glide smoothly through the water.

🎙️ Watch a fish swim, it looks so graceful! Their tails push them forward, and their fins help them balance and change direction.
