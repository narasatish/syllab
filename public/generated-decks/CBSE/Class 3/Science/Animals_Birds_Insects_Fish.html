Special Parts of an Insect

  • All insects have six legs.
  • Their body is usually divided into three parts: head, thorax, and abdomen.
  • Many insects also have feelers, called antennae.

🎙️ This is a very important rule to remember! If an animal has six legs, it's an insect. Spiders are not insects because they have eight legs!

Fish Breathe with Gills

  • We breathe air with our lungs.
  • Fish have special organs called gills to breathe oxygen from water.
  • They cannot breathe out of water.

🎙️ Gills are like magic filters! They take the oxygen dissolved in the water and help the fish breathe. That's why fish need to stay in water to live.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Mistake 1: Calling a spider an insect (Spiders have 8 legs, not 6).
  • Mistake 2: Thinking all birds can fly (Some, like penguins, cannot).
  • Mistake 3: Forgetting that fish breathe with gills, not lungs.

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, but it's even better to learn from them! Remember these common mix-ups so you can be a super animal expert.
