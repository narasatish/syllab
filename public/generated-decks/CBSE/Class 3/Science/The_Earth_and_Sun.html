Why Do We Have Day and Night?

  • Imagine you are spinning around.
  • One side of you sees the light, the other side is dark.

🎙️ Have you ever spun around like a top? When you turn, different parts of you face different directions. Our Earth does something similar!

14 / 24

Earth Spins Like a Top!

  • Earth is always spinning, very slowly.
  • It takes one full spin to make one day and one night.

🎙️ Our Earth is always turning, like a spinning top, but so smoothly we don't even feel it! One complete spin takes 24 hours, which is one full day.

🌎
15 / 24

Day: When Your Side Faces the Sun

  • When your part of Earth faces the Sun, it's daytime.
  • The Sun's light shines brightly on you!

🎙️ As Earth spins, the part of it that is facing the Sun gets all the lovely sunlight. That's when we experience day, and everything is bright.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.
16 / 24

Night: When Your Side is Away

  • When your part of Earth turns away from the Sun, it's nighttime.
  • It becomes dark, and we see the moon and stars.

🎙️ As Earth keeps spinning, your part moves away from the Sun's light. Then it becomes dark, and we have night. That's when the moon and stars twinkle in the sky.

Earth's Big Journey Around the Sun

  • Imagine running around your friend in a big circle.
  • Earth does something similar around the Sun!

🎙️ Besides spinning like a top, our Earth also goes on a very long walk! It walks around the Sun, just like you might run around a friend in a playground.

Earth's Yearly Orbit

  • Earth travels around the Sun in a path called an orbit.
  • This big journey takes one whole year!

🎙️ This path that Earth takes around the Sun is called an orbit. It takes a very long time to complete this journey – exactly 365 days, which is one year!

🪐
20 / 24

This Journey Makes Seasons!

  • Because of this journey, we have different seasons.
  • Like summer, winter, monsoon, and spring!

🎙️ As Earth travels around the Sun, different parts of it get more or less sunlight at different times. This is why we have lovely changes in weather, which we call seasons!

🌸
21 / 24

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Thinking Sun goes around Earth (it's the other way!).
  • Confusing Earth's spin with its journey around Sun.

🎙️ Sometimes, children think the Sun moves around Earth, but it's Earth that moves! Also, remember Earth spins for day/night and travels around the Sun for a year.
