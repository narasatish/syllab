How Day and Night Happen

  • When Earth spins, one side faces the Sun – that's Day!
  • The other side faces away – that's Night!

🎙️ Because Earth is always spinning, different parts of it face the Sun at different times. When your part of Earth faces the Sun, it's day. When it turns away, it's night!
