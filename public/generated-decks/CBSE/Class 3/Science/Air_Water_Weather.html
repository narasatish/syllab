What is Air?

  • Air is all around us.
  • We cannot see air.
  • But we can feel it!

🎙️ Can you see the air around you? No, right? Air is invisible. But when you feel a gentle breeze on your face, or when leaves rustle, that's air moving! It's everywhere, even though we can't see it.

Air is Everywhere

  • Air is even in empty bottles!

🎙️ Think about a balloon. When it's flat, there's not much air. But when you blow into it, the balloon gets big because you fill it with air! This shows air takes up space and is all around us.

Air Has Weight

  • Even though we can't see it, air has weight.
  • A balloon filled with air is heavier.
  • Than an empty balloon.

🎙️ This might sound surprising! If you take two balloons, one empty and one full of air, and put them on a balance, the one with air will be a little heavier. This proves that air has weight.

Air Needs Space

  • Air takes up space.
  • When you blow a balloon, it gets bigger.
  • Because air fills the space inside.

🎙️ Imagine a box. If it's empty, it's full of air. If you put something else in the box, the air has to move out of the way. Air is like a guest that always needs its own space.

Moving Air is Wind

  • When air moves, we call it wind.
  • A gentle wind is called a breeze.
  • A strong wind can be a storm.

🎙️ You know when you see leaves dancing on trees? That's the air moving! When air moves gently, it's a lovely breeze. But sometimes, it can move very fast and strongly, which we call a storm.

Different Kinds of Wind

  • Breeze: A soft, gentle wind.
  • Gale: A strong, powerful wind.
  • Storm: Very strong wind with rain or snow.

🎙️ We have different names for wind depending on how fast it's moving. A breeze is nice and cool. A gale is much stronger and can make things fly. And a storm can be very powerful, sometimes with thunder and lightning.

What is Water?

  • Water is a liquid.
  • It has no colour, taste, or smell.
  • We need water to live.

🎙️ Now let's talk about water! It's a clear liquid that comes out of our taps. Water doesn't have any special colour or taste of its own, but it's one of the most important things for all living beings.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Water is Important

  • All living things need water.
  • Plants need water to grow.
  • Animals and humans drink water.

🎙️ Just like air, water is essential for life. Plants drink water from the soil to grow big and strong. Animals and humans need to drink water every day to stay healthy and active.

Sources of Water

What is Weather?

  • Weather is how the air feels.
  • It can be hot, cold, rainy, or sunny.
  • Weather changes often.

🎙️ Now let's talk about weather! Weather is what it feels like outside. Sometimes it's sunny and warm, sometimes it's cloudy and cool, and sometimes it rains. The weather can change even in one day!

Clouds bring us rain.

Different Kinds of Weather

  • Sunny day: Bright and warm.
  • Cloudy day: Lots of clouds, less sun.
  • Rainy day: Water falling from sky.

🎙️ We see many types of weather. On a sunny day, the sun shines brightly. On a cloudy day, fluffy clouds cover the sun. And on a rainy day, we see raindrops falling from the sky, making everything fresh.
