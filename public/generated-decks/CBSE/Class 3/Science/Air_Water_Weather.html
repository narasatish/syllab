How Does Rain Happen?

  • Sun heats water, it goes up as vapour (Evaporation).
  • Vapour cools and forms clouds (Condensation).
  • Clouds get heavy and water falls as rain (Precipitation).

🎙️ Have you ever wondered how rain is formed? It's a magical journey! Water from rivers and lakes goes up to the sky, forms clouds, and then comes back down as rain.
