The Water Cycle: Step 1 - Evaporation

  • The sun heats water in rivers and oceans.
  • Water turns into water vapour (a gas) and rises up.

🎙️ The sun is like a giant heater! It warms up the water in lakes and seas. This warm water then turns into a gas called water vapour and floats up into the sky.

☀️⬆️
Sun heating water
16 / 25

The Water Cycle: Step 2 - Condensation

  • High up, the water vapour gets cold.
  • It turns back into tiny water droplets and forms clouds.

🎙️ As the water vapour goes higher, it gets really cold. The tiny droplets of water vapour then join together to form big, fluffy clouds that we see in the sky.

☁️
Clouds forming
17 / 25

The Water Cycle: Step 3 - Precipitation

  • When clouds get too full, they release water.
  • It falls back to Earth as rain, snow, or hail.

🎙️ When the clouds become too heavy with water droplets, they can't hold any more. So, the water falls back to Earth as rain, or sometimes as snow or hail in very cold places.

🌧️⬇️
Rain falling down

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Mixing up wind and air: Wind is moving air.
  • Forgetting water's three forms: Ice, Water, Steam.
  • Thinking weather is climate: Weather is short-term.

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused between air and wind, or forget the different forms of water. Remember, wind is just air that is moving, and water can be solid, liquid, or gas.
