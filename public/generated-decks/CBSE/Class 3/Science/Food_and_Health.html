  • Water helps digest our food.
  • It keeps our body working well.

🎙️ Water is not food, but it's super important! It helps our body break down the food we eat so we can use all the good things from it. Drink plenty of water every day!

Healthy Habits: Wash Hands

  • Always wash hands before eating.
  • It removes germs from our hands.

🎙️ Before you touch your food, remember to wash your hands with soap and water! This simple habit removes all the tiny germs that might be on your hands and keeps you safe.

  • It helps your stomach digest easily.

Healthy Habits: Eat Fresh Food

  • Eat freshly cooked food.
  • Avoid stale or uncovered food.

🎙️ Always try to eat food that is freshly cooked and served. Stale or uncovered food can attract germs and might make you sick. Fresh food is always the best for your health!

🎙️ We all love to eat chips, chocolates, and cold drinks sometimes. These are called junk food. They might taste yummy, but they don't give our body the good things it needs.

Why Junk Food is Bad for Us

  • Gives very little energy.
  • Can make us feel tired or sick.

🎙️ Eating too much junk food can make you feel weak or even sick. It doesn't help you grow or stay strong. It's okay to have a little sometimes, but not every day.
