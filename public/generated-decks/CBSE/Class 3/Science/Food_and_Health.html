  • Eating a little of all kinds of food.

🎙️ A balanced diet is like a team where everyone works together. It means eating the right amount of all three types of food – energy-giving, body-building, and protective foods – to keep our body working perfectly.

Always Wash Your Hands

  • Wash hands before and after eating.
  • This removes germs and keeps you safe.

🎙️ Our hands can carry tiny germs that we can't see. Washing them with soap and water before and after every meal is a very important habit to keep those germs away from our food and body.

Eat Meals on Time

  • Eat breakfast, lunch, and dinner at fixed times.
  • This helps your body digest food well.

🎙️ Our body likes a routine! Eating at regular times helps our digestive system work smoothly. Skipping meals can make you feel weak or hungry, so try to eat on time every day.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Chew Your Food Properly

  • Chew your food slowly and well.
  • This makes it easier for your tummy to digest.

🎙️ Don't rush your food! Chewing food properly breaks it down into smaller pieces, which makes it much easier for your stomach to digest. This also helps you enjoy your meal more.

Eat Clean and Fresh Food

  • Always eat fresh and covered food.
  • Avoid eating stale or uncovered food from outside.

🎙️ Fresh food is the best food! Food that is left uncovered or is old can have germs. Always choose fresh food, especially fruits and vegetables, and make sure your food is always covered.
