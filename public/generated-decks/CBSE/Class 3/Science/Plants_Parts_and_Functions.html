Roots: The Plant's Anchor

  • Roots grow under the soil.
  • They hold the plant firmly.
  • Like an anchor holds a ship.

🎙️ Imagine a big ship in the ocean. It uses an anchor to stay in one place. Roots are like the plant's anchor, keeping it steady and strong in the ground, even on a windy day.

⚓
Anchor

Roots: Drinking for the Plant

  • Roots soak up water.
  • They also take in nutrients.
  • From the soil, like a straw.

🎙️ Roots have another very important job. They are like tiny straws that suck up water and food, called nutrients, from the soil. This water and nutrients are essential for the plant to grow big and healthy.

Stem: The Plant's Pillar

  • The stem stands tall.
  • It holds up the leaves and flowers.
  • Like a pillar holds up a building.

🎙️ Look at the stem. It's like the strong pillar or column that supports a building. Without a strong stem, the plant would just fall over.

🏗️
Building Pillar
10 / 24

Stem: The Plant's Highway

  • Stem carries water and food.
  • From roots to leaves, and back.
  • Like a road for vehicles.

🎙️ The stem is also like a busy highway. It transports the water and nutrients absorbed by the roots all the way up to the leaves. It also carries the food made by the leaves to other parts of the plant.

🛣️
Road
11 / 24

Leaves: The Plant's Kitchen

  • Leaves are usually green.
  • They make food for the plant.
  • Like a kitchen makes food for us.

🎙️ Leaves are amazing! They are like the kitchen of the plant. This is where all the plant's food is prepared. Most leaves are green because of a special substance called chlorophyll.

🍳
Kitchen
12 / 24

Leaves: Making Food with Sunlight

  • Leaves use sunlight, water, air.
  • To make yummy food for the plant.
  • This is called photosynthesis.

🎙️ How do leaves make food? They use sunlight, water from the roots, and air around them. This special process of making food is called photosynthesis. It's how plants get their energy.

  • Seeds grow into new plants.
  • Like baby plants in a cradle.

Fruits: The Seed's Home

  • Fruits grow from flowers.
  • They protect the seeds inside.
  • Like a shell protects an egg.

🎙️ After a flower, often a fruit grows. Fruits are delicious, but their main job is to protect the seeds inside them. Think of it as a cozy, safe home for the seeds.

Fruits are tasty and healthy.

Fruits: Spreading Seeds Far

  • Animals eat fruits.
  • They help spread the seeds.
  • Seeds can grow in new places.

🎙️ When animals and humans eat fruits, they often throw away the seeds. This helps spread the seeds to new places, allowing new plants to grow far away from the parent plant.

Animals live all around us.

Watch Out for These!

  • Confusing roots and stems.
  • Thinking all plants have flowers.
  • Forgetting leaves make food.

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused between roots and stems, or think all plants have big, colourful flowers. Remember that leaves are the plant's food factory, even if you can't see a flower.
