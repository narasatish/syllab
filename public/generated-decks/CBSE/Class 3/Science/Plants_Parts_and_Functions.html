What are Plants?

  • Plants are living things.
  • Just like us, they grow and need food and water.
  • They make our air fresh and clean.

🎙️ Plants are amazing living things, just like you and me. They start small and grow big. They need sunlight, water, and good soil to live and grow, and they give us the fresh air we breathe.

Plants make their own food from sunlight.

  • A plant has many different parts.
  • Each part does a special job.
  • Let's learn about them!

🎙️ Imagine a plant like a small house, with different rooms and parts. Each part of the plant has a very important job to do to help the plant live and grow strong. We'll explore each part today.

The Roots: The Anchor

  • Roots grow under the ground.
  • They hold the plant firmly in the soil.
  • Roots drink water and food for the plant.

🎙️ The roots are like the plant's feet, holding it steady in the ground so it doesn't fall over. They also act like straws, sucking up all the water and good food from the soil for the whole plant.

💧
Roots drink water

Roots We Eat!

  • Some roots are very yummy to eat.
  • Carrots and radishes are roots.
  • They are good for our health!

🎙️ Did you know that some of the vegetables we eat are actually roots? Carrots and radishes are popular examples. They are full of vitamins and minerals, making them a healthy snack.

🥕🥕🥕
Yummy Roots

The Stem: The Highway

  • The stem grows above the ground.
  • It helps the plant stand tall and straight.
  • It carries water and food to all parts.

🎙️ The stem is like the plant's backbone, helping it stand tall towards the sun. It also works like a busy highway, transporting water from the roots and food from the leaves to every part of the plant.

⬆️
Stem stands tall

Stems We Eat!

  • We eat some stems too!
  • Sugarcane is a sweet stem.
  • Potatoes are also underground stems.

🎙️ Just like roots, some stems are also delicious to eat. Sugarcane is a tall stem that gives us sugar. Potatoes, which grow underground, are also a type of stem that stores food.

🥔🥔🥔
Tasty Stems

The Leaves: The Food Factory

  • Leaves are usually green and flat.
  • They make food for the whole plant.
  • They are like the plant's kitchen!

🎙️ Leaves are incredibly important; they are the plant's very own food factory! Using sunlight, water, and air, they cook up all the food the plant needs to grow big and strong. This process is called photosynthesis.

A balanced plate keeps us healthy.

Leaves We Eat!

  • Many leaves are healthy for us to eat.
  • Spinach, cabbage, and lettuce are leaves.
  • They keep us strong and healthy.

🎙️ We eat a lot of leaves in our daily meals! Green leafy vegetables like spinach, cabbage, and lettuce are packed with nutrients. Eating them helps us stay healthy and energetic, just like they help the plant.

🥬🥬🥬
Green Leaves

The Flowers: The Beauty

  • Flowers are the most colourful part.
  • They make the plant look beautiful.
  • Flowers grow into fruits and seeds.

🎙️ Flowers are truly the jewels of the plant kingdom, with their vibrant colours and sweet smells. But they are not just for beauty; they have a very important job: they are where the plant makes its seeds and fruits.

Flowers are the colourful part of plants.

Pretty Flowers Around Us

  • Roses, sunflowers, and marigolds are flowers.
  • They brighten up our gardens and homes.
  • Some flowers smell very sweet!

🎙️ Think about all the beautiful flowers you see in parks and gardens! Roses are known for their fragrance, and sunflowers always face the sun. These flowers add so much joy and colour to our lives.

The Fruits: The Protectors

  • Fruits grow from flowers.
  • They hold and protect the seeds inside.
  • Fruits are often sweet and juicy.

🎙️ After a flower blooms, it often turns into a fruit. Fruits are like natural packages, keeping the precious seeds safe inside. They come in many shapes, sizes, and flavours, often sweet and delicious.

Fruits are tasty and healthy.

Fruits We Love to Eat!

  • Mango, apple, banana are all fruits.
  • They are full of vitamins and energy.
  • Eating fruits keeps us healthy.

🎙️ We all love eating fruits, don't we? Mangoes, apples, and bananas are some of our favourites. They are nature's candy, packed with goodness that helps us grow strong and stay healthy every day.

🥭🥭🥭
Sweet Fruits

The Seeds: The Future

  • Seeds are found inside fruits.
  • A seed can grow into a new baby plant!
  • They hold the life of a new plant.

🎙️ Inside every fruit, you'll find seeds. These tiny seeds are truly amazing because each one holds the potential to grow into a brand new plant, just like its parent. They are the future of the plant.

🌱
Seed grows a plant

Seeds We Eat!

  • We eat many seeds too.
  • Rajma (kidney beans) and chana (chickpeas) are seeds.
  • They are good sources of protein.

🎙️ Many of the foods we eat are actually seeds! Rajma, chana, and even nuts are all seeds. They are very nutritious and give us a lot of energy and protein, helping our bodies grow strong.

Recap: All Parts are Important!

  • Roots hold the plant and drink water.
  • Stem helps it stand tall and carries food/water.
  • Leaves make food, flowers make fruits/seeds.
  • Fruits protect seeds, and seeds grow new plants.

🎙️ So, we've learned that every single part of a plant is super important and has a special role. They all work together like a team to help the plant live, grow, and make new plants. Isn't that amazing?
