What is a Story?

  • A story tells us about people or animals.
  • It tells us what happens to them.
  • Stories are fun to read and listen to!

🎙️ A story is like a small movie in our minds! It has characters and things that happen. We can read them in books or listen to them from our elders.

Why Read Stories?

  • Stories help us imagine new worlds.
  • They teach us new words.
  • We learn good things from stories.

🎙️ Reading stories is like magic! It takes us to new places without moving. We also learn new words and good lessons that help us in life.

Who are the Characters?

  • Characters are the people or animals in a story.
  • They do all the actions.
  • Think of your friends, they are characters in your life story!

🎙️ Every story has characters, just like how you have friends in your life. They are the ones who make the story interesting by doing different things.

Where does the Story Happen? (Setting)

  • The setting is where the story takes place.
  • It could be a house, a park, or a jungle.
  • Look for clues in the pictures or words!

🎙️ The setting tells us the place and sometimes the time of the story. Is it happening in a big city or a quiet village? Is it day or night? The setting helps us feel like we are there!

The Beginning of a Story

  • This is how the story starts.
  • It introduces the characters and the setting.
  • Sometimes, a problem begins here!

🎙️ Just like how your day starts in the morning, a story also has a beginning. It's when we meet everyone and see where they are.

What Happens Next? (Middle)

  • The middle part has most of the events.
  • Characters try to solve problems.
  • Many exciting things happen here!

🎙️ After the beginning, things start to happen! This is the longest part where the characters face challenges and try to find a way out.

How does the Story End?

  • The end tells us how the story finishes.
  • The problem is usually solved.
  • Characters often learn something new.

🎙️ Every story comes to an end, just like your school day finishes. The end tells us if the characters found a solution or what happened to them finally.

How to Read a Story Well

  • 1. Look at the pictures carefully.
  • 2. Read the words slowly and clearly.
  • 3. Try to understand what is happening.

🎙️ When you read a story, remember these steps. First, look at the pictures, they tell you a lot! Then read the words carefully, and always try to understand the meaning.
