How to Read a Story Well

  • Let's learn to be super readers!
  • It's like learning steps to a fun dance.

🎙️ Reading a story well is a skill, and we can all learn it! We will follow a few simple steps to make our story reading even better and more enjoyable.

1Look at pictures
2Read slowly
3Use your voice
4Understand words
14 / 24

Step 1: Look at the Pictures!

  • Pictures tell us a lot about the story.
  • They can help us guess what will happen next.
  • Look at them carefully!

🎙️ Before you even start reading, take a moment to look at all the colourful pictures in the book. They are like clues that give you hints about the story and its characters.

🖼️
Look at the pictures
15 / 24

Step 2: Read Slowly and Clearly

  • Don't rush, enjoy each word.
  • Read one sentence at a time.
  • This helps you understand better.

🎙️ Reading is not a race! Take your time, like a slow turtle. Read each word and each sentence carefully. This way, you won't miss any important details and will understand the story fully.

🐢
Read like a turtle
16 / 24

Step 3: Use Your Voice!

  • Read with feelings and expression.
  • Make your voice soft for a mouse, loud for a lion!
  • This makes the story come alive.

🎙️ When you read aloud, use your voice to show how the characters are feeling. If a character is scared, make your voice soft. If they are angry, make it a bit louder. This makes the story exciting for everyone!

🗣️
Read with expression
17 / 24

Step 4: Understand New Words

  • If you see a new word, don't worry.
  • Look at the picture for a clue.
  • Or ask your teacher or parents.

🎙️ Sometimes, you might find words you don't know. That's okay! Try to guess the meaning from the pictures or the other words in the sentence. If you still don't know, always ask for help. It's how we learn!

🧐
Understand new words

Common Mistakes in Reading

  • Skipping words or sentences.
  • Reading too fast without understanding.
  • Not paying attention to punctuation (like full stops).

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes while reading, like skipping words or reading too quickly. Remember to slow down and pay attention to every word and punctuation mark. This helps you understand everything better.

Exam Tips for Story Reading

  • Read the story carefully, twice if needed.
  • Look for characters, setting, and what happened.
  • Answer questions in full sentences.

🎙️ In exams, you might get a story to read and then answer questions. Always read the story at least twice. Find out who the characters are, where the story takes place, and what the main problem and solution were. Write your answers clearly.

📝
