What is a Story?

  • A story tells us about people, animals, or things.
  • It tells us what they do and what happens to them.

🎙️ Think of your favourite cartoon or a bedtime tale. That's a story! It's like a little adventure in words, waiting for you to discover.

🎙️ Reading stories is like playing a game with your imagination. You can visit new places and meet new friends, all from your comfy chair!

Who is in the Story? (Characters)

  • Characters are the people, animals, or things in a story.
  • They do all the actions!

🎙️ Every story needs someone to be in it! These are called characters. They can be a brave prince, a talking animal, or even a magical tree!

Where and When Does it Happen? (Setting)

  • The setting is where and when the story takes place.
  • Is it in a forest? Or at school?

🎙️ Just like your home is a place, a story also has a place and a time. It could be a bustling city, a quiet village, or even outer space!

What Happens First? (Beginning)

  • The beginning introduces the characters and the setting.
  • It sets the scene for the story.

🎙️ Every story starts somewhere. The beginning tells us who is there and where they are. It's like opening the first page of a new adventure.

How Does it Finish? (End)

  • It brings the story to a close.

🎙️ After all the adventures, the story needs to finish. The end tells us what happens to the characters and how everything turns out in the end.

How to Read a Story Well

  • Imagine the story in your mind!

🎙️ Reading is not just saying words. It's like watching a movie in your head! Try to see what is happening as you read and feel the story.
