What is Time? ⏰

  • Time tells us WHEN things happen.
  • It can be 'before now', 'right now', or 'after now'.

🎙️ Think about your day. Some things already happened, some are happening right now, and some will happen later. That's what time is all about!

Tenses are Time Words!

  • Tenses help us know WHEN an action happens.
  • They are like magic words for time.

🎙️ When we use words to tell stories or talk about our day, tenses help us show if something happened in the past, is happening now, or will happen in the future.

Past Tense: What Happened?

  • Past Tense talks about things that are OVER.
  • It happened already! It's gone by.

🎙️ Imagine something that finished a moment ago, or yesterday, or even last year. We use the Past Tense to talk about all those things that are now behind us.

🔙
Gone By

Yesterday, I played football.

  • 'Played' tells us the game is finished.
  • It happened in the past, yesterday.

🎙️ See the word 'played'? It means the game is over. You finished playing football yesterday. This is a Past Tense word, showing an action that is completed.

Present Tense: What is Happening Now?

  • Present Tense talks about things happening NOW.
  • Or things that happen every day.

🎙️ This tense is for everything that is going on at this very moment. It also tells us about things we do regularly, like brushing our teeth every morning.

➡️
Right Now
8 / 20

Today, I read a book.

  • 'Read' tells us you are reading now or every day.
  • It's happening in the present.

🎙️ When you say 'I read a book', it means you are doing it now, or you do it often, like every day. This is a Present Tense action, happening in the current time.

Right now, I am smiling.

  • It's a happy present action!

🎙️ Look at your face! If you are smiling right now, that's a Present Tense action. It's happening as we speak, at this very moment.

Future Tense: What Will Happen?

  • Future Tense talks about things that are YET to happen.
  • They will happen later, in the coming time!

🎙️ This tense is for all the exciting things that are going to happen next! It's about tomorrow, next week, or even when you grow up. It's about what's coming.

🔜
Coming Soon
11 / 20

Tomorrow, I will go to school.

  • 'Will go' means it's not happened yet.
  • It will happen tomorrow, in the future.

🎙️ When you say 'I will go to school tomorrow', you are talking about something that has not happened yet. It's an action for the future, planned for tomorrow.

Next week, I will draw a picture.

  • 'Will draw' means you plan to do it later.
  • It will happen next week, in the future.

🎙️ You haven't drawn the picture yet, have you? You are planning to do it next week. So, 'will draw' is a Future Tense word, indicating a future action.
