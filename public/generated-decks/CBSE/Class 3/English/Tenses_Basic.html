  • They happened sometime before NOW.
  • Think of things you did yesterday!

🎙️ Past tense is like looking back at a photo album. It shows us all the things that have already taken place. The action is done and over.

Past Tense: Special Words

  • Many past tense words end with '-ed'.
  • Like 'walked', 'jumped', 'cleaned'.
  • Some words change completely, like 'eat' becomes 'ate'.

🎙️ Listen carefully for the '-ed' sound at the end of many words. That's a big clue for the past tense! But remember, some words are tricky and change their form.

Present Tense: Helping Words

  • We often use 'is', 'am', 'are' with actions happening now.
  • For daily actions, we add '-s' or '-es' to the verb.
  • Like 'read' becomes 'reads', 'go' becomes 'goes'.

🎙️ These helping words like 'is', 'am', 'are' are very important for present tense. Also, remember to add 's' or 'es' when the action is done by one person or thing.

🗣️

Future Tense: The Magic Word 'Will'

  • We almost always use 'will' before the action word.
  • Like 'will sing', 'will dance', 'will play'.
  • Sometimes we use 'shall' too, but 'will' is more common.

🎙️ The word 'will' is like a crystal ball for verbs! It tells us that the action is going to happen in the future. Just add 'will' before the main verb.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Mixing 'is' (present) with 'was' (past).
  • Forgetting '-ed' for past actions (e.g., 'jump' instead of 'jumped').
  • Not using 'will' for future actions (e.g., 'I go' instead of 'I will go' for tomorrow).

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, but it's even better to learn from them! Remember these common errors so you can avoid them in your sentences.
