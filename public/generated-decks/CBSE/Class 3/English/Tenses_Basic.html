What are Tenses?

  • Tenses tell us WHEN an action happens.
  • It's like a time machine for words!

🎙️ Imagine you do many things in a day. Some you did already, some you are doing right now, and some you will do later. Tenses help us show that time!

Three Main Times

  • There are three main times for our actions:
  • Past, Present, Future.

🎙️ Just like we have morning, afternoon, and night, actions also have three main times. Let's look at each one carefully.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Past Tense: What Happened?

  • Past tense tells us about things that already happened.
  • It's done!

🎙️ Think about what you did yesterday, or even a few minutes ago. That's the past! The action is finished.

⏪
Done Already
5 / 20

Past Example: I Ate

  • Yesterday, I ate a yummy apple.
  • The eating is finished.

🎙️ See? The word 'ate' tells us the action of eating is over. It happened in the past, maybe yesterday or last week.

🍎
One Apple

Present Tense: What is Happening?

  • Present tense tells us about things happening NOW.
  • It's happening right now!

🎙️ Look around you! What are you doing right now? What am I doing right now? That's the present tense. It's happening at this very moment.

▶️
Happening Now
7 / 20

Present Example: I Eat

  • Now, I eat my lunch.
  • I am eating it right this minute.

🎙️ The word 'eat' shows that the action is happening at this moment. You might be eating your lunch, or I might be teaching you.

🥪
One Sandwich

Future Tense: What Will Happen?

  • Future tense tells us about things that will happen later.
  • It's yet to come!

🎙️ Think about what you will do after school, or tomorrow. Those actions are in the future. They haven't happened yet, but they will!

⏩
Will Happen
9 / 20

Future Example: I Will Eat

  • Tomorrow, I will eat a big cake.
  • It has not happened yet.

🎙️ The words 'will eat' tell us that the action of eating the cake will happen later, maybe tomorrow or next week. It's something to look forward to!

🎂

Quick Recap: Three Tenses

  • Past: Happened already.
  • Present: Happening now.
  • Future: Will happen later.

🎙️ So, remember these three important times. They help us understand when things are happening in our stories and sentences.

1Past (Yesterday)
2Present (Now)
3Future (Tomorrow)

Clue Words Help Us!

  • Some words tell us about time.
  • Yesterday, Last week (Past).
  • Now, Today (Present).
  • Tomorrow, Next week (Future).

🎙️ These special words are like little helpers! When you see them in a sentence, they give you a big hint about which tense to use.
