What are Verbs?

  • Verbs are 'doing words'.
  • They tell us what someone or something does.
  • Think of all the actions you do!

🎙️ Simply put, verbs are words that show action. If you are doing something, that 'doing' word is a verb! Like running, jumping, or even thinking.

Verbs also tell us 'what someone IS'.

  • These are called 'being words'.
  • They tell us about someone's state.
  • Words like 'is', 'am', 'are'.

🎙️ Sometimes, verbs don't show a physical action, but they tell us about someone's state or existence. These are called 'being verbs'. The most common ones are 'is', 'am', and 'are'.

🧍
Being
15 / 20

Examples of 'Being Words': Is, Am, Are

  • He **is** happy. (He is in a state of being happy)
  • I **am** a student. (I exist as a student)
  • They **are** friends. (They exist as friends)

🎙️ We use 'is' for one person or thing, 'am' only with 'I', and 'are' for many people or things. These words connect the subject to what it is or what it is like.
