  • Verbs are very important words.
  • They tell us what is happening.

🎙️ Verbs are like the engines of our sentences. They make sentences move and tell us what is happening. Without verbs, our sentences would be very boring!

Verbs are 'Doing Words'!

  • They show actions we do every day.
  • Like running, jumping, eating!

🎙️ The easiest way to understand verbs is to think of them as 'doing words'. These are words that show an action. Think about all the things you do in a day!

Verbs Also Tell 'Is' or 'Has'!

  • Sometimes verbs don't show action.
  • They tell us about things.

🎙️ Now, sometimes verbs don't show action, but they tell us what someone 'is' or 'has'. These are also very important verbs. Let's learn about them!

12 / 20

'Being' Verbs: Is, Am, Are

  • I am a student.
  • She is happy.
  • We are friends.

🎙️ Words like 'is', 'am', and 'are' tell us about someone's state or what they are. For example, 'I am a student' tells us what I am. 'She is happy' tells us about her feeling.

😊
Being Happy
13 / 20

'Having' Verbs: Has, Have

  • I have a red pen.
  • He has a new toy.

🎙️ Words like 'has' and 'have' tell us what someone possesses or owns. 'I have a red pen' means the pen belongs to me. 'He has a new toy' means he owns a new toy.
