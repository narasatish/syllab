What is a Letter?

  • A letter is a message we write.
  • We send it to someone special.

🎙️ Think of a letter like a paper hug or a paper chat. You write down what you want to say, put it in an envelope, and send it. It's like sending a piece of your heart to someone!

Part 1: Your Address

  • This is where YOU live.
  • We write it at the top right side.

🎙️ The first thing in a friendly letter is your address. This tells the person who you are sending the letter to, where you live. It helps them know who the letter is from, even before they see your name.

🎙️ Next, we write the date. This tells the reader when you wrote the letter. It's important to know if the news is new or old! It usually comes right below your address.
