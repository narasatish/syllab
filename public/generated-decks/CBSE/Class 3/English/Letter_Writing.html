What's Inside a Letter?

  • A letter has different parts.
  • Like a house has different rooms!

🎙️ Just like a yummy cake has different layers, a letter also has different parts. Each part has its own special job to make the letter complete and easy to understand.

Part 1: Your Home Address

  • It tells where YOU live.
  • So the person knows who sent the letter.
  • Write it at the top right corner.

🎙️ First, you write your own address. This is important because it tells the person receiving the letter where it came from. It's like putting your return ticket on a bus!

Part 2: The Date

  • Write the day the letter was written.
  • Like '15th August 2023'.
  • It comes just below your address.

🎙️ Next, we write the date. This tells the person when you wrote the letter. It helps them remember when they received your special message.

Part 3: Say Hello! (Greeting)

  • Start with 'Dear' and the person's name.
  • Like 'Dear Papa,' or 'Dear Rina,'
  • It's how you say hello to them.

🎙️ After the date, we write a greeting. This is how you start talking to the person you are writing to. It's like saying 'Hi!' to them on paper.

Part 4: Your Main Message (Body)

  • This is where you write everything!
  • Share your news, feelings, or questions.
  • Keep it simple and clear.

🎙️ This is the most exciting part! The body of the letter is where you write all the things you want to tell. It's like telling a story to your friend.

Our body has many organs working together.

Part 5: How to End (Closing)

  • Say goodbye politely.
  • Use words like 'Yours lovingly,' or 'With love,'
  • It comes after your main message.

🎙️ After you've shared your message, it's time to say goodbye. The closing shows how you feel about the person. For family and friends, we use loving words.

Part 6: Your Name

  • Write your own name below the closing.
  • So the person knows who sent the letter.
  • Always sign off with your name!

🎙️ Finally, you write your name. This tells the person exactly who sent them the letter. It's like putting your unique stamp on your special message!
