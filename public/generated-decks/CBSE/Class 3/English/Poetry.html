Rhyming Words: Sounds Like Friends!

  • Words that sound similar at the end.
  • Like 'cat' and 'mat'.

🎙️ Rhyming words are like best friends because they sound so good together! They have the same ending sound, which makes them fun to say and easy to remember.

Finding Rhyming Words

  • Does it sound like another word you know?

🎙️ To find rhyming words, you need to use your ears! Say the word aloud and really focus on the sound it makes at the very end. That's where the magic happens.

Rhythm in Poems: The Poem's Beat!

  • Rhythm is like the beat of a drum.
  • It makes poems flow like a song.

🎙️ Every poem has a special beat, just like your favourite song! This beat is called rhythm. It's what makes a poem fun to read aloud and gives it a musical feel.

🥁
Drum Beat

Listening to the Beat

  • Some words are said stronger.
  • Some words are said softer.

🎙️ When you read a poem, try to listen for the ups and downs in the sound. Some parts might feel like a strong 'boom,' and others like a gentle 'tap.' This is the rhythm.

🎶
Musical Flow

🎙️ Think about why you can remember song lyrics so easily. It's often because of the rhythm! Rhythm helps our brains remember poems and makes them delightful to say out loud.

Stanzas: Poem Paragraphs!

  • Stanzas are like small groups of lines.
  • They are like paragraphs in a story.

🎙️ Just like a story is divided into paragraphs, a poem is divided into stanzas. Think of them as building blocks that make up the whole poem, each holding a little idea.

🧱
Building Blocks

What is a Stanza?

  • A stanza is a group of lines in a poem.
  • It usually has 2, 3, or 4 lines.

🎙️ When you see a poem, you'll notice spaces between groups of lines. Each group is a stanza. It helps to organize the poem and sometimes marks a new idea.

📄
Poem Lines

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Forgetting to listen for rhyming words.
  • Not feeling the poem's rhythm.

🎙️ It's easy to miss some things when reading poems for the first time. Remember to always listen for those rhyming friends and try to feel the poem's beat. Don't rush!

Exam Tips for Poetry!

  • Read the poem carefully, twice!
  • Look for rhyming words and underline them.
  • Try to read it aloud to feel the rhythm.

🎙️ When you have a poem in your exam, take your time. Reading it a few times will help you understand it better. Don't forget to look for rhymes and feel the rhythm, it will help you answer questions.
