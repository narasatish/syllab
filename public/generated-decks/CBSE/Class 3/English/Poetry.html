What is a Poem?

  • A poem is like a short song.
  • It uses beautiful words.
  • It tells a story or shares a feeling.

🎙️ Imagine a story, but shorter and sung with words. That's a poem! Poems are special because they use words in a very pretty way. They can make us feel happy, sad, or even thoughtful, just like a good story.

Poems Have Rhyming Words

  • Rhyming words sound alike.
  • Like 'cat' and 'hat'.
  • They make poems musical!

🎙️ One super fun thing about poems is rhyming words! These are words that have the same ending sound, like 'star' and 'car'. When words rhyme, they create a lovely sound pattern, making the poem feel like a song.

Poems Have a Rhythm

  • Rhythm is the beat of the poem.
  • Like a drum's 'boom-boom'.
  • It makes poems fun to read.

🎙️ Besides rhyming, poems also have a rhythm. Think of it like a song with a steady beat, like when you clap your hands. This rhythm helps the poem flow nicely and makes it enjoyable to listen to and read aloud.

🥁

How to Read a Poem?

  • Read it slowly and clearly.
  • Listen to the sounds.
  • Feel the rhythm as you read.

🎙️ When you read a poem, it's not like reading a normal story. You should read it slowly, paying attention to the words and how they sound. Try to feel the beat and let the words dance in your mouth!
