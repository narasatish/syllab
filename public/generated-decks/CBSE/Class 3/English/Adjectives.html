What are Describing Words?

  • Describing words tell us more about naming words.
  • They tell us 'how' something is.
  • They are also called ADJECTIVES!

🎙️ Now, imagine you have a naming word like 'ball'. How would you tell someone more about this ball? You might say it's big, or red, or bouncy! These words – big, red, bouncy – are describing words, or adjectives.

Adjectives Tell About Size

Adjectives Tell About Number

  • Adjectives tell us 'how many' there are.
  • Like 'one apple', 'two birds', 'many stars'.
  • How many fingers do you have?

🎙️ Sometimes, adjectives tell us how many of something there are. If you see 'three' pencils, 'three' is an adjective. If you say 'some' cookies, 'some' is also an adjective telling us about the quantity.

Adjectives Tell About Feelings

Adjectives Make Sentences Interesting!

  • Without adjectives: 'I saw a cat.'
  • With adjectives: 'I saw a **fluffy black** cat.'
  • Adjectives add details and make stories better!

🎙️ See how much more interesting a sentence becomes with adjectives? Instead of just 'I saw a cat', we can say 'I saw a fluffy black cat'. It paints a clearer picture in our minds. Adjectives are truly amazing!
