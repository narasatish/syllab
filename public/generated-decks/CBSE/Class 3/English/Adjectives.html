What are Describing Words?

  • These words tell us more about people, animals, places, or things.
  • They paint a picture in our minds!
  • We also call them Adjectives.

🎙️ Imagine you see a beautiful flower. What words can you use to tell me about it? Maybe 'red', 'big', or 'pretty'. These are describing words!

Adjectives Describe Nouns!

  • Remember nouns are names of people, animals, places, or things?
  • Adjectives tell us more about these nouns.

🎙️ So, adjectives are like little helpers for nouns. They add extra information and make our sentences more colourful.

Adjectives Tell 'How Many'?

  • Some adjectives tell us the number of things.
  • Like 'one', 'two', 'many', or 'few'.

🎙️ If I ask you how many apples are there, you would say 'three'. The word 'three' is describing the number of apples.

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

  • 'Five' tells us the number of balloons.

🎙️ Here, the word 'five' is an adjective. It describes the noun 'balloons' by telling us how many there are.

Adjectives Tell 'What Kind'?

  • Other adjectives tell us about the quality of something.
  • They describe its colour, size, shape, or how it feels.

🎙️ These adjectives help us imagine what something looks like, feels like, or even sounds like! They give us more details.

Adjectives Tell 'Which One'?

  • Some adjectives point out a specific noun.
  • Like 'this' book or 'that' tree.

🎙️ These words help us identify exactly which person, animal, place, or thing we are talking about. They are like pointing with words.

👉
Pointing out

Example: Which Kitten?

  • 'This' kitten is very cute.
  • 'This' tells us exactly which kitten we mean.

🎙️ If there were many kittens, saying 'this kitten' helps us know which specific one is cute. 'That' would be for something further away.
