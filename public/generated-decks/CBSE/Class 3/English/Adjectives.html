What is an Adjective?

  • Words that describe nouns.
  • They tell us more about people, animals, places, or things.

🎙️ An adjective is a special word that tells us more about a noun. Remember, a noun is a naming word. Adjectives give us more information about these names.

Adjectives Tell 'How Many?'

  • They tell us the number of things.
  • Like 'one', 'two', 'many', 'few'.

🎙️ Sometimes, adjectives tell us exactly how many items there are. For example, 'three apples' or 'five pencils'. They help us count!

🍎🍎🍎
Three Apples
9 / 24

More 'How Many?' Examples

  • **Four** birds are flying.
  • **Many** stars twinkle.
  • **Some** children are playing.

🎙️ Here, 'four' tells us the number of birds. 'Many' tells us a quantity of stars, and 'some' tells us about a group of children. These are all adjectives of quantity.

Birds have feathers and can fly.

Adjectives Tell 'What Kind?'

  • They tell us about the quality of a noun.
  • Like 'beautiful', 'tall', 'soft', 'brave'.

🎙️ These adjectives describe the nature or quality of a noun. They tell us what kind of person, animal, or thing it is. Is it big or small? Happy or sad?

🌈
Colours & Qualities

  • A **beautiful** flower.
  • A **brave** soldier.
  • A **soft** pillow.

🎙️ The word 'beautiful' tells us what kind of flower it is. 'Brave' tells us about the soldier, and 'soft' tells us about the pillow. They describe qualities.

Common Mistakes with Adjectives

  • Confusing adjectives with verbs (action words).
  • Forgetting to use adjectives to make writing interesting.

🎙️ Sometimes, children confuse adjectives with verbs. Remember, verbs are action words, and adjectives are describing words. Also, don't forget to use adjectives to make your stories more lively!

Exam Tips for Adjectives

  • Look for words that answer 'What kind?', 'How many?', 'Which one?'.
  • Practice identifying adjectives in sentences.
  • Write sentences using different adjectives.

🎙️ In your exams, you might be asked to identify adjectives or use them in sentences. Always ask yourself what the word is describing. Practice makes perfect!
