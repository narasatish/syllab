What is a Noun?

  • A Noun is a naming word.
  • It names a person, place, animal, or thing.

🎙️ Imagine everything around you has a name, right? Your name, your school's name, your pet's name, even your toy's name! All these naming words are called Nouns.

Nouns Name People

  • Names of people are Nouns.
  • Like: boy, girl, teacher, father, mother.

🎙️ Look around your home or classroom. You see many people! Each person has a name or a common name like 'boy' or 'girl'. These are all Nouns.

  • Like: home, school, park, market.

🎙️ Where do you live? That's your home! Where do you study? That's your school! These are names of places, and they are Nouns.

Nouns Name Animals

  • Names of animals are Nouns.
  • Like: dog, cat, lion, elephant.

🎙️ Do you have a pet? What is its name? 'Dog' or 'cat' are animal names. Even animals you see in the zoo, like 'lion' or 'elephant', are Nouns.

  • Like: ball, book, table, toy.

Nouns can be Common!

  • Some nouns are general names.
  • They are called Common Nouns.

🎙️ When we say 'boy', it can be any boy. When we say 'school', it can be any school. These general names that don't point to one special person, place, animal, or thing are called Common Nouns.

Examples of Common Nouns

  • boy, girl
  • school, city
  • dog, bird
  • book, chair

🎙️ These are words we use all the time. They are not specific names like 'Rohan' or 'Delhi Public School'. They are general names for people, places, animals, and things.

What is a Pronoun?

  • A Pronoun is a word that takes the place of a Noun.
  • It helps us avoid repeating the same noun again and again.

🎙️ Imagine you have a friend named Rahul. If you keep saying 'Rahul went to the park. Rahul played with Rahul's ball. Rahul ate Rahul's snack.' It sounds a bit funny, right? Pronouns come to our rescue!

🔄
Replacing Nouns
12 / 20

Why Use Pronouns?

  • Pronouns make our sentences sound better.
  • They make our language smooth and easy to understand.

🎙️ Instead of repeating 'Rahul' many times, we can say 'Rahul went to the park. He played with his ball. He ate his snack.' See how much better that sounds? 'He' is a Pronoun!

Meet 'He' and 'She'

  • 'He' is used for a boy or a man.
  • 'She' is used for a girl or a woman.

🎙️ If the noun is a boy's name like 'Rohan', we use 'He'. If the noun is a girl's name like 'Priya', we use 'She'. These are very common pronouns.

Boy and Girl

Meet 'It'

  • 'It' is used for an animal or a thing.
  • We use 'it' when we talk about one animal or one thing.

🎙️ If you are talking about your pet cat, you can say 'The cat is fluffy. It loves to play.' Or if you're talking about a book, 'The book is new. It has many pictures.' 'It' replaces the noun.

🐈
For animals and things

More Pronouns: I, You, We, They

  • 'I' is for yourself. 'You' is for the person you are talking to.
  • 'We' is for you and others. 'They' is for many other people, animals, or things.

🎙️ These pronouns help us talk about ourselves and groups of people or things. 'I am happy.' 'You are smart.' 'We are friends.' 'They are playing.' These are all very useful pronouns.

Let's Use Pronouns!

  • "Rohan likes to play. **He** plays cricket."
  • "The cat is cute. **It** loves milk."
  • "My friends and I went to the park. **We** had fun."

🎙️ See how 'He' replaces 'Rohan', 'It' replaces 'The cat', and 'We' replaces 'My friends and I'? This makes the sentences much smoother and nicer to read or hear.

What We Learned Today!

  • Nouns are naming words for people, places, animals, and things.
  • Pronouns are words that replace nouns to make sentences better.

🎙️ We learned that Nouns give names to everything around us, and Pronouns are their clever little helpers that step in when we don't want to repeat the names. Both are super important for speaking and writing!
