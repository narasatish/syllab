What is a Noun?

  • A Noun is a naming word.
  • It names people, places, animals, or things.
  • Everything around us has a name!

🎙️ Look around you! Everything you see has a name. Your name, your friend's name, your school, your pet, your toy – all these names are Nouns. They help us identify everything.

Common Nouns: General Names

  • These are general names for people, places, animals, or things.
  • They do not name a *particular* one.
  • Examples: boy, city, dog, book.

🎙️ Imagine you say 'boy'. There are many boys, right? 'Boy' is a common noun because it's a general name. It doesn't tell us *which* boy specifically.

Proper Nouns: Special Names

  • These are special names for *particular* people, places, animals, or things.
  • They always begin with a capital letter.
  • Examples: Rohan, Mumbai, Pluto (a dog's name), The Jungle Book.

🎙️ Now, if I say 'Rohan', you know exactly *which* boy I am talking about. 'Rohan' is a proper noun. Proper nouns are like special tags for specific things, and they always start with a big letter!

Common vs. Proper Nouns

What is a Pronoun?

  • A Pronoun is a word that takes the place of a Noun.
  • It helps us avoid repeating the same noun many times.
  • Think of it as a helpful substitute!

🎙️ Sometimes, using the same name over and over again can sound a bit boring. That's where pronouns come to help! They step in and take the noun's place, making our sentences sound smoother.

Pronouns for People and Animals

  • He: for one boy/man (e.g., My father -> He)
  • She: for one girl/woman (e.g., My sister -> She)
  • It: for one animal (e.g., The dog -> It)
  • They: for many people/animals (e.g., The children -> They)

🎙️ We use 'He' for boys and men, and 'She' for girls and women. For a single animal, we use 'It'. And when there are many people or animals, we use 'They'.

Pronouns for Things

  • It: for one thing (e.g., The ball -> It is round)
  • They: for many things (e.g., The books -> They are new)

🎙️ Just like with animals, we use 'It' for one thing. If you have many things, like many pencils or many toys, we use 'They' to talk about them.

More Useful Pronouns

  • I: when you talk about yourself (e.g., I am happy)
  • You: when you talk to someone (e.g., You are smart)
  • We: when you talk about yourself and others (e.g., We like to play)

🎙️ 'I' is for me! 'You' is for you! And 'We' is for all of us together! These are very common pronouns we use every day when we talk or write.
