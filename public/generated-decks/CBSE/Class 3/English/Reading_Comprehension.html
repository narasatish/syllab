Step 1: Read Every Word

  • Read slowly and clearly.
  • Don't skip any words!

🎙️ The first step is to read every single word. Read slowly, like you are walking in a garden and looking at every flower. This helps your brain see everything.

👀
Eyes on words
6 / 20

Step 2: Think About What You Read

  • Ask yourself: What is happening?
  • Who is in the story? What are they doing?

🎙️ While you are reading, keep thinking. Ask yourself questions like, 'Who is this character?' or 'What are they doing now?' This helps you connect the ideas.

🤔
Think and ask
7 / 20

Step 3: Answer the Questions

  • After reading, answer questions.
  • Look back at the story if you forget.

🎙️ After you finish reading, you might get some questions. Use what you remember from the story to answer them. If you are unsure, you can always read again.
