What is Reading Comprehension?

  • It means understanding what you read.
  • Like understanding a story your friend tells you.

🎙️ Reading comprehension is simply understanding the meaning of what you read. It's not just saying the words, but knowing what the words mean together. Imagine your friend tells you a story; you listen and understand what happened, right? That's comprehension!

📖
Understand stories

Why is it important?

  • You enjoy stories more.
  • You learn new things from books.
  • It helps you answer questions.

🎙️ When you understand what you read, stories become more fun! You can imagine the characters and places. Also, books are full of knowledge, and reading comprehension helps you learn all those amazing things. It also helps you do well in your school work.

How to Read a Story Well

  • Let's learn some simple steps.

🎙️ There are some easy steps we can follow to become super readers. We will look at each step one by one. These steps will make reading very easy and fun for you.

1Read Carefully
2Understand Words
3Find Main Idea
4Look for Details
5 / 20

Step 1: Read Carefully

  • Read slowly, not too fast.
  • Don't miss any words.
  • Like looking closely at a drawing.

🎙️ The first step is to read every word carefully. Don't rush! Imagine you are looking at a beautiful drawing; you look at every line and colour. Reading carefully means you don't skip any part of the story.

🧐
Read slowly
6 / 20

Step 2: Understand New Words

  • If you see a new word, try to guess its meaning.
  • Look at other words around it.
  • Like guessing what's in a gift box!

🎙️ Sometimes, you might find a new word. Don't worry! Try to guess what it means from the other words in the sentence. It's like when you get a gift, you shake it and try to guess what's inside before opening it!

🤔
Guess meaning
7 / 20

Step 3: Find the Main Idea

  • What is the story mostly about?
  • What is the most important part?
  • Like finding the biggest fruit in a basket.

🎙️ Every story has a main idea. It's like the biggest thing the story wants to tell you. After reading, ask yourself: 'What was this story mostly about?' Is it about a brave cat, or a happy family, or a lost toy? That's the main idea.

💡
The big picture
8 / 20

Step 4: Look for Details

  • Who are the characters?
  • What happened?
  • Where did it happen?
  • When did it happen?

🎙️ Details are the small but important parts of the story. They tell us 'who' did something, 'what' happened, 'where' it happened, and 'when' it happened. These details help you build the whole picture in your mind, just like finding clues in a treasure hunt.

🔎
Find clues
