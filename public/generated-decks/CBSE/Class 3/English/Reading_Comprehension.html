What is Reading Comprehension?

  • It means understanding what you read.
  • Like understanding what your friend tells you.
  • You get the message clearly!

🎙️ Reading comprehension is just like listening comprehension! When your friend tells you a story, you listen carefully and understand it. Reading comprehension means doing the same thing when you read words on a page.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Reading too fast and not understanding.
  • Not re-reading when you are confused.
  • Guessing answers without checking the story.

🎙️ Even super readers make mistakes sometimes! But we can learn to avoid them. Don't read too fast like Ria did. If you don't understand, read it again. And always check the story for answers, don't just guess!

Exam Tips for Reading Comprehension

  • Read the questions first.
  • Then read the passage carefully.
  • Underline important lines or words.

🎙️ When you have a test, here are some smart tips! Read the questions first so you know what to look for. Then read the story carefully. You can even use your pencil to underline important parts that answer the questions.
