Meet Our First Friend: Ones Place

  • Every number has a special 'place' for its digits.
  • The first place from the right is called the 'Ones Place'.

🎙️ Imagine numbers are standing in a line. The digit at the very end, on the right side, is in the Ones Place. It's the simplest place and tells us about single items.

Let's See Ones Place!

  • If you have 5 candies, the digit '5' is in the Ones Place.
  • It just means 5 single candies.

🎙️ Look at these yummy candies! There are 5 of them. The number 5 shows us how many single candies we have. So, 5 is in the Ones Place, meaning 5 units.

Meet Our Second Friend: Tens Place

  • The place next to the Ones Place (on its left) is the 'Tens Place'.
  • It tells us how many groups of ten we have.

🎙️ If you move one step to the left from the Ones Place, you find the Tens Place. This place is very important because it helps us count bigger numbers faster!

What is Place Value?

  • Place value is the value of a digit based on its position in a number.
  • The digit '1' in 12 means 10 (Tens Place).
  • The digit '2' in 12 means 2 (Ones Place).

🎙️ This is the main idea! The same digit can mean different things depending on where it sits. It's like a player on a team; their role changes based on their position.

📍
Position matters!

Finding Place Value: Example 1 (18)

  • In the number 18:
  • The digit '8' is in the Ones Place. Its value is 8.
  • The digit '1' is in the Tens Place. Its value is 10.

🎙️ Let's look at 18. The 8 is easy, it's just 8 ones. But the 1 is in the Tens Place, so it's not just 1, it's 1 ten, which is 10! The place gives it value.

18 in Ones Place (value 8)
21 in Tens Place (value 10)

Finding Place Value: Example 2 (30)

  • In the number 30:
  • The digit '0' is in the Ones Place. Its value is 0.
  • The digit '3' is in the Tens Place. Its value is 30.

🎙️ What about 30? The 0 in the Ones Place means there are no single items. The 3 in the Tens Place means we have 3 groups of ten, which is 30.

10 in Ones Place (value 0)
23 in Tens Place (value 30)
