What is Place Value?

  • Each digit in a number has a special place.
  • This place tells us its value.

🎙️ Imagine numbers living in special houses. Each room in the house is a 'place', and depending on which room a digit lives in, its 'value' changes! This is what Place Value is all about.

🏠
Number Homes

Meet the Ones Place!

  • The first place from the right is the Ones place.
  • It's for single things, like 1, 2, 3...

🎙️ The Ones place is like the smallest room in the number house. It holds single items. If you have 4 stars, they all live in the Ones place because there are less than ten.

Meet the Tens Place!

  • The second place from the right is the Tens place.
  • It's for groups of ten.

🎙️ Now, if you have ten things, they don't fit in the Ones place anymore! They form a 'group of ten' and move to the Tens place. The Tens place is for these special groups.

Number 10: One Ten and Zero Ones

  • Ten single items make one group of Ten.
  • So, 10 has '1' in the Tens place and '0' in the Ones place.

🎙️ What happens when we have 10 lollipops? We can tie them together to make one big bunch! This bunch is 'one ten'. Since there are no single lollipops left, we write '0' in the Ones place.

Let's Break Down 23!

  • The number is 23.
  • The digit '3' is in the Ones place.
  • The digit '2' is in the Tens place.

🎙️ For the number 23, the '3' is in the Ones place, meaning 3 single units. The '2' is in the Tens place, meaning 2 groups of ten, which is 20. So, 23 is 20 + 3.

Let's Break Down 40!

  • The number is 40.
  • The digit '0' is in the Ones place.
  • The digit '4' is in the Tens place.

🎙️ For the number 40, the '0' in the Ones place tells us there are no single units. The '4' in the Tens place means 4 groups of ten, which is 40. So, 40 is just 40!

Why is Place Value Important?

  • It helps us read and write big numbers.
  • It helps us add and subtract easily.

🎙️ Knowing about Place Value is super useful! It helps us understand how big or small numbers are, and it makes doing sums like addition and subtraction much, much easier as you grow up.

Recap: Our Place Value Journey

  • Numbers have Ones and Tens places.
  • Ones are for single items (1-9).
  • Tens are for groups of ten.
  • Place tells us the digit's value.

🎙️ We've learned so much today! Remember, every digit has a special home, either in the Ones place or the Tens place. This home tells us how much that digit is worth. Great job, everyone!
