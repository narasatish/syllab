Ask Your Neighbour for Help!

  • The 'tens' place is like a helpful neighbour.
  • When 'ones' place needs more, it asks 'tens'.
  • The 'tens' place lends 1 'ten'.

🎙️ Think of the numbers in tens and ones places as neighbours. If the ones place doesn't have enough, it can knock on the door of its neighbour, the tens place, and ask for help. The tens place is always kind!

How Borrowing Works

  • The 'tens' gives 1 ten to 'ones'.
  • 1 ten becomes 10 ones!
  • So, 'ones' place gets 10 extra.

🎙️ When the tens place gives 1 ten, it's like giving 10 small candies to the ones place. So, if the ones place had 2, and gets 10 more, it now has 12! But remember, the tens place becomes 1 smaller now.

Always Start from Ones!

  • Always begin subtraction from the right side.
  • This means we start with the 'ones' place.

🎙️ Just like we add from the right, we also subtract from the right. So, always look at the 'ones' column first. This is our golden rule!

Borrow from the Tens Place!

  • Cross out the 'tens' digit, make it 1 less.
  • Add 10 to the 'ones' digit.
  • Now subtract the 'ones' column.

🎙️ This is the core of borrowing! You take 1 from the tens place (so it becomes one less), and that 1 ten becomes 10 ones, which you add to your ones place. Now you have enough to subtract!

Let's Try: 34 - 17

  • Look at 'ones': 4 minus 7. Can we do it? No!
  • Borrow from 'tens' (3 becomes 2).
  • Add 10 to 'ones' (4 becomes 14).

🎙️ Let's do our first example! We have 34 minus 17. First, look at the ones column: 4 minus 7. Since 4 is smaller, we need to borrow. The 3 in the tens place becomes 2, and the 4 in the ones place becomes 14.

14 is < 7
2Borrow 1 from 3 (now 2)
3Add 10 to 4 (now 14)
14 / 24

Finish the Subtraction!

  • Now, subtract 'ones': 14 - 7 = 7.
  • Then, subtract 'tens': 2 - 1 = 1.
  • So, 34 - 17 = 17.

🎙️ Now that our ones place has enough, we can subtract! 14 minus 7 is 7. Then, we move to the tens place. Remember, the 3 became 2, so now it's 2 minus 1, which is 1. Our answer is 17!

One More Example: 52 - 28

  • Ones: 2 minus 8. Borrow!
  • Tens: 5 becomes 4.
  • Ones: 2 becomes 12. (12 - 8 = 4)

🎙️ Let's do another one together. For 52 minus 28, we first look at 2 minus 8. We need to borrow! The 5 becomes 4, and the 2 becomes 12. Now, 12 minus 8 is 4. Then, 4 minus 2 is 2. So the answer is 24.

Watch Out for These Mistakes!

  • Forgetting to reduce the 'tens' digit after borrowing.
  • Borrowing from the wrong place.
  • Not starting from the 'ones' column.

🎙️ Everyone makes mistakes sometimes, and that's okay! But we can learn from them. The most common mistakes are forgetting to make the tens digit smaller after borrowing, or trying to subtract from left to right. Always be careful!
