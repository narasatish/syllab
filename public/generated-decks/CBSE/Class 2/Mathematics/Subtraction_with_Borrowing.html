Always Start with Ones

  • Look at the numbers in the Ones place.

Ask for Help from Tens

  • When Ones can't subtract, they ask Tens for help.
  • Tens is our helpful neighbour!

🎙️ When the Ones place doesn't have enough to give, it goes to its neighbour, the Tens place, and asks for some help. This is what we call 'borrowing'.

Tens Becomes One Less

  • The Tens place had 3. It gave 1 Ten away.
  • So, 3 Tens become 2 Tens.

🎙️ Since the Tens place gave away one of its tens, its value decreases by one. If it was 3 tens, it now becomes 2 tens.

Ones Becomes Bigger

🎙️ Now, the Ones place is happy! It had 2, and it got 10 more from the Tens place. So, 2 plus 10 makes 12. Now it has enough to subtract!

  • So, 32 - 15 = 17.

🎙️ So, in the Tens column, we now have 2 minus 1, which equals 1. Put this 1 in the Tens column of your answer. Ria can give 17 sweets to her friend!

Recap: Subtraction with Borrowing

  • Start with Ones column.
  • If top digit is smaller, borrow from Tens.
  • Tens digit becomes one less.
  • Ones digit becomes 10 more.
  • Subtract Ones, then Tens.

🎙️ Let's quickly review what we learned today. Always start with Ones. If you can't subtract, borrow from Tens. Remember how both digits change. Then, subtract!
