The "Borrowing" Magic!

  • When a Ten lends to Ones, it gives 1 Ten.
  • But 1 Ten becomes 10 Ones!

🎙️ When the Tens place is kind and lends one of its Tens, it becomes one less. But that one Ten, when it goes to the Ones place, becomes 10 little Ones! So the Ones place gets much bigger!

1Tens place gives 1 Ten
2Tens place becomes 1 less
3Ones place gets 10 more

Let's Solve Together! Example 1

  • Always start from the Ones place!

🎙️ Let's try an example: 33 minus 17. Imagine you have 33 candies and you want to give away 17. We always start subtracting from the Ones place, which is on the right.

Step 1: Look at the Ones Place

  • We have 3 Ones, and we need to take away 7 Ones.
  • Can we do it? No!

🎙️ Look at the Ones place. We have 3 on top and 7 below. Can we take 7 from 3? No, 3 is smaller than 7! So, what do we do? We ask our neighbour, the Tens place, for help!

Top Ones: 3
Bottom Ones: 7
10 / 20

Step 2: Borrow from the Tens!

  • The Tens place (3) lends 1 Ten. It becomes 2 Tens.
  • The Ones place (3) gets 10 more! It becomes 13 Ones.

🎙️ The 3 in the Tens place is kind. It gives away 1 Ten, so it becomes 2 Tens. Now, the 3 in the Ones place gets that 1 Ten, which is 10 Ones. So, 3 + 10 = 13 Ones!

13 Tens becomes 2 Tens
23 Ones becomes 13 Ones

Step 3: Subtract the Ones!

  • Now we have 13 Ones.
  • We can easily take away 7 Ones.
  • 13 - 7 = 6.

🎙️ Now that our Ones place has 13, we can subtract 7 easily! Count 13 stars and take away 7. How many are left? Yes, 6! Write 6 in the Ones place of your answer.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
13 stars, take away 7
12 / 20

Step 4: Subtract the Tens!

  • Remember, the Tens place is now 2.
  • We take away 1 Ten from 2 Tens.
  • 2 - 1 = 1.

🎙️ Now let's move to the Tens place. Remember, the 3 Tens became 2 Tens because it lent one. So, we have 2 Tens minus 1 Ten. That leaves us with 1 Ten. Write 1 in the Tens place of your answer.

📏📏
2 rulers, take away 1
