What is Money?

  • Money helps us buy things.
  • It comes in coins and notes.

🎙️ Money is like a special ticket that helps us get the things we need and want, like toys, food, or books. We see money in two main forms: small, round coins and flat, rectangular notes.

The rupee (₹) is our money.

Counting Money: Let's Add!

  • We can add money to find the total.
  • Just add the numbers on the coins and notes.

🎙️ Now that we know our coins and notes, let's learn to count them! Counting money means adding up all the values to find out how much money we have in total.

Example: Adding Coins

🎙️ If you have one 5 Rupee coin and one 2 Rupee coin, how much money do you have? Just add 5 and 2. Five plus two makes seven! So, you have 7 Rupees.

1You have a 5 Rupee coin
2And a 2 Rupee coin
3Total is 7 Rupees!

Example: Adding Notes

🎙️ What if you have a 20 Rupee note and a 10 Rupee note? Add 20 and 10. Twenty plus ten equals thirty! So, you have 30 Rupees. It's just like adding numbers!

1You have a 20 Rupee note
2And a 10 Rupee note
3Total is 30 Rupees!
