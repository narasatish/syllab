Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing coins of similar size (e.g., 1 and 2 Rupee).
  • Forgetting to add all values.
  • Not knowing the value of each note.

🎙️ Sometimes we make small errors, and that's okay! Just remember to look carefully at the number on each coin and note. Always count every single one you have.
