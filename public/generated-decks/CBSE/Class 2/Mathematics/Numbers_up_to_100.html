Writing Numbers: From 11 to 50

  • The first digit tells us the number of 'Tens'.
  • The second digit tells us the number of 'Ones'.
  • For example, 23 means 2 Tens and 3 Ones.

🎙️ When we write numbers like 23, the '2' is in the tens place, meaning two groups of ten. The '3' is in the ones place, meaning three single units. This pattern helps us write all numbers up to 50.

Who Has More? Comparing Numbers

  • We can find out which number is bigger or smaller.
  • First, compare the 'Tens' digit.
  • If Tens are same, then compare the 'Ones' digit.

🎙️ Imagine you and your friend are comparing how many chocolates you have. We compare numbers to see who has more or less. The easiest way is to look at the number of 'Tens' first. The number with more 'Tens' is bigger.

Putting Numbers in Order

  • We can arrange numbers from smallest to biggest (Ascending Order).
  • Or from biggest to smallest (Descending Order).
  • It's like climbing up or down a staircase.

🎙️ Just like we arrange our books by size, we can arrange numbers too! When we go from smallest to biggest, it's like climbing up a ladder, we call it Ascending Order. When we go from biggest to smallest, it's like coming down the ladder, we call it Descending Order.

Steps to Order Numbers

  • Step 1: Look at the Tens digit of all numbers.
  • Step 2: If Tens are same, look at the Ones digit.
  • Step 3: Arrange them from smallest to biggest or vice versa.

🎙️ Here are the simple steps to order numbers. First, always check the Tens place. If the tens are the same, then move to the Ones place. This will help you correctly arrange any group of numbers.

Oops! Common Mistakes

  • Confusing Tens and Ones (e.g., writing 32 instead of 23).
  • Forgetting to compare Tens first when ordering.
  • Mixing up 'greater than' and 'less than' signs.

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, but it's good to know what they are so we can avoid them! Always remember the position of Tens and Ones. And when comparing, always start with the Tens digit. Practice will make you perfect!
