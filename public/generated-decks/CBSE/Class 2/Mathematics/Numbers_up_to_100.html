What is 15?

  • 15 is made of 1 'ten' and 5 'ones'.
  • The 'tens' place tells us how many groups of 10.
  • The 'ones' place tells us how many single items are left.

🎙️ When we see a number like 15, we can break it down. It has one group of ten, and five single units. This is called place value and it helps us understand what numbers mean.
