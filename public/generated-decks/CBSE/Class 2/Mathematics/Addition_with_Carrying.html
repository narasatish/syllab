Adding Bigger Numbers (No Carrying)!

  • We add the ones place first.
  • Then we add the tens place.
  • Example: 12 + 5 = 17.

🎙️ When we add bigger numbers like 12 and 5, we always start from the ones place. Here, 2 ones plus 5 ones make 7 ones. And 1 ten plus 0 tens makes 1 ten. So, the answer is 17.

Remember Place Value!

  • Every number has a place.
  • In 13, '3' is in the Ones place.
  • '1' is in the Tens place, meaning one group of ten.

🎙️ It's very important to remember place value. The '3' in 13 means three single units. The '1' in 13 means one group of ten units. Understanding this helps us with carrying.

Let's Add with Carrying! Step 1: Ones

  • We want to add 25 + 17.
  • First, add the numbers in the Ones place: 5 + 7.
  • 5 + 7 = 12.

🎙️ Let's try a full example! We always start with the Ones column. Here, we add 5 and 7, which gives us 12. Now, what do we do with this 12? We can't write both digits in the Ones place.

Step 2: Carry Over!

  • Since 12 has 1 Ten and 2 Ones...
  • Write '2' in the Ones place of the answer.
  • Carry the '1' (which is 1 Ten) to the top of the Tens place.

🎙️ This is the 'carrying' part! We keep the 2 in the Ones place and send the 1 (which is really 10) to join the other tens. We write a small '1' above the tens column to remember it.

Step 3: Add the Tens!

  • Now, add all the numbers in the Tens place.
  • Remember the '1' you carried over!
  • 1 (carried) + 2 + 1 = 4.

🎙️ Finally, we add all the digits in the Tens column, including the '1' we carried over. So, we add 1 plus 2 plus 1, which gives us 4. We write this 4 in the Tens place of our answer.

Let's Try One Together: 34 + 28

  • First, add the ones: 4 + 8 = 12.
  • Write '2' in ones place, carry '1' to tens place.
  • Now, add the tens: 1 (carried) + 3 + 2 = 6.
  • So, 34 + 28 = 62!

🎙️ Let's do this one step-by-step. Remember to always start from the right-most column, which is the ones place. Don't forget to add the carried number when you move to the tens place!

1Add Ones (4+8=12)
2Carry 1 to Tens
3Add Tens (1+3+2=6)
4Total: 62

Solving the Mango Problem!

  • We need to add 18 + 15.
  • Ones: 8 + 5 = 13. Write '3', carry '1'.
  • Tens: 1 (carried) + 1 + 1 = 3.
  • Total = 33 mangoes!

🎙️ See how we used carrying to solve the mango problem? First, add the ones, then carry over any tens, and finally add all the tens. So, Papa and Mummy have 33 mangoes together!

2Ones: 8+5=13 (carry 1)

Oops! Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting to add the carried number.
  • Carrying the number to the wrong place (e.g., to the hundreds).
  • Not aligning numbers correctly in columns.

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, but it's even better to learn from them! Always remember to add the carried number. Also, make sure your numbers are lined up neatly, ones under ones, and tens under tens.

What We Learned Today!

  • We always add the Ones place first.
  • If the sum of Ones is 10 or more, we 'carry over' the Ten to the Tens place.
  • Then, we add all the numbers in the Tens place, including the carried number.

🎙️ Today, we mastered the art of addition with carrying! Remember these three simple steps, and you'll be able to add any two-digit numbers. It's like a superpower for numbers!
