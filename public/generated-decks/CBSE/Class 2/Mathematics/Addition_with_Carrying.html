Tens and Ones Houses

  • Numbers live in special houses.
  • The 'Ones' house is for single digits.
  • The 'Tens' house is for groups of ten.

🎙️ Remember our Tens and Ones houses? Every number has digits that live in these houses. The rightmost digit lives in the Ones house, and the next digit to its left lives in the Tens house. This helps us keep numbers organized.

Too Many in the Ones House!

  • What if we have more than 9 ones?
  • The Ones house gets too crowded!
  • It can only hold numbers 0 to 9.

🎙️ Imagine the Ones house can only hold 9 little babies. What happens if 10 babies try to fit in? It's too crowded! This is where 'carrying' helps us manage the numbers when there are too many in one place.

Carrying: A New Friend!

  • When 10 Ones are together...
  • They become 1 Ten!
  • We send this 1 Ten to the Tens house.

🎙️ When we have 10 or more in the Ones house, we 'bundle' 10 ones together. This bundle of 10 ones becomes 1 ten. We then 'carry' or send this 1 ten to join the other tens in the Tens house. It's like a promotion!

12 Cars: Carrying Time!

  • We have 12 ones in total.
  • We keep 2 in the Ones house.
  • We carry 1 (Ten) to the Tens house.

🎙️ So, from 12 cars, we keep the '2' in the Ones place. The '1' from 12 is actually '1 ten'. We carry this '1 ten' over to the Tens place. This is the magic of carrying!

Steps to Add with Carrying

  • Step 1: Add the Ones.
  • Step 2: Carry over to the Tens.
  • Step 3: Add the Tens.

🎙️ Here are the three simple steps we follow for addition with carrying. Always remember to start from the Ones house, then move to the Tens house. Let's look at each step carefully.

1Add Ones
2Carry Ten
3Add Tens

Step 1: Add the Ones

  • Always start from the right side.
  • Add the numbers in the Ones column first.

🎙️ The first and most important step is to add the numbers that are in the Ones column. This is always where we begin our addition, just like reading from right to left in some languages.

🔢
Add Ones First
11 / 20

Step 2: Carry Over to Tens

  • If the sum of Ones is 10 or more:
  • Write the Ones digit below.
  • Carry the '1' (Ten) to the Tens column.

🎙️ If your total in the Ones column is 10 or more, you write the last digit (the Ones digit) in the Ones answer box. Then, you take the '1' (which is actually 1 ten) and write it above the Tens column, ready to be added there.

👆
Carry Up
12 / 20

Step 3: Add the Tens

  • Now, add all numbers in the Tens column.
  • Don't forget to add the carried '1'!

🎙️ Finally, move to the Tens column. Add all the numbers there, including the '1' that you carried over from the Ones column. This will give you the complete answer for the Tens place.

Add All Tens

Let's Try One Together! (25 + 17)

  • Ones: 5 + 7 = 12.
  • Write '2' in the Ones place.
  • Carry '1' to the Tens place.

🎙️ Let's do an example together: 25 + 17. First, add the Ones: 5 + 7 makes 12. We write '2' in the Ones answer box, and carry the '1' (which is 1 ten) to the top of the Tens column.

Finishing Our Sum (25 + 17)

  • Tens: 2 + 1 + (carried 1) = 4.
  • So, 25 + 17 = 42.

🎙️ Now, let's add the Tens. We have 2 tens, plus 1 ten, plus the 1 ten we carried over. That makes 2 + 1 + 1 = 4 tens. So, our final answer for 25 + 17 is 42. Great job!
