What is Addition?

Meet Our New Friends: Two-Digit Numbers!

  • Numbers like 10, 25, 30 are two-digit numbers.
  • They have two places: Ones and Tens.

🎙️ Until now, we mostly added small numbers. But what about numbers like 15 or 23? These are two-digit numbers, and we need a special trick to add them when they get tricky!

Understanding Ones Place

  • The first digit from the right is the Ones place.
  • It tells us how many single items we have.

🎙️ Look at the number 23. The '3' is in the Ones place. It means we have 3 single stars. The Ones place can hold numbers from 0 to 9.

🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟
9 single stars
6 / 20

Understanding Tens Place

  • The second digit from the right is the Tens place.
  • It tells us how many groups of ten we have.

🎙️ In the number 23, the '2' is in the Tens place. It means we have 2 groups of ten. So, 2 tens is equal to 20. Each box here has 10 blocks.

📦📦📦
3 groups of ten blocks

Always Start with Ones!

  • When we add numbers written one below the other,
  • We always start adding from the Ones place first.

🎙️ This is a very important rule! Imagine you have two towers of blocks. You always start building from the bottom, right? Similarly, in addition, we start adding from the Ones column.

Start with Ones

Oh No! Too Many Ones!

  • What happens if the sum of Ones is 10 or more?
  • We can't write 10 or 12 in the Ones place!

🎙️ If we add 5 candies and 7 candies, we get 12 candies. But we can only write a single digit in the Ones place. So, what do we do with the '1' from '12'?

🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬
12 candies

Making a New Ten: Carrying!

  • When we have 10 or more Ones,
  • We make a new group of ten and 'carry' it to the Tens place.

🎙️ This special action is called 'carrying'. It's like you have 12 candies, you bundle 10 of them together to make one big packet of ten, and the remaining 2 are left as single candies.

⬆️
Moving up to the next place

Our Special Carry Bag

  • Imagine a small bag for our new 'ten'.
  • We put the 'ten' in the bag and take it to the Tens column.

🎙️ This carry bag helps us remember to add the new 'ten' when we are adding the numbers in the Tens place. It's a little reminder on top of the Tens column.

Step 1: Write Neatly

  • Write the numbers one below the other.
  • Make sure the Ones are under Ones, and Tens under Tens.

🎙️ Good handwriting helps a lot in maths! Always write your numbers in straight columns. This way, you won't get confused about which numbers to add.

✍️
Write numbers in columns
13 / 20

Step 2: Add the Ones Column

  • First, add the numbers in the Ones place.
  • For 15 + 8, add 5 + 8.

🎙️ In our problem, 15 + 8, we add 5 and 8. Counting them together, we get 13. Remember, 13 has '3' ones and '1' ten.

🍓🍓🍓🍓🍓🍓🍓🍓🍓🍓
5 + 8 = 13
14 / 20

Step 3: Carry the Ten!

  • Write '3' in the Ones place.
  • Take the '1' (which is one ten) to the Tens place.

🎙️ Since we have 13, we write the '3' in the Ones column. The '1' from 13 is actually one 'ten'. We carry this '1' to the top of the Tens column.

👆
Carry the 1 to Tens column
15 / 20

Step 4: Add the Tens Column

  • Now, add the numbers in the Tens place.
  • Don't forget to add the '1' you carried over!

🎙️ In our example, we have '1' in the Tens place from the number 15. And we carried over '1' from the Ones place. So, we add 1 + 1, which gives us 2.

📦📦
1 ten + 1 carried ten = 2 tens
