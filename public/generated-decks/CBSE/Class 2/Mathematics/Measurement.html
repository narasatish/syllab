What is Measurement?

  • It's finding out how long, how heavy, or how much something holds.
  • We use tools to measure things.

🎙️ Measurement is like asking a question: 'How long is this table?', 'How much water can this jug hold?', or 'How heavy is this book?'. And then we find the answer! We use different tools for different types of measurement.

  • Length tells us how long or short something is.

🎙️ First, let's talk about 'Length'. When we measure length, we want to know how far one end of an object is from the other. Think about a snake – is it long or short? Or a building – is it tall or short?

Measuring with Your Body!

  • Long ago, people used their hands and feet to measure.
  • This is called using 'non-standard' units.

🎙️ Before rulers were invented, people used parts of their body to measure. You can try it too! Measure your table using your handspan – that's the distance from your thumb to your little finger. Or measure your room using your footspan!

A Little Problem!

  • My hand is smaller than your hand.
  • So, my measurement will be different from yours!

🎙️ But here's a problem: everyone's hands and feet are different sizes! If I measure a table with my hand, and you measure it with your hand, we might get different numbers. This can be confusing, right? We need something that is always the same for everyone.

Standard Units: The Centimeter (cm)

  • To measure small things, we use 'centimeters'.
  • We write it as 'cm'.

🎙️ To make sure everyone gets the same measurement, we use 'standard units'. For small things, like your pencil or a book, we use a unit called a 'centimeter'. You can see centimeters marked on your ruler. Look at the numbers on it!

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Standard Units: The Meter (m)

  • To measure big things, we use 'meters'.
  • We write it as 'm'.

🎙️ What about really big things, like the length of your classroom or the height of a door? For these, we use a bigger unit called a 'meter'. One meter is equal to 100 centimeters. We usually use a big measuring tape for meters.

Measuring Weight: How Heavy?

  • Weight tells us how heavy or light something is.
  • We use a weighing scale to find weight.

🎙️ Next, let's learn about 'Weight'. Weight tells us if something is heavy or light. Have you ever picked up a feather and then a big stone? The stone feels much heavier, right? We use a special tool called a weighing scale to measure weight.

Heavier or Lighter?

  • Look at the apple and the feather.
  • Which one is heavier? Which one is lighter?

🎙️ Just like length, we can compare weights. An apple is much heavier than a feather. If you put them on a balance scale, the apple side would go down, and the feather side would go up. That's how we know!

Measuring with Blocks!

  • We can use small blocks to measure weight.
  • How many blocks balance the toy car?

🎙️ Before standard scales, people might have used small, identical blocks to see how many blocks equal the weight of an object. If a toy car balances with 5 blocks, then its weight is '5 blocks'. It's a fun way to understand weight!

Standard Units: The Kilogram (kg)

  • To measure heavy things, we use 'kilograms'.
  • We write it as 'kg'.

🎙️ For heavier things, like a bag of rice or your school bag, we use a standard unit called a 'kilogram'. You might see 'kg' written on food packets. One kilogram is equal to 1000 grams. Your parents might buy vegetables in kilograms.

Standard Units: The Gram (g)

  • To measure light things, we use 'grams'.
  • We write it as 'g'.

🎙️ What about very light things, like a small candy or a pinch of salt? For these, we use a smaller unit called a 'gram'. Many small food items, like spices or small chocolates, have their weight written in grams.

Standard Unit: The Litre (L)

  • For liquids, we use 'litres' to measure capacity.
  • We write it as 'L'.

🎙️ For measuring liquids like milk, water, or juice, we use a standard unit called a 'litre'. You often see 'L' written on milk cartons or juice boxes. One litre is a common amount for many drinks.

What Did We Learn Today?

  • We measure Length (cm, m) for how long/tall.
  • We measure Weight (g, kg) for how heavy/light.
  • We measure Capacity (L) for how much liquid it holds.
