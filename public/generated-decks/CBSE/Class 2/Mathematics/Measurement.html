What is Measurement?

  • Measurement helps us know size, length, and weight.
  • It answers questions like 'How long?', 'How heavy?', 'How much?'
  • We use different tools to measure different things.

🎙️ Imagine you want to know if your toy car is longer than your pencil. Or if your school bag is heavier than your lunchbox. Measurement helps us find these answers! We use special tools for each kind of measurement.

🎙️ First, let's talk about 'length'. When we measure length, we want to know how far one end of an object is from the other end. Think about your height – that's a kind of length too!

Measuring with Handspans

  • We can use our hands to measure length.
  • One handspan is from your thumb to your little finger.
  • Try measuring your desk with handspans!

🎙️ Before we had rulers, people used their body parts to measure! A handspan is a common way. Put your hand flat on the table, spread your fingers, and measure how many times your hand fits across the table. That's its length in handspans.

✋

Measuring with Footspans

  • We can also use our feet to measure length.
  • One footspan is the length of your foot.
  • Try measuring your room with footspans!

🎙️ Just like handspans, we can use our feet! Place one foot in front of the other, heel to toe, and count how many steps it takes to cross a room. This gives you the length in footspans. It's a fun way to measure!

🦶

Why Do We Need Same Tools?

  • My hand is small, your dad's hand is big.
  • So, your handspans and his handspans will be different!
  • We need a 'standard' tool for everyone.

🎙️ If I measure the table with my hand and you measure it with your hand, we might get different numbers. This is because our hands are different sizes! To make sure everyone gets the same measurement, we need a special tool that is the same for everyone.

Meet the Ruler!

  • A ruler is a 'standard' tool for measuring length.
  • It has numbers and small lines.
  • We use 'centimetres' (cm) with a ruler.

🎙️ This is a ruler! It's a very important tool. Everyone's ruler is the same, so we all get the same answer when we measure. The numbers on the ruler tell us the length in units called 'centimetres'.

📏
Ruler
9 / 20

Let's Measure a Pencil!

  • Place the pencil at the '0' mark on the ruler.
  • See where the other end of the pencil stops.
  • Read the number, that's its length in cm!

🎙️ To measure with a ruler, always start at the '0' mark. Place the object right against the ruler. Then look at where the object ends and read the number. That number tells you how many centimetres long the object is. Easy peasy!

Measuring Weight: How Heavy?

  • Weight tells us how heavy something is.
  • Is an apple heavier than a feather?
  • We use a 'weighing scale' to measure weight.

🎙️ Now let's talk about 'weight'. When we talk about weight, we want to know if something is heavy or light. We use a special tool called a weighing scale for this. You might have seen one in the market or at home.

Heavier or Lighter?

  • Look at the items on the scale.
  • Which one makes the scale go down more?
  • That one is heavier!

🎙️ Imagine you put a book on one side of a balance scale and a leaf on the other. The book would make its side go down because it's much heavier! The leaf is lighter. This is how we compare weight.

Measuring Capacity: How Much Can It Hold?

  • Capacity tells us how much a container can hold.
  • How much water can a glass hold?
  • How much milk can a bottle hold?

🎙️ Our last type of measurement for today is 'capacity'. Capacity is about how much liquid or other substance a container can hold. Think about filling a glass with water or a bucket with sand.

More or Less Water?

  • Look at the two containers.
  • Which one can hold more water?
  • Which one holds less water?

🎙️ If you have a small glass and a big water bottle, which one can hold more water? The big bottle, right? So, the big bottle has more capacity, and the small glass has less capacity. This is how we compare capacity.
