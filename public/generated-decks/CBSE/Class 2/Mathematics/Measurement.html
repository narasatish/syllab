Measuring with Handspan

  • Your handspan is the distance from your thumb to your little finger when spread out.
  • We can use handspans to measure smaller things, like a book or a desk.

🎙️ Before rulers, people used their body parts to measure! A handspan is a fun way to measure. But remember, everyone's handspan is a little different!

Measuring with Footspan

  • Your footspan is the length of your foot.
  • We can use footspans to measure bigger things, like the length of a room or a mat.

🎙️ Just like handspans, footspans help us measure. If you walk across a room, you are using your footspans! But again, different people have different foot sizes.

Meet the Ruler: Centimetres

  • To measure accurately, we use a ruler.
  • A ruler has small markings called 'centimetres' (cm).
  • Everyone's ruler is the same, so measurements are exact!

🎙️ Handspans and footspans are fun, but sometimes we need exact measurements. That's where our friend, the ruler, comes in! Centimetres help everyone get the same answer.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

How to Use a Ruler

  • Place '0' mark of ruler at start of object.
  • See where the object ends on the ruler.
  • That number is its length in centimetres!

🎙️ Using a ruler is easy! Always remember to start from the '0' mark, not the edge of the ruler. This way, you will get the correct length every time.

  • We use a balance or scale to measure weight.

🎙️ Have you ever picked up two things and felt one was harder to lift? That's weight! We measure weight to know how much 'stuff' is inside an object.

Comparing Heavy and Light

  • We can compare things by holding them in our hands.
  • The one that pulls your hand down more is heavier.
  • Like comparing an apple with a grape.

🎙️ This is the simplest way to understand weight. Just lift two objects and feel the difference. You can try this with your pencil box and a single pencil!

Meet the Kilogram (Kg)

  • To measure weight accurately, we use units like 'kilogram' (kg).
  • Many things like rice, sugar, or vegetables are sold in kilograms.
  • A 1 kg packet of sugar is a good example!

🎙️ When your parents buy groceries, they often ask for things in kilograms. It's a standard unit, so a kilogram of rice is the same everywhere in the world.

Comparing More and Less Capacity

Measuring Time: What Time Is It?

  • Time tells us when things happen and for how long.
  • We use clocks and calendars to measure time.
  • Morning, afternoon, evening, night – all about time!

🎙️ Time is very important! It helps us know when to wake up, when to go to school, and when to play. Clocks are our best friends for knowing the time.

Reading the Clock

  • A clock has numbers from 1 to 12.
  • The short hand tells the hour (like 3 o'clock).
  • The long hand tells the minutes (at 12 for o'clock, at 6 for half past).

🎙️ Learning to read a clock is super useful! The hour hand moves slowly, and the minute hand moves faster. Soon, you'll be able to tell time all by yourself!

Watch Out! Common Mistakes

  • Not starting from '0' on the ruler.
  • Confusing heavy with big size (a small rock can be heavier than a big pillow).
  • Guessing instead of measuring carefully.

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, but it's even better to learn from them! Always remember these tips to become a measurement pro and avoid common errors.
