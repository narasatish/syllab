  • It is the shorter hand.
  • It tells us the hour.

🎙️ Now, look at the clock's hands. There are usually two main hands. The first one is the short, fat hand. We call it the 'Hour Hand'. It points to the numbers that tell us what hour it is.

  • It tells us the minutes.

🎙️ The other hand is longer and thinner. This is called the 'Minute Hand'. It moves faster than the hour hand and tells us about the minutes. Together, both hands help us read the time.

Reading 'O'Clock' Time

  • This is the easiest time to read!
  • When the long hand points to 12.

🎙️ Let's learn to read 'O'Clock' time. It's super easy! When the long minute hand is pointing exactly at the number 12, and the short hour hand is pointing to any number, that's the 'O'Clock' time.

Reading 'Half Past' Time

  • Long hand points to 6.
  • Short hand is between two numbers.

🎙️ Sometimes, the minute hand is not on 12. What if it's on 6? This is called 'Half Past' time. When the long hand is on 6, it means half an hour has passed. The short hand will be between two numbers.
