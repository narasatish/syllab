Reading 'O'clock' Time

  • When the long hand points to 12.
  • The short hand tells us the hour.

🎙️ This is the easiest time to read! When the long hand is straight up at 12, we look at where the short hand is pointing. That number is our 'o'clock' time.

Reading 'Half Past' Time

  • When the long hand points to 6.
  • It means 'half past' the hour.

🎙️ When the long hand moves from 12 all the way down to 6, it has completed half of its journey. This is called 'half past' the hour.

Common Time Mistakes!

  • Confusing the short and long hands.
  • Saying '6 o'clock' instead of 'half past' when the long hand is on 6.

🎙️ Sometimes, we can get confused. Remember, the short hand is for hours, and the long hand is for minutes. Also, when the long hand is on 6, it's 'half past', not '6 o'clock'!
