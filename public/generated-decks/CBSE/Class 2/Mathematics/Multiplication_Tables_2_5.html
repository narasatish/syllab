What is Multiplication?

  • It means 'repeated addition'.
  • Adding the same number many times.

🎙️ Imagine you have 2 apples, and your friend gives you 2 more, and then another friend gives you 2 more. You keep adding 2! Multiplication helps us do this faster.

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

Making Equal Groups

  • Multiplication is about making groups.
  • Each group must have the same number.

🎙️ Look at these cookies! We have two groups, and each group has 3 cookies. So, we have 3 + 3 cookies, which is 6. This is the start of multiplication!

🍪🍪🍪🍪🍪🍪
2 groups of 3 cookies

2 times 1 is 2

  • One group of two things.
  • Like your two eyes!

🎙️ Look at your face in the mirror! You have one nose, but how many eyes? Two! So, 1 group of 2 is 2. We write it as 2 x 1 = 2.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

2 times 2 is 4

  • Two groups of two things.
  • Like two pairs of shoes.

🎙️ If you have two pairs of shoes, how many shoes are there in total? Each pair has two shoes. So, 2 + 2 makes 4 shoes. We write this as 2 x 2 = 4.

👟👟👟👟
2 groups of 2 shoes

2 times 3 is 6

  • Three groups of two things.
  • Three bicycles have 6 wheels.

🎙️ A bicycle has 2 wheels. If you see three bicycles parked together, how many wheels are there? Count them! 2 + 2 + 2 equals 6 wheels. So, 2 x 3 = 6.

3 groups of 2 wheels
