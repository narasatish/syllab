What is Multiplication?

  • It's a quick way to add the same number many times.
  • It helps us count groups of things.

🎙️ Imagine you have many groups, and each group has the same number of items. Instead of adding them one by one, multiplication helps us find the total quickly!

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

Multiplication as Repeated Addition

  • Look at these yummy apples!
  • 2 apples + 2 apples + 2 apples = 6 apples.

🎙️ See, we added '2' three times. That's what repeated addition means. Multiplication is just a shortcut for this!

🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎
3 groups of 2 apples

Counting in Groups

  • We have 3 groups of 2.
  • So, 3 times 2 equals 6.

🎙️ When we say '3 times 2', it means we have 3 groups, and each group has 2 items. The total is 6.

Table of 2: Double the Fun!

  • Let's learn to count in twos!
  • It's like having pairs of things.

🎙️ The table of 2 is all about doubling! If you have one thing, doubling it makes two. If you have two things, doubling them makes four!

2️⃣
Table of Two
6 / 20

Table of 2: Example

  • You have 2 hands.
  • Each hand has 5 fingers.
  • How many fingers on 2 hands?

🎙️ We have 2 hands, and each hand has 5 fingers. So, it's 2 times 5. That means 5 + 5 = 10. So, 2 x 5 = 10!

🖐️🖐️
2 groups of 5 fingers

Practice Table of 2

  • 2 x 1 = 2 (1 group of 2)
  • 2 x 2 = 4 (2 groups of 2)
  • 2 x 3 = 6 (3 groups of 2)

🎙️ Remember, 2 times 1 means one group of 2. 2 times 2 means two groups of 2. Keep practicing!

🎈🎈🎈🎈🎈🎈
3 groups of 2 balloons
