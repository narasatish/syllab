When Do We Use Capital Letters? (Rule 1)

  • Rule Number 1:
  • Always start a new sentence with a Capital Letter.
  • It's like giving your sentence a grand beginning!

🎙️ This is a very important rule! Every time you start a new thought or sentence, the very first letter must be a big, strong Capital Letter. It's like a signal that a new idea is coming!

When Do We Use Capital Letters? (Rule 2)
