Rule 1: Starting a Sentence

  • Every new sentence starts with a BIG letter.
  • It's like giving a grand welcome to the sentence!

🎙️ This is a very important rule! When you start writing a new sentence, always use a capital letter for the very first word. It's like putting a special flag at the beginning.

Eight planets orbit the Sun.

Look at These Sentences!

  • **T**he sun is shining. (T is capital)
  • **M**y dog loves to play. (M is capital)

🎙️ See how the first letter of 'The' and 'My' is a capital letter? This tells us that a new sentence has begun. Always remember this rule when you write.

1The sun is shining.
2My dog loves to play.

Oops! Watch Out for These!

  • Forgetting capital at sentence start.
  • Not capitalizing names of people/places.
  • Capitalizing random words in a sentence.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes, and that's okay! But try to avoid these common ones: always check the first letter of your sentence and any names you write. Don't make just any word capital.
