What is Reading Comprehension?

  • It means understanding what you read.
  • Like understanding what a friend says.

🎙️ When you read a story, your brain works to understand it. That's what reading comprehension is – understanding the words and ideas in the story.

Why is it Important?

  • You can answer questions about stories.
  • You can enjoy books even more!

🎙️ If you understand what you read, you can tell others about the story. You can also answer any questions your teacher asks you about it. It makes reading much more fun!

How Do We Understand Stories?

  • We follow some simple steps.
  • It's like baking a cake!

🎙️ There are easy steps to help us understand stories better. Just like when we follow steps to make something delicious, we follow steps to understand our books.

1Read Carefully
2Think About It

Step 1: Read the Story Carefully

  • Read every word slowly.
  • Don't skip any lines.

🎙️ The first and most important step is to read the story very carefully. Read each word, one by one, like a tortoise walking slowly. This helps your brain catch all the information.

🐢
Read Slowly
6 / 20

Step 2: Look at the Pictures

  • Pictures tell us many things.
  • They help us understand the story better.

🎙️ Stories often have beautiful pictures. These pictures are like clues! They show us what is happening, who the characters are, and where the story takes place. Always look at them carefully.

🖼️
Visual Clues
7 / 20

Step 3: Find the Main Idea

  • What is the story mostly about?
  • What is the biggest point?

🎙️ After reading, think: 'What was this story mostly about?' Is it about a dog, a school day, or a birthday party? This is the main idea, like the biggest cloud in the sky.

💡
Big Idea
8 / 20

Step 4: Find the Details

  • Who is in the story?
  • What happens? Where? When?

🎙️ Now, let's look for the small pieces of information. Who are the characters? What did they do? Where did the story happen? When did it happen? These are the details, like tiny stars in the sky.

🔍🔍🔍
Look Closely
