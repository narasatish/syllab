What is Reading?

  • Reading means looking at words.
  • Words tell us stories and facts.
  • We use our eyes to read.

🎙️ When we read, our eyes look at letters and words on a page. These words come together to make sentences. Sentences then tell us something interesting!

What is Comprehension?

  • Comprehension means understanding.
  • It's like your brain thinking about what you read.
  • Do you know what the story is about?

🎙️ After reading words, our brain works hard to understand them. It tries to make sense of the story. This is what comprehension is!

🧠
Our Brain Understands

Why is Reading Comprehension Important?

  • It helps us understand stories.
  • We know what characters are doing.
  • We learn new things every day!

🎙️ When you understand what you read, stories become more fun! You can imagine the characters and places. It also helps you learn new things from books.

Tip 1: Look at the Pictures!

  • Pictures tell us a lot.
  • They give clues about the story.
  • Look at them before you read!

🎙️ Before you start reading, always look at the pretty pictures in the book. They are like hints! They can give you an idea of what the story might be about.

🖼️
See the Pictures

Tip 2: Read Slowly and Clearly

  • Don't rush when you read.
  • Read each word carefully.
  • This helps your brain understand.

🎙️ Imagine a tortoise walking slowly. That's how we should read! If we read too fast, our brain might miss some important words. Reading slowly helps us understand every part.

🐢
Slow like a Tortoise
7 / 20

Tip 3: Read Words Carefully

  • Sound out new words.
  • Ask for help if you don't know a word.
  • Every word is important!

🎙️ Sometimes we find new words. It's okay! Try to sound them out. If you still don't understand, ask your teacher or parents. Every word helps build the picture of the story.

🗣️
Sound Out Words
