The Full Stop Says 'Stop'!

  • We use a full stop at the end of a sentence.
  • It tells us that the sentence is complete.
  • It looks like a tiny dot ( . )

🎙️ Every time you finish telling someone something, like 'I love ice cream', you put a full stop. It's like taking a breath before you start a new idea.

When to Use Capital Letters

  • Always at the start of a sentence.
  • For names of people, places, and pets.
  • For days of the week and months.

🎙️ There are clear rules for when to use capital letters. The most important one is at the beginning of every new sentence. Also, your name, your city's name, and even your pet's name start with a capital letter.

Watch Out For These Mistakes!

  • Forgetting full stops or question marks.
  • Not starting a sentence with a capital letter.
  • Using a full stop instead of a question mark.

🎙️ Even big students sometimes forget these rules! Always check your writing. Did you end every sentence correctly? Did you start every sentence with a capital letter? Practice makes perfect!

How to Score Well in Exams!

  • Read the sentence carefully to understand its meaning.
  • Ask: Is it telling, asking, or showing strong feeling?
  • Check for capital letters at the start and for names.

🎙️ In your exams, you might get sentences to correct or fill in the missing punctuation. If you follow these steps, you will easily get full marks! Always re-read your answers.
