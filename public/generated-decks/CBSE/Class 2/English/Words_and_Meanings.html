What Are Words?

  • Words are like building blocks for talking.
  • We use them to speak and write.
  • Every word has a special meaning!

🎙️ Think of words as tiny tools we use every day. They help us share our thoughts and feelings with others, just like we chat with our friends.

Words That Are Opposites!

  • Some words mean the exact opposite thing.
  • Like 'hot' and 'cold'.
  • They are very, very different!

🎙️ Imagine two friends who are very, very different from each other. That's how opposite words are! They stand against each other.

Opposite: Day and Night

  • We play in the day when it's bright.
  • We sleep at night when it's dark.
  • Day and night are opposites!

🎙️ During the day, the sun shines bright and we go to school. At night, the moon and stars come out, and we go to sleep. They are complete opposites!

Opposite: Hot and Cold

  • The sun is hot.
  • Ice cream is cold.
  • Can you feel the difference?

🎙️ Be careful with hot things, like a cup of tea. And how nice is a cold drink on a sunny day! These feelings are totally opposite.

Word with Many Meanings: "Bat"

  • A flying animal called a bat.
  • A wooden bat for playing cricket.
  • Two very different things!

🎙️ Isn't that interesting? The same word 'bat' can mean a creature that flies at night or something you use to play cricket! Context is key.

Another Word: "Bank"

  • A place where we keep money.
  • The side of a river.
  • Both are called 'bank'!

🎙️ We go to the bank to save our money. But also, the land next to a river is called a river bank. Two different places with the same name!

What Did We Learn Today?

  • Words have meanings and help us talk.
  • Some words are opposites (like big-small).
