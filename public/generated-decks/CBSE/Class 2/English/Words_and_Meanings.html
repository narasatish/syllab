Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Confusing synonyms and antonyms.
  • Not reading the full sentence for words with many meanings.
  • Guessing instead of thinking.

🎙️ Sometimes we make small mistakes. Remember, synonyms are friends, antonyms are opposites! Also, when a word can have many meanings, always read the whole sentence to understand which meaning is correct. Don't rush, think carefully!
