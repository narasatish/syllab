What is Singular?

  • Singular means ONE.
  • It is for one person, one animal, one place, or one thing.

🎙️ When we talk about just one thing, we call it 'singular'. Think of it as 'single', like a single flower or a single toy. It always means only one.

What is Plural?

  • Plural means MORE THAN ONE.
  • It is for many people, animals, places, or things.

🎙️ Now, what if there are many things? Like many boys, or many trees? Then we call it 'plural'. Plural means more than one, it can be two, three, or even a hundred!

Most words just add 's'!

  • This is the most common rule.
  • We simply add 's' at the end of the word.

🎙️ The easiest way to change a singular word to a plural word is to simply add the letter 's' to its end. Let's see some examples of this simple rule!

Words ending in s, ss, sh, ch, x, z add 'es'!

  • If a word ends with 's', 'ss', 'sh', 'ch', 'x', or 'z',
  • We add 'es' to make it plural.

🎙️ This is a special rule for some words. If a word ends with certain sounds, like 's' or 'sh', we add 'es' instead of just 's'. This helps us say the word properly.

Words ending in a consonant + 'y' change 'y' to 'ies'!

  • If a word ends with a consonant letter (like b, c, d) followed by 'y',
  • We remove 'y' and add 'ies'.

🎙️ This is another special rule! If a word ends with a 'y' and before the 'y' there is a consonant sound, we change the 'y' to 'i' and then add 'es'. It sounds a bit tricky, but it's fun!

What did we learn today?

  • Singular means ONE.
  • Plural means MORE THAN ONE.
  • Most words add 's'.
  • Words ending in s, ss, sh, ch, x, z add 'es'.
  • Words ending in consonant + 'y' change 'y' to 'ies'.
