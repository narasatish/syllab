What is Singular?

  • When a word tells us about ONE thing, person, animal, or place.
  • It means just one!

How to Change: Add 's'!

  • Most words become plural by adding 's' at the end.
  • Example: dog becomes dogs.

🎙️ This is the most common way to make a word plural. Just put an 's' at the end and you are done!

When to Add 'es'?

  • If a word ends with s, ss, sh, ch, x, or z, we add 'es'.
  • Example: bus becomes buses.

🎙️ This is a special rule! If the word ends with these specific letters, adding 's' alone might sound funny. So we add 'es' instead.

Words Ending in 'y'!

  • If a word ends in 'y' and has a consonant before it, change 'y' to 'i' and add 'es'.
  • Example: baby becomes babies.

🎙️ This rule is a little trickier, but fun! The 'y' waves goodbye and 'i' comes to join 'es'. It's like a small transformation!

What if 'y' has a Vowel Friend?

  • If a word ends in 'y' and has a vowel (a, e, i, o, u) before it, just add 's'.
  • Example: toy becomes toys.

🎙️ When 'y' has a vowel friend, it doesn't need to change. It just adds 's' like most words, making it much simpler!
