What are Rhyming Words?

  • Words that have the same ending sound.
  • They sound like music together!

🎙️ Rhyming words are like best friends! They have a special connection because their ending sounds are the same. When you say them, your ears will notice the similarity.

👂

Listen for the Same Sound!

  • The beginning can be different.
  • But the end sounds match!

🎙️ It's important to listen to the *end* of the word. The starting part can be different, but if the end part makes the same sound, then they are rhyming words! Think of it like a little song.
