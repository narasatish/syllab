How to Find Rhyming Words?

🎙️ The secret is to listen very carefully to how a word finishes. That's where the rhyming magic happens!

1Say the word aloud.
2Listen to the ENDING sound.
3Find another word with the same ending sound.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

🎙️ Don't just look at the first letter! Always listen to the ENDING sound. For example, 'Mat' and 'Man' do NOT rhyme, even though they start with 'M'.
