Common Mistakes with Opposites

  • Confusing similar words (e.g., 'big' and 'large' are not opposites).
  • Not knowing the correct opposite (e.g., opposite of 'day' is 'night', not 'evening').
  • Not using opposites in sentences correctly.

🎙️ Sometimes children get confused between words that are similar but not opposites. Remember, opposites must mean the exact opposite. Also, always try to use these words in full sentences to practice!
