What Are Opposites?

  • Words that mean the exact opposite.
  • Like 'big' and 'small'.
  • They are very different from each other!

🎙️ Imagine two things that are completely different. That's what opposites are! If one thing is up, its opposite is down. If one is fast, the other is slow. They are like two sides of a coin.
