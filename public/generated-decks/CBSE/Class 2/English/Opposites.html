What are Opposites?

  • Words that mean the exact opposite.
  • Like day is opposite of night.

🎙️ Opposites are words that are completely different from each other. If one word means one thing, its opposite means the complete reverse. Think of 'up' and 'down' – they are so different!

Opposites are Like Two Sides

  • One word is one side.
  • The other word is the other side.

🎙️ Imagine a coin. It has two sides, right? Heads and Tails. Opposites are like that! One word is like 'Heads', and its opposite is like 'Tails'. They are always together but always different.

Hot and Cold

  • The sun is HOT.
  • Ice cream is COLD.

🎙️ When you touch a hot cup of tea, it feels warm. But when you eat yummy ice cream, it feels so cold! 'Hot' and 'cold' are perfect examples of opposites we feel every day.

🎙️ We see the sun in the day and play with our friends. When the sun goes down, the moon and stars come out, and it's night, time to sleep. Day and night are opposites!
