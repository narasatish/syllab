A Sentence Makes Sense!

  • A sentence is a group of words.
  • These words tell us a complete idea.
  • It helps us understand clearly.

🎙️ Look at these two groups of words. 'Ball is the' doesn't tell us much. But 'The ball is big' tells us exactly what it means. That's why 'The ball is big' is a sentence, because it makes complete sense.

Ball is the.
The ball is big.

Who or What, and What They Do

Every Sentence Needs a Big Start!

  • Imagine a sentence is a train.
  • The first coach always looks special and big.
  • The first letter of every sentence is always a CAPITAL letter.

🎙️ Just like a train has a special engine at the front, every sentence has a special first letter. It's a capital letter! So, when you start writing, make sure your first letter is big and proud, like 'A' not 'a'.

9 / 24

Let's Make it Capital!

  • 'my cat is black.'
  • The first letter 'm' is small.
  • It should be 'M'.
  • So, 'My cat is black.'

🎙️ Look at this sentence: 'my cat is black.' The 'm' is small. That's a mistake! We need to make it a big 'M'. So, we change it to 'My cat is black.' See how easy that is?

1my cat is black.
2M is the first letter.
3My cat is black.

Time to Stop!

  • After telling a complete idea, we need to stop.
  • We put a full stop ( . ) at the end of a sentence.
  • It's like a small red light telling you, 'Sentence finished!'

🎙️ When you finish telling your idea, you can't just keep going, right? You need to pause. In writing, that pause is a full stop. It's a tiny dot that tells everyone, 'My thought is complete here!'

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Adding the Full Stop

  • 'The sun shines brightly'
  • This sentence needs a stop sign.
  • Add a full stop at the end.
  • So, 'The sun shines brightly.'

🎙️ Here's a sentence: 'The sun shines brightly'. It tells us a complete idea, but it's missing its stop sign! We need to add a full stop at the end. Now it's perfect: 'The sun shines brightly.'

Big Start, Clear Stop!

  • Every sentence needs a capital letter to start.
  • And a full stop to end.
  • This makes our writing neat and easy to read.

🎙️ So, remember these two golden rules for every sentence: Start with a big letter, and end with a tiny dot! This helps everyone understand your writing perfectly, just like a well-written letter to a friend.

Making Sentences Shine

  • Add descriptive words:
  • 'The *fluffy* dog runs *fast*.' (More interesting!)
  • These words help us imagine better.

🎙️ Let's make a simple sentence shine! 'The dog runs.' is okay. But if we add 'fluffy' and 'fast', we can almost see the dog running, right? These words help paint a picture in our minds.

Joining Friends with 'and'

  • Sometimes, two sentences can be joined using 'and'.
  • This makes our writing flow better.
  • Example: 'I like apples. I like mangoes.' -> 'I like apples and mangoes.'

🎙️ Imagine you have two friends standing separately. You can make them hold hands! Similarly, if two simple sentences are talking about similar things, you can join them with the word 'and'. It makes your writing smoother.

Oops! Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting to start with a CAPITAL letter.
  • Forgetting the FULL STOP at the end.
  • Writing words that don't make a complete sense.

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, but it's important to learn from them! The most common mistakes are forgetting the big letter at the start and the little dot at the end. Also, always check if your words make a complete idea.

Tips for Your Exam

  • Read the picture or question carefully.
  • Always start with a CAPITAL letter.
  • Always end with a FULL STOP.
  • Write neatly and clearly.

🎙️ When you have an exam, remember these tips! Always look at the picture or read the question very carefully. Use capital letters and full stops. And make sure your writing is neat so the teacher can read it easily.
