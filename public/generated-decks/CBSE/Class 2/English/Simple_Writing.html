Rule 1: Always Start Big!

  • Every sentence begins with a Capital Letter.
  • Like your name starts with a big letter!

🎙️ Look at the first letter of your name. It's a capital letter! Sentences are important, so they also start with a big, capital letter.

Eight planets orbit the Sun.

Rule 2: Stop with a Dot!

  • Every sentence ends with a Full Stop (.).
  • It tells us to pause.

🎙️ When you finish talking, you pause, right? A full stop tells us that the sentence is over and we should pause before the next one.
