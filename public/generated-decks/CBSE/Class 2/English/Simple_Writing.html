What is Simple Writing?

  • Writing that is easy to read.
  • Writing that is easy to understand.

🎙️ Imagine you tell your friend a story. If you use simple words and short sentences, your friend will understand quickly, right? Simple writing is just like that!

Why is Simple Writing Important?

  • Everyone can understand you.
  • Your ideas become clear.

🎙️ When you write simply, it's like opening a door for everyone to come in and understand what you mean. Your teacher, your friends, even your parents will find your writing easy to read.

Rule 1: Use Short Sentences

  • Don't make sentences too long.
  • Keep them like small, clear steps.

🎙️ Our first rule is to keep sentences short. Think of it like a small train with just a few carriages. It's easier to see what's inside each carriage.

📏
5 / 20

Short Sentences: An Example

  • Long: The big, fluffy cat sat on the soft, warm mat and looked at the tiny, red ball.
  • Simple: The cat sat on the mat. It looked at the ball.

🎙️ Look at these two sentences. The first one is very long. The second one is shorter and easier to read. Both tell us about the cat, but the simple one is quicker to understand.

Rule 2: Use Simple Words

  • Choose words everyone knows.
  • Like 'big' instead of 'enormous'.

🎙️ Our second rule is to use simple words. Instead of using very fancy or difficult words, pick words that you hear and use every day. This makes your writing friendly and welcoming.

📚
7 / 20

Simple Words: An Example

  • Difficult: The colossal elephant ambled through the verdant jungle.
  • Simple: The big elephant walked in the green jungle.

🎙️ See how 'colossal' and 'big' mean almost the same thing? But 'big' is much easier for everyone to understand. And 'walked' is simpler than 'ambled.'

Rule 3: Have Clear Ideas

  • Say one main thing at a time.
  • Don't mix too many thoughts.

🎙️ Our third rule is about having clear ideas. Imagine you want to draw a picture. You draw one thing at a time, right? Not everything at once. Writing is similar.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.
9 / 20

Clear Ideas: An Example

  • Confused: I went to the park and saw a dog, and then I ate an apple, and my friend was there.
  • Clear: I went to the park. I saw a dog. I ate an apple. My friend was there.

🎙️ In the first example, everything is mixed up. In the second, each sentence tells one clear thing. It's like telling a story step by step, making it easy to follow.
