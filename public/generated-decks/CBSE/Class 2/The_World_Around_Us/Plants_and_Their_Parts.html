Roots Drink Water for the Plant

  • Roots soak up water and food from the soil.
  • They send it up to the rest of the plant.

🎙️ Think of roots as tiny straws. They drink up all the water and good things from the soil, just like you drink juice with a straw. This water is then sent to all other parts of the plant.

Stem Carries Food and Water

  • The stem carries water from the roots to the leaves.
  • It also carries food from the leaves to other parts.

🎙️ The stem is like a highway for the plant! It carries water that the roots drank, all the way up to the leaves. It also carries the food made by the leaves to other parts of the plant.
