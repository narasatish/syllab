Roots: Under the Ground

  • Roots grow deep under the soil.
  • They hold the plant firmly in the ground.

Roots Drink Water

  • Roots soak up water and food from the soil.
  • This helps the plant grow big and strong.

🎙️ Think of roots as tiny straws. They drink water and nutrients from the soil, which is essential for the plant's health.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Stem: The Plant's Backbone

  • The stem stands tall above the ground.
  • It carries water and food to all parts of the plant.

🎙️ The stem is like the plant's spine, giving it support to stand upright. It also acts like a highway, transporting water and food.

  • Others are soft, green, and bend easily.
