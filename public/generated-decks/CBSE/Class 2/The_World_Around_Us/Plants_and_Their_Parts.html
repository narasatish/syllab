  • They grow big and tall.

Parts of a Plant

  • Just like us, plants have different parts.
  • Each part does a special job.

🎙️ Our body has hands, legs, and a head. A plant also has different parts, and each part helps the plant live and grow. Let's learn about them.

🪴

Part 1: Roots!

  • Roots grow under the ground.
  • They hold the plant firmly.

🎙️ The roots are like the plant's feet! They are hidden under the soil. They hold the plant tightly so it doesn't fall down.

👇
Roots under the ground
10 / 20

What do Roots do?

  • Roots soak up water from the soil.
  • They also take food for the plant.

🎙️ Roots are very thirsty! They drink all the water from the soil, just like you drink juice. They also find food for the plant from the soil.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.
11 / 20

Part 2: Stem!

  • The stem grows above the ground.
  • It holds the plant straight up.

🎙️ The stem is like the plant's backbone! It helps the plant stand tall and straight. It's the main body of the plant that we see above the ground.

⬆️
Stem standing tall
12 / 20

What does the Stem do?

  • The stem carries water from roots to leaves.
  • It also takes food to all parts of the plant.

🎙️ Think of the stem as a tiny road. It carries water from the roots all the way up to the leaves. It also carries the food made by leaves to all other parts of the plant.

1Water goes up
2Food goes around

Part 3: Leaves!

  • Leaves are usually green.
  • They come in many shapes and sizes.

🎙️ Look at the beautiful green leaves! Most leaves are green, but some can be other colours too. They are like the plant's hands, making food.

🍃
Green leaves

What do Leaves do?

  • Leaves make food for the plant.
  • They use sunlight and air to do this.

🎙️ Leaves are like tiny kitchens for the plant! They use sunlight, air, and water to cook food for the entire plant. This process is called photosynthesis.

🎙️ Oh, look at the pretty flowers! They come in so many colours and smell so nice. They make our gardens look lovely.

What do Flowers do?

  • Flowers turn into fruits.
  • They also make seeds inside.

🎙️ Flowers are not just pretty; they are very important! After some time, a flower can change into a fruit. And inside the fruit, we find seeds.
