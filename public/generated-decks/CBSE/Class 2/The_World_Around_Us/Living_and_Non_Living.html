  • Welcome to our amazing world, children!
  • Let's learn about everything around us.

🎙️ Hello, my dear students! Today, we are going on a fun adventure to explore all the wonderful things we see every day. We will learn to tell them apart!

What are Living Things?

  • Look at the little chick!
  • It will grow big and strong.

🎙️ Living things are like us! They can do many special things. One big thing they do is grow!

🐥
A growing chick

Living Things Can GROW!

  • A tiny seed becomes a big tree.
  • You were a baby, now you are bigger!

🎙️ Just like a small plant grows taller, and you grow from a baby to a big child, living things change and get bigger over time. This is a super power of living things!

Plants make their own food from sunlight.
4 / 20

Living Things Need FOOD!

  • We eat food to get energy.
  • Plants need sunlight and water.

🎙️ To grow and play, living things need food. We eat chapatis and vegetables, and plants make their own food using sunlight. Food gives them power!

A balanced plate keeps us healthy.
5 / 20

Living Things Can MOVE!

  • A bird flies in the sky.
  • A fish swims in the water.

🎙️ Living things can move from one place to another by themselves. Think about how you run and jump, or how animals walk and fly. They don't need anyone to push them!

Birds have feathers and can fly.
6 / 20

Living Things Can BREATHE!

  • We breathe in air to live.
  • Plants breathe too, through tiny holes.

🎙️ All living things need air to live. We breathe in and out, and even plants take in air through their leaves. It's like a special air pump inside them!

🌬️
Taking a deep breath
7 / 20

Living Things Can FEEL!

  • When you are happy, you smile.
  • A dog wags its tail when excited.

🎙️ Living things can feel things like joy, sadness, or even pain. If you touch a hot stove, you feel it! If you pet a cat, it feels good to the cat. They have feelings!

🤗
Feeling happy or sad
8 / 20

Living Things Make BABIES!

  • A hen lays eggs, and chicks hatch.
  • A mother gives birth to a baby.

🎙️ Living things can make more of their own kind. A small puppy grows from a big dog, and a small plant grows from a seed. This is how life continues!

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Family growing

Examples of Living Things: People and Animals

  • We are living things!
  • Dogs, cats, birds are living.

🎙️ Look at your family and pets! We all eat, grow, move, and breathe. These are all examples of living things around us.

Animals live all around us.

Examples of Living Things: Plants

  • Trees, flowers, and grass are living.
  • They also grow and need food.

🎙️ Don't forget plants! Even though they don't run around, they are very much alive. They grow, make their own food, and breathe air.

What are Non-Living Things?

  • Now, let's look at other things.
  • They are very different from us.

🎙️ Now, let's talk about things that are not like us. They don't have the special powers that living things have. They are called non-living things.

🧱
A strong brick wall
12 / 20

Non-Living Things Don't GROW!

  • Your toy car stays the same size.
  • A rock does not get bigger.

🎙️ Unlike us, non-living things do not grow. A small teddy bear will always stay small. It won't become a giant teddy bear!

🧸
A teddy bear doesn't grow
13 / 20

Non-Living Things Don't Need FOOD!

  • Your school bag doesn't get hungry.
  • A book doesn't need to eat.

🎙️ Non-living things don't need any food to live or grow. You don't feed your pencil, right? It works fine without eating!

📚
Books don't eat!
14 / 20

Non-Living Things Don't MOVE by Themselves!

  • Your chair stays where you put it.
  • A ball only moves if you kick it.

🎙️ Non-living things cannot move on their own. They need someone or something else to move them. Like your bicycle, it only moves when you pedal it.

🪑
A chair stays in place
15 / 20

Non-Living Things Don't BREATHE!

  • A table does not need air.
  • Your clothes don't breathe.

🎙️ Non-living things do not need to breathe air. They don't have lungs or tiny holes like plants. They just exist without needing to breathe.

👕
Clothes don't breathe
16 / 20

Non-Living Things Don't FEEL!

  • If you drop a stone, it doesn't cry.
  • A wall doesn't feel cold or hot.

🎙️ Non-living things cannot feel anything. They don't have feelings like happy or sad, and they don't feel pain or pleasure. They are just objects.

🪨
A rock feels nothing
17 / 20

Non-Living Things Don't Make BABIES!

  • A car cannot have baby cars.
  • Your school bag won't have little bags.

🎙️ Non-living things cannot make more of their own kind. A toy train will always be just one toy train. It will never make baby toy trains!

🚗
A car cannot have a baby car

Time to RECAP: Living vs. Non-Living

  • Living things grow, eat, move, breathe, feel, make babies.
  • Non-living things do none of these.
