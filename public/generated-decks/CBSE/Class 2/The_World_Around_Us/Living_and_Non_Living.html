Non-Living Things Do Not Breathe

  • Your chair does not need air.
  • A book does not breathe.

🎙️ Can your toy car breathe? No! Non-living things do not need air to live because they are not alive. They don't have lungs or tiny holes like plants.

🚫🌬️
No breathing
14 / 24

Non-Living Things Do Not Grow

  • Your pencil stays the same size.
  • A mountain does not get bigger.

🎙️ Did your favourite teddy bear grow taller last night? No! Non-living things do not grow. A small stone will always remain a small stone, unless you break it.

📏
Same size
15 / 24

Non-Living Things Don't Need Food or Water

  • Your ball does not get hungry.
  • Your water bottle doesn't get thirsty.

🎙️ You don't need to feed your shoes or give water to your books, do you? That's because non-living things don't need food or water to survive. They don't have energy needs.

❌🍔❌🍔
No hunger
16 / 24

Non-Living Things Don't Move by Themselves

  • A toy car moves only when you push it.
  • A chair stays in one place.

🎙️ Can your school bag walk to school by itself? No! Non-living things need someone or something else to move them. They cannot walk, fly, or swim on their own.

🛑
Stay still
17 / 24

Non-Living Things Do Not Feel

  • If you hit a table, it doesn't cry.
  • A rock doesn't feel happy or sad.

🎙️ If you accidentally drop your pencil, it doesn't feel any pain. Non-living things do not have feelings. They don't react to touch, sound, or light like living things do.

😐
No feelings

Non-Living Things Don't Make More Like Themselves

  • Your crayon won't have baby crayons.
  • A stone won't have baby stones.

🎙️ Imagine if your toy train suddenly had a baby toy train! That sounds funny, right? Non-living things cannot make more of their own kind. They don't reproduce.
