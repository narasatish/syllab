Animals are Living Things Too!

  • A puppy grows into a big dog.
  • Birds eat seeds and fly high in the sky.

🎙️ All the animals we see around us, from tiny ants to huge elephants, are living things. They also grow, move, and need food and water to survive. They are a big part of our living world.

Animals live all around us.

Even Plants are Living!

  • A tiny seed grows into a big tree.
  • Plants need sunlight and water to live.

🎙️ It might be hard to believe, but plants are also living things! They start as small seeds and grow into tall trees or beautiful flowers. They drink water from the soil and use sunlight to make their food.

Plants make their own food from sunlight.

What are Non-Living Things?

  • They do not grow bigger or smaller.
  • They do not need food or water to live.

🎙️ Now, let's look at things that are not alive. These are called non-living things. They don't change size on their own, and they don't get hungry or thirsty like living things do.

🎙️ Think about your favourite toy, like a teddy bear or a doll. Does it ever grow? Does it ask for food? No, right? That's because it is a non-living thing. We play with it, but it doesn't live.

Things in Our Home are Non-Living.

  • Your chair does not grow taller.
  • Your table doesn't get hungry at all.

🎙️ Look around your room. Your bed, your table, your books – all these are non-living things. They don't breathe, they don't eat, and they don't grow. They are always there for us to use.

Living Things Grow, Non-Living Don't.

  • You were a baby, now you are big!
  • Your school bag stays the same size.

🎙️ One big difference is growth. Living things, like you, grow from small to big. But non-living things, like your pencil box, will always remain the same size. They don't grow at all.

Living Things Move, Non-Living Don't (on their own).

  • A bird flies, a fish swims freely.
  • Your pencil only moves if you pick it up.

🎙️ Living things can move by themselves, like when you run or a bird flies. Non-living things can only move if someone or something else moves them. A ball won't roll unless you kick it.

🏃

Living Things Need Food & Water.

  • We eat to get energy to play.
  • Plants drink water to stay healthy.

🎙️ Just like you feel hungry and thirsty, all living things need food and water. Food gives them energy, and water helps them stay alive. Non-living things do not need any food or water.

Living Things Breathe Air.

  • We breathe in and out all the time.
  • A stone does not breathe at all.

🎙️ Another important thing is breathing. All living things, whether it's a person, an animal, or a plant, need to breathe air to live. Non-living things, like your book, do not breathe.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Living Things Can Feel and Respond.

  • If you touch a hot stove, you pull back!
  • A book doesn't feel anything if you drop it.

🎙️ Living things can feel things, like pain, joy, or cold, and they react to them. If you touch something hot, you quickly move your hand away. Non-living things have no feelings and do not react.

Remember Living Things!

  • They grow and change.
  • They move on their own.
  • They need food, water, and air.
  • They can feel and respond.

🎙️ Let's quickly remember the special qualities of living things. They are active, they grow, and they need things to survive. Think about yourself and your pets – you fit all these points!

Remember Non-Living Things!

  • They do not grow.
  • They do not move on their own.
  • They don't need food, water, or air.
  • They don't feel anything.

🎙️ Now, let's recap about non-living things. They are still and unchanging. They don't have any needs like living things. Your toys, books, and furniture are all non-living.
