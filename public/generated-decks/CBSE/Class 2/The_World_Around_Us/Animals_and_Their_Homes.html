Why Do Animals Need Homes?

  • Just like us, animals need a safe place.
  • Homes protect them from rain, sun, and cold.
  • They keep baby animals safe too.

🎙️ Think about why you love your home. It keeps you warm and safe, right? Animals need homes for the very same reasons. It's their special shelter.

Birds Build Nests

  • Little birds make cozy nests.
  • They use twigs, leaves, and feathers.
  • Nests are where birds lay their eggs and raise babies.

🎙️ Have you ever seen a bird's nest high up in a tree? They are so clever to weave all those materials together. It's a perfect cradle for their tiny chicks.

Where Can We Find Nests?

  • Birds build nests in tall trees.
  • Sometimes, they build them under the roofs of our houses.
  • Keep an eye out for them!

🎙️ Next time you go for a walk, look up! You might spot a little nest hidden amongst the leaves. It's a secret world up there.

  • We build them a special house called a kennel.
  • It keeps them warm and dry.

🎙️ Many of us have a furry friend at home. If you have a dog, you might have a kennel for it in your garden. It's their own little doggy house!

  • Farmers take care of cows in sheds.

Horses Live in Stables

  • Beautiful horses live in stables.
  • A stable is like a special barn for horses.
  • Each horse has its own space.

🎙️ Horses are magnificent animals. They need a strong and safe home, and that's what a stable provides. It's a place where they can rest and eat.

Lions Live in Dens

  • The king of the jungle, the lion, lives in a den.
  • Dens are usually caves or rocky shelters.
  • It's a strong, safe home for their family.

🎙️ Lions are powerful animals, and they need a secure place to live. Their dens are often hidden and offer great protection for them and their cubs.

  • These tunnels are called burrows.
  • Burrows keep them safe from other animals.

🎙️ Rabbits are very good at digging! They make a whole network of tunnels underground. It's like their own secret hideaway, safe from harm.

Honeybees Live in Hives

  • Busy honeybees live together in a hive.
  • Hives are made of wax, with many small cells.
  • This is where they store honey!

🎙️ Next time you enjoy honey, think of the bees and their amazing homes! Beehives are incredible structures, full of busy bees making delicious honey.

Spiders Spin Webs

  • They spin sticky webs from their bodies.
  • Webs are homes and also traps for insects.

🎙️ Spider webs might look fragile, but they are very strong and sticky! They serve as a home for the spider and a clever way to catch their food.
