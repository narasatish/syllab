Why Do Animals Need Homes?

  • To stay safe from danger.
  • To protect from bad weather.
  • To keep their babies safe.

🎙️ Animals hide in their homes from bigger animals that might hurt them. Their homes also protect them from heavy rain, strong winds, and very hot or cold weather. It's also where they keep their little ones safe.

Cow Lives in a Shed!

  • A cow's home is called a shed.
  • It is a big, open shelter.

🎙️ Look, this is a cow! Cows give us milk. Their home, a shed, is usually a simple roof with open sides, keeping them dry and cool.

🐄
8 / 24

Horse Lives in a Stable!

  • A horse's home is called a stable.
  • It has separate rooms for each horse.

🎙️ Horses are strong animals that help us. Their stable is a special building with many small rooms, one for each horse, where they can rest.

🐎

Dog Lives in a Kennel!

  • A dog's home is called a kennel.
  • It is a small house just for dogs.

🎙️ Dogs are our best friends! They love to play and guard our homes. Their kennel is a cozy little house, often made of wood, where they can sleep.

  • Birds build nests on trees using twigs and leaves.
