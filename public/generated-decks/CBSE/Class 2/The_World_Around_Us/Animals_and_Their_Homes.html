Animals and Their Homes

  • Welcome to our lesson!
  • Today we will learn about where animals live.

🎙️ Hello, dear children! Today, we will go on an exciting journey to explore the wonderful world of animals and their special homes. Just like we have our houses, animals also have places they call home.

Why Do Animals Need Homes?

  • Homes keep animals safe.
  • They protect from rain, sun, and cold.

🎙️ Imagine it's raining outside. Where do you go? Inside your home, right? Animals also need a safe place to live, to protect themselves from bad weather and other dangers.

Animals live all around us.

  • Each animal has a special home.
  • Some build, some find, some live in nature.

🎙️ Just like people live in different types of houses – flats, bungalows, or huts – animals also have many different kinds of homes. Some animals are very clever and build their own homes!

Birds Build Nests

  • Birds collect twigs and leaves.
  • They weave them into cozy nests.
  • Baby birds grow up here.

🎙️ Look up in the trees, and you might spot a bird's nest! Mama bird and Papa bird work very hard to build these soft homes for their little chicks, high up and safe.

Birds have feathers and can fly.

Lions Live in Dens

  • Lions are big, wild animals.
  • They rest and raise cubs in dens.
  • Dens are often caves or hidden spots.

🎙️ The majestic lion, the king of the jungle, needs a strong home too! They find or make dens, which are like secret rooms or caves, to stay safe and keep their babies warm.

Rabbits Dig Burrows

  • Rabbits dig holes in the ground.
  • These tunnels are called burrows.
  • They are cool and safe underground.

🎙️ Little rabbits are amazing diggers! They dig long tunnels under the ground called burrows. These burrows are their cozy homes, safe from other animals and the hot sun.

Rabbit's Burrow

  • Many bees live together in a hive.
  • Hives are made of wax.
  • They store honey here.

🎙️ Buzz, buzz! Bees live in big families inside their homes called hives. They build these amazing structures from wax and fill them with delicious honey. It's a very busy home!

Ants Make Anthills

  • Tiny ants build huge anthills.
  • Anthills have many rooms and tunnels.
  • They work together to build them.

🎙️ Even tiny ants have incredible homes! They build anthills using soil and sand. These anthills are like busy cities with many rooms and paths for all the ants to live and work.

🐜
Ant's Anthill

  • Sometimes they have a kennel.
  • Kennels are small dog houses.

🎙️ Our furry friends, dogs, are often part of our families. If they don't sleep inside with us, they might have a special dog house outside called a kennel, which keeps them sheltered.

  • Cows are farm animals.
  • Farmers build big sheds for them.
  • Sheds protect cows from weather.

🎙️ On a farm, you'll see big, gentle cows. Farmers build large, open shelters called sheds for them. These sheds protect the cows from too much sun or heavy rain.

  • Horses are strong and beautiful.
  • They live in buildings called stables.
  • Each horse has its own stall.

🎙️ Horses, with their flowing manes and tails, also live on farms or special ranches. Their homes are called stables, which have individual spaces for each horse to rest.

🐎
Horse Stable

Fish Live in Water

  • Fish live in ponds, rivers, and oceans.
  • Water is their natural home.
  • They cannot live without water.

🎙️ Some animals don't build houses, their whole environment is their home! Fish, for example, live happily in water, whether it's a small pond or a vast ocean. Water is where they breathe and swim.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Monkeys Live in Trees

  • Monkeys jump and play in trees.
  • Trees provide food and shelter.
  • They are very agile climbers.

🎙️ Monkeys are excellent climbers and love to swing from branch to branch! For them, the tall trees of the forest are their homes, offering both food and a safe place to sleep.
