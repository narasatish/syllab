Be Careful with Sharp Things!

  • Knives, scissors, blades are sharp.
  • They can cut your fingers.
  • Ask an elder for help.

🎙️ Look at this knife! It's very sharp. Things like knives, scissors, or broken glass can cut you badly. Always ask a grown-up if you need to use something sharp.

No Playing with Fire!

  • Matches, lighters are dangerous.
  • Fire can burn you badly.
  • Keep away from stoves and candles.

🎙️ Fire is useful for cooking, but it can be very dangerous if we play with it. Never touch matchsticks, lighters, or go near a burning stove or candle without an adult.

Stay Away from Electric Sockets!

  • Electric sockets have power.
  • Touching them can give a shock.
  • Never put fingers or objects inside.

🎙️ These are electric sockets. They have electricity, which can give you a very bad shock if you touch them or put anything inside. Always keep your hands away from them.

Be Careful on Stairs!

  • Running or jumping on stairs is risky.
  • You might fall down.
  • Walk slowly, holding the railing.

🎙️ Stairs help us go up and down. But running or jumping on them can make you slip and fall, which can hurt a lot. Always walk slowly and hold the railing.

Safety on the Road

  • Roads are busy places.
  • Many cars and vehicles.
  • Let's learn road safety rules.

🎙️ When we step out of our homes, we go on roads. Roads are busy with many cars, bikes, and buses. We need to be very careful there to stay safe.

Always Walk on the Footpath!

  • Footpaths are for walking.
  • Keep you safe from vehicles.
  • Never walk in the middle of the road.

🎙️ See this path next to the road? That's a footpath. It's made especially for people to walk safely. Always use the footpath and never walk in the middle of the road.

🚶‍♀️

Cross the Road at Zebra Crossing!

  • Look left, then right, then left again.
  • Walk when the road is clear.
  • It's like stripes of a zebra!

🎙️ When you need to cross the road, look for these black and white stripes. They are called a zebra crossing. Always stop, look both ways, and then cross when it's safe.

🦓

Never Play on the Road!

  • Roads are for vehicles, not games.
  • A car might not see you.
  • Play in parks or playgrounds.

🎙️ The road is a dangerous place to play. Cars and other vehicles move very fast, and the drivers might not see you. Always play in safe places like parks or playgrounds.

🚗
12 / 20

Obey Traffic Lights!

  • Red light means STOP!
  • Yellow light means WAIT!
  • Green light means GO!

🎙️ Traffic lights help control the flow of vehicles and people. Remember: Red means stop, Yellow means wait, and Green means go. Always follow these signals.

🚦

Be Kind, Don't Push!

  • Pushing can make friends fall.
  • Someone might get hurt.
  • Always walk gently.

🎙️ It's never good to push your friends, especially when you are running or in a line. Someone might fall and get hurt. Always be gentle and kind to your friends.

  • You might bump into someone.
  • Walk slowly and carefully.

🎙️ Running in corridors can be dangerous. You might slip, fall, or bump into a teacher or another friend. Always walk slowly and carefully inside the school building.
