What are Safety Rules?

  • They are rules to protect us.
  • They keep us away from harm.

🎙️ Safety rules are like special instructions that tell us what to do and what not to do. They are designed to keep us safe from getting hurt or being in danger. Think of them as your personal protection shield.

Why are Safety Rules Important?

  • To avoid getting hurt.
  • To stay healthy and happy.

🎙️ Following safety rules helps us avoid accidents like falling down or getting cuts. When we are safe, we can play and learn happily. It's important to stay safe so we can always have fun!

Don't Play with Fire!

  • Never touch matches or lighters.
  • They can cause dangerous burns.

🎙️ Fire can be very dangerous. Matches and lighters are not toys. Always ask an adult if you need fire for anything, like lighting a candle. Playing with fire can cause serious burns.

Be Careful with Electricity!

  • Don't touch electric sockets.
  • Never put fingers in plug points.

🎙️ Electricity helps power our lights and fans, but it can be very harmful if not handled properly. Never touch electric switches or put anything into plug points. This can give you a nasty shock.

An electric circuit lets current flow.

Stay Away from Sharp Objects!

  • Knives, scissors are not toys.
  • Ask an elder for help if needed.

🎙️ Sharp objects like knives, scissors, and blades can cut you badly. Always remember that these are tools for adults. If you need to use scissors for craft, always ask an adult to help you.

Don't Run in Corridors!

  • Walk slowly in school hallways.
  • Running can cause falls and bumps.

🎙️ School corridors can be busy places. If you run, you might bump into someone or slip and fall. Always walk calmly and slowly in the corridors to keep yourself and others safe.

🚶

Don't Push Your Friends!

  • Be gentle and kind to everyone.
  • Pushing can make others fall.

🎙️ Playing with friends is fun, but always be gentle. Pushing can make your friends lose their balance and fall, which can hurt them. Always be kind and play nicely with everyone.

🤝

Stay in a Line!

  • When moving, form a neat line.
  • It helps everyone move safely.

🎙️ When you go to the assembly, library, or playground, always walk in a line. This helps everyone move in an organised way and prevents people from getting lost or bumping into each other.

Safety Rules on the Road

  • Roads can be busy with vehicles.
  • Always be careful when outside.

🎙️ When we are outside, especially near roads, we must be extra careful. Roads have many cars, buses, and bikes. Following road safety rules keeps us safe from accidents.

🛣️
Busy Road
12 / 19

Use the Zebra Crossing!

  • Look left, then right, then left.
  • Walk only when the road is clear.

🎙️ A zebra crossing is a special place marked on the road for pedestrians to cross. Always use it! Before you step onto the road, look carefully both ways to make sure no vehicles are coming.

🦓
Crossing Safely
13 / 19

Walk on the Footpath!

  • Walk on the pavement next to road.
  • Keep away from moving vehicles.

🎙️ When you are walking on the road, always use the footpath or pavement. This keeps you away from the moving traffic on the main road. If there is no footpath, walk facing the traffic.

🚶‍♀️
Walk on Path
14 / 19

Don't Play on the Road!

  • Roads are for vehicles, not games.
  • Play in a park or safe ground.

🎙️ Roads are very dangerous places to play. Vehicles move very fast, and drivers might not see you. Always choose a safe place like a park or playground to play your games.
