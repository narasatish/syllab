Why Do We Need Safety Rules?

  • Safety rules are like traffic lights for our lives.
  • They tell us when to 'stop' and 'go' to stay safe.
  • They protect us from getting hurt.

🎙️ Just like traffic lights guide cars and people on the road, safety rules guide us to make good choices. Following them keeps us away from danger and allows us to play and learn freely.

Safety at Home: Electric Power

  • Electric plugs are like sleeping monsters.
  • Never touch electric sockets or wires.
  • You could get a dangerous shock!

🎙️ Electricity helps us run many things like lights and fans, but it can be very dangerous. Never put your fingers or anything else into an electric socket. Always ask an adult for help with electric devices.
