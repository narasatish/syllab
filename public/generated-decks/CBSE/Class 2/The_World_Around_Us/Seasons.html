  • It feels very hot.
  • Days are long, nights are short.

🎙️ Summer is the season when the sun is at its strongest. Everything feels warm, and we often look for ways to stay cool. It's a great time for outdoor play early in the morning or evening.

Monsoon Season: Rainy Rains!

  • It rains a lot!
  • Everything becomes green and fresh.
  • The air feels cool.

🎙️ After the hot summer, the monsoon arrives, bringing much-needed rain! The plants and trees look so happy and green after getting water. The weather becomes pleasant and cool.

  • We make paper boats!

Winter Season: Cold & Cozy!

  • The weather becomes cold.
  • Sometimes we see fog.
  • Days are short, nights are long.

🎙️ After the rains, winter arrives, bringing chilly winds. We often feel like staying indoors and snuggling under blankets. Sometimes, morning fog makes everything look mysterious.

Why do Seasons Change? Earth's Dance!

  • Earth travels around the Sun.
  • Different parts get different sunlight.
  • This causes seasons to change.

🎙️ The Earth is always moving, like a dancer, around the Sun! As it dances, different parts of our Earth get more or less direct sunlight. This amazing movement is why we have different seasons.

Spring and Autumn: Little Helpers!

  • Spring comes after winter.
  • Autumn comes before winter.
  • They are shorter seasons.

🎙️ Besides our three main seasons, we also have two shorter, beautiful seasons. Spring is like a bridge from cold to warm, and Autumn is like a bridge from warm to cold.

Animals and Seasons!

  • Some animals sleep in winter (hibernate).
  • Some birds fly to warmer places (migrate).
  • Animals also adapt to seasons.

🎙️ It's not just us who feel the seasons, animals do too! Some animals like bears take long sleeps during winter, which is called hibernation. Many birds fly far away to warmer lands when it gets cold.

Animals live all around us.

Farmers and Seasons!

  • Farmers depend on seasons.
  • Different crops grow in different seasons.
  • Monsoon is very important for crops.

🎙️ Our farmers are very clever; they know exactly which crops to plant in each season. For example, rice grows well in the monsoon, while wheat grows in winter. Seasons help us get all our food!
