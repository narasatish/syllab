What are Seasons?

  • Seasons are different times of the year.
  • The weather changes a lot in each season.

🎙️ Imagine a year as a big circle. As we move around this circle, the weather changes. These long periods of changing weather are called seasons. It's like nature's way of changing its clothes!

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Why Do Seasons Change?

  • The Earth goes around the Sun.
  • This movement makes seasons happen.

🎙️ Our Earth is like a big ball that dances around the Sun. As it dances, different parts of the Earth get more or less sunshine. This special dance is why we have different seasons!

🎙️ Oh, Summer! It's like a giant oven outside! The sun is so bright and warm. We feel very hot and sweat a lot. It's a great time for holidays and playing outdoors, but remember to stay hydrated!

  • We wear light cotton clothes.

🎙️ In summer, we try to keep cool. We drink lots of water and fresh juices. My favourite is eating ice cream! We wear clothes that are light and airy, like cotton shirts and shorts, to feel comfortable.

Welcome, Monsoon!

  • Monsoon brings lots of rain.
  • The sky is full of dark clouds.

🎙️ After the hot summer, the Monsoon season comes with a big sigh of relief! Dark clouds fill the sky, and then 'pitter-patter' goes the rain. Everything looks fresh and green after the rain.

🌧️

Monsoon Magic!

  • We use umbrellas and raincoats.
  • We love hot pakoras and tea!
  • Sometimes we see rainbows!

🎙️ During monsoon, we need to protect ourselves from the rain. So, we use colourful umbrellas and raincoats. It's also a lovely time to enjoy hot snacks like pakoras with our family, watching the rain outside. And sometimes, a beautiful rainbow appears!

  • Winter is the coldest season.
  • The mornings can be foggy and chilly.

🎙️ After the rain, comes the cold, cold winter! We feel chilly, especially in the mornings and evenings. Sometimes, there is fog, and it's hard to see far away. But winter also has its own charm!

Winter Warmth!

  • We wear warm woollen clothes.
  • We like hot drinks and soups.
  • Sometimes we sit by a bonfire!

🎙️ To keep ourselves warm in winter, we wear sweaters, jackets, caps, and gloves. We love to drink hot milk, tea, or yummy soups. Sitting around a bonfire with friends and family is also a special winter activity!

Lovely Spring!

  • Spring comes after winter.
  • New flowers bloom everywhere.
  • The weather is very pleasant.

🎙️ Before summer gets too hot, we have a beautiful season called Spring. It's like nature wakes up from its sleep! New leaves sprout, and colourful flowers bloom, making everything look so fresh and pretty. The weather is just perfect!

Cool Autumn!

  • Autumn comes after monsoon.
  • Leaves on trees turn yellow and fall.
  • The weather starts getting cooler.

🎙️ After the rainy monsoon, we have Autumn. This is when the leaves on many trees change colour to yellow, orange, or red, and then gently fall to the ground. It's a beautiful sight, and the weather starts to get a little cool.

Fun Fact About Seasons!

  • Did you know that some animals change their fur colour?
  • This helps them hide in different seasons!

🎙️ Here's a super cool fact! Some animals, like the Arctic Fox, actually change the colour of their fur when the seasons change. In winter, their fur is white to blend with snow, and in summer, it turns brown to blend with rocks and soil. Isn't that amazing?
