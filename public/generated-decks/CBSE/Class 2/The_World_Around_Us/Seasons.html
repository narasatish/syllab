What are Seasons?

  • Seasons are different types of weather.
  • Like hot, cold, or rainy days.
  • They come one after another in a cycle.

🎙️ Seasons are simply the different kinds of weather we experience throughout the year. It's like the Earth has different outfits it wears! Sometimes it's a sunny outfit, sometimes a rainy one, and sometimes a warm, cozy one. These weather patterns repeat every year, like a cycle.

Clouds bring us rain.

Why Do Seasons Change?

  • Our Earth goes around the Sun.
  • This long journey brings different weather to us.

🎙️ Do you know why the seasons change? It's because our Earth is always moving! It goes around the Sun, like a little dance. As the Earth moves, different parts of it get more or less sunlight, and this causes the weather to change, bringing us new seasons.

Eight planets orbit the Sun.

How Many Main Seasons?

  • We mainly have three big seasons in India:
  • Summer, Monsoon, and Winter.
  • Some places also have Spring and Autumn.

🎙️ In India, we mostly talk about three main seasons that are very clear: Summer, when it's very hot; Monsoon, when it rains a lot; and Winter, when it's cold. But in some parts of the world, and even in some parts of India, we also see Spring and Autumn, which are transition seasons.

Monsoon: The Rainy Season!

  • Dark clouds fill the sky, and then it rains!
  • Everything becomes fresh and green.

🎙️ After the hot summer, comes the Monsoon! This is when we see lots and lots of rain. The sky gets covered with dark clouds, and the rain makes everything look clean and green. It's a very important season for farmers and plants.
