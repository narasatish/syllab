  • When wind blows, we feel air on our skin.

🎙️ Even though we can't see air, we can definitely feel it! When a fan is on, or when you feel a gentle breeze, that's the air moving. Close your eyes and feel the air around you.

We Need Air to Breathe

  • All living things need air to live.
  • We breathe in fresh air to stay healthy and strong.

🎙️ This is one of the most important jobs of air! Just like we need food and water, we need air to breathe. If we don't breathe air, we cannot live. It keeps our bodies working.

Animals Also Need Air

  • Birds, dogs, fish – all animals need air.
  • Fish breathe air that is mixed in the water!

🎙️ It's not just humans! Look at the animals around you. Your pet dog or a bird flying in the sky – they all need air to breathe. Even fish in the water take in air that is dissolved there.

Animals live all around us.

Plants Need Air Too!

  • Plants take air to make their food and grow.
  • They give us fresh air in return!

🎙️ Even our green friends, the plants, need air! They use air, sunlight, and water to make their own food. And guess what? Plants help us by making the air fresh for us to breathe. So, plants are very important!

Air Fills Things Up!

  • We use air to fill up balloons.
  • Air fills bicycle tyres so we can ride smoothly.

🎙️ Have you ever blown into a balloon? It gets bigger and bigger because you are filling it with air! We also put air into bicycle tyres, footballs, and swimming tubes to make them useful.

Air Helps Things Move

  • Wind is moving air.
  • Wind helps kites fly high and sailboats move on water.

🎙️ When air moves, we call it wind. Wind is very powerful! It can push kites up into the sky, help clothes dry on a line, and even make big windmills turn to make electricity.

Does Air Have Weight?

  • Yes, air has weight, even though we can't see it.
  • A balloon full of air is a little heavier than an empty one.

🎙️ It's a little tricky to imagine, but air does have weight! If you take two balloons, one empty and one filled with air, the one with air inside will be a tiny bit heavier. This shows air has weight.

We Need Clean Air

  • Clean air keeps us healthy and happy.
  • Dirty air can make us cough and feel sick.

🎙️ Just like we like to drink clean water and eat clean food, we need to breathe clean air. Clean air helps us play, learn, and grow strong. Dirty air, on the other hand, can make us unhealthy.

How Does Air Get Dirty?

  • Smoke from cars and factories makes the air dirty.
  • Burning garbage also adds bad things to the air.

🎙️ Sometimes, air can get dirty. Have you ever seen smoke coming out of cars or big factories? That smoke mixes with the air and makes it unhealthy to breathe. We should try to keep our air clean.

How Can We Keep Air Clean?

  • Plant more trees, they give us fresh air.
  • Walk or cycle for short distances instead of using cars.

🎙️ We can all help to keep our air clean! Planting more trees is a great way, as trees clean the air for us. Also, walking or cycling instead of always using a car helps reduce smoke in the air.
