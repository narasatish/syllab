  • It gives us energy to play and learn.
  • It keeps us strong and healthy.

🎙️ Just like plants need water and sunlight to grow, our bodies need food to grow. Food is like fuel for our bodies. It helps us run fast, think clearly, and stay well.

  • It helps us fight sickness.

Why Less Unhealthy Food?

  • Eating too much unhealthy food can make us feel tired.
  • It can give us a tummy ache.
  • It can make us get sick easily.

🎙️ If we eat too much unhealthy food, our body doesn't get the good things it needs. We might feel sleepy, get a stomach upset, or even catch a cold more often. So, it's best to eat them only sometimes.

  • Each meal is important for energy.

🎙️ We usually eat three main meals in a day. Each meal is important to give us energy for different parts of the day. Skipping meals is not a good habit.

Good Food Habits 2

  • Chew your food slowly and properly.
  • Don't talk while eating.
  • Don't waste food.

🎙️ Chewing your food slowly helps your stomach digest it better. Also, remember not to talk with food in your mouth. And always take only as much food as you can eat, so you don't waste any.
