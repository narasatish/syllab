What is Unhealthy Food?

  • Foods that are tasty but not good for us often.
  • They can make us lazy or sick if eaten too much.
  • Examples: chips, chocolates, sugary drinks.

🎙️ Unhealthy foods are often very yummy, like chips and chocolates! But if we eat too much of them, they can make us feel tired, lazy, and sometimes even upset our tummy. It's okay to have them sometimes, but not every day.

Good Food Habits: Be a Smart Eater!

  • Always wash your hands before eating.
  • Eat slowly and chew your food well.
  • Don't talk while eating.

🎙️ Eating healthy food is great, but having good habits while eating is also very important. Always wash your hands to keep germs away. Chew your food properly so your tummy can digest it easily. And remember, no talking with food in your mouth!

More Good Food Habits!

  • Eat your meals at fixed times every day.
  • Don't waste food; take only what you can eat.
  • Drink plenty of water throughout the day.

🎙️ Eating at the same time every day helps your body stay regular. Remember not to waste food – many people don't have enough. And don't forget to drink lots of water; it's super important for your body, just like food!
