  • Rivers usually begin high up in the mountains.
  • Imagine tall, snowy peaks!

🎙️ Our river friend is born in big, tall mountains. These mountains are so high that their tops are covered in snow, like a white hat!

Tiny Drops, Big Start

  • The sun melts the snow on mountains.
  • Rain also falls there.
  • These tiny drops become the river's first water.

🎙️ When the sun shines, the snow on the mountains melts into tiny drops of water. Rain also adds more water. All these tiny drops come together to start our river's journey.

What is a Delta?

  • Near the sea, the river slows down a lot.
  • It drops all the sand and soil it carried.
  • This forms new land called a delta.

🎙️ Just before joining the sea, the river becomes very slow. It drops all the soil and sand it has been carrying for so long. This soil piles up and creates new land, often shaped like a triangle, which we call a delta.
