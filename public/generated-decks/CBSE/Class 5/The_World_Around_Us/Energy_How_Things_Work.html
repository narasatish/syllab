The Sun: Our Biggest Energy Helper!

  • The Sun gives us light and warmth.
  • It is the biggest source of energy for Earth.

🎙️ Look up at the sky during the day! The bright sun is not just for making the day sunny. It gives us so much energy that helps plants grow and keeps us warm.

Light Energy Helps Us See!

  • Light energy helps us see things.
  • The sun, a bulb, or a torch give us light.

🎙️ Without light, everything would be dark, right? Light energy makes everything visible. Just like when you switch on a bulb, light energy fills the room.

Light bounces back from a mirror — angle in equals angle out.

Heat Energy Makes Things Warm!

  • Heat energy makes things feel warm or hot.
  • The sun, a fire, or a heater give us heat.

🎙️ When you sit in the sun, you feel warm. That's heat energy! When your mom cooks food, the stove uses heat energy to make it hot and tasty.

Sound Energy Helps Us Hear!

  • It travels through the air to our ears.

  • Electrical energy makes our TV, fan, and fridge work.
  • It travels through wires to our homes.
