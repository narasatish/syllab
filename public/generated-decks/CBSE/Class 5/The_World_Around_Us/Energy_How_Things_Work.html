Why do we Need Energy?

Light Energy

  • A bulb gives us light at night.

🎙️ Without light, we wouldn't be able to see anything! The sun gives us natural light during the day. At night, we use light bulbs or lamps, which convert electrical energy into light energy, helping us see in the dark.
