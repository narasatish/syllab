High Tablelands: Plateaus!

  • These are like tables – flat on top, but higher than the ground around them.
  • They are often rocky and have many minerals.
  • The Deccan Plateau covers much of central India.

🎙️ Plateaus are interesting because they are not as high as mountains, but they are still elevated. They are often rich in valuable stones and metals.

Common Mistakes!

  • Confusing mountains with plateaus (one is very pointy, the other is flat-top).
  • Thinking that 'diversity' means 'being separate' (it means being different but together).
  • Forgetting to give examples when asked about culture.

🎙️ It's easy to mix up some terms, but remember the key features. Also, remember that our differences make us strong, not weak!
