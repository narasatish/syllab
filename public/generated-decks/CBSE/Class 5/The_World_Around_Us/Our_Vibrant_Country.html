Tall Mountains of India

  • We have very tall mountains in the north.
  • They are called the Himalayas.
  • People love to visit them.

🎙️ Look at these huge, snow-capped mountains! The Himalayas are like giant guardians standing tall in the north of India. Many rivers start from these mountains, bringing water to us.

Himalayan Mountains

Flat Green Plains

  • Below the mountains are flat lands.
  • Farmers grow lots of food here.
  • These are the Northern Plains.

🎙️ After the tall mountains, we find big, flat areas called plains. These lands are very fertile, meaning they are great for farming. Most of our food grains like wheat and rice come from here.

🌾
Farm Fields

Sandy Deserts

  • Some parts of India have hot, sandy deserts.
  • Very little water is found here.
  • Camels help people travel in deserts.

🎙️ Now, let's imagine a very hot place with lots of sand! That's a desert. The Thar Desert in India is a famous one. Camels are called the 'ships of the desert' because they help people move around easily there.

🐪
Desert Camel

High Flat Plateaus

  • Plateaus are like tables on the land.
  • They are high but have flat tops.
  • The Deccan Plateau is very big.

🎙️ Imagine a big, flat table raised above the ground. That's a plateau! India has a large plateau region called the Deccan Plateau. These areas are rich in minerals and have unique forests.

⛰️
Tableland

Beautiful Coasts and Islands

  • India has a long coastline with beaches.
  • We also have islands in the sea.
  • Andaman and Nicobar are famous islands.

🎙️ India is surrounded by water on three sides, giving us lovely beaches and coastal areas. We also have beautiful islands far out in the sea, like the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, which are home to many unique plants and animals.

Beach and Sea

Different Kinds of Weather
