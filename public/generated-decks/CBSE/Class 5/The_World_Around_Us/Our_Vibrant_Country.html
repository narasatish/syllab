High Mountains

  • Like the Himalayas.
  • They are very cold.

🎙️ Imagine huge, tall peaks reaching the sky! These are our mountains, like the mighty Himalayas. They are so high that their tops are covered with snow all year round. Many rivers begin here.

Mountains

Flowing Rivers

  • Rivers give us water.
  • Ganga and Yamuna are big rivers.
  • They make land fertile.

🎙️ After the mountains, we have many beautiful rivers that flow across our land. Rivers are like lifelines, giving us water to drink and grow food. The Ganga and Yamuna are very important to us.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Flat Plains

  • Flat land is called a plain.
  • Good for farming.
  • Many villages are here.

🎙️ Most of the food we eat comes from farms in the plains. These are large, flat areas of land where farmers can easily grow crops like wheat and rice. It's where many people live and work.

Hot Deserts

  • Very hot and sandy.
  • Less water here.
  • Camels live in deserts.

🎙️ Now, let's go to a very hot place, the desert! It's mostly sand and gets very little rain. Animals like camels are specially adapted to live here, storing water to survive the heat.

🏜️
Desert

Tableland Plateaus

  • High land, flat on top.
  • Like a big table.
  • Many minerals are found here.

🎙️ Imagine a big, flat table in the middle of a room. That's what a plateau looks like! It's higher than the surrounding land but flat on top. We find many useful rocks and minerals in these areas.
