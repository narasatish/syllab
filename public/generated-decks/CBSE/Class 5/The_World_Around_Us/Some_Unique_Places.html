  • Penguins live in the Antarctic.
  • They waddle and slide on ice.

🎙️ Look at these cute birds! Penguins cannot fly, but they are fantastic swimmers. They have thick feathers and a layer of fat to stay warm in the icy waters of the Antarctic. They live in big groups, like a happy family.

Animals of the Desert: Camels

  • Camels are called 'ships of the desert'.
  • They can store water and food.
  • They walk easily on hot sand.

🎙️ Camels are perfectly made for desert life. They can go many days without water, storing it and food in their bodies. Their wide feet help them walk on the soft sand without sinking. They are very helpful to people in deserts.

Plants of the Desert: Cactus

  • Cactus plants grow in deserts.
  • They have thick stems to store water.
  • They have sharp thorns instead of leaves.

🎙️ Even in the hot desert, some plants find a way to live! Cacti are amazing plants that store water in their thick bodies. Their sharp thorns protect them from animals and also help reduce water loss. Be careful not to touch them!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Don't mix Arctic and Antarctic.
  • Not all deserts are only sand.
  • Remember unique animals for unique places.

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused between the two cold poles, Arctic and Antarctic. Also, remember that some deserts have rocks, not just sand. Always connect the special animals and plants to their unique homes.
