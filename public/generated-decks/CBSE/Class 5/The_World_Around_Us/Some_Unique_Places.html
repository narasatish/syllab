How a Volcano Works

  • Deep inside Earth, hot rock is called magma.
  • Magma pushes up through a crack.
  • It erupts and flows out as lava!

🎙️ It's like a giant pressure cooker! Inside the Earth, there's super hot melted rock. When the pressure builds up, it finds a way out through a volcano, exploding to the surface as lava.
