  • Let's solve the mystery of food together!

🎙️ Hello, children! Today we are going on an exciting journey to uncover 'The Mystery of Food'. We eat food every day, but do we truly understand why it's so important? Let's find out!

What is Food?

  • Food is anything we eat and drink.
  • It comes in many shapes, sizes, and colours.

🎙️ Food is simply what we put into our bodies to keep us going. Think of all the delicious things you eat – fruits, vegetables, rice, roti. All of these are food!

🍎
Yummy Food

Why Do We Need Food? (Energy)

  • Food gives us energy to play and run.
  • It helps us study and do our homework.

🎙️ Imagine a car needing petrol to run. Our body needs food for energy! Without food, we would feel tired and wouldn't be able to play or learn properly. Food gives us the power to do everything.

Power Up!

Food Helps Us Grow

  • Food helps our bones and muscles grow strong.
  • It makes us taller and bigger every day.

🎙️ Look at how much you've grown since you were a baby! Food is like a magic potion that helps you grow. It builds new cells and makes your body parts bigger and stronger.

⬆️
Growing Tall

Food Keeps Us Healthy

  • Food protects us from falling sick.
  • It helps our body repair itself if we get a small cut.

🎙️ When we eat good food, our body becomes like a superhero, fighting off germs and illnesses. If you get a small bruise or cut, food helps your body heal quickly too.

❤️
Healthy Heart

Different Kinds of Food

  • Not all food is the same.
  • Each kind helps us in a special way.

🎙️ Just like there are different types of toys, there are different types of food. Each type has a unique job to do inside our body. Let's explore them!

Energy-Giving Foods

  • These foods give us power to work and play.
  • Examples: Rice, bread, potatoes, butter.

🎙️ Think of these as the fuel for your body. When you eat rice or a potato, you're filling up your energy tank to run and jump all day long!

🍞🍞🍞
Energy Boosters

Body-Building Foods

  • These foods help us grow strong muscles and bones.
  • Examples: Milk, eggs, pulses (dal), chicken.

🎙️ These foods are like the bricks and cement for your body. They help you grow tall and make your muscles and bones super strong. That's why milk is so important!

Our body has many organs working together.

Protective Foods

  • These foods keep us safe from illness and germs.
  • Examples: Fruits and green vegetables.

🎙️ These are your body's shield! Fruits and vegetables are full of good things that protect you from getting sick. Remember to eat your greens!

🥦🥦🥦
Germ Fighters

What is a Balanced Diet?

  • Eating the right amount of all food types.
  • It's like a superhero meal, giving you everything!

🎙️ A balanced diet means eating a little bit of energy food, body-building food, and protective food every day. It's like a perfect mix to keep you super healthy and strong.

⚖️
Balance is Key

  • Roti (energy), Dal (body-building), Sabzi (protective).
  • Add a little curd or salad for extra goodness!

🎙️ Look at your thali! It often has roti, dal, and sabzi. This is a great example of a balanced meal because it has foods from all the different groups we talked about.

Where Does Our Food Come From?

  • Many foods come from plants.
  • Fruits, vegetables, grains like wheat and rice.

🎙️ Think of an apple growing on a tree, or rice growing in a field. Many of our favourite foods start as tiny seeds and grow into big plants, giving us delicious food.

Plants make their own food from sunlight.

Food From Animals

  • Some foods we get from animals.
  • Milk, eggs, and meat come from animals.

🎙️ Cows give us milk, hens give us eggs, and some animals give us meat. These are also very important foods for our health and growth.

Animals live all around us.

Good Eating Habits

  • Always wash your hands before eating.
  • Chew your food slowly and properly.

🎙️ Having good eating habits is just as important as eating good food. Washing hands keeps away germs, and chewing slowly helps your stomach digest food better.

🧼
Clean Hands

More Good Eating Habits

  • Don't waste food, take only what you can eat.
  • Eat your meals on time every day.

🎙️ Many people don't have enough food, so we should never waste it. Also, eating at regular times helps your body stay healthy and energetic throughout the day.
