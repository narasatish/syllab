Food from Plants

  • Most of our food comes from plants.
  • Examples: Grains (rice, wheat), fruits, vegetables, nuts.

🎙️ Look around your kitchen! So many things like rotis, sabzi, and fruits come from plants. Plants are amazing because they make their own food using sunlight, and then we eat their parts.

Plants make their own food from sunlight.

Food from Animals

  • Some of our food we get from animals.
  • Examples: Milk, eggs, meat (chicken, fish).

🎙️ Animals also give us many yummy and healthy foods. Cows give us milk, hens give us eggs, and some animals give us meat. These are also important for our diet.

Animals live all around us.

Eat a Balanced Diet

  • Eat a mix of energy, body-building, and protective foods.
  • Don't eat too much of one type of food.

🎙️ A balanced diet means eating a little bit of everything. Your plate should have some dal, some roti, some sabzi, and maybe a fruit. This gives your body all it needs.

Good Food Habits

  • Wash your hands before and after eating.
  • Chew your food slowly and properly.
  • Do not waste food.

🎙️ These simple rules can make a big difference. Washing hands keeps away germs. Chewing properly helps digestion. And remembering not to waste food shows respect for farmers and resources.

Common Mistakes About Food

  • Eating only yummy junk food: It has little nutrition.
  • Not eating enough fruits and vegetables: You miss protective foods.
  • Wasting food: It's precious and takes effort to grow.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes. Remember, too much junk food isn't good. Also, don't skip your green veggies and colourful fruits. And always value the food on your plate.

Exam Tips: How Food Questions Appear

  • You might be asked to list food types or sources.
