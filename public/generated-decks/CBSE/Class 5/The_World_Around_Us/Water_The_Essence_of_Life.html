Why is Water So Important?

  • We need water to live.
  • Just like food and air, water keeps us healthy and alive.

🎙️ Imagine a day without water! It's impossible, right? Every living thing on Earth needs water to survive and grow. It's truly a gift of nature.

Water Helps Plants Grow Big and Strong

  • Plants drink water through their roots.
  • Water helps them make their own food.

🎙️ Look at the plants in your garden. They need water every day! When we water them, they stand tall and healthy. Without water, plants would dry up and die.

Plants make their own food from sunlight.

Animals Need Water Too!

  • Animals drink water to stay alive.
  • Some animals even live in water!

🎙️ Just like us, animals get thirsty. A dog drinks water from a bowl, and birds drink from puddles. Fish, turtles, and many other animals call water their home!

🎙️ Have you ever danced in the rain? Rain is super important because it brings fresh water to the Earth. It's like nature's way of refilling all our water bodies.

Ponds and Wells

  • Ponds are small water bodies.
  • Wells are deep holes dug to get groundwater.

🎙️ You might see ponds in villages or parks. Wells are like underground taps, where people draw water using buckets. These are important sources for many communities.

Why Should We Save Water?

  • Clean water is precious and limited.
  • If we waste it, there might not be enough for everyone.

🎙️ Water is a gift, but it's not endless. There is only a certain amount of fresh water on Earth. We must be careful not to waste it so that everyone has enough.

Water Goes Up! (Evaporation)

  • The sun heats water in rivers and lakes.
  • Water turns into vapour and goes into the sky.

🎙️ Have you ever seen water disappear from a wet floor? That's evaporation! The sun's heat turns water into an invisible gas called water vapour, which rises up.

⬆️
Water Vapour Rising
19 / 20

Water Comes Down! (Rain)

  • Vapour cools down and forms clouds (condensation).
  • Clouds get heavy and water falls as rain (precipitation).

🎙️ High up in the sky, the water vapour gets cold and turns back into tiny water droplets, forming clouds. When too many droplets gather, they fall back to Earth as rain.
