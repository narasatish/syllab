What is Water?

  • It has no smell or taste.
  • We cannot live without it!

🎙️ Look at this drop of water! It's clear, right? Water doesn't have a specific taste like juice or smell like flowers. But it's super important for us to drink every day.

  • You can see it above boiling water.

🎙️ If you boil water, you see white smoke coming out. That's steam! It's water, but so hot it has turned into a gas. It floats in the air.

The Water Cycle - Evaporation

  • The sun heats up water in rivers and oceans.
  • Water turns into vapor and goes up into the sky.
  • This is called evaporation.

🎙️ The sun is very powerful! It makes the water on Earth warm. This warm water then turns into tiny tiny drops, like a gas, and floats up into the sky. This is the first step of the water cycle.

1Sun heats water
2Water becomes vapor
3Vapor goes up
14 / 20

The Water Cycle - Condensation

  • High in the sky, the vapor gets cold.
  • It turns back into tiny water droplets.
  • These droplets join to form clouds.

🎙️ When the water vapor goes very high, it meets cold air. The vapor gets cold and changes back into tiny water drops. Many tiny drops together make a cloud!

1Vapor gets cold
2Forms tiny drops
3Makes clouds
15 / 20

The Water Cycle - Precipitation

  • When clouds become heavy, water falls back to Earth.
  • It falls as rain, snow, or hail.
  • This is called precipitation.

🎙️ When the clouds get too full of water droplets, they can't hold them anymore. So, the water falls down as rain. Sometimes, if it's very cold, it can fall as snow or hail. This completes the cycle!

1Clouds get heavy
2Water falls down
3As rain/snow

How Can We Save Water?

  • Close taps when not in use.
  • Take shorter baths.
  • Use a bucket instead of a shower.

🎙️ We can all help save water every day! Remember to turn off the tap while brushing your teeth. Taking a quick bath and using a bucket can also save a lot of water.
