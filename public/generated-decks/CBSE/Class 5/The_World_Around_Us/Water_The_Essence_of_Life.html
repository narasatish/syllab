Water for Plants to Grow

  • Plants need water to make their food.
  • Without water, plants cannot grow big and strong.

🎙️ Just like we eat food to grow, plants 'drink' water through their roots. This water helps them make their own food using sunlight. So, a plant needs both sun and water to thrive!

Rain, Rain, Go Away... Or Stay!

  • Rain is the main source of fresh water.
  • It fills up rivers, lakes, and ponds.

🎙️ When it rains, water falls from the sky. This rainwater is very important! It's like nature's way of refilling all the water bodies around us, making sure we always have enough.

🌧️
Falling Rain
11 / 24

Rivers, Lakes, and Ponds

  • These are natural water bodies.
  • Many cities get their water from rivers and lakes.

🎙️ After rain falls, much of it flows into rivers, collects in lakes, and sits in ponds. These are called surface water sources because they are on the surface of the Earth. They are very important for drinking and farming.

🌊
Water Bodies
12 / 24

Water Under the Ground!

  • Some rainwater seeps into the ground.
  • We dig wells or use handpumps to get this water.

🎙️ Not all rainwater stays on the surface. Some of it goes deep into the soil and gets stored underground. This is called groundwater. People use wells and borewells to bring this water up for use.

Solid Water: Ice!

  • When water gets very, very cold, it turns into ice.
  • Ice cubes in your drink are solid water.

🎙️ Think about putting water in the freezer. What happens? It becomes hard and cold, right? That's ice! This is the solid form of water. You can hold it in your hand.

Liquid Water: The Water We Drink!

  • This is the water that flows and we drink.
  • It takes the shape of the container it's in.

🎙️ The water you drink, bathe with, and see in rivers is liquid water. It's the most common form. If you pour it into a glass, it looks like a glass of water. If you pour it into a bottle, it looks like a bottle of water!

Gaseous Water: Water Vapour!

  • When water gets very hot, it turns into a gas.
  • We call this gas 'water vapour'.

🎙️ Have you seen steam rising from a hot cup of tea or boiling water? That's water turning into a gas! It's invisible, but it's still water, just in a different form, floating in the air.

Simple Ways to Save Water

  • Turn off the tap while brushing.
  • Use a bucket for bathing instead of a shower.
  • Report leaky taps at home or school.

🎙️ There are many easy ways we can save water every day. Just by making small changes, we can make a big difference. Remember, every drop saved is a drop earned!

Common Mistakes We Make

  • Leaving taps running unnecessarily.
  • Washing vehicles with a hosepipe.
  • Not fixing leaky pipes quickly.

🎙️ Sometimes, without realizing, we waste a lot of water. Forgetting to turn off the tap or letting water overflow can lead to huge wastage. Let's be mindful and avoid these mistakes.
