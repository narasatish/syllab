Meet Cotton!

  • Cotton comes from the cotton plant.
  • It grows in a fluffy ball, like white clouds.

🎙️ Let's start with cotton. Cotton is a plant fibre. If you visit a cotton field, you'll see beautiful white, fluffy balls on the plants. These are cotton bolls, ready to be picked!

Plants make their own food from sunlight.

From Plant to Yarn: Step 1 & 2

  • Step 1: Farmers pick cotton from plants.
  • Step 2: Seeds are removed from cotton (ginning).

🎙️ Making cotton cloth is a step-by-step process. First, farmers carefully pick the cotton. Then, machines or people remove the tiny seeds from the cotton fibres. This step is called ginning.

2Ginning

From Plant to Yarn: Step 3 & 4

  • Step 3: Cotton is spun into long threads called yarn.
  • Step 4: Yarn is woven into fabric on a loom.

🎙️ After ginning, the fluffy cotton is twisted and stretched into long, strong threads called yarn. This is called spinning. Finally, these yarns are crisscrossed on big machines called looms to make a flat piece of fabric, which is weaving.

Meet Wool!

  • Wool comes from sheep.
  • It is their thick, warm hair.

🎙️ Now, let's talk about wool. Wool comes from animals, mainly sheep! The thick, fluffy hair that covers a sheep's body is called its fleece, and that's where we get wool.

From Sheep to Sweater: Step 1 & 2

  • Step 1: Sheep's wool is cut off (shearing).
  • Step 2: The wool is washed to make it clean.

🎙️ Making woollen clothes also has many steps. First, the sheep's wool is carefully cut off. This is called shearing, and it doesn't hurt the sheep! Next, the wool is thoroughly washed to remove dirt and grease.

From Sheep to Sweater: Step 3 & 4

  • Step 3: Clean wool is sorted by quality.
  • Step 4: Wool is dyed in many colours.

🎙️ After washing, the wool is sorted into different piles based on its quality and texture. Then, if we want colourful sweaters, the wool is dyed using various colours. Imagine all the beautiful shades!

From Sheep to Sweater: Step 5 & 6

  • Step 5: Coloured wool is spun into yarn.
  • Step 6: Yarn is knitted to make sweaters and caps.

🎙️ Just like cotton, the wool fibres are then spun into yarn. Finally, this woollen yarn is used to knit warm sweaters, scarves, and caps. Knitting is like making loops with the yarn to create a fabric.

Meet Silk!

  • Silk is a very smooth and shiny fibre.
  • It comes from tiny silkworms.

🎙️ There's another amazing animal fibre called silk! Silk is known for being super smooth, soft, and shiny. It's often used for special clothes like saris and ties. And guess what makes it? Tiny silkworms!

How Silkworms Make Silk

  • Silkworms spin cocoons.
  • We get silk threads from these cocoons.

🎙️ Silkworms eat mulberry leaves and then spin a protective covering around themselves called a cocoon. This cocoon is made of a single, long silk thread! We carefully unroll these threads to get silk.

Let's Care for Our Clothes!

  • Wash clothes regularly to keep them clean.
  • Mend torn clothes.
  • Store them properly.
