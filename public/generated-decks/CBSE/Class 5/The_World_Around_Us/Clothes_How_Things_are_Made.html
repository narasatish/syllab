  • Some clothes are made in factories.
  • These are called synthetic materials.

🎙️ Scientists in factories create these materials using special processes. Synthetic clothes are often very strong, durable, and have unique properties.

Cotton's Journey: Growing

  • Farmers grow cotton plants in fields.
  • The plants produce fluffy white balls called bolls.

🎙️ Imagine a field full of white fluffy clouds! That's what a cotton field looks like when the bolls are ready to be harvested. It's a beautiful sight.

🌱
Cotton Seedling
9 / 20

Cotton's Journey: Picking

  • Workers pick the cotton bolls from the plants.
  • They separate the seeds from the soft cotton fiber.

🎙️ This process is called ginning, and it's very important. It makes sure that only the pure, soft cotton fibers are left to be made into thread.

Cotton's Journey: Spinning

  • Cotton fibers are twisted together tightly.
  • This makes long, strong threads called yarn.

🎙️ Think of how you twist strands of hair together to make a braid. It's a similar process to how these tiny cotton fibers are spun into a continuous, strong yarn.

🧵
Thread Spool
11 / 20

Cotton's Journey: Weaving

  • Yarn threads are woven together on a machine called a loom.
  • This creates a big piece of cotton fabric.

🎙️ Weaving is like making a mat by crossing threads over and under each other very carefully. This makes the strong, flat cloth we use for our clothes.

↔️
Interlacing

Wool's Journey: Shearing

  • Farmers gently cut the wool from sheep.
  • This process is called shearing.

🎙️ Don't worry, it doesn't hurt the sheep at all! It's just like getting a haircut for us, and the wool grows back again. It helps the sheep stay cool in summer too.

✂️
Shearing

Wool's Journey: Cleaning & Spinning

  • The wool is cleaned to remove dirt.
  • Then it is spun into soft, warm woollen yarn.

🎙️ The wool needs to be very clean and soft before it can be made into yarn. This yarn is then ready to become cozy sweaters and blankets.

🧼

Wool's Journey: Knitting

  • Woollen yarn is knitted together.
  • This makes warm sweaters, caps, and scarves.

🎙️ Knitting uses needles to make loops of yarn that join together. Maybe your grandma or someone you know enjoys knitting beautiful warm clothes!
