How Do We Know?

  • When a ship sails far, it slowly disappears.
  • Its bottom goes out of sight first.
  • Astronauts see it round from space!

🎙️ If Earth were flat, we would see the whole ship disappear at once. But because it's round, the ship goes over the curve of the Earth. Also, people who go to space can see our round Earth very clearly!
