What are Rhythms?

  • Rhythms are like a beat or a pattern.
  • They are things that happen regularly, again and again.
  • Like the tick-tock of a clock!

🎙️ Imagine your favourite song. It has a beat, right? That beat repeats. Rhythms in nature are similar – they are repeating patterns that nature follows.

Nature has its own Clock!

  • Nature follows a special clock, but without hands!
  • This clock tells plants and animals when to do things.
  • It helps everything stay in order.

🎙️ Just like we have a clock to tell us when to eat or sleep, nature has its own invisible clock. It makes sure everything happens at the right time.

Day and Night - Our First Rhythm

  • The most common rhythm we see is Day and Night.
  • It happens every single day, without fail!
  • Day brings light, and Night brings darkness.

🎙️ Look outside! Is it day or night? This change is the simplest and most important rhythm. It's like nature's switch for light and dark.

  • Birds sing, children play, and we go to school.
  • Plants make their food using sunlight.

What Happens During the Night?

  • The moon and stars appear.
  • Most people and animals sleep.

🎙️ As the day ends, night arrives. It's a time for rest and quiet. Look up at the sky at night – you can see the beautiful moon and twinkling stars!

🎙️ Animals are very smart! They know exactly when to wake up, when to find food, and when to prepare for cold weather, all thanks to nature's rhythms.

  • Plants open their leaves to the sun in the day.

🎙️ Plants also follow these natural beats. They know when to grow tall, when to make flowers, and when to rest. It's all part of the rhythm!
