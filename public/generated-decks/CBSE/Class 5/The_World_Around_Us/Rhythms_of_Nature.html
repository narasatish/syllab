5 / 24

Earth's Own Spin

  • This spinning movement is called 'Rotation'.
  • Earth takes 24 hours to complete one full spin.
  • This spin gives us day and night!

🎙️ Yes, our Earth is always spinning! One complete spin takes exactly one day, which is 24 hours. This constant spinning is what makes the sun appear to rise and set.

🌍
Earth Rotates
6 / 24

Sunlight and Shadow

  • One side of Earth faces the Sun: Day.
  • The other side is away from Sun: Night.

🎙️ Imagine a torch shining on a ball. Only the part of the ball facing the torch gets light. Similarly, the Sun shines on Earth, giving light to one half (day) and leaving the other half in darkness (night).

Seasons: Earth's Big Trip

  • Earth not only spins, it also goes around the Sun!
  • This journey around the Sun is called 'Revolution'.
  • It takes one whole year (365 days) for one revolution.

🎙️ Our Earth is a busy planet! While it spins, it also travels in a big circle around the Sun, like a car on a racetrack. This big trip takes a full year.

🪐
Earth's Orbit
9 / 24

Earth's Tilt & Seasons

  • Earth is slightly tilted, not straight up and down.
  • This tilt, combined with revolution, causes seasons.
  • Some parts get more direct sunlight at different times.

🎙️ This is the trickiest part! Imagine holding a ball slightly tilted and walking around a lamp. The tilted ball gets more light on different parts as it moves. That's how our Earth gets different seasons!

Sunlight on the Moon

  • The Moon itself does not produce light.
  • It only reflects sunlight, like a mirror.
  • As it moves, we see more or less of its lit side.

🎙️ The moon is like a giant ball in space. It doesn't glow on its own. It's actually reflecting the sun's light, just like a mirror. As the moon travels around Earth, we see different amounts of this reflected light.

New Moon: The Hidden Moon

  • The Moon is between the Earth and the Sun.
  • We cannot see the Moon at all or just a tiny sliver.
  • This night is called 'Amavasya'.

🎙️ Sometimes, the moon is completely dark in the sky. This is called a New Moon. It's because the side of the moon facing us is not lit by the sun.

🌑
Dark Night

Full Moon: The Bright Moon

  • The Earth is between the Sun and the Moon.
  • We see the entire side of the Moon lit up.
  • This bright night is called 'Purnima'.

🎙️ And then, there are nights when the moon is a beautiful, bright, full circle! This is the Full Moon. It's when we can see the entire sunlit face of the moon.

Steps of the Water Cycle

  • Evaporation: Water turns into vapour.
  • Condensation: Vapour forms clouds.
  • Precipitation: Water falls as rain/snow.
  • Collection: Water gathers in rivers/oceans.

🎙️ The water cycle has four main steps. Let's imagine water going on a trip! It starts from the ground, goes up to the sky, and then comes back down.

Watch Out! Common Mistakes

  • Confuse Rotation (day/night) with Revolution (seasons).
  • Think Moon makes its own light.
  • Forget Earth's 'tilt' for seasons.

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused with these big words. Remember, 'Rotation' is Earth spinning, like a top, giving us day and night. 'Revolution' is Earth going around the Sun, giving us seasons!
