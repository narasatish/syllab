How Far is Far? - Distance

  • Distance tells us how much space is between two places.
  • Like from your home to the park.
  • Or from your school to the market.

🎙️ When we travel, the first thing we often ask is, 'How far do we have to go?' This 'how far' is what we call distance. It's the length of the path between two points.

Metres and Kilometres: Friends!

  • 1 kilometre (km) is equal to 1000 metres (m).
  • Imagine 1000 little steps making one big jump!
  • So, 2 km is 2000 m.

🎙️ It's important to remember this connection. If you walk 1000 metres, you've walked 1 kilometre! They are different units but measure the same thing: distance.

Measuring Time: Hours and Minutes

  • We use 'hours' (hr) and 'minutes' (min) to measure time.
  • Your school day is measured in hours.
  • A short break is measured in minutes.

🎙️ We see hours and minutes on our clocks every day. They help us know when to do things, like when to wake up or when our favourite TV show starts.

Hours and Minutes: Their Link

  • 1 hour (hr) is equal to 60 minutes (min).
  • The minute hand on a clock takes 60 small steps to complete one hour.
  • So, 2 hours is 120 minutes.

🎙️ Just like metres and kilometres, hours and minutes are linked. Knowing this helps us calculate journey times and understand schedules better.

Watch Out! Common Mistakes

  • Confusing metres and kilometres (e.g., saying 100 m instead of 100 km for a city distance).
  • Not converting units when needed (e.g., adding hours and minutes directly).
  • Forgetting to write the correct unit (m, km, hr, min) in the answer.

🎙️ These are small errors that can change your answer completely. Always double-check your units and conversions to avoid these common mistakes.
