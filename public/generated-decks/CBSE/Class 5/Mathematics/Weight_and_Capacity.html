What is Weight?

  • Weight tells us how heavy something is.
  • It's how hard gravity pulls on an object.

🎙️ Imagine holding a feather and then holding a big stone. Which one feels heavier? That 'heaviness' is what we call weight. It's how much something pushes down.

How do we measure Weight?

  • We use special units to measure weight.
  • The main units are grams and kilograms.

🎙️ Just like we use 'metres' for length, we have specific words, or units, for weight. These units help us talk about how heavy things are in a clear way.

Kilograms (kg) for Heavy Things

  • Kilograms are used for heavier items.
  • Like a bag of rice or a big watermelon.

🎙️ When you go to the shop with your parents and buy a big bag of flour or a large pumpkin, their weight is usually measured in kilograms. Kilograms are for things that are quite heavy.

Grams (g) for Light Things

  • Grams are used for lighter items.
  • Like a few sweets or a pencil.

🎙️ Think about a small packet of biscuits or a few strawberries. These items are much lighter, so we use grams to measure them. One kilogram is equal to 1000 grams!

Adding Weights: Combining Heaviness

  • When we put things together, their weights add up.
  • Like putting two bags of groceries into one.

🎙️ Imagine you have a bag of apples and another bag of oranges. If you put them all into one big basket, the total weight will be the sum of the apple bag's weight and the orange bag's weight.

Let's Add Weights!

  • Sita bought 1 kg of potatoes and 2 kg of onions.
  • What is the total weight she bought?

🎙️ Here's a simple problem. Sita bought some vegetables. To find the total weight, we just add the individual weights together. So, 1 kg plus 2 kg gives us 3 kg.

1Potatoes: 1 kg
2Onions: 2 kg
3Total: 1 kg + 2 kg = 3 kg

Subtracting Weights: Taking Away Heaviness

  • When we use up some quantity, we subtract its weight.
  • Like eating some sweets from a packet.

🎙️ What if you have a big bag of rice, and you use some of it to cook dinner? The weight of the rice remaining in the bag will be less than what you started with. We subtract the used amount.

Let's Subtract Weights!

  • A bag of sugar weighs 5 kg. Mom used 2 kg.
  • How much sugar is left?

🎙️ Here's another example. If Mom starts with 5 kg of sugar and uses 2 kg, we take away the used amount from the total. So, 5 kg minus 2 kg leaves 3 kg of sugar.

1Total Sugar: 5 kg
2Used Sugar: 2 kg
3Left: 5 kg - 2 kg = 3 kg

What is Capacity?

  • Capacity tells us how much a container can hold.
  • It's about liquids, like water or milk.

🎙️ Now, let's talk about capacity! Imagine a glass and a big bucket. Both can hold water, but the bucket can hold much more. Capacity is about how much liquid a container can fit inside it.

Millilitres (mL) for Small Amounts

  • Millilitres are used for smaller amounts of liquid.
  • Like a spoon of medicine or a small juice box.

🎙️ For very small amounts of liquid, like the medicine you take with a spoon or a small juice box, we use millilitres. One litre is equal to 1000 millilitres.
