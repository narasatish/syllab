Finding Total Coconuts

  • Number of trees = 4
  • Coconuts per tree = 8

🎙️ That's right! We multiply the number of trees by the coconuts on each tree. So, 4 times 8 equals 32. Rohan has 32 coconuts!

Total Coconuts from Baskets

  • Coconuts in first basket = 15
  • Coconuts in second basket = 12
  • Total = 15 + 12 = 27

🎙️ Super! We add 15 and 12. First, add the ones place: 5 + 2 = 7. Then, add the tens place: 1 + 1 = 2. So, Rohan has 27 coconuts in total.

16 / 20

Remaining Coconuts

  • Total coconuts = 30
  • Coconuts sold = 18
  • Coconuts left = 30 - 18 = 12

🎙️ Great job! We subtract 18 from 30. First, we borrow from the tens place. 10 - 8 = 2. Then, 2 - 1 = 1. So, Rohan has 12 coconuts left.

1Had: 30
2Sold: 18
3Subtract: 30 - 18
4Left: 12 Coconuts
