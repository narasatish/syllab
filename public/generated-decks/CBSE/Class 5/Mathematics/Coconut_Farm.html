Example: Adding Harvest!

  • Rohan picked 25 coconuts on Monday.
  • He picked 30 coconuts on Tuesday.
  • How many coconuts did he pick in total?

🎙️ Let's try an example. If Rohan picked 25 coconuts on Monday and 30 on Tuesday, we add 25 and 30 together. We start by adding the ones place, then the tens place. The total is 55 coconuts.

125 coconuts (Monday)
2+ 30 coconuts (Tuesday)
3= 55 coconuts (Total)

Example: Coconuts Left!

  • Rohan had 55 coconuts.
  • He sold 20 coconuts.
  • How many coconuts are left with him?

🎙️ Imagine Rohan had 55 coconuts, and he sold 20 of them. We subtract 20 from 55. We subtract the ones, then the tens. He is left with 35 coconuts. Good job!

155 total coconuts
2- 20 coconuts sold
3= 35 coconuts left

Example: Sharing Coconuts!

🎙️ If Rohan has 20 coconuts and wants to share them among his 4 family members, we divide 20 by 4. Each family member will get 5 coconuts. Division helps us distribute things fairly.

120 coconuts

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Not reading the question carefully.
  • Making calculation errors (e.g., carrying/borrowing).
  • Using the wrong operation (+, -, x, ÷).

🎙️ Sometimes we make small errors. Always read the question twice to understand what it's asking. Be very careful with your addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division steps. And make sure you choose the right math operation for the problem!
