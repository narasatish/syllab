The Compass Rose: Your Direction Helper!

  • Maps have a special drawing called a Compass Rose.
  • It shows us all the main directions.
  • It usually has an arrow pointing North (N).

🎙️ Look closely at a map, and you'll often see a star-like symbol with letters N, S, E, W. This is called a Compass Rose. It's like a mini compass right on your map, helping you find North, and then all other directions!

Map Scale: Small Drawing, Big Distance!

  • A map is a small drawing of a big place.
  • The 'Scale' tells us how much smaller it is.
  • 1 cm on map could be 1 km in real life!

🎙️ You can't draw your whole city on a piece of paper in real size, right? So, maps use a scale. It's like saying, 'Every little bit on this map means a much bigger distance in the real world.' It helps us guess how far things actually are.

Map Symbols: The Map's Secret Pictures!

  • Maps use small pictures called Symbols.
  • A symbol can mean a school, a hospital, or a tree.
  • They make maps easy to read without writing many words.

🎙️ Instead of writing 'school' or drawing a big school building, maps use tiny pictures or symbols. For example, a little cross might mean a hospital, and a tiny tree might mean a forest. It's like a secret code!

The Map Key: Unlocking the Symbols!

  • How do we know what each symbol means?
  • We look at the Map Key, also called the Legend.
  • The key is like a dictionary for map symbols!

🎙️ If symbols are like a secret code, then the Map Key is the book that tells you what each code means! It's usually in a corner of the map, showing each symbol and its meaning. Always check the key first!
