Map Scale: Shrinking Things!

  • Maps shrink everything down.
  • 1 cm on the map might mean 1 km in real life!
  • This is called the 'scale' of the map.

🎙️ Imagine you have a big toy car, and then a tiny toy car that looks just like it. The tiny car is a 'scaled-down' version. Maps do the same thing! They use a scale to show big distances in a small way.

📏
Measuring distances

Using the Scale

  • If 1 cm on your map is 1 km in real life.
  • And the distance to the park is 3 cm on the map.
  • How far is the park in real life? (3 km!)

🎙️ Let's try an example! If your map says 1 cm on the map is equal to 1 km in the real world, and you measure 3 cm to the park, then the park is 3 kilometers away! Isn't that neat?

2Real: 1 km
