Finding North with the Sun

  • The Sun rises in the East.
  • It sets in the West.
  • If you face the rising sun, North is to your left.

🎙️ Do you know how to find North without a compass? The sun is our friend! If you stand facing where the sun comes up in the morning (East), then North will be on your left hand side. Try it!

What is Map Scale?

  • Maps are small, but real places are big!
  • Scale tells us how much smaller the map is.

🎙️ A map of your city can fit on one page, but the city itself is huge! How does that work? This is where 'scale' comes in. It's like shrinking everything down in a special way so it fits on the paper.

Understanding Map Scale

  • If a map says '1 cm = 1 km'.
  • It means 1 cm on the map is actually 1 km in real life!

🎙️ Let's take an example: If your map says '1 cm equals 1 kilometer', it means if you measure 1 centimeter on the map, in the real world, that distance is 1 whole kilometer! Isn't that amazing?
