Converting Distance: Metres and Kilometres

  • 1 kilometre (km) is equal to 1000 metres (m).
  • So, 2 km is 2000 m, and 5000 m is 5 km.

🎙️ It's important to know how metres and kilometres are related. One kilometre is the same as one thousand metres. This conversion helps us understand distances better, whether they are small or large.

How Long Does It Take? - Understanding Time

  • From when you start to when you reach your destination.

🎙️ Just like distance, time is very important when we travel. It tells us how many hours or minutes we will spend on our journey. We use clocks to measure time.

Units of Time: Hours and Minutes

  • We use 'hours' (hr) and 'minutes' (min) for travel time.

🎙️ For travel, we usually talk about time in hours and minutes. A short bus ride might be 30 minutes, while a train journey could be 5 hours. Always remember the key conversion: 1 hour equals 60 minutes.

Calculating Travel Duration

  • If a train starts at 9:00 AM and reaches at 11:00 AM,
  • It travels for 2 hours (11:00 AM - 9:00 AM).

🎙️ To find out how long a journey took, we simply subtract the starting time from the ending time. If your school bus picks you up at 7:00 AM and you reach school at 7:30 AM, your journey is 30 minutes long.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Adding Travel Times

  • If you travel 1 hour 30 minutes to a hill station,
  • And then 45 minutes more to a viewpoint.
  • Total time: 1 hour 30 min + 45 min = 2 hours 15 minutes.

🎙️ Sometimes, a journey has different parts. We need to add up the times for each part. Remember that once your minutes reach 60, they become another hour! So 75 minutes is 1 hour and 15 minutes.

Cost of Travel: Buying Tickets

  • We pay 'money' to travel by bus, train, or flight.
  • This money is for tickets or fares.

🎙️ Travelling usually costs money. We buy tickets to ride buses, trains, or planes. The money we pay is called the fare. It helps cover the cost of the vehicle and its journey.

The rupee (₹) is our money.

Calculating Total Travel Cost

  • One bus ticket costs ₹50.
  • For a family of 4, total cost is ₹50 x 4 = ₹200.
  • Return journey would be ₹200 x 2 = ₹400.

🎙️ To find the total cost, we multiply the cost of one ticket by the number of people travelling. If you also need to come back, you double the cost for a return journey. It's simple multiplication!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting to write units (m, km, hr, min, ₹).
  • Not converting minutes to hours correctly (e.g., 70 min is 1 hr 10 min, not 1.7 hr).
  • Simple calculation errors in multiplication or subtraction.

🎙️ It's easy to make small mistakes. Always remember to write the correct units with your answers. Also, be careful when converting time; 60 minutes makes an hour, not 100! Double-check your calculations.
