🎙️ When we travel, one important thing to know is 'how far' we are going. This 'how far' is called distance. Just like we use a ruler to measure a line, we use other tools and units to measure how far places are from each other.

Metres: For Short Distances

  • Or the distance you run in the playground.

🎙️ Imagine you are walking across your classroom. The distance you cover can be measured in metres. Metres are good for measuring things that are not too long, like a cricket pitch or the height of a tree.

Kilometres: For Long Journeys

  • We use 'kilometres' for very long distances.
  • Like the distance between two cities.
  • Or from your city to a hill station.

🎙️ Now, if you want to travel from Delhi to Mumbai, metres would be too small to measure! For such long distances, we use a bigger unit called kilometres. Kilometres help us measure how far different cities or towns are from each other.

Metres and Kilometres are Friends!

  • 1 kilometre is equal to 1000 metres.
  • Think of 1000 small metre steps making one big kilometre jump!
  • So, 2 km is 2000 m, and 5 km is 5000 m.

🎙️ Metres and kilometres are related! One thousand metres make up one kilometre. This is a very important relationship to remember. It helps us convert between these units when we are talking about distances.

Units of Time: Hours and Minutes

  • We measure short durations in 'minutes'.
  • We measure longer durations in 'hours'.
  • Remember, 1 hour = 60 minutes.

🎙️ Just like metres and kilometres for distance, we have minutes and hours for time. A minute is a short time, like the time it takes to brush your teeth. An hour is longer, like the time you spend playing outside. Knowing their relationship is key!

Example: Bus Ride Time

  • A bus leaves City A at 9:00 AM.
  • It reaches City B at 11:00 AM.
  • How long was the bus journey?

🎙️ To find out how long the bus journey was, we just count the hours from the start time to the end time. From 9 AM to 10 AM is 1 hour, and from 10 AM to 11 AM is another hour. So, the total journey was 2 hours. Simple, right?

1Start: 9:00 AM
2End: 11:00 AM
3Duration: 2 hours

Example: Buying Bus Tickets

  • One bus ticket costs ₹50.
  • How much will 3 tickets cost?
  • We multiply: ₹50 x 3 = ₹150.

🎙️ Let's say you and your two friends want to go on a bus trip. If one ticket is ₹50, how do we find the total cost for all three of you? We simply multiply the cost of one ticket by the number of tickets. ₹50 times 3 is ₹150. Easy peasy!

Planning a Trip: Total Distance

  • Sometimes, a journey has many parts.
  • From Home to Bus Stop: 1 km.
  • From Bus Stop to Market: 5 km.
  • Total distance = 1 km + 5 km = 6 km.

🎙️ When we plan a trip, we often travel in stages. We might walk to the bus stop, then take a bus, then perhaps a rickshaw. To find the total distance, we simply add up the distances of each part of the journey.

1Home to Bus Stop (1 km)
2Bus Stop to Market (5 km)
3Total Journey (6 km)

Planning a Trip: Total Time

  • Journey Part 1: 15 minutes.
  • Journey Part 2: 45 minutes.
  • Total time = 15 min + 45 min = 60 minutes.
  • That's 1 hour!

🎙️ Just like distances, we can also add up the time taken for different parts of a journey. If the first part takes 15 minutes and the second part takes 45 minutes, together that's 60 minutes. And we know 60 minutes is equal to 1 hour!

1Part 1 (15 min)
2Part 2 (45 min)
3Total (60 min / 1 hr)
