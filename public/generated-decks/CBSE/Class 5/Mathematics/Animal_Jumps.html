What is a Jump?

  • Animals jump to move around, catch food, or escape.
  • Think about when you jump during play!

🎙️ A jump is like taking a quick flight through the air. Just like you jump when you play or skip, animals also jump for many reasons. It helps them move quickly from one place to another.

Measuring How Far Animals Jump

  • How do we know how far an animal jumps?
  • We use a special tool called a measuring tape or ruler.
  • This helps us find the 'length' or 'distance' of the jump.

🎙️ To find out exactly how far an animal jumps, we need to measure it. We use tools like a measuring tape, just like we measure our height or the length of a table. This measurement tells us the distance covered by the jump.

Meters and Centimeters: Our Measuring Friends

  • We measure length in 'centimeters' (cm) and 'meters' (m).
  • 1 meter is equal to 100 centimeters.
  • A meter is longer, like the length of a big step.

🎙️ When we measure, we use special units. For animal jumps, we often use centimeters for shorter jumps and meters for longer ones. Remember, 1 meter is the same as 100 centimeters, like 100 small steps make one big step!

Worked Example 2: How Much Farther?

  • A rabbit jumps 3 meters.
  • A cat jumps 2 meters.

🎙️ Let's solve another problem. If a rabbit jumps 3 meters and a cat jumps 2 meters, we want to find the difference. We subtract the smaller number from the larger one: 3 - 2 = 1 meter. So the rabbit jumps 1 meter farther.

Worked Example 3: Total Jumps

  • A frog takes 2 jumps.
  • Each jump is 1 meter long.
  • Total distance jumped = 1m + 1m = 2 meters.

🎙️ If our frog takes two jumps, and each jump is 1 meter long, what is the total distance? We add the length of each jump: 1 meter + 1 meter equals 2 meters. The frog covered a total of 2 meters.
