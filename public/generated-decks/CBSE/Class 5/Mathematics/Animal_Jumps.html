Problem: Combined Jumps!

  • Froggy jumps 2 times.
  • Then Bunny jumps 1 time.
  • What is the total number of steps covered?

🎙️ Let's solve a problem together! First, Froggy jumps 2 times (2 x 1 step = 2 steps). Then, Bunny jumps 1 time (1 x 2 steps = 2 steps). To find the total, we add Froggy's steps and Bunny's steps: 2 + 2 = 4 steps. Great job!

Problem: Who Jumps More?

  • Kangaroo jumps 2 times.
  • Froggy jumps 8 times.
  • Who covered more total steps?

🎙️ This is a good comparison problem! Kangaroo jumps 2 times, each 5 steps, so that's 2 x 5 = 10 steps. Froggy jumps 8 times, each 1 step, so that's 8 x 1 = 8 steps. Clearly, Kangaroo covered more total steps this time!
