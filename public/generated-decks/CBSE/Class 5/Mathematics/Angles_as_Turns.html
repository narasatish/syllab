What is a Turn?

  • A turn is when something moves around a point.
  • Like when you spin around in a circle!

🎙️ Imagine you are standing in one place and you just spin around. That spinning movement is what we call a 'turn'. It's like changing the direction you are facing.

Where Does a Turn Start?

  • Every turn has a beginning.
  • It's like where you stand before you spin.

🎙️ Before you start spinning or moving, you are at a certain place, right? That's your starting point. A turn always begins from somewhere.

Where Does a Turn Stop?

  • A turn also has an end.
  • It's where you stop spinning or moving.

🎙️ After you spin, you stop facing a new direction. That new direction or position is where your turn ends. So, a turn has both a start and an end.

Full Turn: All the Way Around!

  • You spin completely around.
  • You face the same direction again, like a full circle.

🎙️ Imagine you are facing the blackboard. If you spin around and around until you are facing the blackboard again, you have made a full turn! You completed a whole circle.

⭕
Complete circle
7 / 20

Half Turn: Halfway Around!

  • You spin halfway around.
  • You face the opposite direction from where you started.

🎙️ Now, if you are facing the blackboard and you only spin halfway, you will be facing the wall behind you. That's a half turn! It's exactly half of a full turn.

A pizza cut into slices shows fractions — each slice is a part of the whole!

Quarter Turn: A Small Turn!

  • You spin a small part, like a corner.
  • It's like turning to your side.

🎙️ What if you just turn a little bit, like from facing the blackboard to facing the window on your right? That's a quarter turn! It's one-fourth of a full turn.

Clockwise Turn: Like a Clock!

  • Turns in the same direction as clock hands move.
  • From 12 to 3, then 6, then 9.

🎙️ Look at a clock! The hands of the clock always move in a special direction: from the 12 to the 1, then 2, then 3, and so on. This movement is called a 'clockwise turn'.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Anti-Clockwise Turn: Opposite of Clock!

  • Turns in the opposite direction of clock hands.
  • From 12 to 9, then 6, then 3.

🎙️ Now, imagine the clock hands moving backward! From 12 to 11, then 10, then 9. This opposite movement is called an 'anti-clockwise turn'. It's just the reverse direction.

Turns Make Angles!

  • When we make a turn, we create an angle.
  • Angles show how much something has turned from its start.

🎙️ This is the most important part! Every time you make a turn, you are actually creating something called an 'angle'. An angle is just a way to measure how much you have turned from your starting point.
