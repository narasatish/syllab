What is a Turn?

  • Imagine you are standing and looking straight ahead.
  • When you change the direction you are facing, you make a turn!

🎙️ A turn is simply a change in direction. If you're looking at the board and then you look at your friend next to you, you've made a turn! It's that simple.

The Half Turn

  • If you turn from looking forward to looking completely backward.
  • You have made a 'half turn'!

🎙️ Think about a clock. If the minute hand moves from 12 to 6, it has made a half turn. You've turned exactly halfway around, facing the opposite direction from where you started.

The Quarter Turn

  • If you turn from looking forward to looking to your side (left or right).
  • This is a 'quarter turn'!

🎙️ A quarter turn is like moving just one part of a full circle. Imagine you're standing, and you just turn to look at the wall to your left or right. That's a quarter turn.

➡️
Turning to the Side
8 / 24

Which Way to Turn?

  • Like a clock's hands, we can turn 'clockwise' (to the right side).
  • Or 'anti-clockwise' (to the left side).

🎙️ It's important to know the direction of your turn! 'Clockwise' is the way the hands of a clock move. 'Anti-clockwise' is the opposite direction. Always remember this!

The Full Turn

  • If you turn all the way around, coming back to where you started.
  • This is a 'full turn'!

🎙️ A full turn means you've completed a whole circle. You start at one point, turn completely around, and end up facing the exact same direction you started in. Like a spinning top!

How Turns Connect

  • Two quarter turns make a half turn.
  • Four quarter turns make a full turn.

🎙️ It's like building with blocks! If you do two quarter turns, you've turned halfway. If you do four quarter turns, you've completed a full circle and are back where you began!

Turns are Angles!

  • Every time you make a turn, you are creating an angle!
  • Angles measure how much something has turned.

🎙️ This is the most important part! Turns are not just movements; they are how we understand angles. An angle tells us 'how much' of a turn has happened from one direction to another.

Meet the Right Angle!

  • A quarter turn is also called a 'Right Angle'.
  • It looks like the perfect corner of a book or a window frame.

🎙️ Look around your classroom! The corner of your desk, the corner of the door, the corner of your book – all these are examples of right angles. They are made by a quarter turn.

📖

The Straight Angle

  • A half turn is also called a 'Straight Angle'.
  • It makes a perfectly straight line!

🎙️ Imagine a straight road. That's a straight angle! When you do a half turn, you face the exact opposite direction, creating a straight line. It's like opening a book completely flat.

➖
Flat Line

The Complete Angle

  • A full turn is also called a 'Complete Angle'.
  • You turn all the way around, making a full circle!

🎙️ When you spin around completely, you've made a complete angle. It's a full circle, and you end up exactly where you started, facing the same way. Think of a merry-go-round!

Watch Out for These!

  • Confusing clockwise and anti-clockwise turns.
  • Not remembering where you started turning from.
  • Mixing up half turns with quarter turns.

🎙️ Sometimes students get confused. Always remember the clock for clockwise! And always imagine where you started your turn, that's your starting line for the angle.
