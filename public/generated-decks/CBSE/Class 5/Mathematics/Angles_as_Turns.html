What is a Turn?

  • A turn means moving around a point.
  • Like spinning in a circle!

🎙️ Imagine you are standing in one place and you spin around. That's a turn! When something moves around a fixed point, we call it a turn. It's like a merry-go-round!

Clock Hands Turn

  • Clock hands move around the center.
  • They make different turns to show time.

🎙️ Look at a clock! The short hand and the long hand both move around the middle point. They keep turning and turning to show us the time. This is a perfect example of turns in our daily life.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Door Opens with a Turn

  • Your door swings open and shut.
  • It turns around its hinges.

🎙️ When you open your room door, it doesn't just slide. It swings open! It turns around the hinges attached to the wall. This is another turn we see every day.

Birds have feathers and can fly.

Steering Wheel Turns

  • A car's steering wheel turns.
  • It helps the car change direction!

🎙️ Have you ever seen an adult drive a car? They turn the steering wheel to make the car go left or right. The steering wheel makes turns to guide the car's path. So many turns around us!

What is an Angle?

  • An angle is made when two lines meet.
  • It shows how much 'turn' there is.

🎙️ Now, let's connect turns to something new: angles! When two lines or rays meet at a point, they form an angle. This angle tells us how much one line has turned from the other. It's like measuring the 'openness' of a turn.

📐
Measuring Turns

Angles are Made by Turns

  • The amount of 'opening' is the angle.
  • More turn means a bigger angle.

🎙️ Think about your door again. When it's fully closed, there's no turn. When you open it a little, it makes a small turn and a small angle. If you open it wide, it makes a bigger turn and a bigger angle!

Full Turn: All the Way Around!

  • One complete spin is a full turn.
  • You end up facing the same way!

🎙️ Imagine you are standing and you spin around completely until you are facing the exact same direction again. That's a full turn! It's like going all the way around a circle. This is the biggest turn we can make!

⭕
Complete Circle

Half Turn: Facing Opposite

  • A half turn is spinning halfway.
  • You face the opposite direction.

🎙️ Now, imagine you spin only halfway. If you were facing forward, after a half turn, you would be facing backward! It's like turning from 12 o'clock to 6 o'clock on a clock. You've made half a circle.

A pizza cut into slices shows fractions — each slice is a part of the whole!

Quarter Turn: A Small Turn

  • A quarter turn is a small turn.
  • Like turning from 12 to 3 on a clock.

🎙️ What if you make an even smaller turn? A quarter turn is like turning just a little bit. If you were facing forward, you would now be facing your side, either left or right. Think of turning from 12 o'clock to 3 o'clock on a clock face.

Comparing Our Turns

  • Full turn is the biggest.
  • Half turn is smaller than full.
  • Quarter turn is the smallest of these.

🎙️ Let's compare these turns. A full turn is like taking 4 steps. A half turn is like taking 2 steps. And a quarter turn is like taking just 1 step. See how they are different sizes?
