What is "Near"?

  • Near means very close to us.
  • We can reach it easily.

🎙️ Look around you! What things are very close to you right now? Maybe your book, your pencil, or your friend sitting next to you. 'Near' means something is just a short distance away, easy to get to.

  • It takes time or travel to reach.

How Do We Measure Far and Near?

  • We use special tools to measure distance.
  • These tools help us know exactly 'how far'.

🎙️ When we want to know exactly how far or how near something is, we don't just guess. We use special tools, just like we use scales to measure weight. These tools give us exact numbers!

Our Small Steps: Centimetres and Metres

  • For small distances, we use centimetres (cm).
  • For slightly bigger distances, we use metres (m).

🎙️ Imagine measuring your pencil. It's short, so we use centimetres. Now, imagine measuring the length of your classroom. It's bigger, so we use metres. One metre is like taking a big step!

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

Our Big Steps: Kilometres

  • For very long distances, we use kilometres (km).
  • Kilometres help us measure travel between cities.

🎙️ What if you want to know the distance from your city to another city? Or how far the moon is? For these very, very long distances, we use kilometres. One kilometre is like walking for a long time!

When to use Metre (m)?

  • Length of a room.
  • Height of a tree.
  • Distance you jump.

🎙️ We use metres for things we can easily see and walk around. Like measuring how long your classroom is, or how tall a small tree is outside. Even how far you can jump!

When to use Kilometre (km)?

  • Distance from your home to school.
  • Distance between two towns.
  • Length of a long road.

🎙️ Kilometres are for big journeys! When your parents drive you to school, or when you go on a trip to visit relatives in another city, we measure those distances in kilometres. It's for when things are really far.

Quick Question 3: Shorter or Longer?

  • Path 1 is 50 metres. Path 2 is 70 metres.
  • Which path is shorter?

🎙️ Look at the numbers. 50 is smaller than 70. So, Path 1 is shorter. This means if you walk Path 1, you will reach faster!

Estimating Distances

  • Sometimes, we don't need exact measurements.
  • We can make a smart guess, called an 'estimate'.

🎙️ Imagine you want to know if a shop is far enough to need a bicycle. You don't need to measure it exactly. You can just make a smart guess, or 'estimate', based on what you see. This helps us make quick decisions.
