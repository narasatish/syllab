What is Distance?

  • It's the space between two points.
  • How much gap is there?
  • Like from your hand to your foot.

🎙️ Imagine you are standing in one place, and your friend is standing somewhere else. The space between you and your friend is called distance. It tells us how far apart you both are.

Connecting Units: Kilometres and Metres

  • 1 Kilometre (km) = 1000 Metres (m).
  • This is a very important connection!
  • It helps us compare distances easily.

🎙️ Imagine 1000 small steps make one big journey. That's how 1000 metres make 1 kilometre. Always remember this magic number!

Let's Convert! (km to m)

  • How many metres are in 3 km?
  • We know 1 km = 1000 m.
  • So, 3 km = 3 x 1000 m = 3000 m.

🎙️ It's like having 3 baskets, and each basket has 1000 fruits. How many fruits in total? Same idea here!

1Start with km
2Multiply by 1000
3Get metres

Let's Convert! (m to km)

  • How many kilometres are in 5000 m?
  • We know 1000 m = 1 km.
  • So, 5000 m = 5000 ÷ 1000 km = 5 km.

🎙️ If you have a big number of metres, you can group them into thousands to find out how many kilometres it makes. It's like sharing a big pile of sweets among 1000 friends.

1Start with metres
2Divide by 1000
3Get kilometres
