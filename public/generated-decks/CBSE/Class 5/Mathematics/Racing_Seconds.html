Let's Convert: Minutes to Seconds

  • Problem: How many seconds are in 2 minutes?
  • We know: 1 minute = 60 seconds.
  • So: 2 minutes = 2 x 60 seconds = 120 seconds.

🎙️ To change minutes into seconds, we just multiply the number of minutes by 60. It's like saying, 'I have 2 groups of 60 seconds each!'

1Start with minutes
2Multiply by 60
3Get seconds

Let's Convert: Hours to Minutes

  • Problem: How many minutes are in 3 hours?
  • We know: 1 hour = 60 minutes.
  • So: 3 hours = 3 x 60 minutes = 180 minutes.

🎙️ Similarly, to change hours into minutes, we multiply the number of hours by 60. It's a simple rule to remember for time conversions!

2Multiply by 60
3Get minutes
