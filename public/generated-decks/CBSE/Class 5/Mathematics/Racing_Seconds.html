Minutes to Seconds: Multiply!

  • To change minutes into seconds,
  • we multiply the number of minutes by 60.

🎙️ This is a very important rule! Whenever you have minutes and you want to know how many seconds are in them, just take the number of minutes and multiply it by 60. Simple!
