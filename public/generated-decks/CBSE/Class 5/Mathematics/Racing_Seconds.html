Adding Time: With Carry Over

  • If seconds total more than 60, subtract 60.
  • Add 1 to the minutes column (because 60 seconds = 1 minute!).

🎙️ What if your seconds add up to more than 60? We can't have 70 seconds! We know 60 seconds make 1 minute. So, we 'carry over' 1 minute to the minutes column and keep only the remaining seconds. This is similar to carrying over tens in regular addition.

1Total Seconds > 60
2- 60 seconds
3+ 1 minute (carry over)
18 / 24

Example: Time Addition with Carry Over

  • Add 12 minutes 45 seconds and 8 minutes 25 seconds.
  • Seconds: 45 + 25 = 70. (70 - 60 = 10 seconds, carry 1 minute).
  • Minutes: 12 + 8 + 1 (carried) = 21 minutes. Total: 21 minutes 10 seconds.

🎙️ Let's solve this! Add the seconds: 45 + 25 = 70 seconds. Since 70 is more than 60, we take out 60 seconds (which is 1 minute). So, 70 - 60 = 10 seconds left. We carry over 1 minute. Now add minutes: 12 + 8 + (carried 1) = 21 minutes. So, the total is 21 minutes 10 seconds.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Forgetting 60 is the magic number (not 100) for time.
  • Adding seconds and minutes directly without separating.

🎙️ Be careful! The biggest mistake is forgetting that time works with 60, not 100. So, when seconds reach 60, it becomes 1 minute, not when it reaches 100. Also, always add seconds with seconds and minutes with minutes separately to avoid confusion.
