Buying More Milk

  • One litre of milk costs ₹50.
  • How much will 3 litres cost?

🎙️ This is a simple calculation. If one litre is ₹50, and you want 3 litres, you multiply the price by the quantity. So, 3 times 50 gives us 150 rupees.

1Cost of 1 L = ₹50
2For 3 L, multiply: 3 x ₹50
3Total Cost = ₹150
