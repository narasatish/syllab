Example: Total Milk

  • Morning: 12 liters
  • Evening: 15 liters
  • Total Milk = ?

🎙️ If Farmer Ramu gets 12 liters in the morning and 15 liters in the evening, we add them. First, add 2 and 5 to get 7. Then, add 1 and 1 to get 2. So, 12 + 15 equals 27 liters!

1Write numbers one below other
2Add the ones place (2+5=7)
3Add the tens place (1+1=2)
4Total milk is 27 liters

  • How much for 4 liters?

🎙️ To find out the total cost for many liters, we multiply! If one liter is ₹50, and you want 4 liters, you multiply ₹50 by 4. Let's practice this.

✖️
Multiplication Sign
13 / 20

Example: Total Money

  • Price per liter: ₹50
  • Quantity: 4 liters
  • Total Money = ?

🎙️ If one liter is ₹50, and we buy 4 liters, we multiply 50 by 4. First, 4 times 0 is 0. Then, 4 times 5 is 20. So, 50 multiplied by 4 is ₹200. We need ₹200 for 4 liters of milk.

1Write numbers (50 x 4)
2Multiply ones place (4x0=0)
3Multiply tens place (4x5=20)
4Total money is ₹200
