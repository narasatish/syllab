Flat Shapes: The Square

Flat Shapes: The Rectangle

Symmetry: A Balanced Look

  • Symmetry means one half mirrors the other.
  • If you fold it, both sides match perfectly.
  • Like a butterfly's wings or your face!

🎙️ Symmetry is a fancy word for balance. If an object is symmetrical, you can draw a line through it, and both sides will look exactly the same, like a mirror image. A butterfly's wings are a beautiful example of symmetry.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing 2D (flat) with 3D (solid) shapes.
  • Not finding the 'rule' in a pattern.
  • Thinking all shapes are symmetrical.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes. Remember, a square drawn on paper is 2D, but a dice is a 3D cube. Always look for the rule that makes a pattern, and remember, not every shape or object is symmetrical. Practice makes perfect!

Exam Tips for Shapes and Patterns

  • Draw shapes clearly and label them.
  • Identify the rule for number/shape patterns.
  • Practice drawing lines of symmetry.

🎙️ In your exams, you might be asked to draw shapes, complete patterns, or identify symmetrical objects. Always draw neatly, clearly state the rule you found in a pattern, and practice drawing the line that makes an object symmetrical. You've got this!
