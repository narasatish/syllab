Simple Repeating Patterns

  • This pattern goes: Apple, Banana, Apple, Banana...
  • The 'AB' part repeats.
  • What comes next? An apple!

🎙️ See how the apple and banana take turns? This is an ABAB pattern. One thing, then another, then the first thing again. It's easy to guess what comes next!

A Little More Complex Pattern

  • This pattern goes: Blue, Blue, Red, Red...
  • The 'AABB' part repeats.
  • What comes after two reds? Two blues!

🎙️ Here, two blue balls are followed by two red balls. Then it repeats! This is an AABBAABB pattern. Can you make a sound pattern like this?
