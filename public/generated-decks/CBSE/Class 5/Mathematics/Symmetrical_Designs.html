Steps to Draw Symmetry!

  • Count squares from the line.
  • Draw points at the same distance.
  • Join the points carefully.

🎙️ Here's a trick to draw symmetrical shapes easily, especially on grid paper. First, count how many squares away a point is from the line of symmetry. Then, mark a new point the same distance away on the other side. Finally, connect all your new points!
