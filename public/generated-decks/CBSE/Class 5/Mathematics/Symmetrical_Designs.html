What is Symmetry?

  • It means one side is exactly like the other side.
  • If you fold it, both halves match perfectly!

🎙️ Imagine cutting a shape in half. If both halves are exactly the same, like two identical twins, then the shape is symmetrical. It's like having a perfect match!

Your Face is Symmetrical!

  • If you imagine a line down the middle of your face, both sides look similar.
  • One eye is like the other, one ear like the other!

🎙️ Now, look at your friend, or even yourself in a mirror! If you draw an imaginary line right down the middle of your face, from your forehead to your chin, you'll see that your left side looks very much like your right side. Your body has symmetry too!

The Magic Line of Symmetry

  • This is an imaginary line that divides a shape into two identical halves.
  • It's like the fold line on a piece of paper.

🎙️ Every symmetrical shape has a special line called the 'Line of Symmetry'. This line is like a magic mirror that shows you where to split the shape so both parts are exactly the same. We can draw it with a ruler!

Shapes are all around us.

Let's Fold Paper!

  • Take a piece of paper and cut out a shape.
  • If you fold it and both halves match perfectly, it's symmetrical!

🎙️ Here's a fun way to test for symmetry! Cut out a shape, then try to fold it in half. If the two sides perfectly overlap and match, then your fold line is a line of symmetry! If they don't match, it's not symmetrical along that line.

  • A square has many lines of symmetry.
  • You can fold it in different ways and still get matching halves.

🎙️ Let's look at a square. You can fold it in half vertically, horizontally, and even diagonally, and each time the halves will match perfectly! A square is a very symmetrical shape, having four lines of symmetry.

Symmetry in a Rectangle

  • A rectangle also has lines of symmetry.
  • Can you guess how many?

🎙️ Now, think about a rectangle. Can you fold it in half horizontally? Yes! How about vertically? Yes! But can you fold it diagonally like a square and make the sides match? No, not perfectly. So, a rectangle has two lines of symmetry.

Symmetry in a Circle

  • A circle has endless lines of symmetry!
  • Any line that passes through its center is a line of symmetry.

🎙️ What about a circle? This is super interesting! You can draw a line through the center of a circle in any direction, and it will always divide the circle into two identical halves. This means a circle has infinite, or endless, lines of symmetry!

What if there's NO Symmetry?

  • Some shapes cannot be folded to make two matching halves.
  • These shapes are called 'asymmetrical'.

🎙️ Just like some things are symmetrical, others are not. If you try to fold a shape in any way and the two halves never match up perfectly, then that shape is called 'asymmetrical'. It means 'without symmetry'.
