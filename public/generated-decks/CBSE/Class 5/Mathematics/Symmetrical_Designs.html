What is Symmetry?

  • It means one side looks exactly like the other side.
  • Like a perfect match!

🎙️ Imagine you have a picture, and you can fold it in half, and both halves match perfectly. That's symmetry! It's like having a twin image on both sides.

The Magical Line of Symmetry

  • This is an imaginary line.
  • It divides a shape into two identical halves.

🎙️ To find symmetry, we imagine a special line. This line, called the 'Line of Symmetry', cuts a shape exactly in half, so one side is a mirror image of the other.

Mirror Magic and Symmetry

  • Hold a mirror on the line of symmetry.
  • You will see the whole object!

🎙️ Another cool way to understand symmetry is with a mirror. If you place a mirror along the line of symmetry of an object, the reflection will complete the other half, showing you the whole symmetrical object!

How Many Lines in a Circle?

  • A circle has endless lines of symmetry.
  • Any line passing through its center is a line of symmetry.

🎙️ Now, what about a circle? This is tricky! You can fold a circle along any line that passes through its center, and it will always match. So, a circle has infinite lines of symmetry!

How Many Lines in a Triangle?
