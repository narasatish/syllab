The Secret Key

  • Every pictograph has a key.
  • The key tells us what each picture means.
  • For example, one apple picture = 2 real apples.

🎙️ Just like a treasure map has a key to tell you what symbols mean, a pictograph also has a key. This key is super important! It tells us if one picture stands for 1 item, or 2 items, or even 5 items. Always look at the key first!

How to Read a Pictograph

  • Step 1: Look at the title.
  • Step 2: Find the key.
  • Step 3: Count the pictures for each item.
  • Step 4: Use the key to find the total number.

🎙️ Reading a pictograph is like following a recipe. First, see what the pictograph is about. Then, find the key to know what each picture means. After that, count the pictures for each category and multiply by the value in the key to get the actual number.

1Read Title
2Find Key
3Count Pictures
4Calculate Total
