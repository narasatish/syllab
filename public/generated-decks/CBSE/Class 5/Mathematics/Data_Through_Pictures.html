The Secret Code: The Key!

  • The 'key' tells us what each picture means.
  • It's like a secret code for counting.
  • One picture can mean 1, 2, 5, or even 10 things!

🎙️ This is a very important part of a pictograph! The 'key' is like a secret code. It tells us how many items each single picture in the pictograph represents. For example, if you see a picture of a car, the key will tell you if that one picture means 1 car, 2 cars, or maybe even 5 cars!

How to Read a Picture Story!

  • Step 1: Read the Title.
  • Step 2: Check the Key carefully.
  • Step 3: Count the pictures for each item.
  • Step 4: Answer the questions!

🎙️ Reading a pictograph is like reading a story, but with pictures! First, always read the title to know what the data is about. Second, look at the key to understand what each picture symbol means. Then, count the symbols for each category and you can answer any question!

1Read Title
2Check Key
3Count Pictures
4Answer Questions

Watch Out! Common Mistakes!

  • Forgetting to write the 'Key'.
  • Wrong counting of pictures.
  • Not giving a 'Title' to the pictograph.

🎙️ Even experts make mistakes sometimes! When you are working with pictographs, be careful not to forget the key – it's super important! Also, double-check your counting of the pictures, and always give your pictograph a clear title so everyone knows what it's about.

Exam Time! Smart Tips!

  • Read the questions very carefully.
  • Always use the 'Key' to find the real numbers.
  • Check your counts twice before writing the answer.

🎙️ When you see pictograph questions in your exams, remember these smart tips. Always read the question carefully to understand what is being asked. Use the key correctly to convert pictures into numbers, and always, always double-check your counting before you write down your final answer. This will help you score full marks!
