What is Data?

  • Data is simply information or facts.
  • It tells us things about the world around us.

🎙️ Imagine you want to know how many friends like mangoes. The answer, like '5 friends', is data! It's just a piece of information.

The "Key" to Pictographs

  • Every pictograph has a 'key'.
  • The key tells us what each picture stands for.

🎙️ Think of the key as a secret code! It tells you if one drawing of an apple means one apple, or maybe even two apples! Always look for the key first.
