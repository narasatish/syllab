This total distance is called Perimeter.

  • Perimeter is the total length of all sides of a shape.
  • We add up all the side lengths.

🎙️ So, when we add up the lengths of all the sides of any shape, we are finding its 'Perimeter'. It's like drawing a line all the way around the outside of the shape and measuring that line.

📏
Perimeter

Finding Perimeter of a Square Patch.

  • A square has 4 equal sides.
  • Add the length of each side to find the perimeter.

🎙️ Let's take one of Grandma's square patches. To find its perimeter, we just need to measure one side and then add that length four times, because all sides are equal.

Example: Square Patch Perimeter.

  • If a square patch has sides of 5 cm.

🎙️ Imagine a square patch where each side is 5 centimeters long. To find its perimeter, we simply add 5+5+5+5. So, the perimeter is 20 centimeters. Easy, isn't it?

Measuring the Space Inside.

This space is called Area.

  • Area is the amount of surface a shape covers.
  • We measure it in 'square units'.

🎙️ The total space covered by a flat shape is called its 'Area'. When we talk about area, we use 'square units' because we are counting how many small squares fit inside. Like 'square centimeters' or 'square meters'.

🖼️
Area
16 / 20

Finding Area of a Square Patch.

  • To find the area of a square, multiply Side by Side.
  • Area = Side x Side.

🎙️ For a square patch, finding the area is simple. You just multiply the length of one side by itself. For example, if a side is 5 cm, you multiply 5 by 5.

✖️
Side x Side
17 / 20

Example: Square Patch Area.

  • If a square patch has sides of 5 cm.
  • Area = 5 cm x 5 cm = 25 square cm.

🎙️ So, if our square patch has sides of 5 cm, its area is 5 times 5, which is 25. And remember, we say 'square centimeters' because we are counting the little squares inside.

🟪
5x5 squares
18 / 20

Finding Area of a Rectangle Patch.

  • To find the area of a rectangle, multiply Length by Width.
  • Area = Length x Width.

🎙️ For a rectangular patch, we do something similar. We multiply its length by its width to find how much space it covers. This tells us how many small squares fit inside this rectangle.

✖️
Length x Width
19 / 20

Example: Rectangle Patch Area.

  • A rectangular patch is 6 cm long and 4 cm wide.
  • Area = 6 cm x 4 cm = 24 square cm.

🎙️ If our rectangular patch is 6 cm long and 4 cm wide, its area is 6 times 4, which is 24. So, 24 'square centimeters' of fabric are used for this patch. Great job!

Quick Recap!

  • Perimeter is the distance around a shape.
  • Area is the space inside a shape.

🎙️ So, what did we learn today from Grandma's quilt? We learned about Perimeter, which is like the lace border, and Area, which is the fabric inside. Both are important ways to measure shapes!
