How to Find Square Perimeter

  • Add the length of all four sides.
  • Side + Side + Side + Side

🎙️ To find the perimeter of a square patch, it's very easy! Since all sides are the same, you just add the length of each of its four sides. For example, if one side is 5 cm, you add 5+5+5+5.

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

Let's Calculate!

  • A square patch has sides of 3 cm.
  • Perimeter = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12 cm.

🎙️ Here's an example: Imagine a square quilt patch where each side is 3 centimetres long. To find its perimeter, we simply add 3 + 3 + 3 + 3. The total distance around this patch is 12 centimetres.

➕➕➕➕
Adding Sides

How to Find Rectangle Perimeter

  • Add all four sides: Length + Width + Length + Width.
  • Or (2 x Length) + (2 x Width).

🎙️ For a rectangle patch, we also add all four sides. Remember, it has two long sides (length) and two short sides (width). So, you add length + width + length + width. Or, a quicker way is two times length plus two times width.

How Much Space it Covers (Area)

  • Area is the space inside a shape.
  • Imagine how much fabric is needed to cover the patch.

🎙️ Now, let's learn about 'Area'. Area is different from perimeter. It tells us how much flat space is inside a shape. Think about how much fabric is needed to fill up one quilt patch completely.

How to Find Square Area

  • Multiply Length by Width (which is Side x Side for a square).
  • Count the small squares inside!

🎙️ To find the area of a square patch, you multiply the length of one side by itself. So, Side multiplied by Side. You can also imagine covering the square with many tiny unit squares and counting them.

🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩
Counting Squares

Let's Find the Area!

  • A square patch has sides of 3 cm.
  • Area = 3 cm x 3 cm = 9 square cm.

🎙️ For our square patch with sides of 3 cm, its area will be 3 cm multiplied by 3 cm, which equals 9 square centimetres. We use 'square cm' because we are measuring the flat space.

✖️✖️

How to Find Rectangle Area

  • Multiply its Length by its Width.
  • Length x Width

🎙️ For a rectangle patch, finding the area is also simple. You just multiply its Length by its Width. This tells you how many small squares would fit inside it.

Another Area Example!

🎙️ Let's use our rectangle patch that was 5 cm long and 2 cm wide. Its area would be 5 cm multiplied by 2 cm, which gives us 10 square centimetres. This means 10 small 1cm by 1cm squares can fit inside.
