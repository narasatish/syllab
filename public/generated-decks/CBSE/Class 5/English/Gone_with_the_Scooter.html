What is a Story?

  • A story is like a fun journey.
  • It has a start, a middle, and an end.
  • It tells us about people or things.

🎙️ Think of a story like a train journey. Every train has a starting station, many stops in the middle, and a final destination. A story is just like that – it takes us on a journey with words, introducing us to characters and events.

Putting Things in Order

  • This is called Sequencing Events.
  • Events need to be in the right order.
  • Like making a sandwich, step-by-step.

🎙️ Imagine you're making a yummy jam sandwich. You can't put the jam first, then butter, then bread, right? You need to follow steps! First bread, then butter, then jam. This is called sequencing – putting things in the correct order, just like in a story.

1Bread
2Butter
3Jam
4Eat!

Common Mistakes

  • Mixing up the order of events.
  • Not noticing who is speaking.
  • Missing small but important details.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes when reading stories. A common one is mixing up what happened first and what happened later. Also, sometimes we forget who is doing what, or miss out on little clues in the story. Always read carefully!

Exam Tips

  • Read the story carefully, twice!
  • Look for words like 'first', 'then', 'next', 'finally'.
  • Underline character names and settings.

🎙️ When you see a story in your exam, don't rush! Read it at least twice. Look for special words that tell you the order of things. Also, underline the names of people and places to remember them easily. This will help you answer questions correctly.
