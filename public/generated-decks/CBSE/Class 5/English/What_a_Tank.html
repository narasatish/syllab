Building Descriptive Sentences

  • Subject + Verb + Object + Description.
  • Example: The *big blue* tank holds *clean* water.
  • Use your describing words to make sentences better!

🎙️ When we write or speak, we can use these descriptive words to make our sentences more interesting. Instead of 'The tank holds water', say 'The *big, blue* tank holds *clean* water'.

  • Not making sentences clear.

Exam Tips

  • Read questions carefully for descriptions.
  • Identify characters, setting, problem, solution.
  • Practice writing descriptive sentences.
