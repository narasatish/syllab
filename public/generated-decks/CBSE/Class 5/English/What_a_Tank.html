What is a Tank?

  • It holds water for us.
  • It is usually big in size.

🎙️ A tank is like a very big container. It is made to keep water safe for us to use later. Think of it like a giant bottle for water.

How does water get into tanks?

  • Rain fills big tanks.
  • Pumps push water into small tanks.

🎙️ Sometimes, big tanks collect rainwater directly. For smaller tanks on our roofs, motors pump water up from the ground or other sources to fill them up.

  • For drinking and cooking.
  • For bathing and washing.

🎙️ We use this stored water for so many things every day. From cooking our food to washing our clothes, and even watering plants, tanks help us a lot!
