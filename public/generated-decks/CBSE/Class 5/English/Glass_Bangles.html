Step 1: Melting Glass

  • First, hard glass is heated.
  • It becomes very, very hot.
  • Hot glass turns soft like dough.

🎙️ The first step is to take pieces of hard glass and put them in a very hot oven. The glass gets so hot that it melts and becomes soft, like clay that you can shape.

🔥
Heating the Glass
8 / 20

Step 2: Shaping the Glass

  • The soft glass is pulled into a long strand.
  • This strand is then shaped into a circle.
  • It looks like a big, soft noodle!

🎙️ Once the glass is soft, the maker pulls it into a long, thin string. Then, they quickly join the ends to form a perfect circle. This is how the basic bangle shape is made.

⭕
Making a Circle
9 / 20

Step 3: Colours & Designs

  • Now, artists add bright colours.
  • They might add glitter or tiny beads.
  • Each bangle becomes unique and pretty.

🎙️ This is the fun part! Different colours are added, sometimes with special paints or powders. They might even put tiny sparkling stones or patterns to make each bangle beautiful and special.

Step 4: Cooling Down

  • The decorated bangles need to cool.
  • They become hard and strong again.
  • Now they are ready to wear!

🎙️ After all the decorating, the bangles are carefully cooled down. This makes the glass hard and strong again, so they don't break easily. Once cool, they are ready for you to wear!
