  • First, we melt glass.
  • It becomes soft and gooey.
  • Like warm clay!

🎙️ Imagine glass becoming super hot! It turns into a soft, glowing liquid. This is the very first step to make a bangle.

Making Glass Bangles: Step 2

  • Then, we shape the soft glass.
  • It is carefully made into a round ring.
  • This needs skilled hands.

🎙️ Once the glass is soft, skilled people quickly twist and turn it to make a perfect circle. It's like magic how they make a round bangle so fast!
