How Are Bangles Made? (Part 1)

  • First, big pieces of glass are heated.
  • They become very soft, like melted chocolate.
  • This soft glass is then shaped into long strands.

🎙️ Making bangles is an art! It starts with heating large chunks of glass in very hot furnaces. The glass melts and becomes soft, like dough or melted chocolate, ready to be shaped.

1Melt Glass
10 / 24

How Are Bangles Made? (Part 2)

  • Next, these long glass strands are twisted.
  • They are carefully joined to make a perfect circle.
  • This is how the round shape of a bangle is formed.

🎙️ Once the glass is soft, skilled artisans quickly twist the hot glass into long threads. Then, with great care, they join the ends to form a perfect, smooth circle. It takes a lot of practice to get it right!

1Shape Circle

How Are Bangles Made? (Part 3)

  • Finally, the bangles are decorated.
  • They can have glitter, stones, or beautiful patterns.
  • This makes them extra special and shiny!

🎙️ After shaping, the bangles are cooled and then decorated. Some are painted, some have glitter added, and others are adorned with tiny stones or beads. This is where the magic happens and they become truly beautiful.
