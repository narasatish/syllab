What is a Frog?

  • A frog is a small animal.
  • It can live both in water and on land.

  • They like places with lots of water.

🎙️ Frogs are often found near water bodies like ponds, rivers, and even puddles after rain. They need water to keep their skin moist and for laying eggs.

Why do Frogs Love Water?

  • Frogs need water to keep their skin wet.
  • They can breathe a little through their skin.
  • They also lay their eggs in water.

🎙️ Water is very important for frogs. It helps them breathe and keeps their skin from drying out. They can't live long without water.

  • Frogs have a very long, sticky tongue.

Frog Life Cycle: Step 1 - Eggs

  • A mother frog lays many tiny eggs.
  • She lays them in water, often in a big clump.
  • They look like jelly dots floating in water.

🎙️ The life of a frog starts as tiny eggs. The mother frog lays them in water, and they float together. They are very delicate.

Frog Life Cycle: Step 2 - Tadpole

  • The eggs hatch into small tadpoles.
  • Tadpoles live only in water and swim like fish.
  • They have a tail but no legs yet.

🎙️ From the eggs, little tadpoles come out! They look like tiny fish with a long tail. They swim around in the water and eat small plants.

Frog Life Cycle: Step 3 - Froglet

  • Tadpoles start to grow legs.
  • First, hind legs, then front legs appear.
  • They are now called froglets and can hop a little.

🎙️ As tadpoles grow, they start to change! They grow tiny legs, first at the back, then at the front. They are now called froglets, small frogs that still have a tail.

Frog Life Cycle: Step 4 - Adult Frog

  • The froglet's tail slowly disappears.
  • It becomes a full-grown adult frog.
  • Now it can live both in water and on land.

🎙️ Finally, the tail vanishes completely, and the froglet becomes a beautiful adult frog! It's ready to jump and explore the world, both in water and on land.

Frogs vs. Fish

  • Both live in water, but frogs also live on land.
  • Fish have fins, frogs have legs.
  • Fish breathe with gills, frogs use lungs and skin.

🎙️ Frogs and fish both love water, but they are very different! Fish stay in water all their lives, but frogs can hop out onto land too. They also breathe differently.
