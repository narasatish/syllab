Life of a Frog: Starting with Eggs

  • A mother frog lays many tiny eggs.
  • She lays them in water, in a big group.
  • They look like tiny jelly dots.

🎙️ Now, let's learn how a tiny frog becomes a big frog! It all starts with very small eggs. Mother frogs lay these eggs in water, and they float together in a big cluster, looking like jelly.

From Egg to Tadpole

  • The eggs hatch into tiny tadpoles.
  • Tadpoles live in water and look like small fish.
  • They have a tail but no legs yet.

🎙️ After some time, these eggs hatch, and out come tiny creatures called tadpoles! They live only in water, just like fish, and swim around using their tails. They don't have legs at this stage.

🐠🐠🐠🐠
Tiny Tadpoles

Tadpoles Grow Legs

  • Slowly, the tadpole starts to change.
  • It grows two small back legs first.
  • Then, two front legs also appear!

🎙️ This is where the magic begins! The tadpole starts to grow. First, two small legs appear at the back, and then, two more legs grow at the front. It's slowly becoming more like a frog!

🦵
Growing legs

Losing the Tail

  • As legs grow, the tadpole's tail gets shorter.
  • It slowly disappears completely.
  • Now it looks like a small frog!

🎙️ Once all four legs are grown, something else happens – the tadpole's tail starts to shrink and eventually disappears! Now, it looks like a miniature frog, ready to explore land.

Becoming a Big Frog

  • The small frog keeps growing bigger.
  • It can now live both in water and on land.
  • This whole journey is called a 'life cycle'.

🎙️ Finally, the small frog grows into an adult frog, just like the ones we see in ponds. This amazing journey, from egg to tadpole to a grown-up frog, is called the life cycle of a frog.

Common Mistakes About Frogs

  • Mistake 1: Thinking frogs are fish.
  • Frogs are not fish; tadpoles look like fish.
  • Mistake 2: Thinking frogs only live in water.
  • They live both in water and near water on land.

🎙️ Sometimes, we can get a little confused about frogs. Remember, even though tadpoles look like fish, frogs are not fish. Also, they don't just live *in* water; they love to hop around *near* water too!

Fish live and breathe in water.
