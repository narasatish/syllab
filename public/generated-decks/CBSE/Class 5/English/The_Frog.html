Stage 1: Tiny Eggs

  • A mother frog lays many tiny eggs.
  • The eggs float in the water.
  • They look like jelly dots.

🎙️ These eggs are very delicate and are usually laid in a big clump, sometimes called frogspawn. They stay safe in the water until they hatch.

🥚
Frog eggs
8 / 20

Stage 2: Little Tadpoles

  • Eggs hatch into tadpoles.
  • Tadpoles look like small fish.
  • They live only in water and eat plants.

🎙️ Tadpoles have gills to breathe underwater, just like fish. They swim around, eating tiny water plants, and growing bigger every day.

🐠
Baby tadpole
9 / 20

Stage 3: Growing Froglets

  • Tadpoles start to grow legs!
  • First back legs, then front legs.
  • They still have a tail but can hop a little.

🎙️ This is an exciting stage! The tadpole is slowly changing its body to prepare for life on land. It's like a baby learning to walk.

Eight planets orbit the Sun.

Stage 4: Big Adult Frogs

  • The froglet's tail slowly disappears.
  • It becomes a full-grown frog!
  • Now it can live on land and in water.

🎙️ And finally, a beautiful adult frog emerges! It's ready to explore the world, hop around, and maybe even start its own family of frogs.

Frogs Are Amphibians!

  • Frogs are special animals.
  • They can live both in water and on land.
  • Animals like this are called amphibians.

🎙️ This is a big word, but it just means they are comfortable in two different worlds. They can swim in the pond and also hop on the grass.

Animals live all around us.

  • They eat many harmful insects.

🎙️ Frogs are like little guardians of our environment. By eating pests, they help farmers and gardeners keep their crops and flowers safe without using harmful chemicals.
