Common Mistakes

  • Thinking Algu was wrong to judge against Jumman.
  • Believing Jumman was unfair the second time.
  • Not understanding the meaning of 'Panchayat'.

🎙️ Sometimes, students think that because Algu was Jumman's friend, he should have helped Jumman. But that's not what justice is about. Also, remember that Jumman was fair in the end, even though he wanted revenge at first.
