🎙️ Hello, little learners! Today we will read a very interesting story called 'The Decision of the Panchayat'. It's about how people in a village solve their problems.

What is a Panchayat?

  • It's a group of wise people.
  • They help solve problems in a village.

🎙️ Imagine a small village. Sometimes people have small fights or problems. A Panchayat is like a small court in the village.

🏘️
3 / 20

Who is in a Panchayat?

  • It has a few elders.
  • They are called 'Panchs'.
  • One is the 'Sarpanch' or Head Panch.

🎙️ These are usually older, respected people from the village. They listen to everyone and try to make fair decisions.

Why a Panchayat?

  • To keep peace in the village.
  • To make sure everyone is treated fairly.

🎙️ Just like we have rules at home and school, villages have rules too. The Panchayat makes sure these rules are followed and everyone gets justice.
