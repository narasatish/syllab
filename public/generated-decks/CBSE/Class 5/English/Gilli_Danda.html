How to Play: Hit the Gilli!

  • Place the Gilli over the pit.
  • Hit one end of the Gilli with the Danda to make it jump up.

🎙️ Once the Gilli is on the pit, use the Danda to hit its pointy end. This makes the Gilli jump into the air.

How to Play: Hit it Far!

  • While the Gilli is in the air, hit it again with the Danda.
  • Try to hit it as far as you can!

🎙️ As the Gilli flies up, quickly hit it again with the Danda. The goal is to send it as far away as possible!

Scoring: How Far?

  • The distance the Gilli travels is measured.
  • One Danda-length equals one point.

🎙️ After hitting the Gilli, we measure how far it went. Each length of the Danda is counted as one point. The further it goes, the more points!

Let's Count Points!

  • More distance means more points!

🎙️ Imagine the Gilli flew really far! If it's five Danda lengths away, you score five points! It's simple to count.
