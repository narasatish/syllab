What is Gilli Danda?

  • It's an outdoor game.
  • Played with two sticks.

🎙️ Gilli Danda is a very popular game, especially in India. It is played outside, in open fields or grounds. You need two main things to play this game: two sticks!

Two sticks!

Meet the Gilli!

  • It is a small, short stick.
  • Both ends are pointy.

🎙️ One stick is called the 'Gilli'. It is a small stick, about the size of your hand. It's special because both its ends are shaped like points, making it easy to hit.

Small stick

Meet the Danda!

  • It is a long, big stick.
  • Used to hit the Gilli.

🎙️ The other stick is called the 'Danda'. This stick is much longer, like a cricket bat, but thinner. You use the Danda to hit the small Gilli stick.

How to Play: Step 1

  • First, dig a small pit.
  • It's like a tiny hole in the ground.

🎙️ To start playing, you need to find a good spot in the ground. You dig a small, shallow pit, just big enough for the Gilli to rest on. This pit is important for the game.

How to Play: Step 2

  • Place the Gilli over the pit.
  • One pointy end should be in the pit.

🎙️ Once the pit is ready, carefully place the Gilli over it. One pointy end of the Gilli should be inside the pit, and the other end should be sticking out a little.

2Place Gilli

How to Play: Step 3

  • Use the Danda to hit the Gilli's end.
  • Make it jump up in the air!

🎙️ Now for the exciting part! You use the Danda to hit the Gilli's end that is sticking out. You hit it hard so that the Gilli jumps up from the pit and flies into the air.

How to Play: Step 4

  • While the Gilli is in the air, hit it again!
  • Try to hit it far away.

🎙️ This is a tricky but fun step. As soon as the Gilli is in the air, you quickly hit it again with the Danda. The goal is to hit it as far away as possible from the pit.

How to Score Points

  • The distance the Gilli travels is your score.
  • One Danda-length is one point.

🎙️ After you hit the Gilli far, you measure how far it went. You use the Danda to measure the distance from the pit to where the Gilli landed. Each Danda-length counts as one point!
