What is Gilli Danda?

  • It's an outdoor game.
  • Played with two sticks.
  • Like a simple version of cricket!

🎙️ Gilli Danda is a very popular game, especially in villages and small towns. It's played outside, in an open field. Think of it like a very simple version of cricket, but without a ball or big bat!

Meet the Gilli!

  • It's a small, short stick.
  • It has pointed ends.
  • Like a tiny boat!

🎙️ One of the two main things we need is the 'Gilli'. It's a small piece of wood, usually about 3 to 6 inches long. The special thing about it is its pointed ends, which help it jump when hit.

🛶
Small stick with pointed ends

Meet the Danda!

  • It's a long, thick stick.
  • Used to hit the Gilli.
  • Like a cricket bat, but simpler!

🎙️ The other important thing is the 'Danda'. This is a longer, thicker stick, usually about 1 to 2 feet long. This is what you use to hit the Gilli and send it flying!

  • First, dig a small pit.

🎙️ Before playing, you need to prepare the ground. Dig a small, shallow hole in the earth. This pit is where the Gilli will be placed to start each turn.

First Hit: Uchaalna!

  • Place the Gilli on the pit.
  • Hit one pointed end with the Danda.
  • Make the Gilli jump up!

🎙️ To start your turn, you place the Gilli across the pit. Then, with your Danda, you gently tap or hit one of the pointed ends of the Gilli. This makes the Gilli 'uchaalna' or jump into the air.

⬆️
Making the Gilli jump
10 / 24

Second Hit: Maarana!

  • When the Gilli is in the air...
  • Hit it hard with the Danda!
  • Send it flying far away!

🎙️ As soon as the Gilli jumps up, while it's still in the air, you quickly swing the Danda and hit it with full force. The goal is to 'maarana' or strike it as far as you possibly can!

🚀
Hitting the Gilli far
11 / 24

Scoring Points: Measuring

  • Points are measured by distance.
  • Use the Danda to measure.
  • One Danda-length is one point.

🎙️ How do you get points? After you hit the Gilli, the other players try to catch it. If they don't, you measure the distance from the pit to where the Gilli landed. You use your Danda as a measuring tape! Each Danda-length counts as one point.

Measuring with the Danda

Who Plays When? Taking Turns

  • Players take turns hitting.
  • One player hits, others try to catch.
  • It's fair for everyone!

🎙️ Just like in many games, players take turns. One person hits the Gilli, while the other players stand ready to catch it. Once a player is out, the next person gets a turn.

15 / 24

You're Out!

  • If an opponent catches the Gilli.
  • If you miss hitting the Gilli three times.
  • Then it's the next player's turn.

🎙️ In Gilli Danda, there are ways to get 'out'. If someone from the other team catches your Gilli before it touches the ground, you are out. Also, if you miss hitting the Gilli three times in a row, you are out. Then, the next player gets to bat.

How to Play: Step-by-Step

  • 1. Dig a small pit.
  • 2. Place Gilli on the pit.
  • 3. Hit Gilli to make it jump (Uchaalna).
  • 4. Hit jumping Gilli far (Maarana).
  • 5. Measure points with Danda.
