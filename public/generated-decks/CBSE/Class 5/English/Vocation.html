Common Mistakes

  • Confusing hobbies with vocations.
  • Thinking some jobs are 'better' than others.

🎙️ Sometimes children confuse a hobby with a vocation. A hobby is something you do for fun, like drawing or playing. A vocation is usually your main work, often to earn a living. Also, remember, all honest work is good work!
