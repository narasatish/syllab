How is a Rainbow Formed?

  • Sunlight goes through tiny water drops.
  • The water drops split the sunlight into colours.

🎙️ It's like magic, but it's science! The sun's light passes through the small drops of water left in the air after rain. These tiny drops act like little glass balls.

Water Droplets are Tiny Prisms

  • These droplets act like small glass prisms.

Splitting of Light

  • It splits into all its different colours.

The Colours of the Rainbow

  • There are seven main colours in a rainbow.
  • We can remember them using 'VIBGYOR'.

🎙️ Now we know how rainbows are made! Let's learn the names of these seven beautiful colours. We have a special secret word to help us remember them all!

VIBGYOR: V for Violet

  • Violet is a deep purple colour.
  • It is at the very bottom of the rainbow.

🎙️ The first letter 'V' stands for Violet. It's a dark, beautiful purple, like some flowers or grapes. It's the innermost colour of the rainbow.

Violet colour

VIBGYOR: I for Indigo

  • Indigo is a dark blue colour.
  • It is between Violet and Blue.

🎙️ Next is 'I' for Indigo. It's a very deep blue, almost like the night sky just before it gets completely dark. It sits right next to violet.

Indigo colour

VIBGYOR: B for Blue

  • Blue is the colour of the sky and ocean.
  • It is a calm and cool colour.

🎙️ Then comes 'B' for Blue! This is a colour we see every day – the big blue sky and the vast blue ocean. It's a very common and soothing colour.

Clouds bring us rain.

VIBGYOR: G for Green

  • Green is the colour of grass and leaves.
  • It is in the middle of the rainbow.

🎙️ After blue, we have 'G' for Green. Think of all the trees, plants, and grass around us. Green is the colour of nature and life. It's right in the middle of our rainbow.

VIBGYOR: Y for Yellow

🎙️ Next is 'Y' for Yellow! This is the bright colour of the sun, happy smiley faces, and delicious lemons. It's a very cheerful colour in the rainbow.

VIBGYOR: O for Orange

  • Orange is the colour of an orange fruit.
  • It is a mix of red and yellow.

🎙️ Then comes 'O' for Orange. Just like the yummy fruit! It's a warm colour, a mix of red and yellow, and it's very bright and inviting.

🎙️ Finally, 'R' for Red! This is a strong, bold colour, like juicy apples, beautiful roses, and even a stop sign. It's the outermost colour of the rainbow.
