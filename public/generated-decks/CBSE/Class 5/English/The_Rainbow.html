What is a Rainbow?

  • It is a beautiful arch of many colours.
  • We see it in the sky, usually opposite the sun.

🎙️ A rainbow is like a giant, colourful bridge painted across the sky. It's not a physical thing you can touch, but a beautiful optical illusion.

  • They split sunlight into different colours.

🎙️ Each tiny raindrop in the air acts like a very small mirror or a special piece of glass called a prism. These tiny raindrops can bend and split light.

How a Rainbow Forms: Step 1

  • Sunlight enters a raindrop.
  • The light bends a little as it goes in.

🎙️ First, the white sunlight travels from the sun and enters a tiny raindrop. As it goes from air into the water of the raindrop, it changes direction slightly.

1Sunlight enters
2Light bends
9 / 24

How a Rainbow Forms: Step 2

  • Light bounces off the back of the raindrop.
  • Like a tiny mirror reflecting light.

🎙️ After bending inside, the light hits the back inner surface of the raindrop. It then bounces back, just like a ball hitting a wall and coming back to you.

1Light bends
2Light bounces
10 / 24

How a Rainbow Forms: Step 3

  • As light leaves the raindrop, it splits.
  • We see the beautiful colours of the rainbow!

🎙️ When the light finally comes out of the raindrop, it has split into all its beautiful colours. Millions of these tiny raindrops create the big rainbow we see!
