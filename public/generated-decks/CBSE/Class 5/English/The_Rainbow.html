🎙️ Hello, my dear students! Today, we are going to talk about something truly beautiful that appears in the sky. It's called the Rainbow!

What is a Rainbow?

  • It's a big colourful arc.
  • It shines brightly in the sky.

🎙️ A rainbow is like a big, colourful bridge made of light. It's not a solid thing you can touch, but a beautiful sight!

When Do We See a Rainbow?

  • Usually after it rains.
  • When the sun also shines.

🎙️ Rainbows love to play hide-and-seek! They usually appear after a gentle rain shower, when the sun peeks out from behind the clouds.

How is a Rainbow Formed?

  • Sunlight shines through tiny raindrops.
  • The raindrops act like little prisms.

🎙️ Imagine tiny raindrops floating in the air after the rain. When sunlight passes through these little water drops, they bend the light and split it into many colours.

💧

The Colours of the Rainbow

  • A rainbow has seven beautiful colours.
  • They always appear in the same order.

🎙️ Isn't it amazing? Every rainbow always shows us seven special colours! They are like a secret code, always in the same sequence.

Rainbows in Everyday Life

  • Sometimes, you can see mini rainbows.
  • In water spray from a hose pipe.
  • Or through a glass of water in sunlight.

🎙️ Rainbows aren't just in the sky! If you spray water from a hose pipe on a sunny day, you might see tiny rainbows. You can even make one with a glass of water and sunlight!

Can We Touch a Rainbow?

  • No, a rainbow is not a solid object.
  • It's just light bending in the air.

🎙️ Even though rainbows look so close and real, we cannot actually touch them. They are made of light, not a solid thing, so they disappear as you get closer.
