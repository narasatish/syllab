🎙️ When we use our money to get something, that's called spending. For example, when you buy a yummy ice cream from the shop, you are spending money. Or when your parents buy you new books for school, they are spending money.

Needs vs. Wants

  • Needs are things we must have to live.
  • Like food, water, and clothes.
  • Wants are things we desire, but don't need.

🎙️ This is very important! Needs are things we cannot live without, like healthy food to eat, clean water to drink, and clothes to wear. Wants are things we really desire, like a new video game or a fancy toy, but we can live without them.

How to Start Saving?

  • Step 1: Get a piggy bank.
  • Step 2: Put some money in it regularly.
  • Step 3: Don't open it often!

🎙️ Starting to save is easy! First, find a nice piggy bank. Then, try to put a little bit of money into it every day or every week. The most important rule is: don't open it every time you want to buy something small! Let your money grow.

Smart Spending vs. Smart Saving

  • Smart spending is buying needs first.
  • Smart saving is keeping money for goals.
  • Both help you manage money well.

🎙️ Being smart with money means both spending wisely and saving wisely. Smart spending means buying things you truly need and comparing prices. Smart saving means putting money aside for your dreams. Both are important for being good with money.

Common Mistakes When Saving

  • Opening the piggy bank too often.
  • Not having a clear saving goal.
  • Spending all money without saving any.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes. One common mistake is opening the piggy bank every time we want something small. Another is not having a clear goal, so we don't know what we are saving for. And remember, try not to spend all your money as soon as you get it!
