🎙️ Money is simply a tool. We use special papers and coins to exchange for things we want or need. It helps us get food, clothes, and even fun experiences.

What is Saving?

  • Saving means keeping some money aside.
  • Not spending all of it right away.

🎙️ Imagine you have ten chocolates. If you eat all of them today, you won't have any for tomorrow. Saving money is like keeping some chocolates for later. It's putting money aside for future use.

Meet Your Piggy Bank!

  • A special friend for your money.
  • It's a safe home for your savings.

🎙️ Do you know where we can keep our money safe? Yes, a piggy bank! It's a fun and easy way for children to start saving. It can be a cute pig, a box, or any container with a slot.

How Does It Work?

  • Drop coins and notes inside.
  • Watch your savings grow slowly.
  • Don't take it out unless needed!

🎙️ It's very simple! Every time you get a little money, maybe from your parents or as a gift, you put a part of it into your piggy bank. The more you put in, the more your savings will grow!
