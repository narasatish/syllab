Trading Things: Barter System

  • Long, long ago, people traded items.
  • Like giving apples for bananas, or milk for eggs.

🎙️ Before money, if someone had extra apples and wanted bananas, they would find someone who had bananas and wanted apples. This was called the barter system.

Countries export and import goods across the world.

Common Mistakes About Money

  • Confusing coins and notes (e.g., calling a note a coin).
  • Not knowing the exact value of different money.
  • Thinking a 1 Rupee coin is bigger in value than a 10 Rupee coin.

🎙️ Sometimes, children might mix up coins and notes, or forget the value of each. Remember, the size of a coin doesn't always tell you its value; a 10 Rupee coin is worth more than a 1 Rupee coin.
