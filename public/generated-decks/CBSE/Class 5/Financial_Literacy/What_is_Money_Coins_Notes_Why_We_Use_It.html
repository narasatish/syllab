What is Money?

  • Money is special paper or metal.
  • We use it to buy things we need and want.

🎙️ Imagine you want to buy a yummy chocolate or a cool toy. How do you get it? We use money! Money is like a magic key that helps us get things from shops. It can be made of metal, which we call coins, or special paper, which we call notes.

Before Money: Barter System

  • Long, long ago, there was no money.
  • People exchanged things directly.

🎙️ Many years ago, people didn't have money. If someone wanted apples and had wheat, they would find someone who wanted wheat and had apples, and swap! This was called the Barter System. It was a simple way to trade.

New Way (Money)

Barter Example

  • A farmer exchanged apples for bread.
  • No money was used, just swapping!

🎙️ Imagine a farmer who has many apples but needs bread. He would go to a baker who has bread but needs apples. They would then swap! The farmer gives apples, and the baker gives bread. Simple, right?

🍎🍎🍎
Swapping Apples

Problem with Barter

  • What if you have apples, but no one wants them?
  • It was hard to find the right person to swap with.

🎙️ But what if the baker didn't want apples? Or what if the farmer wanted something else entirely? It was very difficult to find the perfect match for every trade. Sometimes, people couldn't get what they needed.

Hello, Money!

  • Money made buying and selling easy.
  • Everyone accepts money!

🎙️ To solve this problem, people invented money! With money, you don't need to find someone who wants what you have. You can just sell your apples for money, and then use that money to buy anything you want from anyone!

Money in India: Coins

  • Coins are small, round pieces of metal.
  • They have different values.

🎙️ In India, we use two main types of money: coins and notes. Let's look at coins first. Coins are usually made of metal, they are round and small. You might find them jingling in your pocket!

Money in India: Notes

  • Notes are made of special paper.
  • They are also called currency notes.

🎙️ Now let's talk about notes! Notes are made of special paper, not metal. They are usually rectangular and come in different colours and sizes. You might hear adults call them 'currency notes'.

Indian Notes We Use

  • We have notes like ₹10, ₹50, ₹100, ₹500.
  • Mahatma Gandhi's picture is on most notes.

🎙️ Here are some of the notes we use in India. We have ten, fifty, one hundred, and even five hundred rupee notes! You can see the picture of Mahatma Gandhi, the Father of our Nation, on most of these notes.

Why We Use Money: Buying Things

  • The most common use of money is to buy things.
  • We pay money to get food, toys, clothes.

🎙️ So, why do we use money? The most important reason is to buy things! When you go to a shop for snacks, or when your parents buy groceries, they use money to pay for them. Money helps us get all the things we need and want.

Why We Use Money: Saving

  • We also save money for future needs.
  • Saving means keeping money safely.

🎙️ Another very smart way to use money is to save it! Saving means keeping some money aside safely, usually in a piggy bank or a bank. We save money so that we can use it later for bigger things or for emergencies.

Example: Saving for a Bicycle

  • You want a new bicycle.
