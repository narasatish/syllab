Long Ago: The Barter System

  • People traded things directly.
  • Give eggs for milk, clothes for rice.

🎙️ Many, many years ago, before money existed, people used something called the 'Barter System'. This meant they exchanged things they had for things they needed. For example, a farmer might give eggs to a neighbour for some milk.

Countries export and import goods across the world.
3 / 19

Problems with Barter

  • Hard to find someone with what you need.
  • What if your friend doesn't want your eggs?

🎙️ But the barter system had many problems. Imagine you have a lot of mangoes, but you want a pair of shoes. You have to find someone who has shoes AND wants mangoes! It was often very difficult to find the perfect match.

😟
It was difficult!

The Solution: Money!

  • People needed a common thing.
  • Something everyone would accept.

🎙️ To make trading easier, people came up with a brilliant idea! They decided to use one special thing that everyone would agree to accept in exchange for goods and services. And that special thing is what we call money!

The rupee (₹) is our money.

What is Money?

  • It's a medium of exchange.
  • We use it to buy and sell things.

🎙️ Simply put, money is anything that people generally accept as payment for goods and services. It's like a special ticket that lets you get what you want from others. It makes buying and selling very simple.

Coins: Metal Money

  • Made of metal, small and round.
  • We have ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10 coins.

🎙️ Coins are usually small, round pieces of metal. They have different numbers on them, like 1, 2, 5, or 10, which tell us their value. They are strong and last a long time!

Notes: Paper Money

  • Made of special paper.
  • We have ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500 notes.

🎙️ Notes are made of a special kind of paper. They are colourful and come in different sizes, also with different numbers on them. These numbers show their value, like ten rupees or five hundred rupees.
