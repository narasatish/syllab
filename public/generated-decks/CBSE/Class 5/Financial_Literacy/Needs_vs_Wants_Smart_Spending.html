Smart Spending: How to Decide

  • Step 1: Ask, 'Do I need this to live?'
  • Step 2: If yes, it's a Need.
  • Step 3: If no, it's a Want.

🎙️ Here's a simple trick! Before you buy something, ask yourself: 'Do I really need this to be healthy and safe?' If the answer is yes, it's a need. If not, it's a want.
