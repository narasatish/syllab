What are Our Needs?

  • Things we MUST have.
  • We cannot live without them.
  • They keep us safe and healthy.

🎙️ Needs are things that are very important for us to live and be healthy. Just like a house protects us from the rain and sun, our basic needs keep us safe and sound.

  • Without them, no cake.

🎙️ Think about baking a cake. You absolutely need flour, sugar, and eggs, right? Without these main ingredients, you can't make a cake. These are like our basic needs.

Examples of Needs

  • Food to eat (like roti, dal)
  • Water to drink
  • Clothes to wear
  • A home to live in
  • Medicines when sick

🎙️ These are some important needs. We need food to grow strong, water to stay hydrated, clothes to keep us warm, and a home for shelter. If we are sick, medicines are also a need.

What are Our Wants?

  • Things we LIKE to have.
  • They make us happy.
  • But we can live without them.

🎙️ Wants are things that are nice to have and make us feel good, but they are not essential for our survival. Like a delicious ice cream, it makes you happy, but you don't need it to live.

Needs vs. Wants: The Big Difference

  • Needs are essential for survival.
  • Wants are things we desire.
  • Needs come first, always!

🎙️ The main difference is that needs are a must-have for life, while wants are nice-to-haves. When we have money, we should always take care of our needs first before thinking about our wants.

  • Helps us use money wisely.
  • We can save for bigger goals.
  • Avoids running out of money.

🎙️ Being a smart spender means making good choices with your money. It helps you have enough for important things and even save for something special you really want later.

14 / 24

How to Spend Smartly: Step 1

  • First, list all your Needs.
  • Buy your Needs first.
  • They are the most important.

🎙️ The very first step to smart spending is to identify what you truly need. Make a mental list or even write it down. Always use your money to fulfill these needs first.

1Identify Needs
2Buy Needs First
15 / 24

How to Spend Smartly: Step 2

  • After Needs, look at Wants.
  • Think: 'Do I really need this NOW?'
  • Is it worth my money?

🎙️ Once your needs are met, you can look at your wants. But don't just buy them! Take a moment to think if you truly want it and if it's a good use of your money right now.

1Consider Wants
2Think Before Buying

How to Spend Smartly: Step 3

  • If you have extra money.
  • Save some for future wants!
  • Or for emergencies.

🎙️ The final step is to save any extra money you have. This money can be used for a bigger want later, or kept aside for unexpected situations, like needing medicine.

2Plan for Future

  • Not thinking before buying.
