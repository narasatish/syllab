Literary Device: Metaphor

  • Direct comparison between two unlike things.
  • States one thing IS another, without 'like' or 'as'.
  • Adds depth and meaning to the description.

🎙️ A metaphor is a figure of speech where you directly compare two things that aren't alike, but share some quality. Instead of saying 'it's like something', you say 'it IS something', creating a stronger connection.

🎭
Hidden Comparison

Literary Device: Simile

  • Comparison between two unlike things.
  • Uses 'like' or 'as' to make the comparison explicit.
  • Helps readers understand a concept by relating it to something familiar.

🎙️ Simile is another type of comparison, but it's more direct than a metaphor. It uses the words 'like' or 'as' to show how two different things are similar in some way.

Direct Comparison
