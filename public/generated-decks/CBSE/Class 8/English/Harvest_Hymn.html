Introduction: What is a Hymn? What is Harvest?

  • A 'Hymn' is a song of praise or adoration.
  • 'Harvest' is the season of gathering crops.
  • A 'Harvest Hymn' is a song praising the bounty of harvest.

🎙️ A hymn is essentially a song of praise, often dedicated to a deity or a significant event. When we combine it with 'harvest', it becomes a song of gratitude and praise for the abundance of crops gathered from the fields. It's a celebration of nature's gifts.

🎙️ Harvest isn't just a single event; it's the culmination of a long process. It begins with sowing seeds, watching them sprout and grow, and finally, collecting the mature crops. This cycle is vital for our food supply.

Literary Device 1: Imagery

  • Words that create mental pictures.
  • Appeals to our five senses.
  • Helps us 'see', 'hear', 'smell', 'taste', 'feel' the poem.

🎙️ Imagery is a powerful literary device where the poet uses descriptive language to create vivid pictures in our minds. It's like painting a scene with words, allowing us to experience the poem through our senses, making it more engaging and memorable.

Literary Device 2: Alliteration

  • Repetition of the same initial consonant sound.
  • Occurs in words close together.
  • Adds musicality and emphasis to the lines.

🎙️ Alliteration is a fun literary device where the same starting sound is repeated in words that are near each other. It creates a pleasing rhythm and helps to draw attention to certain phrases, making the poem more enjoyable to read aloud and remember.

Sound play
