The Threat: Tribal Raiders and Pakistan

  • In October 1947, tribal invaders supported by Pakistan attacked Kashmir.
  • Their goal was to capture Srinagar and its vital airfield.

🎙️ Soon after independence, a serious threat emerged in Kashmir. Armed tribal raiders, backed by Pakistan, began an offensive. Their primary target was Srinagar, the capital, and more critically, its airfield, which was essential for India to send reinforcements.

The Battle of Badgam: A Strategic Location

  • Badgam was a small village near Srinagar, strategically important.
  • It was crucial to defend Badgam to protect Srinagar Airfield.
  • Losing Badgam would mean losing the gateway to Srinagar.

🎙️ Badgam might sound like a small, unassuming place, but its location made it incredibly vital. It was the last defensive line before the Srinagar Airfield, and its fall would have severe consequences for India's efforts to defend Kashmir. This is where Major Sharma and his men stepped in.

India's First Param Vir Chakra

  • Major Somnath Sharma was posthumously awarded the Param Vir Chakra.
  • It is India's highest military decoration for valour.
  • He was the very first recipient of this prestigious award.

🎙️ For his 'unparalleled courage and self-sacrifice', Major Somnath Sharma was awarded the Param Vir Chakra. This is the highest honour a soldier can receive in India, and it's a testament to his extraordinary bravery, making him a legend in Indian military history.
