Understanding Cosmic Rays

  • Invisible particles from outer space
  • Constantly bombard Earth's atmosphere
  • Carry vital information about the universe

🎙️ Cosmic rays are like messengers from the cosmos. They are invisible, high-energy particles that originate from distant stars and galaxies. By studying them, scientists can learn more about the universe's composition and phenomena.

Detecting Cosmic Rays (Early Methods)

  • Used 'nuclear emulsion plates'
  • Particles leave tracks on special films
  • These tracks reveal particle properties

🎙️ In Bibha's time, detecting cosmic rays was quite a challenge. She used a technique involving nuclear emulsion plates, which are like special photographic films. When cosmic ray particles passed through these plates, they left tiny, visible tracks that could be analyzed under a microscope.

What is Stellar Astronomy?

  • The study of stars and their properties
  • Examines star formation, evolution, and death
  • Uses telescopes to observe distant stars

🎙️ Stellar astronomy is the branch of astronomy dedicated to studying everything about stars. This includes how they are born, how they live their lives, and how they eventually die. Astronomers use powerful telescopes to gather light and information from these distant celestial bodies.
