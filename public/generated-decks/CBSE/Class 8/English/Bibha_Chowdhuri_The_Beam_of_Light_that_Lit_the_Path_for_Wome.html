Who Was Bibha Chowdhuri?

  • Born in 1913 in Kolkata, India.
  • An eminent Indian physicist and researcher.
  • One of the very few women in science during her era.

🎙️ Bibha Chowdhuri was born over a century ago in a time when opportunities for women, especially in scientific fields, were extremely limited. Despite this, she pursued her passion for physics with remarkable determination.

Understanding Cosmic Rays

  • High-energy particles originating from outer space.
  • They constantly bombard Earth's atmosphere.
  • Scientists study them to understand the universe.

🎙️ Imagine tiny, incredibly fast particles zipping through space and hitting our planet all the time! These are cosmic rays, and understanding them helps us learn about distant stars, galaxies, and even the origins of the universe.

Detecting Cosmic Rays

  • Scientists use special equipment to 'see' cosmic rays.
  • One method involves photographic emulsions.
  • These plates record the tracks of particles.

🎙️ Since cosmic rays are invisible, scientists developed clever ways to detect them. Bibha Chowdhuri was skilled in using techniques like photographic emulsions, which are like special films that capture the invisible paths of these particles.

Her Groundbreaking Research

  • Contributed to the early understanding of particle physics.

🎙️ Bibha Chowdhuri's research was truly pioneering. She was among the first to study specific subatomic particles called mesons, contributing significantly to a field that was still very new and exciting at the time.
