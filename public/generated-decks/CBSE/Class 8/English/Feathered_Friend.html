The Music of Words: Rhyme Scheme

  • If 'Feathered Friend' is a poem, it might have a rhyme scheme.
  • A rhyme scheme is the pattern of rhymes at the end of lines.
  • Common patterns: AABB, ABAB, ABCB.

🎙️ A rhyme scheme adds a musical quality to a poem. To find it, you look at the last word of each line. If two lines rhyme, you give them the same letter. For example, if line 1 ends in 'sky' (A) and line 2 ends in 'high' (A), and line 3 ends in 'tree' (B) and line 4 ends in 'free' (B), that's an AABB rhyme scheme.
