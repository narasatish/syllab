Word Power: Vivid

  • Meaning: Producing powerful feelings or strong, clear images in the mind.
  • Example: Liáng's paintings were so vivid, they almost seemed real.

🎙️ When we say something is 'vivid,' we mean it's incredibly lifelike and striking. Think of bright colours or very clear descriptions that make you feel like you're right there, experiencing it.

Word Power: Yearn

  • Meaning: To have an intense feeling of longing for something.

Painting with Words: Imagery

  • Language that appeals to our senses (sight, sound, smell, touch, taste).
  • It helps us visualize and feel the story.
  • Authors use descriptive words to create mental pictures.

🎙️ Imagery is a powerful tool authors use to make stories come alive. By using words that appeal to our five senses, they help us not just read, but truly experience the story, seeing, hearing, and even feeling what the characters do.

Imagery in 'The Magic Brush'

  • 'The village houses, made of mud and straw, huddled together like tired old friends.' (Sight)
