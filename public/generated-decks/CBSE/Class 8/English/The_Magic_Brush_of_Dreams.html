Avoid These Common Mistakes!

  • Confusing Plot with Theme: Plot is WHAT happens, Theme is the BIG IDEA.
  • Not identifying the Climax correctly: It's the turning point, not just any exciting part.
  • Ignoring character motivations: Always ask 'Why did they do that?' for deeper understanding.

🎙️ Students often mix up these literary elements. Remember, the plot is the sequence of events, while the theme is the underlying message. Pay attention to character's motives too.
