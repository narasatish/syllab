Amul: A Brand is Born

  • Kaira District Co-operative Milk Producers' Union
  • Later named 'Amul' (Anand Milk Union Ltd.)
  • Became a symbol of quality and trust

🎙️ From a small cooperative, Amul grew into a household name. It wasn't just about milk; it was about trust, quality, and the collective strength of thousands of farmers working together under one brand.

The White Revolution: A National Movement

  • India's massive dairy development program
  • Transformed India from milk-deficient to largest producer
  • Inspired by the 'Anand Pattern' cooperative

🎙️ The success of Amul in Anand became a blueprint for the entire nation. The 'White Revolution', also known as 'Operation Flood', was a groundbreaking initiative to replicate this cooperative model nationwide and make India self-sufficient in milk.

🚀
Rapid Growth

🎙️ The revolution stood on three strong pillars: organizing farmers into cooperatives, establishing modern dairies to process milk efficiently, and ensuring that this milk reached consumers across the country at fair prices.

Operation Flood: Spreading the Success

  • Nationwide program launched in 1970
  • Aimed to create a national milk grid
  • Connected milk producers to urban consumers

🎙️ Operation Flood was the ambitious project that scaled up the Anand model. It created a vast network, linking rural milk producers directly to millions of urban consumers, ensuring efficient distribution and fair prices.

🗺️
National Reach

How Milk Cooperatives Work

  • Farmers bring milk to village collection centres
  • Milk is tested, chilled, and transported
  • Processed and sold as Amul products

🎙️ It's a simple yet powerful system. Every morning and evening, farmers bring their milk, which is then tested for quality, chilled to preserve it, and transported to larger dairies for processing into various products.
