Meet the Author: Ruskin Bond

  • An iconic Indian author
  • Known for simple, engaging stories
  • Often writes about nature and childhood
  • Recipient of Padma Shri and Padma Bhushan

🎙️ Ruskin Bond is a name synonymous with Indian literature, especially for young readers. His stories are always heartwarming and easy to relate to, often set in the beautiful hills of Mussoorie, where he lives.

Introduction to 'The Cherry Tree'

Literary Device 1: Imagery

  • Words that create vivid pictures in our minds
  • Engages our senses (sight, smell, touch)
  • Example: 'white, fragile, and quickly falling'
  • Helps us visualize the scene and feelings

🎙️ Just like a painter uses colors, a poet uses words to create images. Ruskin Bond uses beautiful imagery to help us see the tiny blossoms, the red cherries, and the strong tree, making the poem come alive in our imaginations.

🎨
Painting with words
22 / 30

Literary Device 2: Personification

  • Giving human qualities to inanimate objects or animals
  • Example: 'The cherry tree, reaching up to the sky'
  • Makes the tree seem more alive and relatable
  • Connects us emotionally to the natural world

🎙️ When the poet says the tree is 'reaching up to the sky,' he's giving it a human action. This personification makes the tree feel like a character in itself, making its struggles and growth more impactful and relatable to us.

🧍‍♀️➡️🌳
Tree with human traits

Literary Device 3: Symbolism

  • An object or idea representing something else
  • The cherry tree symbolizes hope, life, and growth
  • It also represents the simple joys of nature
  • Rakesh's journey symbolizes human effort and reward

🎙️ The cherry tree is not just a tree; it's a powerful symbol. It stands for the enduring spirit of nature, the rewards of patience, and the simple, yet profound, happiness found in connecting with the natural world. It represents hope for the future.

Poetic Devices: Rhyme Scheme & Rhythm

  • The pattern of rhymes at the end of lines
  • Contributes to the poem's musicality and flow
  • Often uses a simple AABB or ABCB pattern
  • Makes the poem enjoyable to read aloud

🎙️ Just like a song has a rhythm and rhyme, poems often use these devices to create a pleasing sound. The Cherry Tree uses a simple rhyme scheme that makes it easy to read and remember, adding to its charm and accessibility.
