Wit vs. Sarcasm

  • Wit aims to entertain and enlighten.
  • Sarcasm often intends to mock or hurt.
  • Wit builds connections; sarcasm creates distance.
  • One is clever, the other can be cruel.

🎙️ It's important to distinguish between wit and sarcasm. While both can be clever, wit is generally positive and inclusive, meant to bring joy or understanding. Sarcasm, however, often carries a negative, mocking tone that can be hurtful.
