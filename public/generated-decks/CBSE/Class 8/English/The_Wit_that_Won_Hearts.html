  • Wit: quick, clever application of intelligence.

🎙️ While related, wit and intelligence are not exactly the same. Intelligence is like having a big library of knowledge, while wit is knowing exactly which book to pull out and how to use it brilliantly in a moment.

Types of Wit: Sarcasm, Puns, Irony

  • Sarcasm: saying the opposite of what you mean, often critically.
  • Puns: playing on words with similar sounds but different meanings.
  • Irony: a contrast between what is expected and what actually happens.

🎙️ Wit comes in many forms! Sarcasm, puns, and irony are just a few examples of how people use language cleverly. Understanding these types can help you appreciate witty dialogues even more.
