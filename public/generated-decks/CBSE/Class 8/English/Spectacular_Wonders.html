Two Main Types: Natural vs. Man-made

  • Natural wonders are formed by nature (e.g., mountains, rivers).
  • Man-made wonders are built by humans (e.g., monuments, cities).

🎙️ We can broadly categorize wonders into two groups. Natural wonders are gifts from Mother Earth, while man-made wonders are incredible achievements of human ingenuity and effort. Both inspire us in different ways.

Reading Strategy: Identifying Main Ideas

  • Summarize the core message in your own words.

🎙️ When tackling a descriptive passage, don't get lost in every detail immediately. First, grasp the overall point the author is trying to make. The main idea is the central theme or message of the text.

Steps to Effective Descriptive Writing

  • 1. Brainstorm: List ideas, adjectives, feelings.

Using Figurative Language: Similes & Metaphors

  • Simile: Compares two unlike things using 'like' or 'as'.
  • Metaphor: Compares two unlike things by stating one IS the other.
  • They make descriptions more imaginative and powerful.

🎙️ To elevate your descriptions, try using similes and metaphors. These literary devices create vivid comparisons, making your writing more engaging and memorable. They add a touch of poetic flair.

✨
Adding Sparkle

Examples: Simile & Metaphor

  • Simile: 'The waterfall cascaded down the cliff *like a silver ribbon*.'
  • Metaphor: 'The desert was *a golden ocean* under the midday sun.'

🎙️ See how these comparisons immediately create a strong image? The waterfall isn't just falling; it's a graceful ribbon. The desert isn't just sand; it's a vast, shimmering ocean. These add depth to your writing.

Common Mistakes in Describing Wonders

  • Using clichés (e.g., 'beautiful beyond words').
  • Lack of specific details (too general).
  • Poor vocabulary choices (repetitive words).
  • Ignoring sensory details (only visual).

🎙️ Even experienced writers make mistakes. Be mindful of overused phrases, and always strive for specific, fresh language. Remember to engage all senses, not just sight, to create a truly immersive description.
