What is a Magnifying Glass?

  • An optical tool with a curved lens
  • Makes small things look larger
  • Helps us see details clearly

🎙️ A magnifying glass is a simple lens that helps us observe tiny objects or fine print. It's designed to make things appear bigger than they actually are.

How It Works: Making Things Bigger

  • A curved lens bends light
  • Light spreads out as it passes through
  • Our eyes perceive an enlarged image

🎙️ In simple terms, the special shape of the lens in a magnifying glass changes the path of light. This makes the image of the object appear stretched out and therefore larger to our eyes.

The Word 'Magnifying': What Does It Mean?

  • Derived from Latin 'magnus' meaning 'great'
  • To make something appear larger
  • To emphasize or exaggerate its importance

🎙️ The word 'magnify' comes from a Latin root meaning 'great' or 'large.' So, when we magnify something, we are essentially making it appear greater or more significant than its actual size.
