What is "Somebody's Mother" About?

  • An old woman struggling to cross a busy street
  • A group of boys pass by her, unconcerned
  • One boy stops to offer her help
  • The woman's heartfelt gratitude for his kindness

🎙️ This poem tells a simple yet powerful story. It revolves around an elderly woman who finds herself in a difficult situation. We'll see how different people react to her plight and what impact one kind act can have on her.

Literary Device: Rhyme Scheme

  • The pattern of rhymes at the end of each line of a poem
  • Adds musicality and rhythm, making the poem pleasing
  • Helps in memorizing the poem and understanding its structure
  • Common patterns include AABB, ABAB, or ABCB

🎙️ A rhyme scheme is the pattern of rhymes at the end of each line of a poem. It's usually denoted by letters. For example, if the first and third lines rhyme, and the second and fourth lines rhyme, it's an ABAB rhyme scheme. This adds to the poem's flow and appeal.

Literary Device: Imagery

  • Using descriptive language to create mental pictures
  • Appeals to our five senses (sight, sound, smell, touch, taste)
  • Helps us visualize the scene and characters vividly
  • Makes the poem more engaging and relatable

🎙️ Imagery is when a poet uses descriptive language to create vivid pictures in our minds. It appeals to our senses – sight, sound, smell, taste, and touch. In 'Somebody's Mother', words like 'ragged and old' or 'chill and deep' create strong images that help us connect with the poem.
