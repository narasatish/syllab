Rhyme Scheme: AABB

  • Each stanza follows an AABB pattern
  • First two lines rhyme, next two rhyme
  • Creates a smooth, musical flow

🎙️ The poem uses a simple and consistent AABB rhyme scheme. This means the first and second lines of each stanza rhyme, and the third and fourth lines rhyme. This pattern makes the poem easy to read and remember, giving it a gentle, flowing rhythm.
