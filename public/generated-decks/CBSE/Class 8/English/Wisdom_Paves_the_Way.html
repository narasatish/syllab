Understanding Wisdom: What is it?

  • More than just knowing facts.
  • Ability to apply knowledge and experience.
  • Good judgment and sound decision-making.

🎙️ Wisdom isn't just about how many facts you know. It's about using what you know, your experiences, and your understanding to make good decisions and judgments in different situations. It's about thinking deeply before acting.

Wisdom vs. Knowledge: The Key Difference

  • Knowledge: Acquiring facts and information.
  • Wisdom: Applying knowledge with good judgment.
  • Knowledge is knowing a tomato is a fruit; wisdom is not putting it in a fruit salad.

🎙️ While knowledge is about gathering information, wisdom is about how you use that information. Think of it this way: knowing facts is like having ingredients, but wisdom is knowing how to cook a delicious meal with them. One is about 'what,' the other is about 'how' and 'why'.

Cultivating Wisdom: Step 1 - Observe & Listen

  • Pay close attention to your surroundings.
  • Listen carefully to others' advice and experiences.
  • Don't rush to conclusions.

🎙️ The first step to becoming wise is to be a keen observer and a good listener. Take time to notice details, understand different perspectives, and truly hear what others are saying. Don't jump to conclusions; instead, gather information.

Cultivating Wisdom: Step 2 - Think Critically

  • Consider different outcomes.
