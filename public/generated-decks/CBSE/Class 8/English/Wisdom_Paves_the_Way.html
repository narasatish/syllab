Vocabulary of Wisdom

  • Prudent: Acting with care and foresight.
  • Sagacious: Having profound wisdom and good judgment.
  • Astute: Shrewd and clever, especially in practical matters.
  • Discerning: Showing good judgment, perceptive.

🎙️ Here are some words you can use to describe wisdom and wise people. 'Prudent' means being careful about the future. 'Sagacious' means having deep understanding. 'Astute' describes someone who is sharp and clever in practical situations. 'Discerning' means having excellent judgment.
