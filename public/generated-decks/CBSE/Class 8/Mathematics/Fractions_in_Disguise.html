  • Write as fraction with power of 10 denominator.
  • Simplify the fraction.

🎙️ To convert a decimal to a fraction, first count the number of digits after the decimal point. This count tells you the power of 10 for your denominator. Finally, simplify the resulting fraction to its lowest terms.

Decoding Ratios

  • A ratio a:b can be written as the fraction a/b.
  • The second quantity (b) cannot be zero.
  • Always simplify the fraction.

🎙️ When you have a ratio a:b, it directly translates to the fraction a/b. It's crucial that the second quantity, b, is not zero. Just like other fractions, always simplify ratios to their lowest terms.

Example 2: Simplifying 15:20

  • Ratio 15:20 becomes fraction 15/20.
  • Divide by GCD (5) for both numbers.
  • Simplified fraction is 3/4.

🎙️ To simplify 15/20, we find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 15 and 20, which is 5. Dividing both the numerator and denominator by 5 gives us the simplified fraction 3/4. So, the ratio in simplest form is 3:4.

Example: The Tricky Tank

  • Tank 1/4 full, +10 litres makes it 1/2 full.
  • Difference: 1/2 - 1/4 = 1/4 of tank capacity.
  • So, 1/4 capacity = 10 litres.
  • Total capacity = 40 litres.

🎙️ This problem requires us to find the fraction that the 10 litres represents. By subtracting the initial fraction from the final fraction, we find that 10 litres is 1/4 of the tank's capacity. From there, we can easily calculate the total capacity.

2+10 litres makes it 1/2 full
3Difference: 1/2 - 1/4 = 1/4

Beware! Common Pitfalls

🎙️ Always double-check your answers, especially for simplification. In word problems, clearly identify the 'whole' and the 'part' before forming your fraction. The word 'of' usually implies multiplication in fraction problems.
