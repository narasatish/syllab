What are "Perfect Squares"?

  • Natural numbers that are squares of integers.
  • Examples: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25...
  • They don't have decimal parts.

🎙️ A perfect square is simply a natural number that can be obtained by squaring another whole number. For instance, 9 is a perfect square because it's 3 squared. Numbers like 2, 3, 5 are not perfect squares.

Interesting Properties of Square Numbers

  • End with 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, or 9.
  • Never end with 2, 3, 7, or 8.
  • Even numbers have even squares.

🎙️ Square numbers have some cool properties. For example, if a number ends in 2, 3, 7, or 8, it can never be a perfect square! Also, the square of an even number is always even.

How to Check if a Number is a "Perfect Square"

  • Look at the last digit first.
  • Use prime factorization.
  • Pair up prime factors.

🎙️ To determine if a number is a perfect square, you can first check its last digit. If it passes that test, a more rigorous method is to find its prime factors. If all prime factors appear in pairs, then it's a perfect square.

1Check last digit (0,1,4,5,6,9?)
2Prime factorize the number
3Are all factors in pairs?

What are "Cube Numbers"?

  • Result of multiplying a number by itself three times.
  • Also called 'perfect cubes'.
  • Denoted by a superscript '3'.

🎙️ Similar to square numbers, cube numbers are obtained by multiplying a number by itself, but this time, you do it three times. It's like finding the volume of a cube with that number as its side length.

³
Power of Three
14 / 25

Method: Finding a Cube Number

  • Multiply the number by itself, then by itself again.
  • Example: 2 cubed = 2 x 2 x 2 = 8.
  • Three multiplications!

🎙️ To find a cube number, you multiply the number by itself three times. For example, if you want to find the cube of 4, you calculate 4 times 4 times 4.

1Choose a number
2Multiply it by itself
3Multiply the result by the original number

How to Check if a Number is a "Perfect Cube"

  • Use prime factorization.
  • Group prime factors into triplets.
  • All factors must be in triplets.

🎙️ To check if a number is a perfect cube, the most reliable method is prime factorization. You need to break down the number into its prime factors. If all these factors can be grouped into sets of three, then it is a perfect cube.

1Prime factorize the number
2Group factors into triplets
3Are all factors in triplets?

🎙️ Let's quickly compare squares and cubes. The main difference lies in how many times you multiply the number by itself – twice for squares and thrice for cubes, representing 2D area versus 3D volume.
