The Distance Formula

  • Distance d = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]
  • Memorize this important formula!

🎙️ So, if we have two points (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂), the distance 'd' between them is given by this formula. It's essentially applying Pythagoras, where the horizontal and vertical differences are the legs of the triangle.

Deriving the Section Formula (Internal Division)

  • Uses similar triangles concept.
  • Relates ratios of sides to coordinates.

🎙️ The Section Formula can be derived using the concept of similar triangles. By drawing parallel lines and constructing triangles, we can establish a relationship between the ratio of the segments and the coordinates of the dividing point.

Similar Triangles

The Section Formula (Internal Division)

  • Point P(x, y) divides A(x₁, y₁) and B(x₂, y₂)
  • Ratio m:n => x = (mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n)
  • y = (my₂ + ny₁)/(m+n)

🎙️ Here is the Section Formula for internal division. If a point P divides a line segment AB in the ratio m:n, then its coordinates (x, y) are calculated using these expressions. Pay attention to the cross-multiplication with the ratios.

Special Case: The Midpoint Formula

  • When the ratio is 1:1.
  • x = (x₁ + x₂)/2
  • y = (y₁ + y₂)/2

🎙️ The Midpoint Formula is a special and simpler case of the Section Formula. When a point divides a line segment exactly in half, the ratio m:n becomes 1:1. This simplifies the formula to finding the average of the coordinates.

Area of a Triangle Formula

  • Vertices: A(x₁, y₁), B(x₂, y₂), C(x₃, y₃)
  • Area = ½ |x₁(y₂ - y₃) + x₂(y₃ - y₁) + x₃(y₁ - y₂)|
  • Absolute value ensures positive area.

🎙️ This formula might look a bit complex, but it's very systematic. Just remember the pattern of x and (y-y) terms, and the cyclic order of the subscripts (1,2,3,1,2,3...). The absolute value is crucial because area is always positive.

Recap: Important Formulas

  • Distance Formula: √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]
  • Section Formula: ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m+n))
  • Midpoint Formula: ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)
  • Area of Triangle: ½ |x₁(y₂ - y₃) + x₂(y₃ - y₁) + x₃(y₁ - y₂)|
