What is a Triangle?

  • A polygon with three sides.
  • Has three vertices and three angles.
  • The sum of interior angles is always 180°.

🎙️ A triangle is the simplest polygon, defined by three line segments meeting at three points. These points are called vertices, and the segments are sides. Remember, the angles inside always add up to 180 degrees.

Elements of a Triangle

  • Vertices: A, B, C (corners)
  • Sides: AB, BC, CA (line segments)
  • Angles: ∠A, ∠B, ∠C (formed at vertices)

🎙️ Every triangle has three main components: its vertices, which are the points where the sides meet; its sides, which are the lines connecting the vertices; and its angles, formed at each vertex.

Types of Triangles (by Sides)

  • Equilateral: All 3 sides equal.
  • Isosceles: 2 sides equal.
  • Scalene: No sides equal.

🎙️ Triangles can be classified based on the lengths of their sides. An equilateral triangle is perfectly symmetrical, an isosceles has two equal sides, and a scalene triangle has all different side lengths.

Types of Triangles (by Angles)

  • Acute-angled: All angles < 90°.
  • Right-angled: One angle = 90°.
  • Obtuse-angled: One angle > 90°.

🎙️ We can also classify triangles by their angles. An acute triangle has all sharp angles. A right triangle has one perfect corner. An obtuse triangle has one wide, open angle.

Congruent Figures

  • Figures that have the exact same shape and size.
  • They can be perfectly superimposed on each other.
  • Symbol: ≅

🎙️ Congruent figures are like identical twins in geometry. If you can place one figure exactly over another, and they match perfectly, they are congruent. Think of two identical stamps.

Similar Figures

  • Figures that have the same shape but not necessarily the same size.
  • One figure is an enlargement or reduction of the other.
  • Symbol: ~

🎙️ Similar figures share the same shape but can be different sizes. Think of a photograph and its enlarged print. They look alike, but one is bigger than the other.

Congruence vs. Similarity

  • Congruent: Same shape AND same size.
  • Similar: Same shape, DIFFERENT size possible.

🎙️ The key difference is size. All congruent figures are also similar, but not all similar figures are congruent. Congruence is a special case of similarity where the scale factor is 1.

Criteria for Similarity of Triangles: AA / AAA

  • If two angles of one triangle are respectively equal to two angles of another triangle, then the triangles are similar (AA Similarity).
  • If all three angles are equal, they are also similar (AAA Similarity).

🎙️ The AA similarity criterion is very powerful. If you know just two pairs of corresponding angles are equal, the third pair must also be equal, hence the triangles are similar. This is often used in proofs.

🅰️
Angle-Angle Similarity

Criteria for Similarity of Triangles: SSS

  • If the corresponding sides of two triangles are proportional, then their corresponding angles are equal, and hence the triangles are similar (SSS Similarity).
  • Sides must be in the same ratio.

🎙️ SSS similarity means that if the ratio of all three corresponding sides is the same, then the triangles are similar. This implies that their shapes are identical, just scaled versions of each other.

↔️
Side-Side-Side Similarity
13 / 25

Criteria for Similarity of Triangles: SAS

  • If one angle of a triangle is equal to one angle of the other triangle and the sides including these angles are proportional, then the two triangles are similar (SAS Similarity).

🎙️ SAS similarity is useful when you have one pair of equal angles and the sides forming that angle are in proportion. This combination is enough to guarantee similarity between the triangles.

Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT) / Thales Theorem

  • If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides, then it divides the two sides proportionally.
  • This theorem is fundamental for similar triangles.

🎙️ The Basic Proportionality Theorem, also known as Thales Theorem, is a cornerstone of this chapter. It explains how a parallel line within a triangle creates proportional segments on the other two sides.

Worked Example: Applying BPT

  • In ΔABC, DE || BC.
  • If AD = 2 cm, DB = 4 cm, AE = 3 cm.
  • Find EC.

🎙️ Using BPT, we know that AD/DB = AE/EC. Plugging in the values: 2/4 = 3/EC. This simplifies to 1/2 = 3/EC, so EC = 6 cm. Simple, right?

Converse of Basic Proportionality Theorem

  • If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line must be parallel to the third side.
  • It's the reverse of the BPT statement.

🎙️ The converse of BPT is equally important. If you observe that a line divides two sides proportionally, you can conclude that this line is parallel to the third side. It helps us prove lines are parallel.

Areas of Similar Triangles Theorem

  • The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.
  • Area(ΔABC) / Area(ΔDEF) = (AB/DE)² = (BC/EF)² = (CA/FD)²

🎙️ This theorem provides a powerful link between the linear dimensions and the area of similar triangles. If sides are in a ratio of 1:2, their areas will be in a ratio of 1:4.

Pythagoras Theorem (Recap)

  • In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.
  • a² + b² = c² (where c is the hypotenuse).

🎙️ We've studied Pythagoras Theorem before, and it's essential for right-angled triangles. It helps us find unknown side lengths when we know two sides of a right triangle.

📐
Right angle rule

Converse of Pythagoras Theorem

  • If in a triangle, the square of one side is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides, then the angle opposite the first side is a right angle.
  • Helps to prove a triangle is right-angled.

🎙️ Just like BPT, Pythagoras also has a converse. If you can show that a² + b² = c² holds true for a triangle, then you can confidently say that the angle opposite side 'c' is 90 degrees.

Recap: Key Takeaways

  • Triangles are fundamental shapes with various types.
  • Congruent means same shape and size; Similar means same shape, different size.
  • AA/SSS/SAS are similarity criteria.
  • BPT and its converse deal with proportional sides.
  • Pythagoras Theorem for right triangles and its converse.
