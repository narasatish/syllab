  • An ancient practice from India.
  • Connects your body, mind, and spirit.
  • Helps you feel calm and strong.

🎙️ Yoga comes from a Sanskrit word 'Yuj', meaning to unite or join. It's about bringing different parts of yourself together to feel whole. Think of it as a special way to tune your body and mind.

Beginner Pose: Tadasana (Mountain Pose)

  • Stand tall, feet together.
  • Lift arms overhead, interlock fingers.
  • Stretch upwards, balancing on toes.

🎙️ Tadasana, or Mountain Pose, is a simple yet powerful pose. It helps improve your posture and balance, making you feel grounded and strong like a mountain. Remember to breathe deeply as you hold the pose.

Beginner Pose: Vrikshasana (Tree Pose)

  • Stand on one leg, bend other knee.
  • Place foot on inner thigh of standing leg.
  • Bring hands together in Namaste mudra.

🎙️ Vrikshasana, or Tree Pose, is fantastic for improving your balance and concentration. It teaches you to find stability even when standing on one leg. Don't worry if you wobble; practice makes perfect!
