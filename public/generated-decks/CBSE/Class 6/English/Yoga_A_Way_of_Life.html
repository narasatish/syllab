Pranayama: Breathing Control

  • Techniques for controlling breath.
  • Increases energy and calms the mind.
  • Connects breath to overall well-being.

🎙️ Pranayama refers to special breathing exercises. Our breath is very powerful, and learning to control it can significantly impact our energy levels and how calm or focused we feel.

Deep Breathing
11 / 25

Example: Bhramari (Humming Bee Breath)

  • Close ears with thumbs, fingers over eyes.
  • Inhale deeply, exhale slowly making a humming sound.
  • Helps relieve stress and quiet the mind.

🎙️ Bhramari, or Humming Bee Breath, is a wonderful technique. The gentle humming sound helps to soothe the nervous system and is very effective for reducing stress and finding inner peace.

2Inhale

Meditation: Mindful Focus

  • Focusing the mind on a single point.
  • Helps develop awareness and clarity.
  • Reduces mental chatter and promotes peace.

🎙️ Meditation is about training your mind to focus. It's not about emptying your mind, but learning to observe your thoughts without getting carried away by them, leading to greater clarity and calm.

🧘‍♀️
Quiet Mind
13 / 25

Example: Simple Meditation Focus

  • Sit comfortably, close your eyes gently.
  • Focus on the sensation of your breath.
  • When thoughts arise, gently return to your breath.

🎙️ A simple way to meditate is to focus on your breath. Just notice the air entering and leaving your body. If your mind wanders, that's okay, just gently bring your attention back to your breath.

1Sit Comfortably
2Close Eyes
3Focus on Breath
