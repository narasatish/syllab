The Power of Neem Leaves

  • Used in traditional medicine
  • Good for skin problems
  • Keeps insects away

🎙️ The leaves of the Neem tree are incredibly powerful. People have used them for centuries in traditional remedies, especially for skin issues. They also have a special scent that helps keep many insects away naturally!

Sweet & Bitter Neem Fruits

  • Small, round, yellowish-green fruits
  • Taste bitter but become sweet when ripe
  • Used for various health benefits

🎙️ Neem trees also produce small, round fruits, often called Neem berries or Neem Goli. When they are raw, they are quite bitter, but as they ripen, they turn a yellowish-green and can even taste a little sweet, offering their own health benefits.

🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢
Neem Berries

Benefits of Neem Bark

  • The outer covering of the tree
  • Used for dental hygiene
  • Has antiseptic properties

🎙️ Even the bark of the Neem tree is useful! It's the tough, outer layer that protects the tree. People traditionally chew on Neem twigs, which are branches with bark, to clean their teeth because it has natural antiseptic qualities.

🪵
Strong Bark

The Versatile Neem Oil

  • Extracted from Neem seeds
  • Used in cosmetics and medicines
  • A natural pest repellent

🎙️ Neem oil is a very precious product, extracted from the seeds of the Neem fruit. It's a golden-yellow liquid used in many beauty products, medicines, and is also an excellent natural way to keep pests away from plants and even pets.

Pure Neem Oil

Neem in Ayurveda

  • Used for purification and healing

🎙️ Ayurveda, our traditional Indian medicine system, has valued Neem for thousands of years. It's considered a powerful herb for cleansing the body, healing various ailments, and promoting overall well-being.
