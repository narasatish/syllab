Key Word: Sturdy

  • Meaning: Strong and solid, not easily broken.
  • Example: A sturdy wooden chair can last for years.
  • Antonym: Fragile, weak.

🎙️ Our first important word is 'sturdy'. When we say a chair is sturdy, it means it's built to last and won't break easily. Think of a strong tree trunk; that's sturdy!

Key Word: Antique

  • Meaning: An object of considerable age, valued for its aesthetic or historical significance.

🎙️ Our third vocabulary word is 'antique'. An antique item is very old, but not just old – it's special because of its age, beauty, or history. Many old chairs are considered antiques and are quite valuable.

Does a Chair Have Feelings?

  • Sometimes authors give objects human qualities.
  • This is called personification.

🎙️ In stories, authors often make inanimate objects like chairs seem alive. They might describe the chair as 'tired' or 'proud'. This literary device, called personification, helps us connect with the object on a deeper level.
