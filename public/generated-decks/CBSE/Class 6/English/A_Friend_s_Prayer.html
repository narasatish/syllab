What is a Prayer?

  • Talking to a higher power or expressing deep wishes.
  • A way to find peace, strength, and guidance.

  • Putting yourself in someone else's shoes.
  • Trying to understand their feelings and perspective.
  • It helps us connect deeply with others.

🎙️ Empathy is a superpower! It allows us to feel what others are feeling, even if we haven't experienced it ourselves. It's the first step towards truly helping and supporting a friend.

Example: Showing Empathy

  • Your friend is sad because they lost a game.
  • You comfort them, saying, 'I understand how you feel. It's okay to be sad.'
  • You offer to play with them again later.

🎙️ When a friend is upset, simply saying 'I understand' and offering a hug or a kind word can make a huge difference. That's empathy in action – making them feel seen and heard.

Vocabulary Corner: Words of Wisdom

  • Solace: Comfort or consolation in a time of distress.
  • Cherish: To hold dear; to care for lovingly.
  • Guidance: Direction or advice about how to do something.

🎙️ Understanding new words helps us appreciate the beauty of the text. 'Solace' is what a friend offers when you're sad, 'cherish' is how you feel about their friendship, and 'guidance' is the advice they give.

More Vocabulary: Expanding Our Minds

  • Empathy: The ability to understand and share the feelings of another.
  • Compassion: Sympathetic pity and concern for the sufferings or misfortunes of others.
  • Virtue: Behavior showing high moral standards.

🎙️ These words are central to the themes of our chapter. Remember, 'empathy' and 'compassion' are about understanding and caring, while 'virtue' describes the good qualities we strive for.

Literary Device: Rhyme Scheme

  • The pattern of rhymes at the end of lines in a poem.
  • Helps create rhythm and musicality.
  • Look for words that sound alike. (e.g., 'day' and 'way')

🎙️ If 'A Friend's Prayer' is a poem, pay attention to how the words at the end of the lines sound. This 'rhyme scheme' adds a beautiful musical quality to the prayer and makes it memorable.

Literary Device: Imagery

  • Language that appeals to our five senses (sight, sound, smell, taste, touch).

  • Makes the text more vivid and engaging.

🎙️ Authors use imagery to make us feel like we are right there in the story or poem. When you read, try to imagine what the words describe – the colours, sounds, and feelings.
