Vocabulary Explorer: Part 1

  • **Exile**: Forced absence from one's home country.
  • **Abduction**: The action of forcibly taking someone away.
  • **Quest**: A long or arduous search for something.

🎙️ Understanding these words helps us grasp the story better. 'Exile' describes Rama's time in the forest, 'abduction' is what happened to Sita, and Rama's journey to find her was a 'quest'.
