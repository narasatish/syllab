Introducing the National War Memorial

  • Located in New Delhi, India.
  • Honours soldiers who fought for India.
  • A symbol of national pride.

🎙️ The National War Memorial, or NWM, is a very important place in our capital city, New Delhi. It was built to remember and honour all the brave Indian soldiers who have given their lives to protect our nation since independence. It stands as a powerful symbol of our country's gratitude.

  • Those who died defending India.

🎙️ The National War Memorial is dedicated to all the soldiers of the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force. It remembers those who made the ultimate sacrifice, laying down their lives to keep our country safe and free. Their names are etched into the memorial as a permanent tribute.

Its Unique Design: The 'Chakra Vyuh'

  • Inspired by an ancient Indian formation.
  • Consists of four concentric circles.
  • Each circle has a special meaning.

🎙️ The design of the National War Memorial is very unique and meaningful. It's inspired by the 'Chakra Vyuh', a famous circular formation from ancient Indian warfare. This design has four main circles, each telling a part of our soldiers' story.

The Amar Jawan Jyoti

  • The 'Eternal Flame of the Immortal Soldier'.
  • Burns continuously at the memorial's centre.
  • Symbolises the immortal spirit of soldiers.

🎙️ At the very heart of the memorial, you'll find the Amar Jawan Jyoti. This is an eternal flame, meaning it never goes out. It represents the undying spirit of our soldiers, reminding us that their sacrifice will never be forgotten and their spirit lives on.

NWM vs. India Gate

  • India Gate: Built by British, WWI soldiers.
  • NWM: Built by India, post-independence soldiers.
  • Both honour soldiers, but for different eras.

🎙️ Many people confuse the National War Memorial with India Gate, which is nearby. India Gate was built by the British to honour soldiers of World War I. The NWM, however, is a modern memorial built by independent India specifically for soldiers who have fought for our nation since 1947.
