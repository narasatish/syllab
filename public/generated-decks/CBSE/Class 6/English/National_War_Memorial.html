Four Main Chakras of the Memorial

  • The memorial has four concentric circles.
  • Each circle is called a 'Chakra'.
  • They represent different aspects of a soldier's life.

🎙️ The National War Memorial is designed in a very special way, with four main circles, or 'Chakras'. Each Chakra tells a different part of the story of our brave soldiers and their service to the nation.

1Amar Chakra
2Veerta Chakra
3Tyag Chakra
4Rakshak Chakra
9 / 25

Amar Chakra: The Eternal Flame

  • The innermost circle, meaning 'Immortal Circle'.
  • It has an 'Eternal Flame' (Amar Jawan Jyoti).
  • Symbolizes the undying spirit of soldiers.

🎙️ The Amar Chakra is the first and most important circle. It means 'Circle of Immortality'. Here, an eternal flame burns day and night, reminding us that the spirit of our fallen soldiers will live forever.

🔥
Eternal Flame
10 / 25

Veerta Chakra: The Circle of Bravery

  • The second circle, meaning 'Circle of Bravery'.
  • It has six bronze murals.
  • These murals depict famous battles fought by Indian Army.

🎙️ Next, we have the Veerta Chakra, or 'Circle of Bravery'. This section features beautiful bronze murals that show us important battles where our soldiers showed immense courage. It's like looking at history through art.

🖼️
Battle Scenes
11 / 25

Tyag Chakra: The Circle of Sacrifice

  • The third circle, meaning 'Circle of Sacrifice'.
  • Walls are inscribed with names of fallen soldiers.
  • A tribute to those who gave their lives.

🎙️ The Tyag Chakra, or 'Circle of Sacrifice', is a very moving part. Its walls are carved with the names of thousands of soldiers who sacrificed their lives for our country. It's a powerful reminder of their ultimate gift.

📜
Names of Heroes
12 / 25

Rakshak Chakra: The Circle of Protection

  • The outermost circle, meaning 'Circle of Protection'.
  • It has tall trees planted in rows.
  • These trees represent the soldiers who protect our nation.

🎙️ Finally, we come to the Rakshak Chakra, the 'Circle of Protection'. This circle has many tall trees, standing strong and tall, just like our soldiers who stand guard and protect our borders, keeping us safe.

🌳🌳🌳🌳🌳🌳🌳
Guardians
