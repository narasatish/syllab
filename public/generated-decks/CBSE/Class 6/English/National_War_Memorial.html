The Design: Four Concentric Circles (Chakras)

  • Designed as four circles, each with a special meaning.
  • Represents different values: Immortality, Bravery, Sacrifice, Protection.
  • Like layers of an onion, each circle tells a story.

🎙️ The memorial's design is very symbolic. It's built as four concentric circles, one inside the other, which we call 'Chakras'. Each Chakra represents a different important value related to our soldiers.

9 / 30

Amar Chakra: The Circle of Immortality

  • The innermost circle.
  • Houses the 'Amar Jawan Jyoti' (Eternal Flame).
  • Symbolizes the undying spirit of fallen soldiers.

🎙️ The Amar Chakra is the very first circle you see. It holds the 'Amar Jawan Jyoti', an eternal flame that burns day and night. This flame reminds us that even though our soldiers are gone, their spirit and sacrifice live forever.

🔥
Eternal Flame
10 / 30

Veerta Chakra: The Circle of Bravery

  • Next circle outwards from Amar Chakra.
  • Features bronze murals depicting battles.
  • Shows stories of soldiers' courage in action.

🎙️ Moving outwards, we find the Veerta Chakra. This circle showcases beautiful bronze murals, like large comic book panels, that tell stories of the brave battles fought by our soldiers. It's a visual tribute to their courage.

🛡️
Shield of Bravery

Tyag Chakra: The Circle of Sacrifice

  • The third circle.
  • Walls inscribed with names of over 25,000 fallen soldiers.
  • A solemn tribute to their ultimate sacrifice.

🎙️ The Tyag Chakra is a very moving part of the memorial. Its walls are inscribed with the names of over 25,000 soldiers who made the ultimate sacrifice for our nation. It's like a grand honour board, ensuring every hero is remembered.

Rakshak Chakra: The Circle of Protection

  • The outermost circle.
  • Comprises over 600 trees.
  • Symbolizes soldiers guarding the nation, like a protective fence.

🎙️ Finally, the Rakshak Chakra forms the outermost circle. It has more than 600 trees, which stand tall and strong, just like our soldiers who guard our borders and protect our country. They symbolize the ever-vigilant protectors.

Protective Trees

The Memorial Obelisk

  • Like a towering pillar of strength.

🎙️ At the very centre of the memorial complex stands a tall, striking obelisk. This pillar represents the supreme sacrifice made by our soldiers, standing as a constant reminder of their unwavering dedication.

Tall Pillar

Param Yodha Sthal: Hall of Heroes

  • Area dedicated to Param Vir Chakra recipients.
  • Features busts (statues) of these brave individuals.
  • India's highest wartime gallantry award.

🎙️ Near the main memorial, there's a special area called the Param Yodha Sthal. It's like a hall of fame for India's bravest soldiers, those who received the Param Vir Chakra – our country's highest award for bravery in war. You can see their statues there.

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Confusing the National War Memorial with India Gate.
  • Forgetting the names and significance of the four Chakras.
  • Not understanding the Eternal Flame's symbolism.

🎙️ Sometimes students get confused. Remember, the NWM is a separate, newer memorial dedicated specifically to fallen soldiers, while India Gate is an older war memorial. Also, pay attention to the specific meaning of each Chakra!
