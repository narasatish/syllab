Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing plot with theme: Plot is 'what happens', theme is 'the message'.
  • Not identifying character motivation: Why do characters do what they do?
  • Summarising without details: Always include key events and feelings.

🎙️ When discussing stories, students sometimes mix up the plot with the theme. Remember, the plot is the sequence of actions, while the theme is the big idea. Also, try to understand *why* characters act the way they do, and always provide specific details when explaining parts of the story.
