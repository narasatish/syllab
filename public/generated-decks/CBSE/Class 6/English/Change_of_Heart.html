What Does “Change of Heart” Mean?

  • It means a big shift in someone's feelings or attitude.
  • Often, it's changing from being unkind to kind.
  • From being selfish to being generous.

🎙️ A 'change of heart' is not about your physical heart, but about your inner self – your thoughts and feelings. It's when someone decides to stop doing bad things and start doing good things, like being kinder or more helpful.

💖
A New Way

Reasons for a Change of Heart

  • Learning from mistakes or bad experiences.
  • Seeing the consequences of one's actions.
  • Being inspired by others' kindness or wisdom.

🎙️ Why do people change? Sometimes, they realise their actions are hurting others or themselves. Other times, a powerful event or a kind person can make them think differently and want to improve.

Empathy: Stepping into Others' Shoes

  • A change of heart helps us understand how others feel.
  • It teaches us to see things from different perspectives.
  • This understanding leads to compassion and better relationships.

🎙️ When our heart changes, we start to think about how our actions affect others. This ability to understand and share the feelings of another is called empathy. It's a very important part of being a good friend and a good person.

Ashoka: The Ruthless Conqueror

  • Initially, Ashoka was known for his fierce ambition.

The Kalinga War: A Turning Point

  • After the devastating Kalinga War, Ashoka saw the destruction.
  • He witnessed the sorrow of countless widows and orphans.
  • This horrific sight shocked him to his core and made him reflect.

🎙️ The Kalinga War was a terrible battle. When Ashoka saw the battlefield afterwards, filled with death and suffering, he was deeply moved. The immense pain he witnessed made him question everything he had done.

Ashoka: The Peaceful Ruler

  • Ashoka decided to give up war forever.
  • He embraced Buddhism and promoted peace and non-violence.
  • He worked for the welfare of his people and spread kindness.

🎙️ After the Kalinga War, Ashoka completely changed. He became a follower of Buddhism and dedicated his life to promoting peace, kindness, and welfare for all. He built hospitals, dug wells, and spread messages of non-violence throughout his empire. His change of heart transformed an entire nation.
