Defining 'Change of Heart'

  • A significant shift in someone's feelings.
  • Or their attitude towards something or someone.
  • It means changing from one way of thinking to another.

🎙️ So, a 'change of heart' means a big change in how someone feels or thinks. It's not just a small mood swing, but a deep change in their core attitude or belief. It's like turning a page in a book and starting a new chapter.

Reasons for a Change of Heart

  • Gaining new understanding or knowledge.
  • Experiencing something impactful.
  • Developing empathy for others.
  • Realizing consequences of actions.

🎙️ People's hearts change for many reasons. Sometimes, it's because they learn something new. Other times, it's an experience that makes them see things differently. Empathy, or understanding another person's feelings, is also a powerful reason. And sometimes, it's seeing the bad results of their own actions.

Showing, Not Telling

  • Through a character's dialogue (what they say).
  • Through their actions (what they do).
  • Through their thoughts and feelings (what they think/feel).
  • Through how other characters react to them.

🎙️ Authors use several ways to show a character's transformation. Pay attention to their words – do they speak differently? Look at their actions – do they start doing things they never did before? And sometimes, the story will even reveal their inner thoughts and feelings. Also, notice how other characters start treating them differently.

Words Before the Change

  • Selfish: only thinking about oneself.
  • Stubborn: unwilling to change one's mind.
  • Grumpy: bad-tempered and irritable.
  • Mean: unkind or unfair.

🎙️ Often, characters who undergo a change of heart start with traits that aren't very positive. Words like 'selfish,' 'stubborn,' 'grumpy,' or 'mean' describe these initial behaviours. It's important to recognise these starting points.

Words After the Change

🎙️ After a change of heart, characters usually display more positive traits. They might become 'generous,' 'kind,' 'helpful,' or 'empathethic.' These words help us describe the transformation they've undergone.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing temporary mood with true change.
  • Ignoring subtle clues of change.
  • Not considering the 'why' behind the change.

🎙️ Sometimes, students confuse a character being temporarily sad or happy with a true change of heart. Remember, it's a significant shift! Also, don't miss the small clues authors give, and always ask yourself 'why' the character changed.

⚠️
Be Careful!

Exam Tips for 'Change of Heart'

  • Look for 'before' and 'after' descriptions.
  • Identify the 'trigger' event for the change.
  • Use specific examples from the story in your answers.
  • Explain the impact of the change on the character and others.

🎙️ In exams, questions about 'change of heart' are common. Always describe the character before and after the change. Pinpoint the exact event that caused the change. Support your answers with details from the story and explain how the change affected everyone.

Recap: What We Learned

  • 'Change of Heart' is a deep shift in attitude.
  • Reasons include new understanding, empathy, experience.
  • Authors show change through actions, dialogue, thoughts.
  • It creates a ripple effect, impacting others.

🎙️ Let's quickly recap! We learned that a 'change of heart' is a significant transformation in a character's feelings or beliefs. It's often sparked by new experiences, understanding, or empathy, and authors cleverly show this through what characters say and do. Remember, these changes almost always affect others too.
