What are Spices?

  • Parts of plants used for flavouring food.
  • Come from seeds, fruits, roots, bark, or flowers.
  • Used in small quantities to enhance taste.

🎙️ Spices are essentially parts of plants! Think about it – some are seeds like cumin, some are roots like ginger, and some are bark like cinnamon. They are powerful little things that add amazing taste to our dishes.

Spice Spotlight: Turmeric (Haldi)

  • A bright yellow spice, a staple in Indian cooking.
  • Comes from the root of the turmeric plant.
  • Often called 'Indian Saffron' for its colour.

🎙️ First up is Turmeric, or Haldi as we call it in India. You see its beautiful golden colour in almost every curry. It's not just for colour; it's a powerhouse of health benefits.

💛
Golden Spice

Turmeric's Healing Powers

  • Anti-inflammatory: Helps reduce swelling and pain.
  • Antiseptic: Kills germs and prevents infection.
  • Good for skin health and healing wounds.

🎙️ Turmeric is like a tiny superhero! It can help reduce swelling if you hurt yourself and acts as an antiseptic, meaning it helps prevent infections. That's why your grandmother might put it on a cut!

Real Example: Turmeric Milk (Haldi Doodh)

  • Warm milk with a pinch of turmeric.

🎙️ Many of you might have had Haldi Doodh when you were sick. It's a wonderful remedy! The turmeric in the milk helps calm your throat and makes you feel better, especially during cold weather.
