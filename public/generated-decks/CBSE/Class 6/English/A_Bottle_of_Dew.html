What is Dew?

  • Tiny water droplets.
  • Formed on cool surfaces overnight.
  • Often seen on leaves and grass.

🎙️ Before we dive into the story, let's understand what 'dew' is. Dew is those tiny, glistening drops of water you sometimes see on leaves and grass early in the morning. It forms when the air cools down overnight, and water vapour condenses.
