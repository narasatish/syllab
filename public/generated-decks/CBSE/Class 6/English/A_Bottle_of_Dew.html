How Does Dew Form?

  • Air cools down at night.
  • Water vapour turns to liquid.
  • Forms drops on surfaces.

🎙️ Scientifically, dew forms when the ground and objects cool down overnight, making the air directly above them cooler too. This cool air can't hold as much moisture, so the excess water vapour condenses into tiny liquid drops.
