What is Dew?

  • Tiny water droplets on cool surfaces.
  • Forms overnight from moisture in the air.
  • Appears like tiny pearls on leaves.

🎙️ Imagine waking up early in the morning and seeing tiny, shiny drops of water on grass blades or flower petals. That's dew! It's like nature's little jewels, formed when the air cools down and moisture settles.
