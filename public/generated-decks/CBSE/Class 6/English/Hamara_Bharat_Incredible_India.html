From Himalayas to Sunny Beaches

  • The mighty Himalayas in the North are the highest mountains.
  • The Thar Desert in the West is a vast sandy area.
  • The long coastline offers many beautiful beaches.

🎙️ For example, in the North, we have the magnificent Himalayas, home to some of the world's highest peaks. Travel to the West, and you'll find the expansive Thar Desert. Then, along the South, East, and West, we have stunning beaches like those in Goa and Kerala.

Many Tongues, One Nation

  • Hindi is the most widely spoken language.

The Taj Mahal and Red Fort

  • The Taj Mahal in Agra is a symbol of love and beauty.
  • The Red Fort in Delhi is a majestic example of Mughal architecture.
  • These are UNESCO World Heritage Sites, attracting visitors worldwide.

🎙️ The Taj Mahal, a breathtaking marble masterpiece, is known worldwide as a symbol of eternal love. And in Delhi, the grand Red Fort stands tall, reminding us of the Mughal Empire's power and artistry. These are just two examples of India's many wonders.

Bharatnatyam and Rangoli

  • Bharatnatyam is a classical dance form from Tamil Nadu.
  • Rangoli is a colourful folk art made on floors.
  • Both showcase India's artistic talent and cultural richness.

🎙️ Bharatnatyam, with its intricate footwork and hand gestures, is a classical dance that originated in Tamil Nadu. And you've probably seen Rangoli, beautiful patterns made with coloured powders, especially during festivals – a lovely folk art!

Home to Unique Animals

  • India has diverse wildlife, including many endangered species.
  • National parks and sanctuaries protect these animals.
  • Our forests are home to tigers, elephants, and rhinos.

🎙️ India is not just about people and culture; it's also a paradise for nature lovers! Our vast forests and national parks are home to magnificent animals like the Royal Bengal Tiger, elephants, and one-horned rhinos. It's important to protect them.

Majestic Bengal Tiger

  • The Royal Bengal Tiger is India's national animal.
  • It lives in various forests, like the Sundarbans.
  • Conservation efforts are crucial to protect these big cats.

🎙️ Our national animal, the majestic Royal Bengal Tiger, is a symbol of India's wild beauty. You can find them in national parks across the country, like Ranthambore or the Sundarbans, where we work hard to keep them safe.
