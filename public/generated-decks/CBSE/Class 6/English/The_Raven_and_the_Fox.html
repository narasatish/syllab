What is a Fable?

  • Short story with animals as characters
  • Teaches a moral lesson
  • Often shows human qualities in animals

🎙️ A fable is a special kind of story. It's usually short, and guess what? The characters are often animals who can talk and act like humans! The most important part is that every fable teaches us a lesson.

Key Elements of a Fable

  • Animal Characters
  • A Clear Moral
  • Simple Plot

🎙️ So, what makes a story a fable? First, you'll find animals talking and acting. Second, there's always a moral, a lesson about how to behave. And usually, the story is quite simple and easy to understand.

The Moral of the Story

  • Do not trust flatterers
  • Beware of those who praise you excessively
  • They often have selfish motives

🎙️ So, what's the big lesson here? The moral of 'The Raven and the Fox' is to be careful of people who flatter you too much. Their sweet words might be a trick to get something from you, just like the Fox tricked the Raven.

What is 'Flattery'?

  • Excessive or insincere praise
  • Often given to gain something
  • Not genuine admiration

🎙️ Flattery is when someone gives you a lot of compliments, maybe even too many, and they don't really mean them. They usually do it because they want something from you, not because they truly admire you.

🍬
Sweet Talk
15 / 25

Real-Life Flattery Example

  • Someone says, 'Your drawing is the best, you are a genius!'
  • Then asks, 'Can you do my homework for me?'
  • The praise was likely just to get help

🎙️ Imagine a classmate tells you, 'Wow, your drawing is absolutely amazing, you're such a genius!' And right after that, they ask, 'Can you please do my homework for me?' In this case, the 'amazing drawing' compliment might just be flattery to get you to do their work.

🤔

What is 'Pride'?

  • A feeling of deep pleasure or satisfaction
  • Can be good, but too much is bad
  • Can make you vulnerable to flattery

🎙️ Pride is a feeling of satisfaction, usually about something you've done or achieved. A little bit of pride is good, but too much can make you feel overly important or think you're better than others, which can be dangerous.

Literary Device: Personification

  • Giving human qualities to animals or objects
  • Fox talking, Raven singing are examples
  • Makes stories more engaging and relatable

🎙️ In fables like this, animals talk and act like humans. This is called 'personification'. It's a literary device that makes the story more fun and helps us understand human behaviors through animal characters.
