Reading Stanza 1: The Egg's World

  • I lived first in a little house,
  • And lived there very well;
  • I thought the world was small and round,
  • And made of pale blue shell.

🎙️ Let's read the first stanza together. Here, the bird is talking about its earliest life, inside the egg. Imagine being inside that tiny, cozy space. What do you think the bird's world felt like then?

  • Thought the world was small and round

🎙️ In this stanza, the bird describes its life inside the egg. It felt safe and thought that the entire world was just this small, round, pale blue shell. This shows us its limited perspective at that very early stage of life.

Reading Stanza 2: The Nest's World

Understanding Stanza 2: The Cozy Nest

  • Felt no desire to explore beyond it

Reading Stanza 3: The Tree's World

🎙️ In the third stanza, a big change happens! The bird finally leaves the nest. What does it discover now? Read carefully to see how its perception of the world changes once again.

Poetic Devices: Painting Pictures with Imagery
