Vocabulary: Wide vs. Small

  • Small: limited, confined, tiny (like the bird's first world).
  • Wide: vast, expansive, enormous (like the world outside).
  • These words show the contrast in the poem.

🎙️ The poem uses 'small' and 'wide' to highlight the dramatic change in the bird's perspective. Understanding these contrasting words helps us grasp the central message.

Poetic Device: Imagery
