Arithmetic Patterns (Addition/Subtraction)

  • Numbers increase or decrease by a constant value.
  • The constant value is called the 'common difference'.
  • Rule: Add or subtract the same number each time.

🎙️ In an arithmetic pattern, you either add the same number repeatedly or subtract the same number repeatedly. For example, in the sequence 2, 4, 6, 8... you are adding 2 each time. The 'common difference' here is 2.

Real-Life Arithmetic Pattern: Counting Steps

  • Counting steps while climbing stairs.
  • Each step adds 1 to your count.
  • Example: 1, 2, 3, 4, ...
  • The common difference is +1.

🎙️ Imagine climbing a staircase. As you go up each step, your step count increases by one. This is a very simple arithmetic pattern. You are constantly adding 1 to the previous number, making it easy to predict your total steps.

Worked Example: Arithmetic Pattern

  • Find the next two numbers in: 5, 10, 15, 20, __, __
  • Step 1: Find the difference between consecutive numbers.
  • Step 2: The difference is 10 - 5 = 5. So, the rule is 'add 5'.
  • Step 3: Apply the rule to find the next numbers.

🎙️ Let's solve this together. The difference between 10 and 5 is 5. Between 15 and 10 is also 5. So, the rule is to add 5. Adding 5 to 20 gives 25, and adding 5 to 25 gives 30. The next numbers are 25 and 30.

1Identify rule
2Add 5
325, 30

Geometric Patterns (Multiplication/Division)

  • Numbers increase or decrease by a constant factor.
  • The constant factor is called the 'common ratio'.
  • Rule: Multiply or divide by the same number each time.

🎙️ Geometric patterns are different because you multiply or divide by the same number. For instance, in the sequence 2, 4, 8, 16... you are multiplying by 2 each time. The common ratio here is 2.

Worked Example: Geometric Pattern

  • Find the next two numbers in: 3, 6, 12, 24, __, __
  • Step 1: Find the ratio between consecutive numbers.
  • Step 2: The ratio is 6 ÷ 3 = 2. So, the rule is 'multiply by 2'.
  • Step 3: Apply the rule to find the next numbers.

🎙️ Here, 6 divided by 3 is 2. 12 divided by 6 is 2. So, the rule is to multiply by 2. 24 multiplied by 2 is 48. 48 multiplied by 2 is 96. The next numbers are 48 and 96.

1Identify rule
2Multiply by 2
348, 96

Fibonacci Sequence: A Special Pattern

  • A unique number pattern.
  • Each number is the sum of the two preceding ones.
  • Starts with 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ...
  • Appears frequently in nature.

🎙️ The Fibonacci sequence is truly special! It's not about adding or multiplying a constant, but about adding the two numbers before to get the next one. It creates a beautiful spiral pattern often seen in nature.

Worked Example: Fibonacci Sequence

  • Find the next number in: 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, __
  • Step 1: Identify the rule for Fibonacci sequence.
  • Step 2: The rule is 'add the previous two numbers'.
  • Step 3: Add the last two numbers (3 and 5) to get the next.

🎙️ To find the next number in a Fibonacci sequence, we simply add the last two numbers. Here, the last two numbers are 5 and 8. So, 5 + 8 equals 13. The next number in the sequence is 13.

2Add 5 + 8
