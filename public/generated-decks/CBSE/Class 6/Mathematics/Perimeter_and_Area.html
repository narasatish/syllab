What is Perimeter?

  • The total length of the boundary of a closed figure.
  • It's like walking around the edge of a shape.
  • Measured in units like cm, m, km.

🎙️ Imagine you're walking around a park. The total distance you cover along its boundary is its perimeter. It's simply the length of the outline of any closed shape.

Perimeter of a Rectangle

  • A rectangle has two long sides (length) and two short sides (width).
  • Opposite sides are equal in length.
  • Perimeter = Length + Width + Length + Width

🎙️ For a rectangle, we know that opposite sides are equal. So, if we add all four sides, we get L + W + L + W. This can be simplified to 2 times the sum of its length and width.

1Step 1: Identify Length (L) and Width (W)
2Step 2: Add all sides: L + W + L + W
3Formula: 2 × (Length + Width)

Worked Example: Rectangle Perimeter

  • A rectangular park is 50 m long and 30 m wide.
  • Find the length of wire needed to fence it once.

🎙️ Let's apply the formula. Length (L) is 50 m and Width (W) is 30 m. Perimeter = 2 * (50 m + 30 m) = 2 * 80 m = 160 m. So, 160 meters of wire are needed.

Perimeter of a Square

  • A square has four equal sides.
  • Let 's' be the length of one side.
  • Perimeter = s + s + s + s

🎙️ A square is a special type of rectangle where all four sides are equal. So instead of adding each side individually, we can simply multiply the length of one side by 4.

Perimeter of Other Shapes

  • For any polygon, just add the lengths of all its sides.
  • Triangle: sum of its three sides.
  • Irregular shapes: sum of all exterior sides.

🎙️ The general rule for finding the perimeter of any closed shape with straight sides, also known as a polygon, is to simply add up the lengths of all its sides. Whether it's a triangle, a pentagon, or an irregular shape, the principle remains the same.

What is Area?

  • The amount of surface a shape covers.
  • It's the space inside the boundary of a closed figure.
  • Measured in square units (cm², m², km²).

🎙️ While perimeter is about the boundary, area is about the space *inside* that boundary. Think of it as the amount of paint you'd need to cover a surface, or the number of tiles to cover a floor.

Units of Area

  • Area is always measured in 'square units'.
  • For example, square centimetres (cm²), square metres (m²).
  • It represents how many unit squares fit inside a shape.

🎙️ Since area measures a two-dimensional space, its units are always squared. A square centimetre is the area of a square with sides of 1 cm. We count how many of these unit squares fit into the shape.

Area of a Rectangle

  • Area of a rectangle depends on its length and width.
  • Imagine rows of unit squares filling the rectangle.

🎙️ To find the area of a rectangle, we multiply its length by its width. This is because the length tells us how many unit squares fit along one side, and the width tells us how many such rows there are.

2Step 2: Identify Width (W)

Perimeter vs. Area

  • Perimeter is the distance around a shape.
  • Area is the space inside a shape.
  • Different units of measurement.

🎙️ It's important not to confuse perimeter and area. Perimeter is like the fence around a field, while area is the actual crops growing inside the field. They measure different aspects of a shape.

Recap: Key Takeaways

  • Perimeter is the distance around a shape (boundary).
  • Area is the space covered by a shape (surface).
  • Formulas: Rectangle (P=2(L+W), A=L×W), Square (P=4s, A=s×s).
  • Units: Perimeter (cm, m), Area (cm², m²).

🎙️ We've learned that perimeter and area are distinct but equally important concepts. Remember their definitions, their formulas for common shapes, and especially their correct units. These are vital for solving real-world problems.
