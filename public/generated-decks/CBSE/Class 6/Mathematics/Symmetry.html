What is Symmetry?

  • A figure has symmetry if it can be divided
  • Divides into two identical halves
  • These halves are mirror images of each other

🎙️ Symmetry is all about balance. Imagine folding a picture exactly in half, and both sides match perfectly. That's what symmetry means – having two identical parts that mirror each other.

How to Find the Line of Symmetry

  • Imagine folding the shape
  • Do both halves match perfectly?
  • The fold line is the line of symmetry

🎙️ To find a line of symmetry, you can try a simple trick: imagine folding the shape. If the two parts align perfectly, then the crease you made is the line of symmetry. This works for many objects and drawings.

Symmetry in Shapes: Rectangle

  • A rectangle has two lines of symmetry
  • One horizontal, one vertical
  • Diagonals are NOT lines of symmetry

🎙️ Unlike a square, a rectangle has two lines of symmetry. You can fold it horizontally or vertically. Try folding it diagonally – the halves won't match, so diagonals are not lines of symmetry for a rectangle.

  • Two sides are equal
  • Two angles are equal
  • Has only one line of symmetry
