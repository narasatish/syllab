Point: The Starting Dot

  • A point is a location in space.
  • It has no size, only position.
  • Represented by a dot and a capital letter.

🎙️ Imagine pressing the tip of your pencil very lightly on a paper. That tiny mark is what we call a point. It tells us 'where' something is, but has no width or length itself.

Line Segment: A Part of a Line

  • A line segment has two distinct endpoints.
  • It has a definite length.
  • Can be measured.

🎙️ Now, if you draw a straight path between two points, say A and B, that's a line segment. It starts at A, ends at B, and has a specific length that you can measure with a ruler.

Line: Extending Forever

  • A line is a straight path that extends infinitely in both directions.
  • It has no endpoints.
  • Represented with arrows on both sides.

🎙️ Think of a line as a line segment that never ends! It goes on and on, forever, in both directions. That's why we draw arrows at its ends, to show it keeps going.

↔️
Line PQ
6 / 25

Ray: One-Way Path

  • A ray has one starting point (endpoint).
  • It extends infinitely in one direction.
  • Like a beam of light.

🎙️ A ray is like a line segment that has decided to only go in one direction forever! It has a definite starting point, but then it just keeps going straight without stopping, like a laser beam.

Intersecting Lines: Crossing Paths

  • Two lines that cross each other at a single point.
  • This common point is called the point of intersection.
  • They share one point.

🎙️ Imagine two roads meeting at a junction. In geometry, when two lines cross each other, they are called intersecting lines. The spot where they meet is their 'point of intersection'.

Parallel Lines: Never Meeting

  • Two lines that never meet, no matter how far they extend.
  • They always maintain the same distance apart.
  • They run side-by-side.

🎙️ Now, imagine two lines that run perfectly alongside each other, always the same distance apart, and never, ever touching. These are parallel lines, like two friends walking side-by-side forever.

Angle: Where Rays Meet

  • An angle is formed when two rays share a common endpoint.
  • The common endpoint is called the 'vertex'.
  • The two rays are called the 'arms' of the angle.

🎙️ When two rays start from the same point and go in different directions, they create an opening between them. This opening is what we call an angle. The point where they meet is super important, it's called the vertex!

📐
Angle formed

Measuring Angles: In Degrees

  • Angles are measured in units called 'degrees'.
  • A full circle is 360 degrees (360°).
  • We use a tool called a protractor to measure angles.

🎙️ Just like we measure length in centimeters or meters, we measure angles in degrees. Imagine a full circle; it has 360 degrees. A protractor is a special tool that helps us find out how many degrees an angle has.

Acute Angle: Small and Sharp

  • An angle that measures less than 90 degrees (0° < Angle < 90°).
  • It looks like a sharp corner.
  • Think of a small bite.

🎙️ An acute angle is a small, sharp angle. If you think of a corner that is smaller than the corner of your book, that's an acute angle. It's 'cute' because it's small!

Right Angle: The Perfect Corner

  • An angle that measures exactly 90 degrees (90°).
  • It looks like a perfect 'L' shape.
  • Often marked with a square symbol.

🎙️ The right angle is very special. It's the angle you see at the corner of a square or a rectangle. It's exactly 90 degrees and is often shown with a small square symbol in the corner.

Obtuse Angle: Wide Open

  • An angle that measures more than 90 degrees but less than 180 degrees (90° < Angle < 180°).
  • It looks wider than a right angle.
  • Think of a wide-open mouth.

🎙️ If an angle is bigger than a right angle, but not yet a straight line, it's an obtuse angle. It looks like a wide-open mouth or a door opened wide. It's 'obtuse' because it's big!

↖️
Wide angle

🎙️ When the hands of a clock show 4 o'clock, they form an obtuse angle. Many roof designs have obtuse angles. Even your laptop screen, when opened wide, forms an obtuse angle with the keyboard.

Straight Angle: A Flat Line

  • An angle that measures exactly 180 degrees (180°).
  • It forms a straight line.
  • It's like two right angles put together.

🎙️ A straight angle is simply a straight line! It measures exactly 180 degrees. Imagine two rays pointing in exactly opposite directions from the same vertex – that forms a straight angle.
