Positive & Negative: The Two Sides

  • Positive Integers: Numbers greater than zero (1, 2, 3...). Often written with a '+' or no sign.
  • Negative Integers: Numbers less than zero (-1, -2, -3...). Always written with a '-' sign.
  • Zero is neither positive nor negative.

🎙️ It's crucial to distinguish between positive and negative integers. Positive numbers represent gains, above, or forward movements. Negative numbers represent losses, below, or backward movements. Zero is the neutral referee.

The Mirror Image: Opposites

  • Every integer (except zero) has an opposite.
  • The opposite of an integer is the same distance from zero but in the opposite direction.
  • Example: The opposite of 5 is -5. The opposite of -10 is 10.

🎙️ Think of a mirror. If you stand 5 steps to the right of the mirror, your reflection is 5 steps to the left. That's how opposites work on a number line – same distance from zero, just on the other side.

Who's Bigger? Comparing Integers

  • On a number line, the number to the RIGHT is always GREATER.
  • The number to the LEFT is always SMALLER.
  • Any positive integer is greater than any negative integer.

🎙️ Comparing integers can be tricky at first, especially with negative numbers. Just remember the number line rule: right is greater, left is smaller. So, -1 is greater than -5, even though 1 is smaller than 5!

Adding Integers: Mastering the Rules

  • Rule 1: Same Signs (++, --) - Add the numbers, keep the sign.
  • Rule 2: Different Signs (+-, -+) - Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger, keep the sign of the larger number.
  • Example: -7 + 3

🎙️ While the number line is great, for larger numbers, rules are faster. If signs are the same, just add and keep the sign. If signs are different, subtract and take the sign of the bigger number (ignoring the sign first).

Subtracting Integers: Adding the Opposite!

  • Subtraction of integers is the same as adding the additive inverse (opposite) of the second integer.
  • Rule: a - b = a + (-b)
  • Example: 5 - (-3) becomes 5 + 3.

🎙️ This is a golden rule for subtraction! Instead of subtracting, we change the operation to addition and change the sign of the number being subtracted. It's like a double negative becoming a positive!

Subtraction Made Easy!

  • Calculate: -8 - 2
  • Change to addition: -8 + (-2)
  • Now, apply addition rule (same signs): 8 + 2 = 10, keep negative sign.
  • Answer: -10

🎙️ Let's try -8 - 2. First, keep -8. Change the minus to a plus. Change the sign of +2 to -2. Now we have -8 + (-2). Both are negative, so add them (8+2=10) and keep the negative sign. Result: -10.

Watch Out! Common Mistakes

  • Confusing comparison: -5 > -2 (Incorrect, -2 is greater)
  • Subtraction errors: 5 - (-3) = 2 (Incorrect, it's 5+3=8)
  • Forgetting signs: -3 + 5 = -8 (Incorrect, it's 2)
  • Treating 0 as positive or negative.

🎙️ It's easy to make small errors with integers, especially with signs. Always remember that numbers to the right are greater, and master the 'add the opposite' rule for subtraction. Double-check your signs!
