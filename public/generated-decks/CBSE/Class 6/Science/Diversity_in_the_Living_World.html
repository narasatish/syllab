What Makes Something Living?

  • Living things grow, move, breathe, and reproduce.
  • They need food and water to survive.
  • Non-living things do not show these characteristics.

🎙️ Think about a plant, an animal, or even yourself. What are some things you all do? These are the basic signs of life that distinguish living things from non-living things like rocks or toys.

What Are Habitats?

  • A habitat is the natural home or environment of an organism.
  • It provides food, water, shelter, and space to live.
  • Different organisms live in different habitats.

🎙️ Just like we have our homes, every living thing has a natural home called a habitat. It's where they find everything they need to survive and thrive.

🏡
Nature's Home
7 / 25

Terrestrial Habitats (Land)

  • These are habitats found on land.
  • Examples include forests, deserts, grasslands, and mountains.
  • Animals and plants in these habitats are adapted to land conditions.

🎙️ Forests are homes to many trees, deer, and tigers. Deserts are hot and dry, home to camels and cacti. These are all examples of terrestrial or land habitats.

Aquatic Habitats (Water)

  • These are habitats found in water.
  • Examples include ponds, lakes, rivers, and oceans.
  • Life in water is very different from life on land.

🎙️ Ponds are freshwater homes for frogs and water lilies. Oceans are vast saltwater homes for fish, whales, and coral reefs. Life in water is just as diverse as life on land.

Adaptation: Fitting In

  • Adaptation is how living things develop special features.
  • These features help them survive better in their habitat.
  • They help find food, protect themselves, or cope with weather.

🎙️ Imagine wearing a thick woollen sweater in a hot desert. It wouldn't work, right? Animals and plants have special ways to 'dress' or change themselves to fit perfectly into their surroundings. This is called adaptation.

🦎

11 / 25

Desert Plant Adaptations

  • Cacti have thick, waxy stems to store water.
  • They have spines instead of leaves to reduce water loss.
  • Their roots spread wide to absorb any rare rainfall.

🎙️ Deserts are very dry, so plants need to be smart about water. A cactus is a great example – its thick stem is like a water tank, and its sharp spines protect it from animals.

🌵
Water Savers
12 / 25

Desert Animal Adaptations

  • Camels have humps to store fat, which provides energy and water.
  • They have thick skin to withstand heat.
  • Long eyelashes keep sand out of their eyes during sandstorms.

🎙️ The camel is the 'ship of the desert' for a reason! Its body is perfectly designed to survive extreme heat and long periods without water, thanks to its amazing adaptations.

Aquatic Plant Adaptations

  • Water lilies have broad, flat leaves that float on the water surface.
  • This helps them absorb maximum sunlight.
  • Their stems are flexible to move with water currents.

🎙️ Plants that live in water also have special features. Water lilies need their leaves to float on top to get sunlight, and their flexible stems help them move with the water currents.

Aquatic Animal Adaptations

  • Fish have gills to breathe oxygen dissolved in water.
  • They have fins for swimming and balancing.
  • Their streamlined bodies help them move easily through water.

🎙️ How do fish breathe underwater? They have special organs called gills! Their sleek bodies and fins are perfect for gliding through water, showing how well they are adapted to their aquatic home.

Grouping Living Things (Classification)

  • Classification is the process of grouping organisms based on similarities.
  • It helps us study and understand the vast diversity of life.
  • We can group them into plants, animals, and more specific categories.

🎙️ Imagine your toy box – you might group cars together, and dolls together. Similarly, scientists group living things with common features. This makes it easier to study them.

Why is Diversity Important?

  • Diversity ensures a healthy and balanced ecosystem.
  • Each living thing plays a unique role in nature.
  • It provides resources like food, medicine, and clean air.

🎙️ Imagine a team where everyone has the same skill. It wouldn't be very effective, would it? In nature, every different plant and animal has a unique job, making the whole system strong and balanced.

Food Chains & Webs (Simple)

  • Plants make their own food using sunlight.
  • Animals eat plants or other animals for energy.
  • Diversity ensures many food options and a stable food web.

🎙️ Think about who eats whom. A plant gets energy from the sun. A deer eats the plant. A tiger eats the deer. If one part is missing, the whole chain is affected. Diversity means there are many such chains, making the ecosystem resilient.
