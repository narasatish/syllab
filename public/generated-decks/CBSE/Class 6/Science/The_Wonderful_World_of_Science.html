What is Experimentation?

  • A systematic procedure carried out to support, refute, or validate a hypothesis.
  • It involves changing one factor at a time.
  • We observe the results carefully.

🎙️ An experiment is like a controlled test. You change one thing, keep everything else the same, and then see what happens. This helps you understand cause and effect.

Watch Out! Common Mistakes

  • Confusing observation with guessing.
  • Thinking science is only for 'smart' people.
  • Believing all scientific ideas are 'facts' (they can evolve).

🎙️ Sometimes students make these common errors. Remember, observations are facts, not guesses. And science is for everyone who is curious, not just a select few. Scientific ideas are tested and refined over time.

Ace Your Science Exam!

  • Understand concepts, don't just memorise.
  • Give real-life examples for definitions.
  • Practice drawing simple diagrams (e.g., plant parts).

🎙️ To do well in science, focus on understanding *why* things happen. Always try to link concepts to examples from your daily life, and don't forget to practice drawing!
