Why Do We Eat?

  • Food gives us energy to play and study.
  • Food helps our body grow and repair itself.
  • Food protects us from sickness.

🎙️ Think about it, why do we feel hungry? It's because our bodies need fuel, just like a car needs petrol. Food provides us with the energy to run, play, learn, and do all our daily activities. It also helps our bodies grow strong and fight off germs.

  • Eating quickly, while distracted, or until very full are common habits.

🎙️ We all have different ways of eating. Some of us eat very fast, while watching TV, or even when we're not truly hungry. These habits can sometimes lead to problems like indigestion or overeating. Today, we'll learn about a better way.

What is Mindful Eating?

  • Paying full attention to your food.
  • Noticing its taste, smell, texture, and how it makes you feel.
  • Eating slowly and without distractions.

🎙️ So, what exactly is mindful eating? It's simply about paying attention. Imagine really noticing your food – its colours, its smell, how it feels in your mouth, and even the sounds it makes as you chew. It's about being present with your meal.

Mindful vs. Mindless Eating

  • Mindful: Aware of hunger/fullness, tastes food, enjoys the moment.
  • Mindless: Eats quickly, distracted, eats out of habit or boredom.
  • Mindful eating helps us make healthier choices.

🎙️ Let's compare. Mindless eating is when we eat without thinking, often while watching TV or playing games. We don't even remember what we ate! Mindful eating is the opposite – we are fully aware of our food and our body's signals.

Why Mindful Eating Matters

  • Helps us listen to our body's hunger and fullness signals.
  • Prevents overeating and indigestion.
  • Makes us appreciate our food more.

🎙️ Why should we bother with mindful eating? Because it helps us understand our body better. We learn to listen when our stomach says 'I'm hungry' and when it says 'I'm full'. This prevents us from eating too much or too little, and helps us truly enjoy our meals.

Step 1: Observe Your Hunger

  • Before eating, ask yourself: Am I truly hungry?
  • Notice if your stomach feels empty or if you just want to eat out of boredom.
  • Rate your hunger from 1 (not hungry) to 5 (very hungry).

🎙️ The first step is to check in with yourself. Before you pick up that snack, pause and ask: 'Am I really hungry, or am I just bored, or sad, or just want to eat because it's there?' This helps us eat when our body actually needs food.

🤔
Check your hunger

Step 2: Engage Your Senses

  • Look at your food: its colours, shapes, and textures.
  • Smell your food: take a deep breath and notice its aroma.
  • Listen to your food: the sounds of chewing or crunching.

🎙️ Next, use all your senses! Before you even take a bite, look at your food. What colours do you see? What does it smell like? Even listen to the sounds it makes as you prepare it or chew it. This makes the eating experience richer.

👃👃👃

Step 3: Chew Slowly and Taste

  • Take small bites and chew your food thoroughly.
  • Notice the different flavours and textures in each bite.
  • Put your fork down between bites.

🎙️ This is a very important step. Don't rush! Take small bites and chew your food slowly. Really focus on the taste. Does it taste sweet, salty, sour? Is it crunchy or soft? This helps your brain register that you are eating and helps digestion.

Benefit 1: Improved Digestion

  • Chewing food properly helps break it down before it reaches the stomach.
  • This makes it easier for your stomach to digest nutrients.
  • Less indigestion, bloating, and stomach discomfort.

🎙️ When you chew your food slowly and properly, you're actually helping your digestive system a lot. Smaller food particles are easier for your stomach to process, leading to better absorption of nutrients and less discomfort like bloating.

⚙️
Smooth digestion

Benefit 2: Better Portion Control

  • Eating slowly gives your brain time to receive signals from your stomach.
  • You feel full sooner and are less likely to overeat.
  • Helps maintain a healthy weight.

🎙️ It takes about 20 minutes for your brain to get the message from your stomach that you're full. If you eat too fast, you might eat a lot more than you need before your brain even gets the signal. Mindful eating helps you stop when you're satisfied, not stuffed.

⚖️
Balanced portions

Benefit 3: Greater Food Appreciation

  • When you pay attention, you notice the delicious flavours and textures.
  • Eating becomes a more enjoyable and satisfying experience.
  • You feel grateful for your food.

🎙️ Think about it – when you truly taste your food, it's so much more enjoyable! Mindful eating helps you discover all the wonderful flavours and textures you might miss when you're rushing. It makes mealtime a joyful experience.

Benefit 4: Reduced Stress

🎙️ Mealtime can be a wonderful break from a busy day. When you focus on your food, it's like a mini-meditation. It helps you calm your mind, reduce stress, and prevents you from eating out of boredom or worry.

Benefit 5: Healthier Choices

  • When you're mindful, you naturally choose foods that nourish your body.

🎙️ Mindful eating also helps you make smarter food choices. When you pay attention to how different foods make you feel, you'll naturally start gravitating towards healthier options that give you energy and make your body feel good, rather than junk food.

Mindful Eating with an Apple

  • Hold the apple: feel its weight, notice its colour and shine.
  • Smell it: does it smell sweet or fresh?
  • Take a small bite: notice the crunch, the juice, the taste.
  • Chew slowly: how does the flavour change as you chew?

🎙️ Let's try a quick mental exercise. Imagine you're eating an apple. Don't just bite into it quickly. First, hold it, feel it, smell it. Then, take a small bite. Notice the crunch, the burst of juice, the sweetness. Chew slowly and really experience it. See how different it feels?

Tip 1: No Distractions!

  • Turn off the TV, put away your phone, and close books during meals.
  • Eat at a table, not in front of a screen.
  • Focus only on your food and the people you're eating with.

🎙️ One of the biggest obstacles to mindful eating is distractions. When you're watching TV or playing games, your brain isn't focused on your food. Make mealtime a screen-free zone. Sit at a table and simply enjoy your meal.

📵
Screen-free meals

Tip 2: Tune into Your Body

  • Ask yourself: Am I still hungry? Am I satisfied? Am I full?
  • Stop eating when you feel comfortably satisfied, not stuffed.
  • It's okay to leave food on your plate if you're full.

🎙️ Your body is very smart! It sends signals. Learn to listen to them. When you start to feel comfortably full, that's your body telling you to stop. You don't have to finish everything on your plate if you're no longer hungry.

🛑

Tip 3: Show Gratitude

🎙️ Take a moment to appreciate your food. Think about the farmer who grew it, the person who cooked it, and how lucky you are to have a meal. This feeling of gratitude can make your eating experience even more meaningful.
