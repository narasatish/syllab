Hunger vs. Appetite: What's the Difference?

  • Hunger is your body's physical need for food.
  • Appetite is the desire for food, often for specific tastes.
  • Understanding this helps you eat when your body truly needs it.

🎙️ This is a very important distinction! Hunger is like your car's fuel light coming on – your body *needs* energy. Appetite, however, is more like seeing an advertisement for ice cream and suddenly wanting it, even if you just ate. It's a desire, not necessarily a physical need.

Recognizing True Hunger Signals

  • Stomach rumbling or feeling empty.
  • Feeling light-headed or low on energy.
  • A general feeling of emptiness in your stomach.

🎙️ How do you know if you're truly hungry? Your body gives you clear signals. You might hear your stomach growl, or feel a bit weak. These are signs that your body needs fuel. It's not about craving a specific food, but a general need for nourishment.

Recognizing Fullness Signals

How to Practice: Step 1 - Engage Your Senses

  • Look at your food: Notice its colours and shapes.
  • Smell your food: Inhale the aromas before you eat.
  • Feel the texture: How does it feel in your mouth?

🎙️ Before you even take a bite, engage all your senses. Look at the vibrant colours of your vegetables, smell the spices in your curry. As you eat, pay attention to the texture – is it crunchy, soft, smooth? This makes eating a much richer experience.

How to Practice: Step 2 - Eat Slowly

  • Put your fork or spoon down between bites.
  • Take small bites and chew them completely.
  • Give your brain time to register fullness (about 20 minutes).

🎙️ Eating slowly is key! It's not a race. By taking your time, you allow your brain to catch up with your stomach. It takes about 20 minutes for your brain to receive the 'I'm full' signal. If you eat too fast, you might overeat before your brain gets the message.

How to Practice: Step 3 - Chew Thoroughly

  • Aim for about 20-30 chews per bite, especially for solid foods.
  • This breaks down food, aiding digestion.
  • It also helps you taste the food more completely.

🎙️ Chewing is the first stage of digestion, and it's super important! Don't just swallow quickly. Chew your food until it's almost liquid. This not only helps your stomach but also allows your taste buds to fully experience the flavours. Try counting your chews for a bite of roti or rice.
