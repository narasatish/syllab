Documents for Account Opening

  • Proof of Identity (e.g., Aadhaar Card).
  • Proof of Address (e.g., electricity bill).
  • Passport-sized photographs.
  • This process is called KYC (Know Your Customer).

🎙️ To open an account, you typically need Proof of Identity, like an Aadhaar card or PAN card, and Proof of Address, like an electricity bill or ration card. This is part of the 'Know Your Customer' or KYC process.
