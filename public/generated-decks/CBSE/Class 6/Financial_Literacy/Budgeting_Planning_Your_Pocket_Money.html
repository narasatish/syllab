Needs vs. Wants

  • Needs: Essential for survival/well-being (e.g., food, school supplies).
  • Wants: Things you desire but can live without (e.g., new game, extra snacks).

🎙️ This is a very important distinction! Needs are things you absolutely must have, like your lunch. Wants are things that would be nice to have, like a new comic book.

  • Pocket Money: ₹200/week.

🎙️ Let's say you get ₹200. Your bus fare and a new pen are needs. An ice cream or a fancy sticker book are wants. Understanding this helps you prioritize.

The 50/30/20 Rule (Simplified)

  • A simple guide for managing money.
  • 50% for Needs, 30% for Wants, 20% for Savings.
  • A good starting point for your budget.

🎙️ This is a popular rule that helps many people budget. It suggests allocating your money into these three big categories. It's a great way to ensure you're saving enough while still enjoying your wants.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not tracking spending: 'Where did my money go?'
  • Not saving anything: No money for future goals.
  • Buying wants before needs: Running out of money for essentials.

🎙️ These are some common pitfalls. It's easy to forget where your money went if you don't track it, or to spend on fun things first and then realize you don't have enough for school supplies.
