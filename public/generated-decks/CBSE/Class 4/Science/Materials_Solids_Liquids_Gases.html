Three Special Forms!

  • All materials can be found in three main forms.
  • We call them Solids, Liquids, and Gases.

🎙️ Imagine water. It can be ice, which is hard. It can be flowing water. And it can also disappear into the air as steam! These are the three forms we will learn about.

Solids: Firm and Fixed!

  • Solids have a fixed shape.
  • They have a fixed size.
  • They do not flow.

🎙️ Think of your school bag or your lunchbox. They always keep their shape, right? You can hold them firmly. That's because they are solids!

Shapes are all around us.
6 / 20

Solids All Around Us!

  • Your chair, a book, a stone, your pencil box.

🎙️ Look around your classroom. The desk, the wall, your shoes – all these are solids. They stay in one place and keep their shape unless you break them.

Liquids: Flowing and Free!

  • Liquids do not have a fixed shape.
  • They take the shape of their container.
  • They can flow easily.

🎙️ Think about water. If you pour it into a glass, it takes the shape of the glass. If you pour it into a bowl, it takes the shape of the bowl! Liquids love to flow.

💧
Water is a liquid

Liquids We See Every Day!

  • Water, milk, juice, oil.

🎙️ Every morning, you might drink milk or juice. These are liquids! They can be poured, and they splash. They don't have a hard shape like a solid.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Gases: Invisible and Everywhere!

  • Gases do not have a fixed shape or size.
  • They fill up any container they are in.
  • We usually cannot see them.

🎙️ Gases are tricky because we often can't see them! But they are all around us. The air we breathe is a mixture of different gases. They spread out to fill everything.

💨
Air is a gas

🎙️ When you blow up a balloon, you are filling it with air, which is a gas! And when water boils, you see steam rising – that's also a gas, called water vapour.

Ice Melts: Solid to Liquid!

  • When ice gets warm, it changes.
  • It turns into water.
  • This is melting.

🎙️ Put an ice cube out on a warm day. What happens? It starts to melt! The solid ice changes into liquid water. This is a common change we see.

1Ice (Solid)
2Water (Liquid)
15 / 20

Water Boils: Liquid to Gas!

  • When water gets very hot, it changes.
  • It turns into steam.
  • This is evaporation.

🎙️ Ask an adult to boil some water safely. You'll see steam rising! The hot liquid water changes into a gas called water vapour. This is how puddles dry up too!

1Water (Liquid)
2Steam (Gas)
16 / 20

Steam Cools: Gas to Liquid!

  • When steam gets cool, it changes back.
  • It turns into tiny drops of water.
  • This is condensation.

🎙️ If you hold a cold plate over steam, you'll see small water droplets form. The gas cools down and turns back into liquid water. This is how clouds are formed!

1Steam (Gas)
2Water (Liquid)

Why Learn About This?

  • Understanding materials helps us use them well.
  • It helps us build things and understand nature.

🎙️ Knowing about solids, liquids, and gases helps us understand so many things around us. Why does a bridge need to be strong? Why do we drink water and not eat it? It's all about their states!
