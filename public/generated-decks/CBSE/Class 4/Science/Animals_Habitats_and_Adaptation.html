What is a Habitat?

  • A habitat is a natural home or environment.
  • It's where an animal or plant normally lives.
  • It gives them food, water, shelter, and space.

🎙️ So, a habitat is like a perfect house for an animal. It's the place in nature where an animal finds everything it needs to survive, grow, and have baby animals.

What Students Often Confuse!

  • Habitat is *where* an animal lives, not *what* it eats.
  • Adaptation is a *feature* or *behaviour*, not just a 'change'.
  • All animals need *both* a suitable habitat and good adaptations to survive.

🎙️ It's easy to mix up some terms. Remember, habitat is the home, and adaptation is how the animal is built or acts to live in that home. They work together for survival.
