What is a Home for Animals? (Habitat)

  • Every animal has a special place to live.
  • This home helps them find food and stay safe.
  • We call this special home a 'habitat'.

🎙️ Just like we have our homes, animals also have their own special homes. This home is called a habitat. It's where they find everything they need to live.
