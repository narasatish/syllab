What is a Habitat?

  • A habitat is an animal's home.
  • It is where an animal lives and finds food.

🎙️ Just like we have our homes, animals also have special places where they live. This special home is called a habitat. It provides everything they need to survive.

Animals live all around us.

Different Homes for Animals

  • Animals live in many different types of homes.
  • Some live on land, some in water, and some in the air!

🎙️ Think about your friends. Do all of them live in the same type of house? No, right? Similarly, animals have different kinds of homes depending on what suits them best.

  • These homes can be rivers, lakes, or oceans.

Meet Our Water Friends!

  • Frogs live near ponds and lakes.

What is Adaptation?

  • Adaptation means how an animal changes.
  • It changes to live well in its home.

🎙️ Now, let's learn a big word: Adaptation. It means how animals get special features or habits that help them live perfectly in their habitat. It's like having special superpowers for their home!

Why Do Animals Adapt?

  • To find food easily.
  • To stay safe from other animals.
  • To live comfortably in their habitat.

🎙️ Animals adapt for very important reasons. They need to find enough food, protect themselves from dangers, and feel good in their home, whether it's hot or cold.

Moving on Land

  • Strong legs help them run fast or walk far.

🎙️ Think about a dog running in the park. Its legs are perfect for running and playing on land. Different land animals have different kinds of legs for different types of movement.

Deer: Built for Speed!

  • Deer have long, thin, strong legs.
  • These legs help them run very fast.
  • They run fast to escape from dangers.

🎙️ A deer needs to be very quick to run away from predators like tigers. Its long, slender legs are a perfect example of adaptation for speed on land.

Force changes how things move.

Moving in Water

  • Animals in water use fins or flippers.
  • These help them swim and steer easily.

🎙️ How do you swim? You use your arms and legs. But fish have special body parts called fins. These fins are adapted to push water and help them move smoothly.

Fish: Master Swimmers!

  • Fish have fins all over their body.
  • The tail fin pushes them forward.
  • Other fins help them balance and turn.

🎙️ Look at a fish in an aquarium. See how its fins move? Each fin has a job to do, making the fish a perfect swimmer in its watery home.

  • Their body colours match their surroundings.

🎙️ Imagine playing hide-and-seek and wearing clothes that look exactly like the wall! Some animals do this naturally. It's called camouflage, and it helps them hide from enemies or catch food.

Chameleon: The Colour Changer!

  • A chameleon can change its skin colour.
  • It changes to match leaves or branches.
  • This helps it hide from birds and snakes.

🎙️ The chameleon is like a magic artist! It can change its skin colour to become almost invisible. This amazing adaptation keeps it safe and helps it sneak up on insects.
