What is Food?

  • Food is anything we eat or drink.
  • It comes from plants and animals.
  • Like fruits, vegetables, milk, and rice.

🎙️ Think about your breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Everything on your plate, from the fluffy roti to the crunchy apple, is food! It can come from plants, like grains and veggies, or from animals, like milk and eggs.

  • Like rice, roti, bread, and potatoes.
  • Eat them to run and play all day!

🎙️ These are our 'power foods'! When you feel tired, eating a little bit of rice or a potato can give you a quick boost to continue playing or studying.

Body-Building Foods

  • These foods help us grow tall and strong.
  • Like milk, dal, eggs, and chicken.
  • They build our muscles and bones.

🎙️ Body-building foods are like the bricks that build a house. They help make your bones strong and your muscles big, so you can lift things and play sports well.

Our body has many organs working together.

Protective Foods

  • These foods protect us from falling sick.
  • Like all fruits and vegetables!
  • They keep our body healthy and safe.

🎙️ Protective foods are like our body's personal guards! They have special powers to keep germs away and make sure we don't catch a cold easily. Eat your fruits and veggies!

Water - Our Best Friend!

  • Water is not food, but it's super important!
  • It helps our body work well.
  • Drink 6-8 glasses of water every day.

🎙️ Water is like a hidden helper for our body. It helps digest food, keeps us cool, and carries good things all around our body. Always keep a water bottle with you!

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

  • Found in whole grains, fruits, and salads.

🎙️ Imagine little brooms cleaning your tummy! That's what roughage does. It helps food move through our body and keeps our digestive system happy and healthy.

Balanced Diet - All Together Now!

  • A balanced diet means eating all types of food.
  • Energy, Body-Building, and Protective foods.
  • In the right amounts, not too much or too little.

🎙️ Just like a perfect team needs all its players, our body needs all types of food. A balanced diet makes sure you get all the nutrients you need to be super healthy!

Healthy Eating Habits

  • Always wash your hands before eating.
  • Chew your food slowly and properly.
  • Don't waste food, take only what you can eat.

🎙️ Good habits make eating even better! Washing hands keeps germs away, chewing properly helps digestion, and not wasting food shows respect for what we have.
