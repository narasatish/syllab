Energy-Giving Foods

  • These foods give us instant power.
  • They are like the 'petrol' for our body.
  • Examples: Rice, chapati, potatoes, butter.

🎙️ These are the foods that quickly fill us with energy. When you feel tired, eating these foods helps you feel strong again, ready to play and study. Think of them as your body's quick energy boost!

🍚🍚🍚🍚🍚
Rice for Energy
11 / 24

Body-Building Foods

  • These foods help us build strong muscles and bones.
  • They are important for growing children.
  • Examples: Milk, eggs, dal (pulses), chicken.

🎙️ These foods are like the bricks and cement for our body. They help make our muscles strong and our bones healthy, so we can grow tall and robust. Drinking milk and eating dal every day is very important.

🥛🥛🥛🥛
Milk for Growth
12 / 24

Protective Foods

  • These foods protect us from diseases.
  • They keep us healthy and prevent us from falling sick.
  • Examples: Fruits (apple, banana), vegetables (spinach, carrot).

🎙️ These foods are like our body's shield and doctors! They contain special powers called vitamins and minerals that fight off germs and keep us from getting sick. Eating colourful fruits and vegetables is super important.

🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎
Fruits for Protection

Don't Forget Water!

  • Our body is mostly made of water.
  • Water helps digest food and keeps us cool.
  • Drink 6-8 glasses of water every day!

🎙️ Water is not a food, but it's super important! Our body needs a lot of water to work properly. It helps carry food around our body and keeps us hydrated, especially when it's hot or we are playing.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.
15 / 24

Roughage: Our Tummy's Friend

  • Roughage is the fiber in our food.
  • It helps clean our digestive system.
  • Found in: Whole grains, fruits with skin, salads.

🎙️ Roughage is like a broom for our tummy! It doesn't give us energy or build muscles, but it helps clean our insides and makes sure everything moves smoothly. Eating whole wheat chapati and salads gives us good roughage.

🥗🥗🥗
Healthy Salad

What is a Balanced Diet?

  • Imagine a seesaw. It needs equal weight on both sides.
  • Our body needs all types of food in the right amount.
  • This is called a 'Balanced Diet'.

🎙️ Think of a seesaw in the park. If one side is too heavy, it won't balance. Our body is similar! It needs a little bit of everything – energy foods, body-building foods, and protective foods – to be perfectly balanced and healthy.

⚖️
Balanced Scale
17 / 24

Eating Right for a Balanced Diet

  • A balanced diet means eating all types of food.
  • Energy-giving, body-building, and protective foods.
  • In the correct quantities every day.

🎙️ So, a balanced diet is not about eating just one type of food. It's about having a mix of all the food superheroes – some for energy, some for growth, and some for protection – along with plenty of water, every single day.

Good Eating Habits

  • Wash your hands before and after eating.
  • Chew your food slowly and properly.
  • Eat at fixed times and don't overeat.
  • Don't waste food!

🎙️ Eating good food is important, but how we eat is also important! Always wash your hands to keep germs away. Chewing slowly helps your tummy digest food better. And remember, never waste food, as many people don't get enough to eat.

🧼
Wash Hands
20 / 24

Habits to Avoid!

  • Eating too much junk food (chips, chocolates).
  • Skipping meals, especially breakfast.
  • Eating very quickly or while watching TV.

🎙️ Just like good habits, there are some habits we should try to avoid. Eating too much junk food can make us lazy and unhealthy. Skipping breakfast is like trying to start a car without fuel! Try to eat mindfully.

❌
Avoid Bad Habits

Common Mistakes About Food

  • Thinking only yummy food is good (often junk food).
  • Not eating vegetables because they don't 'taste good'.
  • Believing that skipping meals helps lose weight (it doesn't!).

🎙️ Many children make some common mistakes. Sometimes, the food that tastes the best, like candies or chips, isn't the best for our health. And remember, vegetables are super important, even if you don't love them yet!
