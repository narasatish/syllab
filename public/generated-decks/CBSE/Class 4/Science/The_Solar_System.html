Day and Night on Earth

  • Earth spins like a top.
  • One spin takes 24 hours.
  • This spinning causes day and night.

🎙️ Have you ever wondered why we have day and night? It's because our Earth is always spinning! As it spins, one side faces the Sun (day) and the other side faces away (night). This takes exactly one day.

Earth Spinning

Earth Goes Around the Sun

  • Earth also goes around the Sun.
  • This takes one whole year (365 days).
  • This movement causes different seasons.

🎙️ Not only does Earth spin, but it also travels around the Sun, like running on a very big track! This long journey takes a whole year and is why we experience different seasons like summer, winter, spring, and autumn.
