  • Planets are big, round objects that go around the Sun.
  • They are like the 'children' of the Sun.

🎙️ Just like children play around their parents, planets keep moving in a fixed path around the Sun. They don't have their own light; they shine by reflecting the Sun's light.

Earth's Spin: Day and Night

  • Earth spins like a top.
  • This spinning causes day and night.

🎙️ Imagine you're spinning a top. That's how our Earth spins! As it spins, different parts face the Sun, giving us day, and then turn away, giving us night. One full spin takes 24 hours.

Earth's Spin

Earth's Journey: A Year!

  • Earth also goes around the Sun.
  • One full trip around the Sun takes one year (365 days).

🎙️ While Earth is spinning, it's also taking a big, long walk around the Sun, like walking around a giant race track. This long journey takes one whole year to complete!

Watch Out for These! (Common Mistakes)

  • Confusing rotation (day/night) with revolution (year).
  • Thinking the Moon makes its own light.
  • Forgetting the number of planets or their order.

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused between Earth spinning and Earth going around the Sun. Also, remember the Moon is a reflector, not a light source! And practice the planet names.
