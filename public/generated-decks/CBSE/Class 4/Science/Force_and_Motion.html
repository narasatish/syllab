What is Force?

  • Force is a push or a pull.
  • It makes things move or stop.
  • It is like magic that we use every day!

🎙️ Have you ever wondered what makes things move or stop? It's called force! Force is simply a push or a pull that we apply to objects. We use force all the time without even realising it.

  • Pushing your toy car to make it roll.
  • Kicking a football to send it flying.
  • You use force to start motion.

🎙️ When your toy car is still, how do you make it move? You push it! That push is a force. So, force can make a still object start moving.

Force Can Stop Things!

  • Catching a ball that is coming towards you.
  • Pressing the brakes on your bicycle.
  • Force can make moving things stop.

🎙️ Just like force can start motion, it can also stop it. When a ball is rolling, you catch it to stop it. When you are cycling fast, you use the brakes to stop. This is another way we use force.

  • Squeezing a soft clay to make a new shape.
  • Crushing an empty plastic bottle.
  • Force can make things look different.

Two Kinds of Force: Push and Pull

  • Every force is either a push or a pull.
  • Push means moving something away from you.
  • Pull means moving something closer to you.

🎙️ All the forces we talked about can be grouped into two main types: push or pull. When you push something, you move it away. When you pull something, you bring it closer.

Examples of Pull!

  • Pulling a toy train with a string.
  • Pulling a chair closer to sit down.
  • Pulling a drawer open to take out clothes.

🎙️ Now, let's think about pull. When you play with your toy train, you pull it along. When you need to open a drawer, you pull it towards you. These are all examples of pull.

Gravity: Earth's Big Pull!

  • Gravity is a special kind of pull.
  • Earth pulls everything towards its center.
  • That's why things fall down, not up!

🎙️ Have you ever seen an apple fall from a tree? It always falls down to the ground. This happens because of gravity! Gravity is Earth's invisible force that pulls everything towards itself.

Friction: The Slow-Down Force!

  • Friction is a force that slows things down.
  • It happens when two surfaces rub against each other.
  • It helps us walk without slipping.

🎙️ Imagine you are trying to slide on a rough floor. It's hard, right? That's because of friction. Friction is a force that acts against motion and tries to slow things down or stop them.

🧤
Rubbing hands
14 / 20

Friction in Our Life!

  • When you walk, friction between your shoes and the ground stops you from slipping.
  • When you ride a bicycle, friction helps the brakes work.
  • It's a very helpful force!

🎙️ Friction is not always a bad thing! It's very important for us. Without friction, we would slip and fall when we walk. It helps car tires grip the road and helps us hold things.
