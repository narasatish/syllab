What is Motion?

  • Motion means when something moves.
  • It changes its place.

🎙️ Look around you! Do you see things moving? A car on the road, a bird flying, even you walking! When something changes its position from one place to another, we say it is in motion.

What is Force?

  • Force is a push or a pull.
  • It makes things move or stop.

🎙️ So, what makes things move? It's called 'Force'! Force is like a superpower. It's when you push something away or pull something towards you.

Force Starts Things Moving!

  • A kick makes a football move.
  • Force helps things begin to move.

🎙️ Imagine a football lying still on the ground. It won't move by itself, right? But if you kick it, it starts rolling! Your kick is a force that makes the ball move.

  • This is muscular force.

🎙️ Our body has muscles, and when we use them to do things like lifting, pushing, or pulling, we are using 'Muscular Force'. It's the force from our own body!

Earth's Special Pull!

  • Earth pulls everything down.
  • This is called gravity.

🎙️ Have you ever wondered why things fall down and not up? It's because our Earth has a special force called 'Gravitational Force'. It pulls everything towards its center.

🎙️ When an apple falls from a tree, or when rain falls from the clouds, it's gravity pulling them down. It's always working, pulling things towards the ground.
