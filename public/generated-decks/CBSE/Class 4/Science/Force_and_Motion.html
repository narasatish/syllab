Force is a Push or a Pull

  • Force is simply a push or a pull.
  • We use force all the time.
  • To move things closer or further away.

🎙️ So, a force is nothing but when you either push something away from you, or pull something towards you. We do this every single day without even realizing it! It's how we interact with everything around us.

  • Or a car on a straight road.

🎙️ One type of motion is when things move in a straight path, like a ruler. Imagine a train going on a track that doesn't bend. That's called motion in a straight line, or linear motion.

Motion in a Circle

  • When something moves around a fixed point.
  • Like a fan's blades spinning.
  • Or a merry-go-round going round.

🎙️ Another type of motion is when things move in a circle. Think of the blades of a ceiling fan when it's on. They keep spinning around a central point. This is called circular motion.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Thinking only big pushes are forces.
  • Forgetting that force can stop things.
  • Confusing straight and circular motion.

🎙️ Sometimes, children think only very strong pushes are forces, but even a gentle tap is a force! Also, remember force doesn't just start motion, it can stop it too. And be careful not to mix up straight paths with circular ones.
