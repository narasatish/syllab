What are Plants?

  • Plants are living things, just like us.
  • They grow all around us in gardens and parks.

🎙️ Just like you need food and water to grow, plants also need them. They are alive and grow bigger every day, making our world green and beautiful.

Many Parts, One Plant

  • A plant has different parts.
  • Each part does a special job to help the plant live.

🎙️ Think of your body; you have hands, legs, and a head. A plant also has different parts like roots, stem, leaves, flowers, and fruits, and each has an important role.

Roots - Holding Tight!

  • Roots grow under the ground.
  • They hold the plant firmly, like an anchor.
  • They drink water and food from the soil.

🎙️ Imagine roots are like the plant's feet, keeping it standing strong. They also act like tiny straws, sucking up all the water and nutrients from the soil for the plant.

🌿🌿🌿
Roots under the soil
5 / 20

Two Kinds of Roots

  • Some plants have one main root (taproot), like a carrot.
  • Others have many small roots (fibrous roots), like grass.

🎙️ A carrot has one big, thick taproot that goes deep. But if you look at grass, it has many thin, hair-like fibrous roots that spread out just below the surface.

Stem - Standing Tall!

  • The stem grows above the ground.
  • It helps the plant stand straight and tall.
  • It carries water and food to all other parts.

🎙️ The stem is like the backbone of the plant, giving it support. It also works like a highway, transporting water from the roots up to the leaves and food from the leaves to other parts.

⬆️
Stem growing upwards

Strong Stems, Weak Stems

  • Big trees have strong, woody stems called trunks.
  • Small plants have soft, green stems.

🎙️ Think of a giant banyan tree with its thick, hard trunk. Now think of a delicate rose plant or a herb with its thin, soft stem. Both are stems, but they are very different!

Leaves - Plant's Kitchen!

  • Most leaves are green and flat.
  • They make food for the plant using sunlight, air, and water.

🎙️ Leaves are super important! They are like tiny kitchens where the plant cooks its own food. They use sunlight, carbon dioxide from the air, and water from the roots to prepare food.

☀️
Sunlight on a leaf
9 / 20

Look at a Leaf!

  • Leaves have a flat part called the leaf blade.
  • They have tiny lines called veins, like lines on your hand.

🎙️ If you look closely at a leaf, you'll see the broad, flat green part. And those tiny lines running through it? They are like small roads, carrying water and food all over the leaf.

A single leaf

Flowers - Pretty and Sweet!

  • Flowers are the most beautiful part of a plant.
  • They come in many colours and smell nice.
  • Flowers later change into fruits.

🎙️ Everyone loves flowers! They make our gardens colourful and fill the air with sweet smells. But they also have a very special job: they are where the plant's fruits will grow from!

🌸🌸🌸🌸🌸
Beautiful Flowers

Fruits - Yummy Homes!

  • Fruits protect the seeds inside them.
  • We love to eat many delicious fruits.

🎙️ Think of an apple, a mango, or a watermelon. Inside, they all have seeds. The fruit is like a cozy, tasty home for these seeds, keeping them safe until they are ready to grow.

🍎🍎🍎
Fruits with seeds inside

Seeds - Tiny Beginnings!

  • Seeds are found inside fruits.
  • A tiny seed can grow into a brand new plant.

🎙️ Every seed holds a baby plant sleeping inside! With the right care – water, sunlight, and good soil – this tiny seed can wake up and grow into a big plant, just like its parent.

Plants Help Us a Lot!

Let's Care for Plants!

  • We should water plants regularly.
  • We should not pluck their leaves or flowers.
  • We should plant more trees and plants.

🎙️ Just like we care for our pets, we should care for plants too. Water them, don't harm them, and try to plant new ones. They make our world beautiful and healthy!
