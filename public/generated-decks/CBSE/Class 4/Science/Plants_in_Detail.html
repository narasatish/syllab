  • They grow under the ground.
  • They hold the plant firmly.
  • They keep the plant from falling.

Roots Drink Water!

  • Roots soak up water from the soil.
  • They also take in food (nutrients).
  • Like a straw, they pull it up!

🎙️ Roots have another super important job! They are like tiny straws. They drink up all the water and yummy food, called nutrients, from the soil. This water and food helps the plant grow big and strong.

Stem is a Highway!

  • It carries water from roots to leaves.
  • It carries food from leaves to other parts.
  • It's a busy road inside the plant!

🎙️ The stem also has a very busy job! It's like a highway inside the plant. It carries the water that roots drink, all the way up to the leaves. And it carries the food made by leaves to all other parts of the plant.

Strong Stems, Weak Stems

  • Trees have strong, woody stems (trunks).
  • Some plants have soft, weak stems.
  • They need support to climb up.

🎙️ Not all stems are the same! Big trees have very strong, thick stems called trunks. But some plants, like money plants, have very weak stems and need something to climb on to grow tall.

Leaves are Sun's Little Chefs!

  • Leaves use sunlight, water, and air.
  • They mix these to make food.
  • This process is called Photosynthesis!

🎙️ How do leaves make food? They are like little chefs! They use sunlight, water from the roots, and air to cook up yummy food for the plant. This special cooking process is called Photosynthesis.
