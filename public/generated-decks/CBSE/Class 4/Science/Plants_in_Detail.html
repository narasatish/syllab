The Roots: Our Plant's Anchor

  • Roots grow under the ground.
  • They hold the plant firmly in the soil.
  • They drink water and food from the soil.

🎙️ Think of roots like tiny straws buried in the ground. They suck up water and nutrients for the plant, and they also act like strong anchors, keeping the plant from falling over.

⬇️
Roots grow downwards
5 / 20

Two Kinds of Roots

  • Some plants have one main, thick root called a taproot (like a carrot).
  • Others have many thin, bushy roots called fibrous roots (like grass).

🎙️ Look at a carrot; it's a taproot! It has one big root going deep. But if you pull out a blade of grass, you will see many tiny roots all tangled together; those are fibrous roots.

Taproot (Carrot)
Fibrous (Grass)

The Stem: The Plant's Backbone

  • The stem grows above the ground.
  • It holds up the leaves, flowers, and fruits.
  • It carries water and food to all parts.

🎙️ The stem is like the plant's main highway! It gives support, helping the plant stand tall, and it also has tiny pipes inside to send water and food from the roots to the leaves and flowers.

⬆️
Stem grows upwards
7 / 20

Strong Stems and Weak Stems

  • Big trees have strong, woody stems called trunks.
  • Some plants have soft, weak stems that need support to grow.

🎙️ Feel the trunk of a big tree; it's very hard and strong! That's a strong stem. But some plants, like a money plant, have soft stems that need something to climb on to stand up.

The Leaf: The Plant's Food Factory

  • Leaves are usually green.
  • They make food for the whole plant.
  • They need sunlight, water, and air to do this.

🎙️ Leaves are like little kitchens for the plant! With the help of sunlight, water from the roots, and air, they cook up all the food the plant needs to grow big and strong.

🍃
A green leaf

Look Closely at a Leaf

  • The flat part is called the leaf blade.
  • The little lines are veins, like tiny roads.
  • A small stalk connects the leaf to the stem.

🎙️ If you look very closely at a leaf, you will see tiny lines. These are like tiny roads that carry water and food all over the leaf. The flat leaf blade is where all the food-making happens.

🔍
Magnifying a leaf

How Leaves Make Food (Photosynthesis)

  • 1. Leaves take in sunlight.
  • 2. They take in air (carbon dioxide).
  • 3. Roots send water up to them.
  • 4. Yummy plant food is made!

🎙️ This special process is called photosynthesis. It's how plants use simple ingredients like sun, air, and water to create their own delicious food, which helps them grow.

1Sunlight
2Air
3Water
4Food!

The Flower: The Plant's Beautiful Face

  • Flowers are often the most colorful part of a plant.
  • They attract insects like bees and butterflies.
  • Many flowers turn into fruits later on.

🎙️ Flowers are not just pretty to look at! Their bright colors and sweet smells attract bees and butterflies, which help them make seeds. And guess what? Many flowers turn into fruits!

🌸
A beautiful flower

What Makes a Flower Pretty?

  • Petals are the soft, colorful parts that make flowers beautiful.
  • Sepals are the small green leaves under the flower that protect the bud.

🎙️ The beautiful petals are what we often admire most about a flower. Underneath, you might see small green leaves called sepals, which protect the flower bud before it opens.

Flowers are the colourful part of plants.
13 / 20

The Fruit: Nature's Sweet Gift

  • Fruits grow from flowers.
  • They are often tasty and juicy to eat.
  • They protect the seeds inside them.

🎙️ We love eating fruits like apples, mangoes, and bananas! But for the plant, the fruit's main job is to keep the precious seeds safe inside, ready to grow into new plants.

Fruits are tasty and healthy.
14 / 20

Seeds: Tiny Packets of Life

  • Seeds are found inside fruits, or sometimes in pods.
  • Each tiny seed can grow into a new plant.
  • They need water, air, and warmth to sprout.

🎙️ A tiny seed holds the promise of a big new plant! If you give a seed enough water, air, and warmth, it will wake up and start to grow, sending out roots and a tiny shoot.

How a Plant Grows (Life Cycle)

  • 1. A tiny seed is planted.
  • 2. It sprouts roots and a shoot.
  • 3. It grows into a small plant.
  • 4. It becomes a big plant with flowers and fruits.

🎙️ Plants have a wonderful life cycle! It all begins with a tiny seed, which grows and changes, eventually making new seeds to start the cycle all over again.

Let's Care for Our Plants!

  • Water them regularly so they don't get thirsty.
  • Give them enough sunlight to make food.
  • Don't pluck leaves or flowers unnecessarily.

🎙️ Since plants do so much for us, it's important that we take good care of them. Give them water, make sure they get sunlight, and always be gentle with their leaves and flowers.
