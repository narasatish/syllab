Water turns into vapour!

  • Sun heats the water.
  • Water changes to invisible gas.
  • This gas is called water vapour.

🎙️ When the sun heats water in rivers, lakes, and oceans, it gets warm. This warm water changes into an invisible gas called water vapour and rises into the sky. This step is called Evaporation.

10 / 24

Vapour gets cold and becomes tiny drops!

  • Vapour rises higher.
  • It gets very cold there.
  • Tiny water drops form clouds.

🎙️ As water vapour rises high in the sky, it gets very cold. The vapour then changes back into tiny water droplets. Millions of these tiny droplets come together to form clouds. This step is called Condensation.

1Vapour goes up
2It gets cold
3Tiny drops form

Clouds get heavy and water falls down!

  • Clouds get too full of water.
  • Water falls back as rain, snow, or hail.
  • This is called precipitation.

🎙️ When clouds gather too many tiny water droplets, they become very heavy. They can't hold any more water! So, the water falls back to Earth as rain, snow, or sometimes hail. This step is called Precipitation.

Rainwater collects in rivers, lakes, and oceans!

  • Rainwater flows into streams.
  • Streams join rivers and lakes.
  • Rivers flow into big oceans.

🎙️ The water that falls back to Earth flows into small streams, which then join bigger rivers and lakes. Eventually, much of this water reaches the vast oceans. This step is called Collection.

Common Mistakes!

  • Mixing up evaporation and condensation.
  • Forgetting 'Collection' is a step.
  • Thinking water disappears forever.

🎙️ Sometimes, students get confused between evaporation (water going up) and condensation (clouds forming). Remember, water never disappears, it just changes its form and location!
