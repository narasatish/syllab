What Happens to Our Food?

  • When we eat, food is big.
  • Our body needs to break it into very tiny pieces.
  • This special process is called Digestion.

🎙️ Imagine eating a big roti! Can your body use it as it is? No, it needs to be made very, very small. Our body has a wonderful system that breaks down food so it can take all the good things from it. This is digestion!

Step 1: In Your Mouth!

  • Digestion starts right here, in your mouth!
  • Our teeth chew the food into smaller bits.
  • Saliva (spit) mixes with food, making it soft and wet.

🎙️ The mouth is the first stop on our food's journey. Our strong teeth are like little grinders, and our tongue helps push the food around. The watery saliva helps make the food easy to swallow.

Eight planets orbit the Sun.
6 / 20

Step 2: Down the Food Pipe

  • After chewing, we swallow the soft food.
  • It goes down a tube called the 'food pipe' (or esophagus).
  • This pipe carries food to your stomach.

🎙️ Once you swallow, the food doesn't just drop into your stomach. It travels down a special tube called the food pipe. It's like a slide for your food, gently pushing it towards the next stop.

⬇️
Food Going Down
7 / 20

Step 3: The Stomach Mixer

  • The food reaches your stomach, a muscular bag.
  • The stomach churns and mixes the food.
  • It adds strong juices to break food down further.

🎙️ Your stomach is like a big mixer! It moves around to mash the food even more. Special liquids, called digestive juices, help turn the food into a thick, soup-like paste. This helps prepare it for the next stage.

🥣
Stomach Mixing
8 / 20

Step 4: The Long, Winding Tube - Small Intestine

  • From the stomach, food goes into the 'small intestine'.
  • It's a very long, winding tube, but it's narrow!
  • Here, our body takes out all the good nutrients from the food.

🎙️ Even though it's called 'small,' the small intestine is actually very long – longer than you are tall! This is where the magic happens: all the vitamins, minerals, and energy from your food get absorbed into your blood to power your body.

🍝
Nutrient Absorption
9 / 20

Step 5: The Last Stop - Large Intestine

  • What the body doesn't need goes to the 'large intestine'.
  • The large intestine takes out water from the leftover food.
  • Finally, the waste leaves our body as stool.

🎙️ Not all food can be used by our body. The parts that are not useful move to the large intestine. Here, any remaining water is soaked up, and the solid waste is prepared to leave our body. It's a natural and important process!

Our body has many organs working together.

Chew Your Food Well!

  • Always chew your food slowly and properly.
  • Small pieces are easier for your stomach to digest.
  • This helps your body get more nutrients.

🎙️ Imagine trying to break a big rock versus small pebbles. It's much easier to break small pebbles! Similarly, when you chew your food well, you're helping your stomach do its job easily and efficiently. So, chew, chew, chew!

Drink Lots of Water!

  • Water is very important for digestion.
  • It helps food move smoothly through your body.
  • Water also helps your body absorb nutrients.

🎙️ Think of water as a lubricant for your food's journey. It helps everything flow nicely and prevents blockages. Drinking enough water also ensures that your body can easily take in all the good things from the food.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Say NO to Junk Food!

  • Chips, candies, and fizzy drinks are 'junk food'.
  • They have less good nutrients and too much sugar.
  • Eating too much junk food can make you unhealthy.

🎙️ While junk food might taste yummy sometimes, it's not good for your body every day. It's like putting low-quality fuel in a car – it won't run well. Try to eat these only once in a while, as a treat, not as your main meal.
