Our Body Needs Energy!

  • Our body is like a car. It needs fuel to run!
  • This fuel comes from the food we eat every day.

🎙️ Just like a car needs petrol to move, our bodies need energy to do all our daily activities. Think about running, playing, or even just thinking – all these need energy!

What is Digestion?

  • Digestion is the process where our body breaks down food.
  • It turns big food pieces into tiny parts our body can use.

🎙️ Imagine eating a big apple. Our body can't use it as a whole. Digestion is like a magical grinder that breaks that apple into very, very small pieces, so small that our blood can carry them everywhere.

The Journey Begins: In Your Mouth!

  • The food journey starts right when you take a bite!
  • Your teeth chew the food into smaller pieces.

🎙️ The first stop for your food is your mouth! Your teeth are super important here. They are like little grinders, breaking down the food so it's easier to swallow and digest.

Chew, Chew, Chew!

  • Chewing well is very important for digestion.
  • Your saliva (spit) also helps make the food soft.

🎙️ Remember to chew your food slowly and properly. The more you chew, the easier it is for your stomach to work. And that little bit of spit in your mouth, called saliva, starts breaking down the food too!

Down the Food Pipe!

  • After chewing, you swallow the food.
  • It goes down a tube called the food pipe, or esophagus.

🎙️ Once you swallow, the food doesn't just fall into your stomach! It travels down a special tube called the food pipe. It's like a slide for your food, leading it safely to the next stop.

Hello, Stomach!

  • The food reaches your stomach, a muscular bag.
  • Your stomach churns and mixes the food with special juices.

🎙️ Your stomach is like a mixer! It has strong muscles that squeeze and churn the food. It also has special liquids, called digestive juices, that help break down the food even more.

Into the Small Intestine!

  • From the stomach, food goes into a long, coiled tube.
  • This is called the small intestine, and it's where most digestion happens.

🎙️ Don't let the name 'small' fool you! The small intestine is actually very long, almost like a garden hose coiled up inside you. It's the main place where all the good parts of your food are absorbed.

Nutrients Go to Your Blood!

  • In the small intestine, tiny nutrients from food are absorbed.
  • These nutrients pass into your blood and travel all over your body.

🎙️ This is where the magic happens! All the vitamins, energy, and good stuff from your food are soaked up by your small intestine. Your blood then acts like a delivery truck, taking these nutrients to every part of your body so you can grow and be strong.

What About Waste? The Large Intestine!

  • Not all food can be digested.
  • The leftover, undigested food goes to the large intestine.

🎙️ Just like when you make juice, there's always some pulp left behind. Our body also has parts of food it can't use. This leftover goes into the large intestine, which is wider than the small intestine.

🔀
Large Intestine
11 / 20

Water Absorption & Waste Removal

  • The large intestine absorbs water from the waste.
  • Then, the solid waste leaves our body when we go to the toilet.

🎙️ The large intestine is important because it takes back any extra water from the waste, so our body doesn't lose too much. Finally, the solid waste, which our body doesn't need, is removed from our body. This is a very natural and important process.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

Recap: Our Food's Amazing Journey!

  • Food starts in the mouth, chewed by teeth.
  • Goes down the food pipe to the stomach.
  • Most nutrients absorbed in the small intestine.
  • Waste goes to the large intestine and out.
