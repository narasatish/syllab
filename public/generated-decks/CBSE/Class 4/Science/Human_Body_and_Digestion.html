The Food Journey Starts: Mouth!

  • We chew food with our teeth.
  • Saliva (our spit) makes it soft.
  • Chewing properly is the first step.

🎙️ The amazing journey of your food starts right in your mouth! When you bite into an apple, your teeth chew it into smaller bits. And that watery stuff in your mouth, called saliva, makes the food soft and easy to swallow. So, chew your food well!

Food Slides Down the Food Pipe

  • After chewing, we swallow the food.
  • It goes down a tube called the food pipe.
  • This pipe takes food to your stomach.

🎙️ Once you've chewed and swallowed, the food doesn't just drop into your stomach. It travels down a special tube called the 'food pipe' or 'oesophagus'. It's like a slide that gently pushes the food down towards your stomach.

The Stomach: A Mixer Grinder!

  • Your stomach is like a big bag.
  • It mixes and grinds the food more.
  • Special juices help break it down further.

🎙️ Once the food reaches your stomach, it's like entering a mixer grinder! Your stomach is a muscular bag that churns and mixes the food. It also adds special juices that help break the food down into an even finer paste. This takes a few hours!

Small Intestine: Soaking Up Goodness

  • Food goes into a long, coiled tube.
  • This is called the small intestine.
  • Here, all the good nutrients are soaked up!

🎙️ From the stomach, the food paste moves into a very long, narrow, and coiled tube called the small intestine. It's like a sponge here! All the good things from the food – the nutrients – are soaked up by your body to give you energy and help you grow.

Large Intestine: Saying Goodbye to Waste

  • What the body doesn't need goes here.
  • Water is absorbed from the waste.
  • Then, the waste leaves our body.

🎙️ Not everything we eat is useful. The parts of the food that your body can't use or absorb move into the large intestine. Here, any remaining water is taken back, and then the waste material is finally ready to leave your body. This keeps your body clean inside!
