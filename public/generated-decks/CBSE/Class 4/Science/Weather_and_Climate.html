What is Weather?

  • Weather is what the sky and air are like right now.
  • It can change many times in one day!

🎙️ Imagine you look out of your window. Is it sunny? Is it cloudy? Is it raining? That's the weather! It's like the mood of the sky, and it can change very quickly.

Clouds bring us rain.

  • It feels warm and happy!

🎙️ On a sunny day, the sun is high up, making everything bright. We love to play outside when it's sunny, don't we? It makes us feel warm and cheerful.

Sometimes it's Cloudy!

  • Big white or grey clouds cover the sun.
  • It might feel a little cooler.

🎙️ Then, sometimes, big fluffy clouds come and hide the sun. The sky looks a bit grey, and it might not be as hot. These clouds can look like cotton balls floating in the sky.

Splish, Splash! A Rainy Day!

  • Water drops fall from the clouds.
  • We use umbrellas and raincoats!

🎙️ And what happens when the clouds get very full of water? Yes, it rains! We hear the pitter-patter sound, and everything gets wet. It's fun to jump in puddles, but don't forget your umbrella!

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

🎙️ Have you ever felt the wind pushing you? That's a windy day! The air moves quickly, making trees sway and kites fly high. It feels cool and refreshing sometimes.

Weather Changes All Day Long!

  • Morning might be cool and fresh.

  • Evening becomes cooler again.

🎙️ Weather is a real quick-changer! In the morning, it might be a bit chilly. By lunchtime, the sun is strong, and it's hot. Then, as evening comes, it cools down again.

Why Does Weather Change? The Sun!

  • The sun warms our Earth.
  • Different amounts of sun make different weather.

🎙️ The biggest reason for weather changes is our sun! It's like a giant heater in the sky. When the sun shines strongly, it's hot. When clouds block it, it's cooler.

  • Wind moves air around the Earth.
  • It can bring cool air or warm air.

🎙️ Wind is simply air moving. This moving air can carry warmth from one place to another, or bring cool breezes. It helps spread clouds and even rain sometimes!

Why Does Weather Change? Clouds & Rain!

  • Clouds are like water storage in the sky.
  • They bring rain, making the weather wet.

🎙️ Clouds are made of tiny water droplets or ice crystals. When these droplets become heavy, they fall as rain, snow, or hail, completely changing the weather from dry to wet!

What is Climate?

  • Climate is the usual weather of a place.
  • It's like the weather story for many, many years.

🎙️ Now, let's talk about 'climate'. Climate is not just what happens today. It's what kind of weather a place usually has for a very long time, like 20 or 30 years.

🌍
Earth's Climate
12 / 20

Weather vs. Climate: What's the Difference?

  • Weather: what you see today or this week.
  • Climate: what you expect over many years.

🎙️ Think of it this way: Weather is like what dress you wear today. Climate is like the type of clothes you have in your cupboard for the whole year, knowing if it's mostly hot or cold where you live.

Weather (Today)
Climate (Many Years)

Hot Climates: Deserts!

  • Places like deserts are hot and dry.
  • The sun shines almost every day!

🎙️ Some places on Earth are always hot, like deserts. They get a lot of sunshine and very little rain, year after year. People there wear light clothes.

Cold Climates: Mountains!

  • Places like high mountains are very cold.
  • They get lots of snow and ice!

🎙️ On the other hand, places like high mountains have a cold climate. It's usually chilly, and they often get snow, even in summer. People wear warm, thick clothes.

🏔️🏔️🏔️

Moderate Climates: Not Too Hot, Not Too Cold!

  • Some places have a climate that is neither too hot nor too cold.
  • They have pleasant weather for most of the year.

🎙️ Then there are places with a moderate climate. This means it's not super hot like a desert, and not super cold like the mountains. It's usually quite pleasant and balanced.
