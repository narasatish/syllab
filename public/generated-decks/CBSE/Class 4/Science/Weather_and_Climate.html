What is Weather?

  • Weather is what the sky and air are like right now.
  • It tells us if it's hot, cold, rainy, or sunny.
  • It changes very quickly!

🎙️ Imagine looking out of your window right now. What do you see? Is the sun shining brightly, or are there dark clouds? That's the weather! It's like the sky's mood, and it can change in just a few hours.

Weather Changes Every Day!

  • One day it can be sunny.
  • The next day it can be rainy.
  • Weather doesn't stay the same.

🎙️ Think about your clothes. You wear different clothes on different days, right? That's because the weather keeps changing. Sometimes you need a raincoat, sometimes sunglasses!

  • These are weather elements.

What is Climate?

  • Climate is the usual weather of a place.
  • It's like the 'average mood' of a place.
  • It stays the same for many, many years.

🎙️ Now let's learn about climate. Imagine if you visited a place, and every time you went there, it was always hot. That's its climate! Climate is the general weather pattern of a region over a very long time.

🌍
Overall Weather

Weather vs. Climate

  • Weather is 'what you get' today.
  • Climate is 'what you expect' generally.
  • Weather changes fast, climate changes slow.

🎙️ This is important! Weather is like your mood today – happy, sad, or sleepy. Climate is like your personality – generally cheerful or generally calm. Weather is short-term, climate is long-term.

Climate Stays for a Long Time

  • Climate is measured over 30 years or more.
  • It tells us what clothes people usually wear.
  • It affects the plants and animals there.

🎙️ Imagine your grandparents telling you about the weather when they were young. If it was always hot in their village, it means their village has a hot climate. Climate helps us know what to expect from a place.

🎙️ Think of a desert. It's always hot and has very little rain. That's why you see special plants like cacti and animals like camels that can live without much water.
