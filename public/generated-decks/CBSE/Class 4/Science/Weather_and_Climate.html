What is Weather?

  • Weather is what the air outside is like, right now.
  • It can be sunny, cloudy, rainy, or windy.
  • It changes many times in a day or week.

🎙️ So, weather tells us if it's hot or cold, wet or dry, sunny or cloudy outside, *right now*. It's what you feel when you step out of your house each morning. It can change very fast!

The Sun: Our Big Heater!

  • The Sun makes the Earth warm.
  • When the sun shines bright, it's hot.
  • When clouds cover the sun, it feels cooler.

🎙️ The biggest boss of our weather is the Sun! It sends us heat and light. When the sun is high and bright, we feel hot. When clouds block the sun, it feels less warm.

Clouds and Rain: Sky's Water!

  • Clouds are like fluffy cotton balls in the sky.
  • They bring rain, which makes everything cool and fresh.

What is Climate?

  • Climate is the average weather of a place over many, many years.
  • It tells us what kind of weather a place usually has.

🎙️ So, if we watch the weather for 30-40 years and see what it's *usually* like, that's the climate. For example, some places are always hot, like deserts, and some are always cold, like the mountains. That's their climate.

🌍
World's Climate
13 / 24

Weather vs. Climate: What's the Difference?

  • Weather changes daily.
  • Climate is the usual weather over many years.
  • Weather is short-term. Climate is long-term.

🎙️ This is very important! Remember, weather is like what you wear *today*. Climate is like knowing you'll *always* need warm clothes if you live in the mountains. Weather is now, climate is forever (or at least for a very long time)!

Where We Are: Hot or Cold?

  • Places near the middle of the Earth (equator) get more direct sunlight.
  • These places are usually very hot.
  • Places far from the equator are usually colder.

🎙️ Imagine a big ball, our Earth. The sun shines most directly on its middle part, called the equator. So, places near the equator, like parts of South India, are generally hot. Places far away, like near the poles, are very cold.

Be Careful! Common Mistakes!

  • Don't confuse 'weather' with 'climate'. Weather is short-term, climate is long-term.
  • Don't think all places near the equator are *always* rainy; some are deserts!

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused between weather and climate. Remember, weather is like a snapshot, and climate is like a whole movie. Also, while equator regions are hot, not all are wet. Some are dry like the Sahara desert!
