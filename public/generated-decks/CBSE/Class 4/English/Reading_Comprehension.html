What is "Reading Comprehension"?

  • It means understanding what you read.
  • It's like solving a puzzle with words!

🎙️ Reading comprehension is a big word, but it just means you understand what the words are trying to tell you. Imagine a puzzle, and each word is a piece. When you put them together, you see the whole picture!

🧩
Understanding words

Why is it Important?

  • It helps you enjoy stories more.
  • You can learn new things easily.

🎙️ If you understand what you read, stories become super fun! You can also learn about animals, space, or anything new from books. It helps you in all your subjects.

Step 1: Read Carefully

  • Read every word slowly.
  • Don't rush! Take your time.

🎙️ The first step is to read like a little turtle, slowly and carefully. Don't just look at the words; let your brain think about what each word means.

🐢
Read slowly like a turtle
4 / 19

Step 2: Find the Main Idea

  • What is the story mostly about?
  • What is the most important thing happening?

🎙️ After reading, ask yourself: What is the main point? Is it about a brave lion, or a girl who loves to paint? This is the main idea, like the biggest star in the sky.

💡
The big idea
5 / 19

Step 3: Look for Details

  • Find small pieces of information.
  • Who, what, when, where, why?

🎙️ Now, act like a detective with a magnifying glass! Look for small details. Who are the characters? What did they do? When did it happen? Where? Why?

🔍
Find the small parts
6 / 19

Step 4: Understand New Words

  • Guess what they mean from other words.
  • Ask your teacher or check a dictionary.

🎙️ Sometimes you find new words. Don't worry! Try to guess their meaning from the other words around them. If you can't, just ask me or look it up later.

❓
What does it mean?

Step 5: Answer Questions

  • Use what you read to answer.
  • Think before you speak or write.

🎙️ Finally, you will answer questions about what you read. Use all the steps we learned. Think carefully, and your answers will be perfect!

Tips for Good Reading

  • Read every day, even a little bit.
  • Ask 'Why?' and 'How?' questions.
  • Talk about what you read.

🎙️ To become a super reader, read a little bit every day. Always ask questions in your mind. And talk to your friends or family about what you read!
