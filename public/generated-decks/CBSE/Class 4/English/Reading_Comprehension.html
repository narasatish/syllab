What is Reading Comprehension?

  • It means understanding what you read.
  • It's not just reading words.
  • It's about getting the meaning.

🎙️ Reading comprehension is like using your brain to understand a story. It's not just saying the words out loud, but really knowing what the author is trying to tell you.

Finding the Main Idea

  • The main idea is what the story is mostly about.
  • It's the most important message.
  • Think: 'What is the big picture?'

🎙️ Every story has a main idea. It's like the biggest star in the sky, telling you what the whole story is shining about. It's the central point the author wants you to know.

🌟

Finding Details

  • Details are small pieces of information.
  • They tell us more about the main idea.
  • They answer questions like 'who?', 'what?', 'where?', 'when?'

🎙️ After finding the main idea, we look for details. These are like the small leaves on a tree, giving us more information about the main trunk. They help us understand the story better.

🔍

🎙️ Think about making your favourite snack. You need many small ingredients like salt, sugar, or spices. Each one is a small detail that helps make the final dish, the main idea, delicious and complete.

Sequencing Events

  • Sequencing means putting things in order.
  • What happened first? What happened next?
  • What happened at the end?

🎙️ Stories happen in an order. Sequencing is about understanding that order, like a train moving from one station to the next. It helps us follow the story's journey.

➡️
Arrow for Order
13 / 24

Analogy: Making Tea

  • First, boil water.
  • Then, add tea leaves and sugar.
  • Finally, add milk and stir.

🎙️ Think about making a cup of tea. There are steps, right? You can't add milk before boiling water. Following these steps in order is sequencing, and it's important for understanding stories too.

Water moves in a never-ending cycle.

🎙️ Sometimes, questions will ask you specific things. Don't guess! Be a story detective. Look back at the text, find the keywords, and the answer will be hiding there for you to discover.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not reading the whole story carefully.
  • Guessing answers instead of finding them.
  • Missing small but important words.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes. It's okay! Just remember to read every word, don't guess, and always look for the answer in the story itself. This will help you avoid these common errors.
