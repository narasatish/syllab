What is Reading Comprehension?

  • It means understanding what you read.
  • Like understanding a story or a poem.

🎙️ Imagine reading a story. Reading comprehension is like knowing what happened in that story, who the characters were, and why things happened.

Step 1: Read Carefully

  • Read every word slowly.
  • Don't rush!

🎙️ The first step is very simple. Read each word, one by one. Don't try to read too fast, or you might miss something important.

Step 2: Find the Main Idea

  • What is the story mostly about?
  • What is the most important thing?

🎙️ After reading, ask yourself: 'What was the biggest idea in this story?' Was it about a brave cat, or a happy child?

Step 3: Look for Details

  • Who is in the story?
  • What happened?
  • Where did it happen?
  • When did it happen?

🎙️ Details are the small pieces of information that make the story complete. Think about the 'who', 'what', 'where', and 'when'.
