What are "Parts of Speech"?

  • Words have different jobs.
  • They help us make good sentences.

🎙️ Imagine words are like building blocks. Each block has a unique shape and helps us build strong and clear sentences. These different jobs are what we call 'Parts of Speech'.

Why do we need them?

  • To understand sentences better.
  • To speak and write correctly.

🎙️ Knowing these parts helps us understand what each word is doing in a sentence. It's like knowing which tool to use for which job to build a strong house.

Meet the "Helper Words": Pronouns!

  • Pronouns take the place of nouns.
  • They help us avoid repeating names.

🎙️ Next, we have Pronouns. Sometimes we don't want to keep saying the same name again and again. Pronouns come to our rescue by replacing those names.

🔄
Replace Me
8 / 20

Common Pronouns

  • Instead of "Rohan went to school.", we say "He went to school."
  • Instead of "The cat ate the fish.", we say "It ate the fish."
  • Other pronouns: she, they, we, you.

🎙️ See how easy that is? We use 'he' for a boy, 'she' for a girl, and 'it' for animals or things. 'They' is used for many people or things.

  • Adjectives tell us more about nouns.
  • They describe how a noun looks or feels.

🎙️ Now, let's meet Adjectives. Adjectives are like artists; they paint a picture of the noun in our minds. They tell us what kind of person, place, animal, or thing it is.

  • A red apple (tells about color).
  • A big elephant (tells about size).

Meet the "How, When, Where Words": Adverbs!

  • Adverbs tell us more about verbs (actions).
  • They tell us how, when, or where an action happens.

🎙️ Adverbs are super helpful! While adjectives describe nouns, adverbs describe verbs. They give us extra details about the action.

❓
More Info

Adverbs Add Details!

  • The rabbit runs quickly (how).
  • He came yesterday (when).
  • Sit here (where).

🎙️ Many adverbs end with '-ly', like 'slowly', 'loudly', 'carefully'. But not all! They make our stories more vivid.

Meet the "Position Words": Prepositions!

  • Prepositions show the position of a noun or pronoun.
  • They tell us where something is.

🎙️ Prepositions are like little signboards. They tell us exactly where one thing is in relation to another. Think about where your book is right now.

🎙️ Conjunctions are the friendly connectors of our language. They help us link ideas together so we don't have to make many short, choppy sentences.

Our Word Journey Recap!

  • Noun: Naming words (person, place, animal, thing).
  • Pronoun: Replaces nouns (he, she, it).
  • Verb: Doing words (action).
  • Adjective: Describes nouns (big, happy).
  • Adverb: Describes verbs (how, when, where).
  • Preposition: Shows position (on, under).
  • Conjunction: Joins words (and, but).

🎙️ We met many word friends today! Each one has a special role to play in building wonderful sentences. Keep looking for these 'Parts of Speech' in everything you read and say!
