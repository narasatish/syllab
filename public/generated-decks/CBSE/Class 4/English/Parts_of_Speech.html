🎙️ Adjectives add sparkle to our sentences! They help us paint a clearer picture in our minds by telling us more details about nouns.

Pronouns: Standing in for Nouns

  • Pronouns are words that replace nouns.
  • They help us avoid repeating the same noun.
  • They make sentences sound smoother.

🎙️ Pronouns are like stand-in actors for nouns! They step in so we don't have to keep saying the same noun many times, making our sentences sound much better.

🔄

Examples of Pronouns

  • *He* (for a boy/man)
  • *She* (for a girl/woman)
  • *It* (for an animal/thing)
  • *They* (for many people/things)

🎙️ Instead of 'My father is tall. My father is strong.', we can say 'My father is tall. *He* is strong.' 'He' is a pronoun. Can you think of sentences using 'she' or 'it'?

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Confusing Verbs and Adjectives:
  • Is 'running' a doing word or describing?
  • Not spotting Pronouns:
  • Look for words that *replace* names.

🎙️ It's okay to make mistakes, that's how we learn! One common mistake is mixing up verbs and adjectives. Remember, verbs are actions, adjectives describe.

How to Score Well in Exams!

  • Read the sentence carefully.
  • Ask: 'Who/What is it?' (Noun)
  • Ask: 'What is happening?' (Verb)
  • Ask: 'What kind/How many?' (Adjective)
  • Ask: 'What word replaces a name?' (Pronoun)

🎙️ In your exams, you might be asked to identify these words. Just follow these simple steps and ask yourself these questions for each word. Practice makes perfect!
