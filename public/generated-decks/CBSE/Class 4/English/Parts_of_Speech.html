Pronouns: Taking the Noun's Place!

  • Pronouns are words that replace Nouns.
  • They help us avoid repeating names.
  • Like 'He', 'She', 'It', 'They', 'I', 'You'.

🎙️ Sometimes, we don't want to keep saying the same name again and again. That's where Pronouns come in handy! They are like helpful substitutes.

👉
Pointing to Names
10 / 20

Using Pronouns

  • 'Ram is playing.' becomes 'He is playing.'
  • 'Sita is happy.' becomes 'She is happy.'
  • 'The dog barks.' becomes 'It barks.'

🎙️ See how easy it is? Instead of saying 'Mummy gave the book to Mummy', we can say 'Mummy gave the book to her'. It makes sentences smoother.

Verbs: The Action Heroes!

  • Verbs are 'doing' words.
  • They tell us what someone or something is doing.
  • Every sentence needs a Verb!

🎙️ Next, meet the 'Verbs'! These are the most exciting words because they show action! Without a verb, a sentence feels incomplete, like a story without any events.

🏃
Action!

What are they doing?

  • The bird sings. (sings is the Verb)
  • I eat food. (eat is the Verb)
  • She runs fast. (runs is the Verb)

🎙️ Think about all the things you do in a day: wake up, eat, play, read, sleep. All these actions are Verbs!

Birds have feathers and can fly.

Adjectives: Describing Nouns!

  • Adjectives tell us more about Nouns.
  • They describe how a Noun looks or feels.
  • Like 'big', 'red', 'happy', 'soft'.

🎙️ Now, let's meet Adjectives! These words are like little painters. They add colour and detail to our Nouns, making our sentences more interesting.

🔍
Details!
15 / 20

How does it look or feel?

  • A red ball. (red describes the ball)
  • A tall tree. (tall describes the tree)
  • A happy child. (happy describes the child)

🎙️ Adjectives help us imagine things better. Is the apple red or green? Is the story interesting or boring? Adjectives tell us!

Adverbs: Describing Verbs!

  • Adverbs tell us more about Verbs.
  • They say HOW, WHEN, or WHERE an action happens.
  • Many end with '-ly', like 'quickly', 'slowly'.

🎙️ Our next helper is the Adverb. If Adjectives describe Nouns, Adverbs describe Verbs! They tell us more about the action.

💨
How Fast?
17 / 20

How is the action done?

  • He runs quickly. (quickly tells how he runs)
  • She sings sweetly. (sweetly tells how she sings)
  • The car moved slowly. (slowly tells how it moved)

🎙️ Adverbs add more information to our actions. They can tell us if something happened fast or slow, loudly or quietly, happily or sadly.

Our Wonderful Word Family!

  • Nouns: Naming words (people, places, animals, things)
  • Pronouns: Replace Nouns (he, she, it)
  • Verbs: Doing words (actions)
  • Adjectives: Describe Nouns (red, happy)
  • Adverbs: Describe Verbs (quickly, sweetly)
