What Happens? (Plot)

  • The problem is what goes wrong.
  • The solution is how it gets fixed.

🎙️ Every good story has some excitement! There's usually a problem that the characters need to solve. And then, they find a way to fix it, which is the solution!

Story Example: The Thirsty Crow

  • Character: A crow.
  • Setting: A hot field.
  • Problem: Crow is thirsty, water is low.
  • Solution: Drops stones to raise water.
