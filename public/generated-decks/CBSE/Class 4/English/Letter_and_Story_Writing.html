Oops! Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting your address in a letter.
  • Not writing your name at the end.
  • Stories without a clear beginning or end.

🎙️ Sometimes we forget small things. Always remember your address in a letter and your name at the end. In stories, make sure it's easy to understand what happens first and last.
