What is a Letter?

  • A letter is a written message.
  • We send it to someone far away or nearby.
  • It helps us share news and feelings.

🎙️ Think of a letter like a special message you put on paper. Just like your parents might get letters from friends or family, you can also write to people you care about. It's a lovely way to stay in touch.

  • To invite friends to a party.
  • To thank someone for a gift.

🎙️ We write letters for many reasons! Maybe you want to tell your grandparents about your new toy, or invite your friend to your birthday party, or simply say 'Thank You' for something nice. Letters make people happy.

Two Kinds of Letters

  • 1. Informal Letters: To family and friends.
  • 2. Formal Letters: To schools or offices (we learn later).
  • Today, we focus on Informal Letters.

🎙️ There are two main types of letters. Informal letters are like a friendly chat on paper, for people you know well. Formal letters are for more serious things, like writing to your principal, but we will learn about those later. For now, let's focus on friendly letters!

Parts of an Informal Letter: Your Address

  • This is where you live.
  • Write your house number, street, city.
  • It helps the other person write back to you!

🎙️ Every letter starts with your address. Imagine if someone wants to send you a reply, they need to know where you live! So, first, we write our own address clearly at the top right side of the paper.

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

Parts of an Informal Letter: The Date

  • After your address, write the date.
  • Example: May 15, 2024.
  • It tells when you wrote the letter.

🎙️ Next, we write the date. This is important because it tells the person when you sent the letter. It helps them remember when they received your news. Just like how we write the date in our notebooks every day.

Parts of an Informal Letter: The Salutation

  • This is your greeting!
  • Start with 'Dear' and the person's name.
  • Example: 'Dear Grandpa,' or 'Dear Priya,'.

🎙️ After the date, we start with a friendly greeting. This is called the salutation. Since it's an informal letter, we use 'Dear' followed by the name of the person we are writing to, like 'Dear Mom' or 'Dear Uncle'.

👋
Saying hello

Parts of an Informal Letter: The Body

  • This is the main message.
  • Write what you want to say.
  • Keep it simple and clear.

🎙️ The body is the most important part! This is where you write everything you want to share. Tell them about your day, ask questions, or describe something fun you did. Use simple sentences, just like you talk.

Our body has many organs working together.

Parts of an Informal Letter: The Closing

  • How you end your letter.
  • Use words like 'Love,' 'Yours lovingly,' or 'Best wishes,'.

🎙️ After you have written your message, it's time to end the letter. This is called the closing. You can use sweet words like 'Love,' 'Yours lovingly,' or 'Best wishes,' to show your affection.

💖
Ending with love

Parts of an Informal Letter: Your Name

  • Write your name below the closing.
  • This is your signature.
  • It tells who sent the letter.

🎙️ Finally, after the closing, you write your own name. This is like signing your artwork, it shows who the letter is from. Make sure to write your name clearly so the person knows it's from you!

What is a Story?

  • A story is an exciting tale.
  • It has characters and things that happen.
  • Stories can be real or imaginary!

🎙️ Now, let's move to stories! Have you ever heard a story from your grandparents? A story is like a magical journey where we meet new people or animals, and exciting things happen to them. Stories can be true or made up.

Parts of a Story: Characters

  • Characters are the people or animals in a story.
  • Who is doing the actions?
  • They make the story interesting!

🎙️ Every good story needs characters! These are the stars of your story – the people, animals, or even magical creatures who do things and have adventures. Who will be in your story today? A brave boy, a clever fox, or a fairy?

Animals live all around us.

Parts of a Story: Setting

  • The setting is where and when the story happens.
  • Is it in a forest, a school, or a magical castle?
  • Is it day or night, long ago or today?

🎙️ The setting tells us where and when the story takes place. Is it a sunny morning in a park, or a dark night in a spooky house? Describing the setting helps us imagine the world of the story clearly.

🏞️
Where and when?

Parts of a Story: Plot (What Happens)

  • The plot is what happens in the story.
  • It has a beginning, a middle, and an end.
  • Like a journey from start to finish.

🎙️ The plot is the sequence of events, what happens from start to finish. Every story has a beginning where we meet characters, a middle where things get exciting, and an end where everything is resolved. Think of it as a path the characters follow.

Parts of a Story: Problem and Solution

  • Most stories have a problem the characters face.
  • Then they find a way to solve it!
  • This makes the story exciting.

🎙️ In many stories, the characters face a problem or a challenge. This makes the story interesting! But don't worry, they usually find a clever solution. This journey from problem to solution is what keeps us hooked.

Parts of a Story: The Moral

  • Some stories teach us a lesson.
  • This lesson is called the moral.
  • Example: 'Honesty is the best policy'.

🎙️ Sometimes, a story teaches us something important about life. This lesson is called the moral of the story. Like 'The Lion and the Mouse' teaches us that even small friends can be helpful. It's a wise thought to take away.
