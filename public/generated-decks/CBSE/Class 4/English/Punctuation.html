What is Punctuation?

  • Punctuation marks are like special signs.
  • They tell us how to read and understand sentences.
  • They make our writing clear and easy!

🎙️ Imagine driving a car without traffic lights or road signs. It would be chaos! Punctuation marks are just like those signs for our words. They guide us.

🚦
3 / 20

The Full Stop (.)

  • The full stop is a small dot.
  • It tells us that a sentence has finished.
  • Time to take a little breath!

🎙️ Our first friend is the full stop. It's a tiny dot, but it has a big job! When you see a full stop, it means the thought is complete, and you can pause.

⚫
4 / 20

Full Stop at the End

  • We use a full stop at the end of a telling sentence.
  • Example: The sun shines brightly.
  • It tells us a complete idea.

🎙️ Look at the sun! When we say 'The sun shines brightly', we put a full stop. It's a simple statement, and the full stop signals its end.

Let's Practice Full Stops

  • My dog loves to play.
  • I like to eat apples.
  • The bird sings a song.

🎙️ Can you see where the full stop goes in these sentences? It always comes at the very end, after the last word. It's like putting a lid on a box.

The Question Mark (?)

  • The question mark looks like a hook.
  • We use it when we ask a question.
  • It wants an answer!

🎙️ Next, we have the question mark. It's a curious little fellow! Whenever you ask something, like 'What is your name?', you use this mark.

❓

Asking Questions

  • Where is my book?
  • Are you coming to play?
  • What is your favourite colour?

🎙️ Think about all the questions you ask your parents or friends every day. Each of those questions needs a question mark at the end. It shows you're seeking information.

The Exclamation Mark (!)

  • The exclamation mark shows strong feelings!
  • Like surprise, excitement, or shouting.
  • It's like a jump of joy!

🎙️ Here comes the exciting exclamation mark! It's used when you're super happy, super surprised, or even a little scared. It adds a lot of emotion to your words.

❗

Showing Strong Feelings

  • Wow!
  • What a beautiful flower!
  • Help!

🎙️ Imagine you see a big, colourful rainbow! You'd say 'Wow!' or 'How beautiful!' These strong feelings need an exclamation mark. It makes your words pop!

The Comma (,)

  • The comma is a small curve.
  • It tells us to take a short pause.
  • We use it to separate items in a list.

🎙️ Our next punctuation friend is the comma. It's like a tiny speed bump in your sentence. It tells you to slow down for just a moment, not a full stop.

〰️
13 / 20

Listing Things

  • I like apples, bananas, and oranges.
  • My bag has pencils, books, and crayons.
  • The comma separates each item.

🎙️ When you go to the market and buy many fruits, you list them. 'Apples, bananas, and oranges.' The comma helps us keep them separate and easy to read.

Practice Commas

  • I saw a cat, a dog, and a bird.
  • My favourite colours are red, blue, and yellow.
  • We play cricket, football, and hide-and-seek.

🎙️ Let's practice! When you're listing three or more things, remember to put a comma after each item, except the last one before 'and'.
