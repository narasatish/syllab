What is a Poem?

  • A poem is a special way to use words.
  • It often tells a story or shares feelings.
  • Poems can make us happy or thoughtful.

🎙️ Imagine a poem as a beautiful painting, but with words instead of colours. It's a creative way to express ideas and feelings, making them sound musical and lovely.

What is a Stanza?

  • A stanza is like a paragraph in a poem.
  • It's a group of lines that belong together.
  • Poems can have many stanzas.

🎙️ Just like a story is divided into paragraphs, a poem is divided into stanzas. Think of each stanza as a small, neat box holding a few lines of the poem.

📦

What is Rhythm?

  • Rhythm is the musical beat of a poem.
  • It's how the words flow when you read them.
  • Like the beat of a drum!

🎙️ Every poem has a special 'beat' or 'flow' to it, just like a song. This is called rhythm. It makes the poem pleasant to listen to and read.

Rhyme Scheme: AABB

  • This is a simple rhyme pattern.
  • Line 1 rhymes with Line 2 (AA).
  • Line 3 rhymes with Line 4 (BB).

🎙️ Poems often follow patterns with their rhymes. One common pattern is AABB. This means the first two lines rhyme with each other, and the next two lines rhyme with each other.

🅰️🅱️
AABB Pattern

Rhyme Scheme: ABAB

  • This is another common rhyme pattern.
  • Line 1 rhymes with Line 3 (A).
  • Line 2 rhymes with Line 4 (B).

🎙️ Another fun rhyme pattern is ABAB. Here, the first line rhymes with the third line, and the second line rhymes with the fourth line. It creates a lovely criss-cross sound!
