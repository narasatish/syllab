What is Poetry?

  • Poetry is like a special way to tell stories or share feelings.
  • It uses beautiful words arranged in a musical way.

🎙️ Imagine you have a lovely feeling or a small story to tell. Poetry helps you tell it in a very creative and often rhythmic way. It's like painting with words!

What are Rhymes?

  • Rhymes are words that sound very similar at the end.
  • Like 'cat' and 'hat', or 'ball' and 'tall'.

🎙️ Listen carefully to the end of words. When they make a similar sound, we say they rhyme! Can you think of any words that sound like 'car'?

Why do we use Rhymes?

  • Rhymes make poems fun to read and listen to.
  • They also make poems easier to remember!

🎙️ Have you ever sung a song that was easy to remember? Rhymes help poems feel like songs, making them catchy and enjoyable. It's like a little game for your ears!

What is Rhythm?

  • Rhythm is the 'beat' or 'flow' of a poem.
  • It's like the music in a song.

🎙️ Every poem has a rhythm, just like your favorite song has a beat. It's what makes you want to clap your hands or tap your feet when you hear it.

  • Clap your hands to the beat of the words!

🎙️ Try reading 'Twinkle, twinkle, little star' and clap your hands. You'll feel the natural rhythm. It helps us enjoy the poem even more.

What are Stanzas?

  • Poems are often written in groups of lines.
  • Each group is called a stanza.

🎙️ Think of a stanza like a paragraph in a story. It's a small section of the poem, usually separated by a space. Each stanza often talks about one idea.

  • It helps babies go to sleep.

🎙️ Have you ever heard your parents or grandparents sing a soft song to a baby? That's often a lullaby! They have a very soothing rhythm and gentle rhymes.
