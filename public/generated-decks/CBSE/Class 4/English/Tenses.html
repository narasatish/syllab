What are Tenses?

  • Tenses tell us WHEN something happens.
  • Did it happen NOW?
  • Did it happen BEFORE?
  • Will it happen LATER?

🎙️ Imagine a clock that tells us not just the hour, but also if an action is happening now, happened in the past, or will happen in the future. That's what tenses do!

Present Tense: What is Happening NOW?

  • Present Tense talks about what happens every day.
  • It also tells us what is happening right now.
  • Like when the sun shines!

🎙️ Our first friend is Present Tense. It tells us about things that are true all the time, or things we do regularly, or even things happening at this very moment. Think of the sun shining every day.

Present Tense Example: I Eat an Apple.

  • 'Eat' tells us I am eating an apple now.
  • Or I eat an apple every day.

🎙️ Look at this apple! When I say 'I eat an apple,' it means I am doing it right now, or maybe I eat an apple every morning. 'Eat' is in the Present Tense.

Past Tense: What Happened BEFORE?

  • Past Tense talks about things that are finished.
  • They happened already.
  • Like yesterday or a long time ago.

🎙️ Our second friend is Past Tense. It's like a sand timer where the sand has already run out. It tells us about actions that are completely over. They are in the past.

⏳
Sand Timer

Past Tense Example: I Played Yesterday.

  • 'Played' tells us the game is over.
  • It happened yesterday, not now.

🎙️ Did you play yesterday? When I say 'I played yesterday,' it means the playing is finished. It's not happening now. 'Played' is the Past Tense of 'play'.

Past Tense Example: She Ate a Cookie.

  • 'Ate' tells us the cookie is all gone.
  • She finished eating it.

🎙️ Mmm, a cookie! If someone 'ate a cookie,' it means the cookie is finished, and the action of eating is over. 'Ate' is the Past Tense of 'eat'.

Future Tense: What Will Happen LATER?

  • Future Tense talks about things that are yet to come.
  • They will happen soon or in the future.
  • We often use 'will' with Future Tense.

🎙️ Our third friend is Future Tense. It's like looking into a crystal ball to see what will happen next. It tells us about actions that are not yet started, but will happen.

🔮

Future Tense Example: I Will Go to School.

  • 'Will go' means I am not at school now.
  • I will go there later today or tomorrow.

🎙️ After our class, you 'will go to school' or maybe 'will go home'. 'Will go' tells us it's an action that will happen in the future, not now.

Future Tense Example: We Will Play.

  • 'Will play' means we are not playing now.
  • We plan to play later, maybe after homework.

🎙️ Look at these kites! If we say 'We will play with kites,' it means we are talking about playing later, not right now. 'Will play' is the Future Tense.

One Action, Different Times: Eat

  • Present: I **eat** an apple now.
  • Past: I **ate** an apple yesterday.
  • Future: I **will eat** an apple tomorrow.

🎙️ See how the word 'eat' changes to tell us when I had the apple? 'Eat' for now, 'ate' for before, and 'will eat' for later. It's like magic!

Why are Tenses Important?

  • Tenses help us tell stories clearly.
  • Everyone understands when things happen.
  • It makes our English better!

🎙️ Tenses are like special helpers for our words. They make sure that when we talk or write, everyone understands if we are talking about now, before, or later. It helps us share our stories perfectly!
