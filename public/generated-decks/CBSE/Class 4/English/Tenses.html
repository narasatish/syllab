Simple Present Tense

  • Used for things that happen regularly.
  • Or for facts that are always true.
  • We add 's' or 'es' to the verb for 'he', 'she', 'it'.

🎙️ The sun rises every morning. This is a simple present tense fact. We use simple present for our daily habits, like 'I brush my teeth' or 'She goes to school'.

Simple Past Tense

  • Used for actions that started and finished in the past.
  • Often ends with '-ed' for regular verbs.
  • Example: "walk" becomes "walked".

🎙️ The simple past tense is like looking at an old clock. It tells us about a time that has passed. Most verbs just add '-ed' to show they are in the past, like 'played' or 'jumped'.

Simple Future Tense

  • Used for actions that will happen later.
  • We use "will" or "shall" before the verb.
  • Example: "I will play."

🎙️ A crystal ball shows us what might happen in the future! The simple future tense helps us talk about our plans or predictions for what is coming. Remember to use 'will' or 'shall'.
