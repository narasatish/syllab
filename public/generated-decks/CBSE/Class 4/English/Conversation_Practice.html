What is a Conversation?

  • It's when two or more people talk.
  • They share ideas, feelings, and news.
  • It's like a friendly chat!

🎙️ A conversation is simply when two, three, or even more people are talking to each other. They are sharing their thoughts, what they feel, or telling each other about their day. It's a friendly exchange of words, just like a chat with your friends or family.

5 / 24

Like Playing Catch!

  • One person 'throws' a thought.
  • The other person 'catches' it and 'throws' back.
  • It's a give and take!

🎙️ Think of a conversation like playing catch with a ball. One person speaks, which is like throwing the ball. The other person listens and understands, which is like catching the ball. Then, they respond, which is like throwing the ball back. It's a turn-taking game!

How to Start? Say Hello!

  • The first step is a friendly greeting.
  • It shows you want to talk.
  • A smile always helps too!

🎙️ To start a conversation, the very first thing you do is say 'hello' or a friendly greeting. This shows the other person that you are interested in talking to them. Don't forget to add a warm smile – it makes you look friendly and approachable!

  • "Hello!"
  • "Good morning!"

🎙️ Here are some simple and friendly ways to say hello. You can say 'Hi!' or 'Hello!'. If it's morning, you can say 'Good morning!'. And a very common way to start is by asking, 'How are you?' These are all great ways to begin a chat.

Ask a Simple Question

  • After 'hello', ask something easy.
  • It helps the other person talk.
  • Show you are interested in them.

🎙️ Once you've said hello, the next step is to ask a simple question. This is like 'throwing the ball' to them. It gives them something to respond to and shows that you are interested in what they have to say. Keep the question easy to answer.

What Can You Ask?

  • "What's your name?"
  • "Do you like playing in the park?"
  • "What is your favourite colour?"

🎙️ Here are some examples of simple questions you can ask. You can ask their name, or if they like doing something that you like too, like playing in the park. Or you can ask about their favourite things, like their favourite colour or animal. These questions are easy for anyone to answer.

How to Keep Talking? Listen!

  • Pay attention to what they say.
  • Don't interrupt them.
  • Listening is as important as talking.

🎙️ Now, to keep the conversation going, the most important thing is to listen! When the other person is speaking, make sure you pay close attention to their words. Don't interrupt them. Listening carefully shows respect and helps you understand what to say next.

👂

Hear and Understand

  • It's not just hearing sounds.
  • It's understanding the meaning.
  • Think about what they said.

🎙️ Listening is more than just hearing the words with your ears. It's about understanding what the other person means with their words. Try to think about what they said and what they are trying to tell you. This helps you respond thoughtfully.

🧠

Reply and Share Your Thoughts

  • After listening, it's your turn.
  • Give your answer or share a related idea.
  • This keeps the 'ball' going!

🎙️ Once you've listened and understood, it's your turn to 'throw the ball back'. Give your answer to their question, or share a thought that is related to what they just said. This is how you keep the conversation flowing smoothly, like a river.

Talk Nicely: Be Polite

  • Always use 'please' and 'thank you'.
  • Speak in a kind and gentle voice.
  • Be respectful to everyone.

🎙️ It's not just about what you say, but how you say it. Always remember to be polite. Use magic words like 'please' when you ask for something and 'thank you' when you receive something. Speak softly and kindly, and always show respect to the person you are talking to.

Take Turns Talking

  • Don't talk over others.
  • Wait for your turn to speak.
  • Everyone should get a chance.

🎙️ Just like in a game, everyone should get a turn to speak. Don't interrupt or talk over someone else. Wait patiently for them to finish, and then it's your turn. This makes the conversation fair and enjoyable for everyone involved.

↔️
Taking turns

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Talking only about yourself.
  • Not listening to the other person.
  • Being rude or using bad words.

🎙️ Here are some things we should try not to do. Don't talk only about yourself; remember to ask about others. Always listen when someone else is talking. And never be rude or use words that might hurt someone's feelings. These mistakes can stop a conversation quickly.
