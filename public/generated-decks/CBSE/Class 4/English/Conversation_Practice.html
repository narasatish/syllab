What is a Conversation?

  • When two or more people talk.
  • They share ideas and feelings.
  • It's like playing 'catch' with words.

🎙️ A conversation is simply when people talk to each other. Imagine you are throwing a ball back and forth; in a conversation, you are throwing words and ideas back and forth.

Why do we Talk?

  • To say hello and greet.
  • To ask questions and learn.
  • To share our happy moments.

🎙️ We talk for many reasons! Sometimes we just want to say hello to a friend. Other times, we want to ask something or tell someone about our day.

Starting a Conversation

  • The first step is to greet.
  • Say 'Hello' or 'Hi' politely.
  • A smile helps too!

🎙️ The easiest way to start talking is by saying a friendly 'Hello!' or 'Hi!'. Remember to smile, it makes others feel welcome.

Example: Friend at Park

  • See Rohan playing in the park.
  • Maya wants to join him.

🎙️ Imagine Maya sees her friend Rohan in the park. She can start by saying 'Hello, Rohan!'. Then she can ask 'How are you?' and then invite him to play.

1Hello, Rohan!
2How are you?
3Let's play!

Asking Questions

  • We ask to know more.
  • Use words like 'What?', 'Where?', 'How?'.
  • It shows you are interested.

🎙️ Asking questions is a great way to keep a conversation going. When you ask 'What is your favourite colour?', you learn something new about the other person.

❓
Asking to know

Example: New Classmate

  • Meet your new friend, Sam.
  • You want to know about him.

🎙️ When a new student joins your class, you can introduce yourself first. Then, you can ask their name and where they are sitting. This helps them feel welcome.

1Hi, I'm Maya.
2What's your name?
3Where do you sit?

Sharing Ideas

  • Tell others what you think.
  • Share your likes and dislikes.
  • This makes the talk interesting.

🎙️ Sharing your thoughts and ideas makes the conversation lively. You can say 'I love ice cream!' or 'My favourite animal is a dog'. It helps others know you better.

Listening is Important!

  • Pay attention when others speak.
  • Look at the person talking.
  • Don't interrupt them.

🎙️ Just like talking, listening is a very important part of a conversation. When someone is speaking, we should listen carefully and not interrupt them.

👂

Good Manners in Talking

  • Say 'Please' and 'Thank You'.
  • Wait for your turn to speak.
  • Speak softly, not too loud.

🎙️ Using good manners makes you a great conversationalist. Always remember to say 'Please' and 'Thank You', and wait for your turn to speak.

Ending a Conversation

  • Say 'Goodbye' or 'Bye-bye'.
  • You can also say 'See you soon!'.
  • End politely and with a smile.

🎙️ Just like starting, ending a conversation politely is important. A simple 'Goodbye' or 'See you tomorrow' shows good manners.
