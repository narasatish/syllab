What is a Conversation?

  • It means talking to one or more people.
  • We share our ideas and feelings.

🎙️ A conversation is simply when two or more people talk to each other. It's like when you chat with your friend during playtime or talk to your parents at home.

Starting a Conversation

  • Always begin with a warm greeting.
  • Say "Hello," "Hi," or "Good morning."

🎙️ Imagine you see your friend. How do you start talking to them? You say "Hello!" or "Hi!" This is a polite way to begin.

What to Say Next?

  • Ask how they are doing.
  • Talk about something common.
  • "How was your day?"

🎙️ After saying hello, you can ask a simple question. This shows you care. You can ask "How are you?" or "How was your day?"

💬
Ask Questions

Listening is Important

  • When someone talks, we must listen.
  • Don't interrupt them.

🎙️ Talking is not just about speaking. It's also very important to listen when others are talking. Let them finish what they are saying.

Talking to Elders

  • Always be respectful and polite.
  • Use "Please," "Thank You," and "Excuse me."

🎙️ When we talk to elders, like our parents or teachers, we must be extra polite. Use magic words like "Please" when you ask for something and "Thank You" when you receive it.

🙏
Be Respectful
12 / 20

Example: Rohan asks his teacher

  • Rohan: "Excuse me, teacher. May I drink water, please?"
  • Teacher: "Yes, Rohan, you may."

🎙️ See how Rohan asked his teacher. He used "Excuse me" and "Please." This is the right way to talk to elders.

Keeping the Conversation Going

  • Make eye contact.

Ending a Conversation

  • It's polite to say goodbye.
  • "See you soon!" or "Bye!"

🎙️ Just like starting, ending a conversation politely is also important. When it's time to go, say "Goodbye" or "See you later!"

Good Conversation Habits

  • Be polite and use kind words.
  • Look at the person you are talking to.
  • Listen well and take turns.

🎙️ These are some golden rules for good conversations. Always be kind, look at the person, and make sure you listen to them when they speak.
