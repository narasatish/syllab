What is a Pattern?

  • A pattern is a design or sequence that repeats.
  • It follows a rule or a plan.
  • It's like a secret code!

🎙️ Think of a pattern like a special arrangement where things happen again and again in the same way. It's not random; there's always a hidden rule that makes it predictable. Once you find the rule, you can guess what comes next!

Repeating Patterns

  • These patterns show the same thing again and again.
  • Like a song that has a chorus.
  • They are easy to spot!

🎙️ The simplest type of pattern is a repeating pattern. It's like when you sing a song and the same lines come back again and again. In these patterns, a group of items or colours just keeps showing up in the same order.

Example: Shape Pattern

  • Circle, Square, Circle, Square...
  • What comes next?
  • The 'rule' is Circle then Square.

🎙️ Look at this pattern: Circle, Square, Circle, Square. Can you guess what shape will come next? Yes, a Circle! The rule here is very simple: first a Circle, then a Square, and this pair keeps repeating.

🎙️ Here's another repeating pattern, but this time with colours: Red, Blue, Red, Blue. If I asked you to complete this pattern, you would know that Red comes after Blue. The rule is simply to alternate between Red and Blue.

Example: Number Pattern (Counting)

  • 1, 2, 3, 4, 5...
  • What is the rule here?
  • We are adding 1 each time.

🎙️ Numbers can also form patterns! Look at the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. What do you notice? Each number is one more than the previous one. So, the rule is to 'add 1' to get the next number. This is a very common number pattern.

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

Growing Patterns

  • These patterns get bigger or increase.
  • Something is added each time.
  • They follow a 'growing' rule.

🎙️ Now, let's look at a different kind of pattern called a growing pattern. Imagine a plant that grows taller every day. In a growing pattern, something is added or increases with each step, making the pattern 'grow'.

  • One star, then two stars, then three stars...
  • What comes next?
  • The rule is 'add one star' each time.

🎙️ Here, we start with one star, then we have two stars, then three stars. What would be the next step? Yes, four stars! The rule for this pattern is to add one star every time. The number of stars keeps growing.

Example: Growing Pattern (Numbers)

  • 2, 4, 6, 8...
  • What is the rule?
  • We are adding 2 each time!

🎙️ Let's look at this number pattern: 2, 4, 6, 8. How do we get from 2 to 4? We add 2. How do we get from 4 to 6? We add 2 again! So, the rule for this growing pattern is to 'add 2' to the previous number.

Shrinking Patterns

  • These patterns get smaller or decrease.
  • Something is taken away each time.
  • They follow a 'shrinking' rule.

🎙️ Just like things can grow, they can also shrink! In a shrinking pattern, something is removed or decreases with each step. Imagine a stack of blocks where you take one block away each time. That's a shrinking pattern!
