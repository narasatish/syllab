  • Time tells us when things happen.
  • It helps us plan our day.
  • Like when to wake up or when to play!

🎙️ Time is like an invisible friend that helps us keep track of everything. It tells us when it's morning, afternoon, or night. Without time, our days would be very confusing!

Our Friend, The Clock!

  • A clock helps us see time.
  • It has numbers from 1 to 12.
  • It has hands: a short one for hours, a long one for minutes.

🎙️ Look at the clock! The short hand tells us the hour, and the long hand tells us the minutes. Some clocks even have a super thin hand that moves very fast, showing seconds!

Reading Hours on the Clock

  • When the long hand points to 12, it's an 'o'clock'.
  • The short hand tells us the hour.
  • Like 3 o'clock or 6 o'clock!

🎙️ It's easy to read hours! Just look at where the short hand is pointing. If the long hand is on 12, then it's exactly that hour. Try to find 10 o'clock on a clock.

Reading Minutes on the Clock

  • Each small mark between numbers is 1 minute.
  • From one number to the next is 5 minutes.
  • We count by 5s for minutes: 5, 10, 15, 20...

🎙️ Reading minutes is like counting by fives! When the long hand moves from 12 to 1, that's 5 minutes. If it moves to 3, that's 15 minutes. Can you find 45 minutes past the hour?

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

Our Indian Money: Rupee and Paisa

  • In India, our money is called Rupee (₹).
  • Smaller parts are called Paisa.
  • 1 Rupee = 100 Paisa.

🎙️ Just like 1 meter has 100 centimeters, 1 Rupee has 100 Paisa. We mostly use Rupee coins and notes now, but it's good to know about Paisa too. Look for the Rupee symbol on notes!

Counting Coins

🎙️ It's fun to count coins! If you have two ₹5 coins, how much money is that? Yes, ₹10! Practice adding different coins to find the total amount.

Counting Notes

  • We have notes like ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100.
  • Notes are bigger than coins.
  • They are used for larger amounts.

🎙️ Notes are paper money. Imagine you have a ₹50 note and a ₹20 note. How much money do you have in total? That's right, ₹70! We use notes for bigger purchases.

Quick Question: How Much Money?

  • You have one ₹20 note.
  • You also have two ₹5 coins.
  • How much money do you have in total?

🎙️ Add up the values of the note and the coins. Remember, two ₹5 coins make ₹10. So, how much is ₹20 plus ₹10? You got it!

What is Measurement? How Big?

  • Measurement tells us how big, long, heavy, or much something is.
  • We use special tools for it.
  • Like a ruler for length or a scale for weight.

🎙️ Measurement helps us understand the world around us better. Is your pencil long or short? Is your school bag heavy or light? We use measurement to answer these questions.

Measuring Length: How Long?

  • We measure length using a ruler or a measuring tape.
  • Small things in centimeters (cm).
  • Bigger things in meters (m).

🎙️ A ruler helps us measure small things like pencils or books in centimeters. For bigger things like the length of your room, we use a measuring tape and measure in meters. Remember, 1 meter is 100 centimeters!

Measuring Weight: How Heavy?

  • We measure weight using a weighing scale.
  • Light things in grams (g).
  • Heavy things in kilograms (kg).

🎙️ When you buy vegetables, the shopkeeper uses a weighing scale. Small things like a chocolate bar are measured in grams. Bigger things like a bag of rice are measured in kilograms. 1 kilogram is 1000 grams!

Measuring Capacity: How Much Can It Hold?

  • Capacity tells us how much liquid a container can hold.
  • Small amounts in milliliters (ml).
  • Larger amounts in liters (l).

🎙️ Think about your water bottle. How much water can it hold? That's its capacity! Small bottles of juice are in milliliters, and big milk cartons are in liters. 1 liter is 1000 milliliters!
