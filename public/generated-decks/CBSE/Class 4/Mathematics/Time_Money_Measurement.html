What is Time?

  • Time tells us when things happen.
  • It helps us plan our day.

🎙️ Time is like an invisible friend that tells us when to wake up, when to go to school, and when to play. We use clocks to see time.

A clock helps us read hours and minutes.

  • The short hand points to the hour.
  • It tells us the big number.

🎙️ Look at a clock! The short hand is called the hour hand. When it points to 3, it's 3 o'clock. Easy, right?

Reading a Clock: Minutes

  • The long hand points to the minutes.
  • Each big number is 5 minutes.

🎙️ The long hand is the minute hand. When it points to 12, it's 'o'clock'. When it points to 3, it's 15 minutes past the hour. We count by fives for minutes!

AM and PM: Day and Night

  • AM is morning time (before noon).
  • PM is afternoon and night time (after noon).

🎙️ We use AM for morning activities like breakfast and school. We use PM for evening activities like playing and sleeping. The day has 24 hours, but we split it into two halves!

What is Money?

  • Money helps us buy things we need.
  • It comes in notes and coins.

🎙️ Money is what we use to buy our favourite snacks, toys, or even books for school. It is very important in our daily lives.

The rupee (₹) is our money.

Indian Currency: Rupee and Paisa

  • In India, we use Rupees (₹).
  • 1 Rupee = 100 Paisa.

🎙️ Our country India uses Rupees. You might have seen notes like ₹10, ₹20, ₹100, and coins like ₹1, ₹2, ₹5. Paisa are smaller units, but we don't use them much now.

  • Let's count these notes.
  • One ₹50 note + One ₹20 note = ₹70.

🎙️ Imagine you have a ₹50 note and a ₹20 note. How much money do you have in total? You add them up: 50 + 20 = 70. So, you have ₹70.

Counting Money: Coins

  • Let's count these coins.
  • Two ₹5 coins + One ₹2 coin = ₹12.

🎙️ Now, let's count coins. If you have two ₹5 coins and one ₹2 coin, how much do you have? 5 + 5 + 2 = 12. So you have ₹12.

What is Measurement?

  • Measurement tells us how big or small something is.
  • It helps us find length, weight, and capacity.

🎙️ Measurement is like finding out 'how much' of something there is. How tall are you? How heavy is your school bag? How much water is in a bottle? Measurement helps us answer these questions.

Measuring Length: Metre and Centimetre

  • We use a ruler or measuring tape.
  • Metre (m) for long things, Centimetre (cm) for short things.

🎙️ To measure how long something is, we use units like metres and centimetres. A metre is used for bigger things like the length of a room. A centimetre is used for smaller things like your pencil.

Measuring Weight: Kilogram and Gram

  • We use a weighing scale.
  • Kilogram (kg) for heavy things, Gram (g) for light things.

🎙️ To find out how heavy something is, we use kilograms and grams. Your school bag might weigh a few kilograms. A single apple might weigh a few grams.

Measuring Capacity: Litre and Millilitre

  • Capacity tells us how much liquid a container can hold.
  • Litre (L) for more liquid, Millilitre (mL) for less liquid.

🎙️ Capacity is about how much liquid, like water or milk, a bottle or jug can hold. We use litres for big bottles and millilitres for small amounts, like medicine.
