Tally Marks: Our Counting Helper

  • Tally marks are special lines we draw.
  • They help us count things quickly and easily.

🎙️ When we collect a lot of data, it can be hard to count everything just by looking. Tally marks are a very simple and smart way to keep track of our counts.

🧮
Counting Tool
6 / 20

How to use Tally Marks

  • Draw a line for each item.
  • For the fifth item, draw a line across the first four.

🎙️ It's like making bundles! One line for one, two for two, and when you reach five, you tie the bundle with a diagonal line. This makes counting bigger numbers much easier.

What is a Pictograph?

  • A pictograph uses pictures to show data.
  • Each picture stands for a certain number of items.

🎙️ Isn't it fun to see pictures? A pictograph is like a picture story of our data. Instead of numbers, we use small pictures to represent the count of things.

What is a Bar Graph?

  • A bar graph uses bars to show data.
  • The height of the bar shows the quantity.

🎙️ A bar graph is another way to show data, but instead of pictures, we use tall rectangles called 'bars'. The taller the bar, the more of that item there is.
