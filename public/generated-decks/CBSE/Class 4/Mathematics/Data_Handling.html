Tally Marks: A Fun Way to "Count"

  • Tally marks help us count things quickly.
  • We draw lines for each item.

🎙️ Tally marks are like little sticks! For every item you count, you draw one stick. It's a very old and simple way to keep track of numbers without writing big digits.

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

Tally Marks: The "Group" of Five

  • For the fifth item, we draw a line across the first four.
  • This makes a neat group of five!

🎙️ When you reach five, you make a diagonal line over the four straight lines. This helps us count big numbers faster, by seeing groups of five, just like your hand has five fingers!

"Pictographs": Pictures Tell a Story

  • A pictograph uses pictures to show data.
  • Each picture stands for a certain number of things.

🎙️ Imagine you want to show how many students like different colors. Instead of numbers, you can draw little pictures of students! It makes data look fun and easy to understand.

🖼️
Picture Chart
11 / 20

Steps to Make a "Pictograph"

  • 1. Collect data.
  • 2. Choose a simple picture.
  • 3. Decide what each picture means (the key).
  • 4. Draw pictures for each item.

🎙️ The 'key' is very important! It tells us if one picture means 1 thing, or 2 things, or even 5 things. Always look at the key first to understand the pictograph.

1Collect Data
2Choose Picture
3Make a Key
4Draw Pictures

"Bar Graphs": Tall Towers of Information

  • A bar graph uses bars to show data.
  • The height or length of the bar shows the amount.

🎙️ Bar graphs are like building blocks or tall towers! Each bar stands for something, and how tall it is tells us how much of that thing there is. Taller bar means more!

📊
Bar Chart
14 / 20

Steps to Make a "Bar Graph"

  • 1. Collect data.
  • 2. Draw two lines (axes).
  • 3. Label what each line means.
  • 4. Draw bars for each item.

🎙️ One line might show the names of things, and the other line might show the numbers. It's important to label them clearly so everyone can understand what the graph is showing.

1Collect Data
2Draw Axes
3Label Axes
4Draw Bars
