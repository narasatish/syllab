Keywords for Addition (+)

  • Look for words like: 'total', 'in all', 'altogether', 'sum', 'add'.
  • Addition means putting things together or getting more.
  • Example: Rina had 2 balloons. Her friend gave her 3 more. How many 'in all'?

🎙️ When you see words like 'total' or 'in all', it usually means you need to add. Think of it as collecting more things or combining different groups into one big group. Like adding more balloons to your collection!

🎈🎈🎈🎈🎈
Adding more balloons

Keywords for Subtraction (-)

  • Look for words like: 'left', 'difference', 'how many more', 'take away'.
  • Subtraction means taking away or finding the gap.
  • Example: You had 5 cookies. You ate 2. How many 'left'?

🎙️ If the problem asks 'how many left' or 'what is the difference', it's a hint for subtraction. Subtraction is like giving away things, or finding out how much bigger one number is than another. Imagine eating some delicious cookies!

Keywords for Multiplication (×)

  • Look for words like: 'times', 'groups of', 'total in equal groups'.
  • Multiplication is fast addition of the same number.
  • Example: 3 plates with 2 ladoos 'each'. How many 'in all'?

🎙️ Multiplication is used when you have equal groups and you want to find the total. It's like adding the same number many times, but much faster! Think of having many plates, and each plate has the same number of sweets.

Keywords for Division (÷)

  • Look for words like: 'share equally', 'distribute', 'each', 'groups of'.
  • Division is about sharing things equally or making equal groups.
  • Example: 8 crayons shared 'equally' among 4 friends. How many 'each'?

🎙️ When you need to share things fairly or divide a big group into smaller, equal groups, you use division. Remember Riya's sweets? That was a division problem! Look for words like 'share equally' or 'each'.

Exam Tips for Problem Solving!

  • Read the problem 2-3 times.
  • Underline keywords and numbers.
  • Show all your steps clearly.
  • Write the final answer with units (e.g., 5 apples).

🎙️ In exams, showing your steps is very important! Even if your final answer is wrong, you might get marks for correct steps. And always remember to write what your answer is about, like '5 apples' not just '5'.
