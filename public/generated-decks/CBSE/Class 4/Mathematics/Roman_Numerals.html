Rule 1: Repeat to Add!

  • You can repeat I, X, C, M.
  • Repeat up to three times only.
  • III means 1 + 1 + 1 = 3.

🎙️ Our first rule is simple: if you see the same Roman numeral repeated, you just add their values! But remember, you can only repeat a numeral up to three times. So, III is 3, and XX is 20.

🚶🚶🚶

11 / 24

Rule 2: Add When Smaller is After!

  • Larger number first, smaller number after.
  • Add their values together.
  • VI means 5 + 1 = 6.

🎙️ This rule is like having a big brother and a little brother walking together. If the smaller number comes AFTER the larger number, you add them up! So, V (5) and I (1) make VI, which is 6.

➕
Adding numbers
12 / 24

Rule 3: Subtract When Smaller is Before!

  • Smaller number first, larger number after.
  • Subtract the smaller from the larger.
  • IV means 5 - 1 = 4.

🎙️ Now, this is a bit tricky! If the smaller number comes BEFORE the larger number, you subtract! So, I (1) before V (5) means 5 minus 1, which is 4. Remember, only I, X, C can be subtracted, and only from certain numbers!

Subtracting numbers

Let's Make 14!

  • 10 is 'X'.
  • 4 is 'IV' (5 - 1).
  • So, 14 is 'XIV'.

🎙️ Let's try putting our rules together! To make 14, we first think of it as 10 and 4. We know 10 is 'X'. And we just learned that 4 is 'IV'. So, combining them gives us 'XIV'.

How to Write 23?

  • Think of 23 as 10 + 10 + 3.
  • 10 + 10 is 'XX'.
  • 3 is 'III'.
  • So, 23 is 'XXIII'.

🎙️ Now let's try 23. We can break 23 into 20 and 3. We know 20 is 'XX' (10+10). And 3 is 'III' (1+1+1). Put them together, and we get 'XXIII'!

Oops! Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Repeating V, L, D. (Never do this!)
  • Subtracting wrong numbers (e.g., VL for 45).
  • Writing IIII instead of IV.

🎙️ Even big mathematicians make small mistakes! Here are some common ones to watch out for. Remember, you can never repeat V, L, or D. And always check if you should add or subtract carefully!
