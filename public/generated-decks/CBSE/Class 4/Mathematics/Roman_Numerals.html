Rule 1: Repeat and Add (I, X)

  • If a symbol is repeated, we add its value.
  • You can repeat I or X up to three times.
  • I = 1, II = 2, III = 3.

🎙️ Our first rule is simple: if you see the same symbol repeated, you just add their values together. For example, two 'I's make 2, and three 'I's make 3. But remember, you can't repeat a symbol more than three times!

1I (1)
2II (1+1=2)
3III (1+1+1=3)
11 / 20

Rule 2: Smaller Before Larger (Subtract)

  • If a smaller value comes before a larger value, we subtract.
  • IV means 5 - 1 = 4.
  • IX means 10 - 1 = 9.

🎙️ This rule is a bit tricky, so pay close attention! If a smaller Roman numeral is placed *before* a larger one, it means we subtract the smaller from the larger. Think of 'IV' as 'one less than five', which is four.

V (5)
IV (4)
12 / 20

Rule 3: Smaller After Larger (Add)

  • If a smaller value comes after a larger value, we add.
  • VI means 5 + 1 = 6.
  • XI means 10 + 1 = 11.

🎙️ This rule is usually easier! If a smaller Roman numeral is placed *after* a larger one, it means we simply add their values together. So, 'VI' is 'five plus one', making six. And 'XI' is 'ten plus one', making eleven.

1V + I = VI (5+1=6)
2X + I = XI (10+1=11)

Let's Make Numbers! (4 and 6)

  • How do we write 4 and 6?
  • Remember our subtract and add rules!

🎙️ For 4, we cannot use IIII because we can only repeat I three times. So, we use the subtraction rule: 'I' before 'V' makes 4. For 6, we use the addition rule: 'I' after 'V' makes 6.
