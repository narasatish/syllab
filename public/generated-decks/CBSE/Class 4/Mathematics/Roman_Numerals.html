Rule 1: Adding Symbols (I, II, III)

  • When symbols are repeated, we add their values.
  • I = 1, II = 1 + 1 = 2, III = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3.
  • We can repeat I, X, C, M up to three times.

🎙️ Our first rule is simple: if you see the same symbol repeated, you just add their values together. So, two 'I's make 2, and three 'I's make 3. But remember, you can't repeat a symbol more than three times!

1I (1)
2I + I = II (2)
3I + I + I = III (3)
11 / 20

Rule 2: Subtracting Symbols (IV, IX)

  • If a smaller value comes before a larger value...
  • We subtract the smaller from the larger one.
  • Example: IV = 5 - 1 = 4.

🎙️ This rule is a bit like a trick! If a small number symbol, like 'I', comes just before a bigger number symbol, like 'V' or 'X', it means we have to subtract. So, 'IV' is not 1 plus 5, but 5 minus 1, which is 4!

1V - I = IV (4)
2X - I = IX (9)

Let's Make Numbers: 4 and 6

  • How do we write 4? Not IIII!
  • It's IV (5 - 1 = 4).
  • How do we write 6? It's VI (5 + 1 = 6).

🎙️ Here's a great example of our rules! For 4, we use subtraction: 'IV'. For 6, we use addition: 'VI'. The order of the symbols is very important! A smaller symbol before a larger one means subtract, but after means add.

IV (4)
VI (6)

Let's Make Numbers: 9 and 11

  • How do we write 9? Not VIIII!
  • It's IX (10 - 1 = 9).
  • How do we write 11? It's XI (10 + 1 = 11).

🎙️ Just like with 4 and 6, the numbers 9 and 11 follow the same rules. For 9, we subtract 1 from 10, so it's 'IX'. For 11, we add 1 to 10, so it's 'XI'. Keep practicing, and it will become easy!

IX (9)
XI (11)

More Symbols: X, XX, XXX

  • Remember X is 10.
  • If we repeat X, we add: XX = 10 + 10 = 20.
  • XXX = 10 + 10 + 10 = 30.

🎙️ The repetition rule works for 'X' too! If you see two 'X's, it's 20. If you see three 'X's, it's 30. This helps us write bigger numbers using just a few symbols.

1X (10)
2X + X = XX (20)
3X + X + X = XXX (30)

Time to Remember!

  • Roman Numerals use letters like I, V, X.
