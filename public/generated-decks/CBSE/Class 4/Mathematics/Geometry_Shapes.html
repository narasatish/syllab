What are Shapes?

  • Shapes are the outlines of things we see.
  • They help us describe how objects look.

🎙️ Look around you! Everything has a shape. From your book to your water bottle, shapes help us understand what we are looking at.

Shapes are all around us.

Flat Shapes (2D)

  • Flat shapes are thin, like a drawing on paper.
  • They only have length and width.

🎙️ Imagine drawing a shape on a piece of paper. That's a flat shape! They don't take up much space and are easy to draw.

📝
Paper with a drawing

Meet the Square!

  • A square has 4 straight sides.
  • All its sides are of the same length.
  • It has 4 corners.

🎙️ Can you see the sides of this box? A square is special because all its four sides are exactly the same size. Think of a carrom board!

📦
A gift box
5 / 20

Hello, Rectangle!

  • A rectangle has 4 straight sides.
  • Its opposite sides are equal in length.
  • It also has 4 corners.

🎙️ Look at a door or a blackboard. A rectangle also has four sides, but only the sides opposite to each other are the same length.

🚪
A door

The Three-Sided Triangle!

  • A triangle has 3 straight sides.
  • It has 3 corners.

🎙️ Triangles are fun! They have only three sides and three corners. Can you see a mountain peak or a slice of pizza? They are often triangles!

⛰️
A mountain peak

Round and Round, a Circle!

  • A circle has no straight sides.
  • It has no corners.
  • It is a perfectly round shape!

🎙️ Circles are smooth and round. They love to roll! Think of a yummy orange or a car wheel. They are perfect circles.

🟠
An orange fruit

Solid Shapes (3D)

  • Solid shapes are fat, not flat!
  • They take up space and you can hold them.
  • They have length, width, and height.

🎙️ Now, let's talk about shapes you can actually hold in your hand, like a brick! These are solid shapes, and they have thickness.

🧱
A brick

Meet the Cube!

  • A cube is a solid shape.
  • All its faces are squares.
  • Think of a dice!

🎙️ A cube is like a fat square! All its sides, or faces, are squares. When you play Ludo, the dice you roll is a cube!

🎲
A dice

Hello, Cuboid!

  • A cuboid is also a solid shape.
  • Its faces are rectangles.
  • Think of a gift box!

🎙️ A cuboid is like a fat rectangle. Its faces are rectangles. A shoebox or a big gift box are good examples of cuboids.

🎁
A gift box
12 / 20

The Rolling Cylinder!

  • A cylinder has two flat circular faces.
  • It has one curved side.
  • It can roll!

🎙️ Cylinders are fun because they can roll! Look at a cold drink can or a battery cell. They have circles on top and bottom and a round body.

🥫
A tin can
13 / 20

Pointy Cone!

  • A cone has one flat circular face.
  • It has a pointy top.
  • Like an ice cream cone!

🎙️ Who loves ice cream? The yummy cone part is a perfect example of a cone shape! It has a round base and a sharp point at the top.

🍦
An ice cream cone
14 / 20

Super Round Sphere!

  • A sphere is perfectly round.
  • It has no flat faces or corners.
  • It can roll in any direction!

🎙️ A sphere is like a perfectly round ball! It has no flat parts at all. A football or a globe are great examples of spheres.

⚽
A football
