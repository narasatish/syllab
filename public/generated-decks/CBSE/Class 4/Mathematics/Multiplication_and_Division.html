Let's Solve: 4 x 3

  • This means 4 groups of 3.
  • Or, 3 + 3 + 3 + 3.
  • The answer is 12.

🎙️ Let's solve this together. 4 x 3 means we add the number 3, four times. So, 3 plus 3 is 6, plus 3 is 9, and plus 3 is 12. The product of 4 and 3 is 12.

Division as Repeated Subtraction

  • Division is like taking away the same number again and again.
  • We count how many times we subtract.

🎙️ Just as multiplication is repeated addition, division is repeated subtraction! To find out how many times 2 goes into 10, we keep subtracting 2 until we reach zero. Count how many times you subtracted!

110 - 2 = 8
28 - 2 = 6
36 - 2 = 4
44 - 2 = 2
52 - 2 = 0

Multiplication and Division: Best Friends!

  • They are opposite operations.
  • If 3 x 4 = 12, then 12 ÷ 4 = 3 and 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
  • Knowing one helps with the other!

🎙️ Multiplication and Division are like two sides of the same coin! They are inverse operations. If you know that 3 times 4 is 12, then you also know that 12 divided by 4 is 3, and 12 divided by 3 is 4. They help each other!
