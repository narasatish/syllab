How to Test for Inc/Dec

  • Find f'(x).
  • Set f'(x) > 0 for Increasing.
  • Set f'(x) < 0 for Decreasing.
  • Identify intervals.

🎙️ The sign of the first derivative, f'(x), is key. If f'(x) is positive, the function is increasing; if negative, it's decreasing. We'll follow these steps to find the intervals.

2Set f'(x) > 0 for Increasing
3Set f'(x) < 0 for Decreasing
4Identify Intervals

Tangents and Normals to a Curve

  • Derivative gives tangent's slope.
  • Tangent touches curve at one point.
  • Normal is perpendicular to tangent.

🎙️ Derivatives give us the slope of the tangent line to a curve at any given point. The normal line is perpendicular to the tangent at that same point.

📐
Lines on Curves
10 / 25

Equation of the Tangent

  • Use point-slope form: y - y₁ = m(x - x₁).
  • m = dy/dx at (x₁, y₁).
  • Substitute point and slope.

🎙️ Recall that the equation of a line is y - y1 = m(x - x1). Here, 'm' is the slope of the tangent, which is dy/dx evaluated at the point (x1, y1).

11 / 25

Equation of the Normal

  • Slope of normal = -1/m_tangent.
  • Use point-slope form.
  • Be careful with zero/undefined slopes.

🎙️ Since the normal is perpendicular to the tangent, its slope will be the negative reciprocal of the tangent's slope. If the tangent's slope is m, the normal's slope is -1/m.

The Approximation Formula

  • f(x + Δx) ≈ f(x) + f'(x)Δx.
  • Δx is a small change.
  • f'(x)Δx is approximate change in f(x).

🎙️ We use the formula f(x + Δx) ≈ f(x) + f'(x)Δx. Here, Δx is a small change in x, and f'(x)Δx represents the approximate change in f(x).

First Derivative Test for Extrema

  • Find critical points (f'(x)=0 or undefined).
  • Check sign of f'(x) around points.
  • + to -: Local Maxima.
  • - to +: Local Minima.

🎙️ This test involves checking the sign of the derivative around critical points. A change from positive to negative indicates a local maximum, and vice-versa for a local minimum.

1Find f'(x)
2Find critical points
3Check sign of f'(x)
4Interpret sign changes
21 / 25

Second Derivative Test for Extrema

  • Find f'(x) and f''(x).
  • Find critical points (f'(x)=0).
  • Substitute into f''(x).
  • f''(x)<0: Maxima; f''(x)>0: Minima.

🎙️ The second derivative test is often quicker. If f''(x) is negative at a critical point, it's a maximum; if positive, it's a minimum. If zero, the test fails.

1Find f'(x) & f''(x)
2Find critical points
3Substitute into f''(x)
4Interpret f''(x) sign
