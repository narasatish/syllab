Spermatogenesis: Sperm Formation

  • Process of sperm production in testes
  • Begins at puberty and continues throughout life
  • Involves mitosis and meiosis

🎙️ Spermatogenesis is a continuous process ensuring a constant supply of male gametes. Understanding its stages and location is key for your exams.

Hormonal Control of Male Reproduction

  • GnRH (Hypothalamus): Stimulates pituitary
  • LH (Pituitary): Stimulates Leydig cells to produce androgens
  • FSH (Pituitary): Stimulates Sertoli cells for spermatogenesis
  • Androgens (Testosterone): Regulate development and function

🎙️ The hypothalamus and pituitary gland play a central role in regulating male reproductive functions through these hormones. Understand the feedback mechanisms involved.

Oogenesis: Ovum Formation

  • Process of ovum (egg) production in ovaries
  • Initiated during fetal development
  • Discontinuous process, unlike spermatogenesis

🎙️ Oogenesis is a fascinating process that begins even before a female is born. Understanding its unique timing and stages is crucial for exams.

Stages of Oogenesis

  • Oogonia (fetal development, mitosis)
  • Primary Oocytes (arrested in Meiosis I)
  • Secondary Oocytes (arrested in Meiosis II)
  • Ovum (after fertilization)

🎙️ Remember the key difference: primary oocytes are formed in fetal life and remain arrested until puberty. Meiosis II only completes upon sperm entry.

Structure of an Ovum

Phases of the Menstrual Cycle

  • Menstrual Phase (Day 1-5): Uterine lining sheds
  • Follicular Phase (Day 5-13): Follicle matures, uterus rebuilds
  • Ovulatory Phase (Day 14): Release of ovum
  • Luteal Phase (Day 15-28): Corpus luteum forms, uterus prepares for implantation

🎙️ Each phase is characterized by specific hormonal changes and ovarian/uterine events. Memorize the approximate duration and key events of each phase.

Early Embryonic Development

  • Cleavage: Rapid mitotic divisions of zygote
  • Morula: Solid ball of 8-16 cells
  • Blastocyst: Hollow ball, implants in uterus
  • Implantation: Attachment to uterine wall

🎙️ After fertilization, the zygote undergoes rapid cell division while traveling towards the uterus. The formation of the blastocyst is key for successful implantation.

Pregnancy and Placental Formation

  • Implantation marks the beginning of pregnancy
  • Placenta: Structural and functional unit between mother and fetus
  • Produces hormones like hCG, hPL, estrogens, progesterone

🎙️ The placenta is a vital organ, facilitating nutrient and oxygen supply to the fetus and removing waste products. It also acts as an endocrine gland.

Gestation and Parturition

  • Gestation period: Approximately 9 months in humans
  • Fetal development: Organogenesis occurs in trimesters
  • Parturition: Childbirth process, expulsion of fetus

🎙️ Gestation involves complex developmental stages. Parturition is a neuroendocrine mechanism initiated by the fully developed fetus and placenta, involving oxytocin.
