Pitfalls to Sidestep

  • Confusing plot with theme.
  • Ignoring the significance of the setting.
  • Not connecting character traits to actions.

🎙️ Students often confuse the 'what happened' (plot) with the 'what it means' (theme). Also, don't overlook how the setting influences the mood, and always link a character's personality traits directly to their decisions and actions in the story.
