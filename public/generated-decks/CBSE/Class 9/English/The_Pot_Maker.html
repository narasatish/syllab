Literary Device: Imagery

🎙️ Imagery is like painting with words. Authors use vivid descriptions to make us feel as if we are right there in the story. It’s how they bring the setting, characters, and actions to life in our imagination, making the narrative more engaging.

Literary Device: Symbolism

  • An object, person, or idea representing something else
  • Adds deeper layers of meaning to the narrative
  • Encourages readers to think beyond the literal interpretation

🎙️ Symbolism is when something concrete stands for an abstract idea. For instance, a dove often symbolizes peace. In literature, symbols enrich the story, allowing authors to convey complex ideas without stating them directly. It invites us to look for hidden messages.
