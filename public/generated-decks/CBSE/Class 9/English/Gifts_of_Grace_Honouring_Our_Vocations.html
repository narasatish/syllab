What is a Vocation?

  • More than just a job or a career.
  • A deep sense of calling or purpose.
  • Often connected to our natural talents and passions.
  • It's about what we are meant to do in life.

🎙️ Think of a vocation not just as what you do for money, but what you feel drawn to do, what truly fulfills you. It's like an inner compass guiding you towards your unique path.

Gifts of Grace: Our Unique Talents

  • Natural abilities and strengths we possess.
  • These are unique to each individual.
  • They can be artistic, scientific, compassionate, or practical.
  • Consider them as blessings given to us.

🎙️ The 'Gifts of Grace' are those special talents that come naturally to us. One person might be a gifted storyteller, another a natural leader, and a third a brilliant problem-solver. These are our unique contributions.

Vocation vs. Profession

  • Profession: A paid occupation, often requiring training.
  • Vocation: A calling, a deep passion or purpose.
  • A profession can be *part* of your vocation, but not always.
  • Your vocation might not always pay you directly, but it fulfills you.

🎙️ While a profession is usually what you do for a living, a vocation is what you feel compelled to do. Sometimes they overlap perfectly, like a doctor who feels called to heal. Other times, your vocation might be a hobby or volunteer work that brings you immense joy.
