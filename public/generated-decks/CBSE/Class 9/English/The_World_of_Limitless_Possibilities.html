The 'Fixed' Mindset: Believing Abilities are Set

  • Believes intelligence and talents are permanent.
  • Avoids challenges to prevent looking 'bad'.
  • Gives up easily when faced with difficulty.

🎙️ Someone with a fixed mindset believes their abilities are 'set in stone.' They might think, 'I'm just not good at this,' and avoid new challenges. This mindset often sees effort as a sign of weakness, rather than a path to improvement.

  • A powerful tool for turning dreams into reality.

🎙️ Setting SMART goals is a proven method for effective goal setting. Each letter stands for a crucial element that ensures your goals are well-defined and have a clear path to completion. Let's break down what each part means.

2Measurable
3Achievable

16 / 30

SMART Goal in Action: Improving English Skills

  • Old Goal: 'I want to be good at English.' (Vague)
  • SMART Goal: 'I will read one English novel per month and write a 100-word summary for each, aiming for an A grade in my next English exam by December.'
  • This goal is clear, trackable, and has a deadline.

🎙️ See the difference? The old goal is just a wish. The SMART goal provides a clear roadmap. It specifies what to do (read novels, write summaries), how to measure success (one novel, 100-word summary, A grade), and when to achieve it (by December).
