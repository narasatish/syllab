Make Your Goals SMART

  • Specific: Clearly defined.
  • Measurable: Track your progress.
  • Achievable: Realistic and attainable.
  • Relevant: Aligned with your values.
  • Time-bound: With a deadline.

🎙️ The SMART framework is an excellent tool for effective goal setting. For example, instead of 'I want to be a better student', a SMART goal would be 'I will improve my English grammar score by 10% in the next month by practicing daily for 30 minutes.' This makes the goal actionable.
