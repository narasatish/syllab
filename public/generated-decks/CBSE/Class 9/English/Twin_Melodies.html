Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Confusing plot with theme: Theme is the message, plot is what happens.
  • Missing subtle character traits: Look at actions AND words.
  • Ignoring symbolism: Don't take everything literally, look for deeper meaning.

🎙️ Students often make a few common errors. Remember, the theme isn't just what happens in the story, but the big idea behind it. Also, pay close attention to *how* characters behave, not just what they say. And always keep an eye out for symbols!
