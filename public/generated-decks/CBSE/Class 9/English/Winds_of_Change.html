Watch Out! Common Pitfalls

  • Confusing plot points with the main theme.
  • Not providing textual evidence for your answers.
  • Missing the subtle nuances of character development.

🎙️ Many students make common mistakes when analysing literature. Remember, the plot is 'what happens', while the theme is 'what it means'. Always support your interpretations with direct examples from the text, and look closely at how characters change, not just what they do.
