What is Change?

  • The act of becoming different.
  • Moving from one state to another.
  • A constant process in life.

🎙️ At its core, change simply means things are not staying the same. It's about a shift, a movement, or an evolution from one condition to another. Think of it like a river that constantly flows and reshapes its banks.

  • Sudden Change: Quick and unexpected.
  • Both have distinct impacts.

🎙️ Change doesn't always happen in the same way. Sometimes it's a slow, unfolding process, like a seed growing into a tree. Other times, it strikes suddenly, like a lightning bolt. Both types reshape our world.
