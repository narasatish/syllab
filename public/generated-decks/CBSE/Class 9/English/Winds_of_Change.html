The Metaphor of 'Winds of Change'

  • Not literal wind, but powerful forces
  • Represents significant shifts or developments
  • Often refers to societal, political, or cultural transformations

🎙️ When we talk about 'winds of change,' we're not talking about a gentle breeze or a storm. We're using a metaphor to describe strong, often invisible, forces that bring about major shifts in our world.

Understanding Metaphor in Literature

  • A figure of speech where a word or phrase is applied to an object or action to which it is not literally applicable
  • Creates a vivid image or idea
  • Example: 'The world is a stage.'

🎙️ The phrase 'winds of change' is a perfect example of a metaphor. It helps us understand a complex idea – societal transformation – by comparing it to something familiar, like the powerful force of wind.

Key Vocabulary for 'Change'

  • Evolve: develop gradually
  • Transform: make a thorough change
  • Adapt: adjust to new conditions
  • Revolution: a dramatic and wide-reaching change

🎙️ To truly understand and discuss 'winds of change,' we need a strong vocabulary. These words will help you articulate the different aspects and degrees of transformation you encounter.
