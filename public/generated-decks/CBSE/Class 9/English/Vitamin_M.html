Vocabulary Technique: Flashcard Fun for New Words

  • Write the word on one side, meaning and example sentence on the other.
  • Review your flashcards regularly, perhaps daily.
  • Use pictures or synonyms to aid memory.

🎙️ Flashcards are a classic and effective tool for building vocabulary. By actively recalling the meaning of words, you strengthen your memory and make new words truly stick. Make it a fun habit!

Vocabulary Technique: Using Context Clues

  • Guess the meaning of an unknown word from surrounding words and sentences.
  • Look for synonyms, antonyms, or explanations within the text.
  • Practice this skill while reading any English material.

🎙️ Be a word detective! Often, a text provides hints about a new word's meaning without you needing a dictionary. Learning to use context clues improves your reading fluency and helps you build vocabulary naturally.

Component 4: Meaning & Comprehension - Grasping the Message

  • Understanding what you read or hear beyond just the words.
  • Grasping the author's intent, main ideas, and supporting details.
  • Essential for answering questions and critical thinking.

🎙️ Comprehension is the ability to truly understand the message, whether it's from a book, a conversation, or a movie. It's not just about knowing individual words, but about connecting them to form a complete and coherent idea.

Comprehension Technique: Active Reading Strategies

  • Preview the text: look at headings, pictures, and summaries.
  • Ask questions as you read: 'What is the main point here?'
  • Summarize paragraphs in your own words after reading them.
  • Identify main ideas and supporting details in each section.

🎙️ Active reading transforms you from a passive receiver into an engaged participant. By using these strategies, you're not just scanning words; you're actively constructing meaning, which leads to much better understanding and retention.

Grammar: Practice Makes Perfect
