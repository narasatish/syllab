What is Self-Belief?

  • Trusting your own abilities and judgment.
  • Having confidence in your worth and potential.
  • It's an inner conviction that you can succeed.

🎙️ Self-belief is essentially the quiet confidence you have in yourself. It's knowing that you have what it takes to face challenges and achieve your goals, even when things get tough. It’s like an inner spark that keeps you going.

Self-Doubt vs. Self-Belief

  • Self-Doubt: Doubting your capabilities, fear of failure.
  • Self-Belief: Trusting your strengths, embracing challenges.
  • One pulls you down, the other lifts you up.

🎙️ These two are like opposite forces. Self-doubt can paralyse you, making you hesitant to even try. Self-belief, on the other hand, empowers you to act and learn. We want to nurture self-belief and diminish self-doubt.

🎙️ Our minds can sometimes be our own worst critics. When a negative thought pops up, don't just accept it. Question it, challenge its validity, and consciously replace it with a more positive or neutral thought. This takes practice but is very effective.
