  • Past negative experiences or setbacks.

🎙️ Self-doubt often creeps in when we feel uncertain, insecure, or when we unfairly compare ourselves to others' perceived perfection.

Setting SMART Goals

  • Specific: What exactly do you want to achieve?
  • Measurable: How will you track your progress?
  • Achievable: Is it realistic and within your reach?
  • Relevant: Does it matter to you and align with your values?
  • Time-bound: By when do you want to achieve it?

🎙️ Setting SMART goals makes them less daunting and more actionable. Each small success in achieving a SMART goal boosts your overall self-belief.

🎯
Targeted Goals
