Literary Device: Simile

  • Comparison using 'like' or 'as'
  • Creates vivid imagery
  • Example: 'Her gaze was like the sky'

🎙️ A simile is a figure of speech that directly compares two different things using 'like' or 'as'. In the poem, Tagore uses simile beautifully to describe the mother's gaze, making it more relatable and imaginative.

🔗
Direct Comparison
18 / 25

Literary Device: Metaphor

  • Direct comparison without 'like' or 'as'
  • States one thing IS another
  • Example: 'The flowers are her whispers' (implied)

🎙️ A metaphor, unlike a simile, makes a direct statement that one thing is another, implying a deeper connection. While not explicit with 'is', the poem uses metaphorical language when associating flowers directly with her presence, like 'her scent lingers in the shiuli flowers'.

✨
