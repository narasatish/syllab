Making Your Goals SMART!

  • Specific: What exactly do you want to achieve?
  • Measurable: How will you know when you've reached it?
  • Achievable: Is it realistic given your resources?
  • Relevant: Does it align with your overall dream?
  • Time-bound: When do you want to achieve it by?

🎙️ The SMART framework is an excellent tool for setting effective goals. Instead of saying 'I want to be a better student,' a SMART goal would be 'I will improve my English grade to 85% by the end of this semester by studying 1 hour daily.' See the difference?
