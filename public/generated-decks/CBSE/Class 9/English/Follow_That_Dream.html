Turning Dreams into SMART Goals

  • Specific: What exactly?
  • Measurable: How to track?
  • Achievable: Is it possible?
  • Relevant: Does it matter?
  • Time-bound: When will it happen?

🎙️ A dream can feel abstract, but a goal is concrete. We use the SMART framework to make our goals effective: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. This gives your dream a clear path.

🎯
Targeting Success
11 / 25

Creating Your Action Plan

  • Step 1: Break down the goal
  • Step 2: List required tasks
  • Step 3: Set deadlines for tasks
  • Step 4: Monitor progress regularly

🎙️ Once you have a SMART goal, create an action plan. Break your big goal into smaller, manageable tasks. Assign deadlines to each task and regularly check your progress to stay on track.

Example: Dream of Becoming an Author

  • Goal: Write a 50,000-word novel in 1 year.
  • Tasks: Outline chapters, write 1000 words/week, edit.
  • Action: Dedicate 2 hours daily to writing.

🎙️ Let's say your dream is to be an author. Your SMART goal could be to write a novel within a year. Your action plan would include daily writing, outlining, and editing, making the dream tangible.
