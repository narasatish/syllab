🎙️ Hello students! Today, we embark on a beautiful journey through 'How I Taught My Grandmother to Read'. This chapter is not just a story; it's a lesson in life, perseverance, and the timeless bond between a grandmother and her granddaughter. Get ready to be inspired!

Chapter Introduction: What's the Story About?

  • A young girl teaches her illiterate grandmother to read.
  • Set in a village, reflecting traditional Indian life.
  • Highlights the grandmother's desire for independence.

🎙️ This story is a poignant narrative about a grandmother who missed out on formal education and her granddaughter who helps her achieve her dream. It beautifully illustrates how age is no bar to learning and how a simple act of teaching can bridge generations.
