The Magic of Compounding!

  • Compounding means earning returns not just on your original investment.
  • But also on the returns you've already earned!
  • It's like a snowball rolling downhill, getting bigger and bigger!

🎙️ This is truly magical! Compounding means your earnings start earning money too. It's like a snowball rolling down a hill – it picks up more snow and gets bigger and bigger, faster and faster!

✨

Compounding: A Simple Illustration

  • Invest ₹1000, earn 10% interest = ₹100.
  • Next year, earn 10% on ₹1100 (original + interest) = ₹110.
  • Your money grows faster each year!

🎙️ Let's illustrate: If you earn 10% on ₹1000, you get ₹100. The next year, you earn 10% on ₹1100, which is ₹110. Your money grows not just on the original amount, but also on the interest earned, accelerating its growth.

Different Goals, Different Strategies

  • Short-term (1-3 years): Saving is better (e.g., new phone, vacation).
  • Long-term (5+ years): Investing is usually better for significant growth (e.g., retirement, house down payment).

🎙️ Your goals dictate your strategy. For short-term needs, saving is usually best. But for long-term aspirations like buying a home or for your retirement, investing is generally more effective due to its growth potential.
