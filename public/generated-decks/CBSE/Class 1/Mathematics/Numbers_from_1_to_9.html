Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Miscounting objects: Point to each one as you count.
  • Confusing numbers: Practice writing them daily.
  • Skipping numbers: Always count in order (1, 2, 3...).

🎙️ Sometimes we get confused or count too fast. Remember to point to each object as you count it. Also, practice writing numbers daily so you don't mix them up!

Exam Tips for Numbers!

  • Write numbers neatly for clear answers.
  • Read questions carefully: 'how many' or 'which number'?
  • Practice counting objects around your home.

🎙️ When you have an exam, make sure your numbers are written clearly so the teacher can easily understand. Always read the question twice and practice counting things at home every day!
