What is Addition?

  • Addition means to put things together.
  • We find out how many there are in total.

🎙️ Imagine you have some toys, and then your friend gives you some more. When you count all of them together, you are doing addition! It helps us know the total number of things.

Addition is "Putting Things Together"

  • We take one group of things.
  • We take another group of things.
  • We join them!

🎙️ Think of it like mixing two different colours of paint. When you put them together, you get a new colour! Addition is just like that, but with numbers and objects.

Finding the "Total"

  • When we add, we find the 'total' number.
  • Total means how many there are in all.

🎙️ If you have 3 cookies and your mom gives you 3 more, how many do you have in total? Yes, 6! The word 'total' helps us ask for the final number after adding.

The "Sum" is the Answer

  • The answer we get after adding is called the 'sum'.
  • It is the total number.

🎙️ When you add 2 apples and 3 apples, you get 5 apples. That number, 5, is called the 'sum'. It's just a special math word for the answer to an addition problem.

What Happens When We Add Zero?

  • Zero means "nothing".
  • If you add zero, the number stays the same.

🎙️ Imagine you have 3 yummy candies. If no one gives you any more candies (which means zero candies), how many do you still have? Yes, 3! Adding zero doesn't change the number.

🍬🍬🍬
3 candies + 0 candies = 3 candies
13 / 20

What Happens When We Add One?

  • Adding one means getting the "next" number.
  • It's like counting forward one step.

🎙️ If you have 3 toy cars, and your friend gives you just one more, how many do you have now? You count one step forward from 3, which is 4! Adding one always gives you the very next number.

🚗🚗🚗🚗
3 cars + 1 car = 4 cars
