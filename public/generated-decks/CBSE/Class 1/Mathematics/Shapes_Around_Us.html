What is a Rectangle?

  • It has four straight sides.
  • Opposite sides are equal in length.
  • It has four corners.

🎙️ A rectangle also has four straight sides and four corners. But here's the difference: only the sides opposite to each other are the same length. Two sides are long, and two sides are short.

How Are Shapes Different?

  • Circles are round, no sides or corners.
  • Squares have 4 equal sides.
  • Rectangles have 4 sides, opposite sides are equal.
  • Triangles have 3 sides and 3 corners.

🎙️ Now let's compare our shapes. Remember, circles are unique because they are round. Squares and rectangles both have four sides, but their side lengths are different. Triangles are special with only three sides.

Watch Out for Common Mistakes!

  • Confusing squares and rectangles.
  • Calling a circle a 'round square'.
  • Forgetting the number of sides.

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused between squares and rectangles. Remember, a square has ALL sides equal, but a rectangle only has opposite sides equal. And a circle is never a 'square'!
