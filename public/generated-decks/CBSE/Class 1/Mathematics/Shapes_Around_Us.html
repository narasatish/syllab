Making Shapes with Sticks!

  • Use three sticks to make a triangle.
  • Use four equal sticks for a square.
  • Use two long and two short sticks for a rectangle.

🎙️ We can also make shapes using small sticks or even matchsticks! It's a fun way to understand how many sides each shape has. Try it at home!

13 sticks -> Triangle
24 equal sticks -> Square
34 sticks (2 long, 2 short) -> Rectangle
