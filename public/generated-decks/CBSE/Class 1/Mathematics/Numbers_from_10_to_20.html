Ten: A Special Group!

How Do We Write Ten?

  • Ten is written as 10.
  • It has 1 'Ten' and 0 'Ones'.

🎙️ Look at the number 10. The '1' on the left means we have one group of ten. The '0' on the right means we have zero single items, or 'ones'. So, 10 is one ten and no ones.

Meet Number Eleven!

  • After ten comes eleven.
  • It's a group of ten and one more.
  • 10 + 1 = 11.

🎙️ Now that we have our special group of ten, what happens if we add just one more apple? We get eleven apples! So, eleven is like having a full basket of ten apples and one extra apple.

Eleven: One Ten and One One

  • Eleven is written as 11.
  • It has 1 'Ten' and 1 'One'.

🎙️ When we write 11, the first '1' tells us we have one group of ten. The second '1' tells us we have one single item, or 'one'. So, 11 is one ten and one one.
