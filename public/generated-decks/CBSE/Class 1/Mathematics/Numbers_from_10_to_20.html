Meet Our Friend: Ones

  • Numbers 1 to 9 are called 'Ones'.
  • They stand alone, like single dots.
  • One, two, three...up to nine.

🎙️ Each single item, like one apple or one finger, is called a 'One'. When we count from one to nine, we are counting 'Ones'. They are like small, individual pieces.

Writing Number Ten

  • Number Ten has 1 Ten and 0 Ones.
  • We write it as '10'.
  • The '1' shows one Ten, the '0' shows zero Ones.

🎙️ Look how we write 'Ten'. The number '1' in '10' stands for one bundle of ten. The number '0' means there are no single items left over. So, 10 means one Ten and zero Ones.
