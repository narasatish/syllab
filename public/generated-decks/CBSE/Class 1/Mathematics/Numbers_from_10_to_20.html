Oops! Common Mistakes

  • Confusing 'teen' numbers with 'ty' numbers (e.g., 13 vs 30).
  • Writing numbers backwards (e.g., 12 as 21).
  • Forgetting the '1' in the tens place for numbers 10-19.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes. It's okay! Just remember to be careful. For example, thirteen and thirty sound similar but are very different. Always check your numbers and their names carefully.
