What is Measurement?

  • It means finding out 'how much' or 'how big' something is.
  • We use it to compare things.

🎙️ Measurement helps us know if something is long or short, heavy or light, or if a bottle holds a lot or a little water. It's like asking a question about an object and finding its answer!

Why Do We Measure Things?

  • To know if clothes will fit us.
  • To share things fairly with friends.

🎙️ Imagine your mom buying you a new dress. How does she know if it will fit you? She measures! Or, if you want to share a rope equally with your friend, you need to measure it. Measurement helps us in many daily tasks.

Measuring with Our Hands!

  • We can use our hand span to measure.
  • It's like counting how many 'hands' long something is.

🎙️ Before we had rulers, people used their body parts to measure! One way is using your 'hand span'. That's the distance from your thumb to your little finger when spread out. Try measuring your desk with your hand span!
