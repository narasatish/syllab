What is Measurement?

  • It means finding out how big or small something is.
  • We compare things like length, height, and weight.

🎙️ Measurement helps us understand things better. Imagine you want to know if your pencil is longer than your friend's, or if your bag is heavier than your lunchbox. That's what measurement helps us do!

Measuring with Handspans

  • We can use our hands to measure!
  • One handspan is from your thumb to your pinky finger.

🎙️ Before we had rulers, people used their own bodies to measure! A 'handspan' is the distance from your thumb to your little finger when your hand is stretched out.

Let's Measure a Table!

  • Count how many handspans long it is.

🎙️ You can try this at home! Place your hand on a table, then move it forward, placing your other hand right where the first one ended. Keep counting how many handspans it takes to cover the whole table.
