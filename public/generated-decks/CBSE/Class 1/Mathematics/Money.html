Calculating Change

  • You gave ₹10, item costs ₹8.
  • Shopkeeper gives back ₹2 (10 - 8 = 2).

🎙️ You gave 10 Rupees, and the ice cream costs 8 Rupees. The shopkeeper will give you back 2 Rupees as change. Always count your change!
