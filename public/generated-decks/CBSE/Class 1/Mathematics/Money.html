What is Money?

  • Money is what we use to buy things.
  • It can be coins or paper notes.
  • We give money to shopkeepers.

🎙️ Money is like a special ticket. When you want to buy a chocolate or a new crayon, you give money to the shopkeeper, and they give you the item. It's how we exchange things.

The rupee (₹) is our money.

  • Money helps us get things we want.

🎙️ Imagine you want a yummy candy! You can't just take it. You need to give money for it. Money helps us get all the things we need and love, like food, clothes, and even go to school.
