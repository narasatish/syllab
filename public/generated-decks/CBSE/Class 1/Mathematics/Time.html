How a Clock Works: Big Hand, Small Hand

  • The small hand tells us the 'hour'.
  • The big hand points to 12 for 'o'clock'.

🎙️ Look closely! The shorter hand is the 'hour' hand. It tells us which hour it is. The longer hand is the 'minute' hand. When the big hand points to 12, and the small hand points to a number, we say 'that number o'clock'.
