The Hour Hand

  • The short hand is the hour hand.
  • It tells us the hour.
  • When the long hand is at 12, it's 'o'clock'.

🎙️ The short hand points to the hour. When the long hand is at the number 12, we say 'o'clock'. So if the short hand is on 3, it's 3 o'clock!
