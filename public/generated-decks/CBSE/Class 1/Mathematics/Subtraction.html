What is Subtraction?

  • It means to 'take away' a part from a whole.
  • Like when balloons fly away!
  • We find out 'how many are left'.

🎙️ Subtraction is like when you have some balloons, and a few fly away. You are finding out how many are still with you. It's about finding what is left.

How Many are Left?

  • Start with 8 apples.
  • Take away 3 apples.
  • Now you have 5 apples left!

🎙️ When we take away 3 apples from 8 apples, we are left with 5 apples. This 'taking away' is called subtraction.

The Minus Sign

  • This is the sign for subtraction.
  • It means 'take away' or 'subtract'.
  • We say 'minus'.

🎙️ Just like we have a plus sign for adding, we have a special sign for subtraction. It's a small horizontal line, and we call it the 'minus' sign.

Let's Write It Down

  • 8 apples - 3 apples = 5 apples
  • We read it as '8 minus 3 equals 5'.

🎙️ So, our apple problem can be written like this: 8 minus 3 equals 5. This is how we write a subtraction sum.

Counting Backwards

  • To subtract, we can count backwards.
  • Let's try 7 - 3.

🎙️ One easy way to subtract is to count backwards. Imagine you are on a number line. If you have 7 and want to take away 3, you jump back 3 steps.

Zero Left!

  • When you subtract a number from itself, the answer is always zero.
  • 4 - 4 = 0.

🎙️ Yes, you have zero cookies left! When you take away all the things you started with, you are left with nothing, which we call 'zero'.

Same Number!
