What is Subtraction?

  • It means 'taking away' things.
  • We find out 'how many are left'.

🎙️ Subtraction is a big word, but it just means we take some things away from a group. Then, we count what is still there.

Counting Backwards to Subtract

  • To subtract, we can count backwards.
  • If we have 5 and take away 2, we count: 5... 4, 3! So, 5 - 2 = 3.

🎙️ Counting backwards is a very helpful trick for subtraction. It's like going down steps, one by one, from a bigger number.

Subtracting with Lines

  • To do 6 - 3: First, draw 6 lines.
  • Then, cross out 3 of those lines.
  • Count the lines that are still there.

🎙️ If you don't want to use your fingers, drawing lines is another super easy way to subtract. Just draw, cross out, and count!
