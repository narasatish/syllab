What is Subtraction?

  • Subtraction means to 'take away' some things.
  • It tells us how many are 'left' or the 'difference'.

🎙️ Imagine you have some toys, and you give a few to your friend. How many toys do you have now? That's what subtraction helps us find out! It's all about taking things away from a group.

The Minus Sign: Our Subtraction Friend

  • We use a special sign for subtraction.
  • It looks like a small sleeping line: '-'.

🎙️ This little line is called the 'minus sign'. Whenever you see this sign between two numbers, it means you need to take away the second number from the first number. It's our special symbol for subtraction.

Subtraction on a Number Line

  • We can also subtract using a number line.
  • To subtract, we jump 'backwards' on the number line.

🎙️ A number line is another super helpful tool for maths. When we add, we jump forward. But for subtraction, we do the opposite! We jump backwards, moving to smaller numbers.

Example: 5 - 2 on a Number Line

  • Start at number 5.
  • Jump back 2 steps. Where do you land?
  • You land on 3! So, 5 - 2 = 3.

🎙️ Let's try 5 minus 2. Find the number 5 on your number line. Now, take two jumps backwards, one jump for each number you are taking away. You will see that you land right on the number 3!
