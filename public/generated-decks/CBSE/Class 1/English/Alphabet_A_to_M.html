Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Mixing up 'b' and 'd'.
  • Writing 'p' instead of 'q'.
  • Not knowing capital vs. small letters.

🎙️ Sometimes, letters can look a bit similar, like 'b' and 'd'. Always remember which way they face! Also, practice writing both big and small letters so you don't get confused.

⚠️
Be Careful!

Exam Tips for Letters A-M

  • Practice tracing and writing daily.
  • Match pictures to starting letters.
  • Identify both capital and small forms.

🎙️ To do well in your exams, make sure you trace and write your letters every day. Try to match objects around you with their starting letters. Remember, knowing both big and small letters is key!
