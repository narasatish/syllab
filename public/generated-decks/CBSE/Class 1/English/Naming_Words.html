What Are Naming Words Called?

  • Naming words have a special English name.
  • They are also called Nouns!

🎙️ In English, we have a special, shorter name for all these naming words. We call them 'Nouns'. So, when you hear 'Noun', just remember it means a naming word for a person, place, animal, or thing.

Common Mistakes

  • Don't confuse naming words with action words (like run, jump).
  • Remember, a naming word is always a name of something.

🎙️ Sometimes, children get confused between naming words and doing words, like 'run' or 'eat'. Remember, a naming word is always a name. 'Run' is an action, not a name. Keep that in mind, and you'll avoid this mistake!

Exam Tips!

  • In exams, you might be asked to 'underline the naming words'.
  • Read carefully and look for names of people, places, animals, or things.

🎙️ When you see questions about naming words in your exams, always remember our lesson. Read the sentence very carefully and ask yourself: 'Is this word a name of a person, place, animal, or thing?' Then, underline or circle it. This will help you score full marks!
