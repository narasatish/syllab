Quick Check: Is 'run' a Naming Word?

  • No! 'Run' is an action.
  • It is not a name for a person, animal, place, or thing.

🎙️ 'Run' tells us what someone is doing, not what they are called. So, 'run' is not a naming word. We will learn about action words later!
