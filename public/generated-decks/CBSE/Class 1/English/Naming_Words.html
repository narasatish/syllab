What are Naming Words?

  • Every person, place, animal, or thing has a name.
  • These names are called "Naming Words."
  • They help us know what we are talking about.

🎙️ Look around you! You have a name, your school has a name, your pet has a name, and even your pencil has a name. All these names are what we call Naming Words.
