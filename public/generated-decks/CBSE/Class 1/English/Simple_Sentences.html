  • It's a group of words.
  • It tells us a full idea.

Rule Number One!

  • Every sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Like the first letter of your name!

🎙️ This is a very important rule! Just like your name starts with a capital letter, every sentence must start with a big, capital letter.

Rule Number Two!

  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • It tells us the sentence is finished.

🎙️ After you finish telling your idea, you put a tiny dot at the end. This dot is called a full stop. It tells everyone that your sentence is complete.

What Makes a Sentence?

  • It needs words that make sense.
  • It needs a capital letter at the start.
  • It needs a full stop at the end.

🎙️ So, remember these three important things! A sentence must have words that make a complete idea, start with a big letter, and end with a full stop.
