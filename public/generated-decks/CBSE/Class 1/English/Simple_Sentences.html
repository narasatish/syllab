Who or What is Doing It?

  • Every sentence talks about someone or something.
  • This 'someone' or 'something' is the Subject.
  • It tells us 'who' or 'what' the sentence is about.

🎙️ In every story, there's a main character, right? In a sentence, there's also a main character! This part tells us who or what the sentence is talking about. We call it the Subject.

👤
The main character

Finding the 'Who' or 'What'!

  • **The boy** is playing.
  • **My mother** cooks food.
  • **The sun** shines brightly.

🎙️ Let's look at some examples. In 'The boy is playing', who is playing? The boy! So, 'The boy' is our Subject. In 'My mother cooks food', who cooks? My mother! Can you find the 'what' in the last sentence?

🧒
The boy
10 / 24

What Are They Doing?

  • After 'who' or 'what', we need to know 'what' they do.
  • This part tells us about the action.
  • It's called the Predicate.

🎙️ So we know who or what the sentence is about. Now, what are they doing? Are they running, eating, sleeping? This part of the sentence tells us the action or what is happening. We call this the Predicate.

🏃‍♀️
The action

Finding the 'Action'!

  • The boy **is playing**.
  • My mother **cooks food**.
  • The sun **shines brightly**.

🎙️ Let's look at our examples again. In 'The boy is playing', what is the boy doing? 'Is playing'! That's the Predicate. In 'My mother cooks food', what does she do? 'Cooks food'. This is the Predicate. See how easy it is to find the action?

  • Like giving the first word a fancy hat.

🎙️ Imagine you're going to a party. You wear your best clothes, right? The first word of every sentence also dresses up! It always starts with a big letter, a Capital Letter. It's like a rule for sentences.

A Little Dot to Say 'Done'!

  • When a sentence finishes, it puts a stop sign.
  • This is a Full Stop (.).
  • It tells us, 'My idea is complete!'

🎙️ After you finish telling your story, you say 'The End', right? A sentence also has a special way to say 'The End'. It's a tiny dot called a Full Stop. It tells us that the thought is complete.

🛑

Building a Perfect Sentence!

  • Let's take: 'boy play ball'
  • 1. Start with Capital: 'Boy play ball'
  • 2. Add Full Stop: 'Boy plays ball.'

🎙️ See how we put it all together? We start with a capital letter for the first word, and we end with a full stop. This makes our sentence look neat and easy to read. Remember these two important rules!

Watch Out for These Mistakes!

  • Forgetting a Capital Letter at the start.
  • Forgetting a Full Stop at the end.
  • Words are mixed up and don't make sense.

🎙️ Sometimes, we make small mistakes. The most common ones are forgetting the big hat (capital letter) for the first word or forgetting the 'done' dot (full stop). Also, remember to keep your words in the right order so they make sense!

Let's Recap: What We Learned!

  • A sentence is a group of words that makes complete sense.
  • It has a 'who/what' (Subject) and 'what they do' (Predicate).
