  • It tells us something complete.

Words Must Be in Order

  • Words like to be in their own places.
  • If words are mixed up, it makes no sense.

🎙️ Imagine you are building a tower with blocks. If you put the blocks in the wrong order, the tower might fall! Similarly, words in a sentence must be in the correct order for it to make sense.

A Sentence Makes Sense

  • A sentence helps us understand.
  • We know what it means.

🎙️ The most important thing about a sentence is that it should make sense to us. When you read a sentence, you should be able to understand the message it is trying to give.

Starts with a Big Letter

  • The first word always starts with a Capital Letter.
  • Like 'A' instead of 'a', or 'T' instead of 't'.

🎙️ Here's a special rule for sentences! Just like a king or queen wears a crown, the first letter of the first word in a sentence always wears a 'capital letter' crown. It's a big letter!

Ends with a Full Stop

  • A sentence ends with a full stop (.).
  • It tells us the sentence is finished.

🎙️ And at the very end of our sentence, we put a little dot, called a full stop. It's like a tiny traffic light saying 'Stop, the sentence is complete!'.

What We Learned Today!

  • A sentence is a group of words that makes sense.

  • It ends with a full stop (.).
  • Words must be in order.
