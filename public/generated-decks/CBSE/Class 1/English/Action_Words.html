What are Action Words?

  • Action words tell us what someone or something is *doing*.
  • They are also called 'doing words'.

🎙️ Imagine a movie! Every character is doing something. Those 'doing' things are what action words tell us. They show movement, thought, or feeling.

Adding '-ing' for NOW!

  • When an action is happening *right now*, we add '-ing' to the word.
  • Example: *run* becomes *running*.

🎙️ Imagine you are running. If someone asks, 'What are you doing?', you say 'I am running!' The '-ing' tells us it's happening right at this moment. It's like a magic suffix!

Count and add using real objects like apples 🍎

See the difference: Drink vs. Drinking

  • I *drink* water every day.
  • He is *drinking* milk now.

🎙️ The first sentence talks about a habit. The second sentence tells us what he is doing at this very moment. Can you hear the '-ing'?

Common Mistakes with Action Words

  • Forgetting '-ing' for current actions (e.g., 'He eat').
  • Confusing similar actions (e.g., *walk* and *run*).

🎙️ Sometimes, we forget to add '-ing' when we are talking about something happening right now. Remember, 'He eat' is not correct, it should be 'He is eating'. Also, make sure you pick the right action word for what is happening!

Exam Tips for Action Words

  • Look for words that show *doing*.
  • Fill in the blanks with correct action words.
  • Match pictures to action words.

🎙️ In your exams, you might be asked to find action words in sentences. Or you might have to fill in a missing action word. Always think: 'What is happening?' and choose the word that describes that action best!
