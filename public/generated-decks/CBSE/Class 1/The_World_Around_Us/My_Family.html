  • Confusing cousins with brothers/sisters.
  • Thinking only a big family is 'complete'.

🎙️ Sometimes, children make small mistakes when talking about family. Remember, grandparents and siblings are very much part of the family. Also, cousins are different from brothers and sisters. And both small and big families are wonderful and complete!
