What are Plants?

  • Plants are living things.
  • They grow big and strong.

🎙️ Just like us, plants are alive! They need food and water to grow. Some grow very tall, and some stay small.

Plants make their own food from sunlight.

  • We see them in parks and forests.

Big Plants: Trees!

  • Trees are very big and tall.
  • They have a thick, woody stem called a trunk.

🎙️ The biggest plants are called trees! They stand tall and strong, like giants. Their strong trunks help them stand firm.

  • Banyan trees have many branches.

🎙️ Think of a mango tree! It's so tall and gives us sweet fruits. The Banyan tree is also a huge tree with many roots hanging down.

Small Plants: Shrubs!

  • Shrubs are smaller than trees.
  • They have many woody branches close to the ground.

🎙️ Now, let's look at plants that are not as tall as trees, but still have strong stems. These are called shrubs. They look bushy!

A bushy shrub

Examples of Shrubs

  • Rose plants have beautiful flowers.
  • Tulsi plants are used for medicines.

🎙️ Do you know the rose plant? It's a shrub! And the Tulsi plant, which our grandmothers often use, is also a shrub.

Very Small Plants: Herbs!

  • Herbs are very small plants.
  • They have soft, green stems.

🎙️ Now, let's talk about the smallest plants. They are called herbs. Their stems are soft, not woody like trees or shrubs.

A tiny herb

Examples of Herbs

  • Mint leaves make our food fresh.
  • Coriander adds flavour to dishes.

🎙️ Think about the mint leaves in your chutney or the coriander sprinkled on your food. These are herbs!

Plants that Climb: Climbers!

  • Climbers have weak stems.
  • They need support to grow upwards.

🎙️ Some plants can't stand on their own because their stems are too weak. They need help, like a wall or another plant, to climb up!

🪜
A ladder for climbing
11 / 20

Examples of Climbers

  • Money Plant climbs on sticks or walls.
  • Pea plants climb to find sunlight.

🎙️ Have you seen a Money Plant at home? It wraps around a stick or grows up a wall. Pea plants also climb to reach for the sun.

Plants that Creep: Creepers!

  • Creepers also have weak stems.
  • They grow along the ground.

🎙️ Just like climbers, creepers have weak stems. But instead of climbing up, they spread and grow along the ground.

🛤️
A path on the ground

Examples of Creepers

  • Pumpkin plants grow big pumpkins on the ground.
  • Watermelon plants spread out wide.

🎙️ Think of a big pumpkin! It grows on a creeper plant that spreads on the ground. Watermelons also grow this way.

🍉🍉🍉
Watermelons growing on the ground

  • They give us grains like rice and wheat.

What else do Plants Give Us?

  • Plants give us fresh air to breathe.
  • They give us shade from the sun.

🎙️ Besides food, plants give us clean, fresh air, which is very important for us to breathe. They also offer cool shade on a hot sunny day.

How to Care for Plants?

  • Water them regularly.
  • Make sure they get sunlight.

🎙️ Plants are our friends, so we must take good care of them. Remember to water them every day and make sure they get enough sunshine to grow.
