Plants that Climb: Climbers

  • Some plants have weak stems.
  • They need support to grow up.
  • These are called Climbers.

🎙️ Imagine a baby trying to stand up, it needs support, right? Climber plants are like that. Their stems are too weak to stand on their own, so they hold onto other plants or walls to grow upwards.

⬆️
A plant climbing up

Examples of Climbers

  • Money plant makes our homes green.
  • Pea plant gives us yummy peas.
  • Grapevine gives us sweet grapes.

🎙️ You might have a money plant in your house; it wraps around a stick or string to grow. Pea plants and grapevines also climb up to reach the sunlight and bear fruits.

Plants that Creep: Creepers

  • Some plants also have weak stems.
  • But they spread on the ground.
  • These are called Creepers.

🎙️ Unlike climbers that go up, creepers spread out. Their fruits are often heavy, so they cannot climb. They just lie down and spread themselves along the ground.

↔️
A plant spreading sideways
14 / 20

Examples of Creepers

  • Watermelon grows big on the ground.
  • Pumpkin also creeps on the ground.
  • Muskmelon is another creeper.

🎙️ Think of a big, round watermelon. It's too heavy to hang from a climbing plant, so it grows on a creeper on the ground. Pumpkins and muskmelons are also examples of creepers.
