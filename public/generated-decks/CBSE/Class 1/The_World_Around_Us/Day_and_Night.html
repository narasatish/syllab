Why Do We Have "Day" and "Night"?

  • Our Earth spins like a top.
  • One part faces the Sun (Day), the other is away (Night).

🎙️ The Earth, where we live, is always spinning! As it spins, one side gets sunlight (day), and the other side is in shadow (night). This happens all the time.

The "Cycle" of Day and Night!

  • Morning: Sun rises, we wake up.
  • Daytime: Sun is high, we play.
  • Evening: Sun sets, we prepare for night.
  • Night: Moon and stars, we sleep.

🎙️ Day and night follow each other in a beautiful cycle. It's like a story that repeats every single day, bringing new adventures and peaceful rest.
