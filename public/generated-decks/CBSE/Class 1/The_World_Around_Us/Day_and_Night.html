  • The Sun is high in the sky.
  • It is bright and warm.

Why Day and Night Happen

  • The Earth is always spinning.
  • It spins like a top!

🎙️ Have you ever seen a top spin? Our Earth also spins, but very slowly. This spinning causes day and night to happen.

🌍
Spinning Earth
11 / 20

Earth Turns Towards Sun

  • When our part of Earth faces the Sun, it is Day.
  • The Sun's light falls on us.

🎙️ Imagine one side of the Earth getting sunlight. That side has day! We are on that side when it's daytime and we see the Sun.

1Earth turns
2Sun shines on us
12 / 20

Earth Turns Away From Sun

  • When our part of Earth faces away from the Sun, it is Night.
  • The Sun's light does not reach us.

🎙️ As the Earth keeps spinning, our part moves away from the Sun. Then it becomes dark, and it's night for us. That's when we see the Moon and stars.

1Earth keeps turning
2Sunlight goes away
13 / 20

Day and Night Keep Changing!

  • After day comes night.
  • After night comes day again!

🎙️ This happens every single day! Day follows night, and night follows day. It's a never-ending dance that helps us keep track of time.
