Is All Water Safe to Drink?

  • No, not all water is safe to drink.
  • Dirty water can make us sick.
  • Always drink clean and pure water.

🎙️ This is very important, children! If water looks dirty, has a strange smell, or has things floating in it, NEVER drink it. Dirty water has germs that can make you very, very sick. Always ask an elder if the water is safe.

🤢

How to Keep Water Clean

  • Always cover water pots and bottles.
  • Boil water to kill germs if it's not clean.
  • Use a filter to make water pure.

🎙️ To make sure our drinking water is always safe, we must keep it clean. Always keep water covered so dust and insects don't fall in. If the water supply is not very clean, your parents might boil it or use a water filter to make it safe.

🎙️ Water is so important for everything, right? But did you know that clean water is limited? Many people in the world do not have enough clean water. That's why we must treat water like a precious jewel and never waste it.

How to Save Water

  • Close the tap tightly after use.
  • Don't let water run while brushing teeth.
  • Take short showers instead of long baths.

🎙️ We can all help save water every day! When you brush your teeth, close the tap. When you are done washing your hands, make sure the tap is fully closed. These small actions make a big difference!
