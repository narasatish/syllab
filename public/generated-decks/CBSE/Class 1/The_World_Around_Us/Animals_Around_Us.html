Animals That Eat Plants

  • These animals are called herbivores.
  • They eat grass, leaves, fruits, and vegetables.
  • Cows, deer, and rabbits are herbivores.

🎙️ Imagine a cow munching on grass all day. These animals love plants! They get all their nutrients from plants, trees, and fruits.

Plants make their own food from sunlight.
17 / 24

Animals That Eat Other Animals

  • These animals are called carnivores.
  • They hunt and eat other smaller animals.
  • Lions, tigers, and wolves are carnivores.

🎙️ Think of a lion hunting in the jungle. These animals are hunters and rely on other animals for their food. They are very strong and fast.

🍖🍖

Animals That Eat Both Plants and Animals

  • These animals are called omnivores.
  • They enjoy a mix of plants and meat.
  • Bears, crows, and even humans are omnivores!

🎙️ Bears love to eat berries, but they also catch fish. These animals are flexible and can find food from different sources.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing wild animals with domestic animals.
  • Mixing up what different animals eat.
  • Forgetting that all animals are living things.

🎙️ Sometimes, we can get a little confused. Remember that pets live with us, but wild animals live in forests. Also, always think about their food and if they are alive!
