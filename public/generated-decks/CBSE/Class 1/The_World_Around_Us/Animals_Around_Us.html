  • Cats like to sleep and chase mice.

  • We get food and other things from them.

Meet the Cow!

  • A cow lives on a farm.
  • It says: 'Moo! Moo!'
  • Cows give us healthy milk.

🎙️ Cows are gentle giants. They eat grass and spend their days grazing. The milk they give us is used to make butter, cheese, and many other yummy things!

Meet the Hen!

  • A hen lives on a farm.
  • It says: 'Cluck! Cluck!'
  • Hens give us eggs to eat.

🎙️ Hens are small birds that live in coops on farms. They scratch the ground looking for food. The eggs they lay are a very good source of protein for us.

Wild Animals

  • Some animals live in jungles or forests.
  • They are called wild animals.
  • They find their own food and shelter.

🎙️ Wild animals live freely in nature, far away from humans. They are very strong and have to hunt for their food or find plants to eat. We should not disturb them in their homes.

🌳
Forest

Meet the Lion!

  • A lion lives in the jungle.
  • It roars loudly: 'Roar!'
  • Lions are called the 'King of the Jungle'.

🎙️ Lions are powerful hunters with big manes. They live in groups called prides. It's amazing to see them in a zoo or on TV, but always remember they are wild!

🦁
King Lion

Meet the Elephant!

  • An elephant lives in the jungle.
  • It has a long trunk.
  • Elephants are the biggest land animals.

🎙️ Elephants are gentle giants with huge ears and a very useful trunk. They use their trunk to drink water, pick up food, and even spray themselves with water. They are very intelligent.

Big Elephant

🎙️ Just like us, animals need a home to be safe and warm. Each animal has a special place where it lives. A bird builds a nest, and a rabbit digs a burrow.
