How Natural Selection Works

  • Variation exists in populations.
  • More offspring produced than can survive.
  • Competition for resources.
  • Fittest individuals reproduce more.

🎙️ This process involves several key steps: natural variation within a species, overproduction of offspring, competition for limited resources, and differential survival and reproduction of the best-adapted individuals.

Similar Structures, Different Functions: Homologous Organs

  • Same basic structural plan.
  • Different functions due to adaptation.
  • Indicate common ancestry.

🎙️ Homologous organs, like the forelimbs of humans, bats, and whales, share a similar basic bone structure, indicating a common evolutionary origin, even though they perform different functions today.

Different Structures, Similar Functions: Analogous Organs

  • Different basic structural plan.
  • Similar functions due to similar needs.
  • No common ancestry implied.

🎙️ Analogous organs, such as the wings of a bird and the wings of an insect, have different basic structures but perform similar functions, like flight. This shows convergent evolution, not common ancestry.

Early Ideas: Lamarck's Theory of Acquired Traits

  • Proposed by Jean-Baptiste Lamarck.
  • Acquired traits are inherited.
