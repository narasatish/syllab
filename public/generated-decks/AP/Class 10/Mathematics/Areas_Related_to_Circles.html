Revisiting the Circle

  • Collection of points equidistant from a center
  • Center: The fixed point
  • Radius (r): Distance from center to any point on circle
  • Diameter (d): 2 times the radius (d = 2r)

🎙️ Remember, a circle is a set of all points in a plane that are at a fixed distance from a fixed point. This fixed point is called the center, and the fixed distance is the radius.

Example 1: Calculating Pizza Area

  • Problem: Find the area of a pizza with radius 14 cm.
  • Use π = 22/7
  • Solution: A = πr² = (22/7) × 14² = 616 cm²

🎙️ If a pizza has a radius of 14 cm, its area would be π * (14 cm)² = 22/7 * 196 cm² = 616 cm². This is how much surface the pizza covers.

Understanding a Sector

  • A part of a circle bounded by two radii and an arc
  • Like a slice of pizza or cake
  • Can be a Minor Sector (smaller angle) or Major Sector (larger angle)

🎙️ A sector is like a slice of pizza or cake. It's the region of a circle enclosed by two radii and the arc connecting their endpoints. We can have minor and major sectors.

Area of a Sector

  • A fraction of the total area of the circle
  • Fraction determined by the central angle (θ)
  • Formula: A_sector = (θ/360°) × πr²

🎙️ The area of a sector is a fraction of the total area of the circle. This fraction is determined by the angle at the center. The formula is (θ/360°) * πr², where θ is the central angle.

Length of an Arc

  • A portion of the circumference of a circle
  • Its length depends on the central angle (θ)
  • Formula: L_arc = (θ/360°) × 2πr

🎙️ An arc is a part of the circumference of a circle. Just like the area of a sector, the length of an arc also depends on the central angle. The formula is (θ/360°) * 2πr.

Understanding a Segment

  • A chord is a straight line connecting two points on the circle
  • Can be a Minor Segment or Major Segment

🎙️ A segment is the region of a circle bounded by a chord and its corresponding arc. It's like cutting off a piece of the circle with a straight line.

Area of a Segment

  • Calculated by subtracting triangle area from sector area
  • Area of Sector - Area of Triangle formed by radii and chord
  • Formula: A_segment = (θ/360°)πr² - (1/2)r²sinθ

🎙️ To find the area of a segment, we subtract the area of the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord from the area of the corresponding sector. Formula: Area of Sector - Area of Triangle.

Example 4: Cookie Segment Area

  • Problem: Radius 14 cm, chord subtends 90°.
  • Area of Sector = (90/360) × π × 14² = 154 cm²
  • Area of Triangle = (1/2) × 14 × 14 = 98 cm²
  • Solution: A_segment = 154 - 98 = 56 cm²

🎙️ If a cookie has a radius of 14 cm and a chord cuts off a 90° sector, the area of the minor segment is (Area of Sector - Area of Triangle). This requires calculating the triangle area (1/2 * r * r * sinθ).

Example 5: Square and Circle

  • Problem: Square lawn, side 10m, circular flower bed, radius 3m.
  • Area of Square = 10 × 10 = 100 m²
  • Area of Circle = π × 3² ≈ 28.28 m²
  • Area of lawn around flower bed = 100 - 28.28 = 71.72 m²

🎙️ Imagine a square lawn with a circular flower bed in the center. To find the area of the lawn *around* the flower bed, we subtract the area of the circle from the area of the square.
